## Overview

[PayFast Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/payfast-payment-gateway/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) is an extension for WP Travel Engine that lets you accept trip and tour payments through PayFast, one of South Africa's popular merchant services.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  PayFast Payment Gateway requires the [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) plugin to be installed.
</Callout>

## Requirements

You must have an active merchant account with [PayFast](https://www.payfast.co.za/) to use this payment gateway. You'll need:

- A PayFast Merchant Key
- A PayFast Merchant ID

Payments from any country can be accepted, as long as you use ZAR as the currency.

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the extension" title-type="p">
    Download the extension from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin folder" title-type="p">
    Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin" title-type="p">
    Activate **WP Travel Engine – PayFast Payment Gateway** from the Plugins menu within WordPress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment settings" title-type="p">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select PayFast" title-type="p">
    You'll see PayFast as an option to enable the PayFast Payment Gateway for trip bookings. Click **PayFast** to configure its settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your merchant credentials" title-type="p">
    Enter your Merchant Key and Merchant ID.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the currency to ZAR" title-type="p">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Miscellaneous** and choose South African Rand (R) as the Currency Code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="true">
  Your PayFast Merchant Key.
</ParamField>

<ParamField param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="true">
  Your PayFast Merchant ID.
</ParamField>

Once configured, when a traveler books a trip through the PayFast Payment Gateway, they're taken to PayFast to complete a secure payment.
