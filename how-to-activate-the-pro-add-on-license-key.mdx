## Why license activation matters

Every WP Travel Engine premium add-on requires an activated license to unlock its features. If an add-on's license isn't activated, all of that add-on's features are disabled until you activate it.

To make installation and license activation seamless across all your premium add-ons, WP Travel Engine provides a dedicated plugin called [WP Travel Engine Pro](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/wptravelengine-pro/). You install this one plugin, then use it to activate the license for every premium add-on in your package.

## Download WP Travel Engine Pro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account" title-type="p">
    Go to [WP Travel Engine My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) and log in with the credentials you used during purchase.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visit the WP Travel Engine Pro sales page" title-type="p">
    While logged in, visit the [WP Travel Engine Pro extension's sales page](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/wptravelengine-pro/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the plugin" title-type="p">
    Download the WP Travel Engine Pro plugin to your local computer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Install and activate WP Travel Engine Pro

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to WordPress" title-type="p">
    Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin" title-type="p">
    Navigate to **Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the file" title-type="p">
    Click **Choose File** and select the downloaded `wptravelengine-pro.zip` file.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the plugin" title-type="p">
    Click **Install Now** and wait for the installation to finish.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin" title-type="p">
    Click **Activate Plugin** to enable WP Travel Engine Pro on your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find the Plugin License section

Once WP Travel Engine Pro is activated, go to **Admin Dashboard > WP Travel Engine > Plugin License**. This page lists every premium add-on available for activation.

## Retrieve your license key

Your license key is provided when you purchase a premium add-on package.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account" title-type="p">
    Log in to your [WP Travel Engine account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your license key" title-type="p">
    Navigate to the **License Key History** tab under your profile section and copy the license key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate the license for an add-on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plugin License page" title-type="p">
    On the **Plugin License** page in your WordPress dashboard, find the field next to the add-on you want to activate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste your license key" title-type="p">
    Paste the license key into that field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Activate" title-type="p">
    Click **Activate**. If the license key is valid and hasn't expired, you'll see a confirmation message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  The same license key activates every premium add-on included in your package — you don't need a separate key for each one.
</Callout>

## Install and activate your add-ons

Once the license is active, install and activate each add-on:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the add-on" title-type="p">
    Click **Install Now** next to the add-on you want to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the add-on" title-type="p">
    Once installed, click **Activate** to enable it on your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat for additional add-ons" title-type="p">
    Repeat this process for every add-on included in your package.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage and monitor your license status

You can check the status of all your activated licenses anytime from **Admin Dashboard > WP Travel Engine > Plugin License**.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If a license expires or isn't activated, the corresponding add-on is disabled automatically and its features become unavailable until you renew or reactivate the license. A notification appears at the top of your dashboard when this happens.
</Callout>

## Troubleshoot activation issues

If your license won't activate despite using a valid key, your site's IP may be temporarily blocked by WP Travel Engine's firewall for security reasons.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Raise a [support ticket](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) and the team will check your IP against the firewall system and unblock it if needed.
</Callout>
