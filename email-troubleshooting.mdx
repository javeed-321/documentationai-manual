title: Email Troubleshooting
description: >-
  Diagnose and fix Admin or Enquiry notification emails that aren't being
  delivered from WP Travel Engine.
type: guide
---
You might run into situations where Admin or Enquiry notification emails aren't being delivered to the user or the site admin. One common cause is that the **PHP&#x20;**``**&#x20;function** is disabled or unsupported by your hosting provider, which prevents WordPress from sending emails properly.

<Callout kind="info">
  WP Travel Engine provides the email templates, but the ability to actually send those emails is managed by your hosting provider, not by WP Travel Engine itself.
</Callout>

## Solution

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Check PHP mail functions">
    Confirm that your host has enabled PHP mail functions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up SMTP">
    Configure SMTP using the [FluentSMTP](https://wordpress.org/plugins/fluent-smtp/) plugin for reliable mail delivery. [FluentSMTP's documentation](https://fluentsmtp.com/docs/) walks through setup step by step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact your hosting provider">
    If the steps above don't resolve it, reach out to your hosting provider.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check for plugin conflicts">
    Deactivate all third-party plugins temporarily, then activate only WP Travel Engine and its add-ons (for premium users). Third-party plugin conflicts can cause unexpected site behavior — see [How to Check Plugin Conflicts in WordPress](https://docs.wptravelengine.com/article/how-to-check-plugin-conflicts-in-wordpress/) for details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Clear your cache">
    Caching issues can also block email notifications. Clear your site's cache to rule this out.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify email addresses">
    Check the **Email Settings** under **Admin Dashboard → WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Settings** to confirm the addresses you've entered are correct.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Email-Notification-Settings-1024x379.png" width="1024" height="379" alt="Email settings screen showing Admin and Enquiry notification email fields" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm admin and enquiry notification addresses">
    - **Admin Notification Emails**: make sure the correct address is entered to receive admin notifications (new bookings, payment updates).
    - **Enquiry Notification Emails**: make sure the correct address is entered to receive enquiry notifications (trip questions, booking inquiries).

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Test-Emails-1024x519.png" width="1024" height="519" alt="Send Test Email option in the email settings" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Click **Send Test Email** to verify your email system works before it reaches real customers, then check your inbox for it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  After sending the test email and seeing a success message, check your inbox. If your server is configured correctly, the test email will arrive. If it doesn't, the issue is likely with your server's email configuration.
</Callout>

If the issue persists after working through these steps, reach out to the [support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for further assistance.
