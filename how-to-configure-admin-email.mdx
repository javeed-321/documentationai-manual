The Admin Email feature lets you send email alerts to the admin when a trip is booked or a payment is made. You can enable or disable these notifications according to your preferences.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Screenshot_042825_020143_PM-1024x509.jpg" width="1024" height="509" alt="Admin email notification settings in WP Travel Engine" />

To access the Admin Email template (Booking and Payment Confirmation), navigate to **Dashboard → WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Notification → Admin**.

## Booking Confirmation settings

When you enable the **Notification** option, additional settings become available for customization.

- **Name**: Enter the name of the email, which is basically what the email tries to deliver.
- **Subject**: Enter the subject line for the booking confirmation email. There are different [personalization tags](/email-notifications/email-personalization-tags) available that you can use to personalize it as needed.
- **Email Content**: Customize the email content and use personalization tags to make it more functional.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Screenshot_042825_020904_PM-1024x593.jpg" width="1024" height="593" alt="Booking confirmation email content editor" />

Payment Confirmation is another admin email notification, and you configure it the same way as the settings described above.

## How to add Admin Notification Email

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Emails settings">
    Navigate to **Dashboard → WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your admin email address">
    Add your email address under the **Admin Notification Emails** section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you've added your email, customer notification emails go directly to your inbox.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Admin-Notification-Email-1-1024x415.png" width="1024" height="415" alt="Admin Notification Emails field under Emails settings" />
