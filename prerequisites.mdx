---
title: Prerequisites
description: |
  Before using this add-on, make sure:
---
- You have WP Travel Engine installed and activated on your WordPress site (Version 6.5.0 or more)
- You have installed and activated the [Trip Fixed Starting Dates](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/trip-fixed-starting-dates/) add-on
- You have added at least one trip.
