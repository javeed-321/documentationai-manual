## What Pro adds to the free version

The free version of WP Travel Engine is a solid starting point for building a travel website, but [WP Travel Engine Pro](https://wptravelengine.com/pricing/) unlocks the advanced features and tools needed to run a professional travel business efficiently.

## Key benefits of WP Travel Engine Pro

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Advanced booking features" href="#" icon="calendar-check" horizontal="false">
    Conditional pricing for group or seasonal discounts, fixed trip start dates for guided tours, partial payment and deposit support, and multi-day tours with flexible date availability.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Enhanced payment options" href="#" icon="credit-card" horizontal="false">
    Accept payments through premium gateways like Stripe and PayPal Pro, beyond the free version's standard PayPal and bank transfer options.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Customizable trip and booking forms" href="#" icon="file-edit" horizontal="false">
    Add custom fields such as passport details or dietary preferences, with advanced logic for a fully tailored booking experience.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Premium themes and extensions" href="#" icon="palette" horizontal="false">
    Access Pro-only themes like [Travel Monster Pro](https://wptravelengine.com/wordpress-travel-themes/travel-monster-pro/) with modern layouts and advanced galleries, plus exclusive add-ons such as Trip Reviews, Multi-Currency, and Wishlist.
  </Card>
</Columns>

### Priority support and lifetime updates

Pro customers get dedicated [support](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) with faster response times, along with regular updates, new features, and security patches.
