## What Altus AI does

Running trips through WP Travel Engine involves two time-consuming tasks: writing good content for every trip you list, and logging into WordPress just to check or update small things like bookings. Altus AI connects AI directly to your WP Travel Engine site to take some of that work off your hands.

Altus AI ships as two independent modules. You choose which one, or both, you actually need:

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Content Generation" href="/altus-ai/content-generation" icon="pen-line" horizontal="false">
    Writes the overview, highlights, day-by-day plan, inclusions/exclusions, and FAQs for a trip. You fill in a few basic details, the AI drafts the content inside the trip editor, and you review and edit before publishing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="MCP" href="/altus-ai/mcp-connection" icon="plug-zap" horizontal="false">
    Short for Model Context Protocol. Connects an AI assistant like Claude or ChatGPT directly to your site, so you can ask it to check bookings or update data instead of clicking through the WordPress dashboard.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The two modules work independently — enabling one doesn't require the other, and you can switch either off later without affecting the other.

This guide covers installing Altus AI and turning on whichever module(s) you want. Once a module is active, a separate, more detailed guide walks you through configuring that module.

## Before you start

Confirm your site meets these requirements before installing Altus AI:

- **WordPress version 7.0** or newer
- **WP Travel Engine version 6.8.5** or newer
- **Altus AI installed and activated** with a valid license key

## Purchase and activate your Altus AI license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a pricing plan" title-type="p">
    Altus AI offers three plans:

    - **Solo** — 1 site license
    - **Business** — 10 site licenses (5 + 5 bonus during the launch offer)
    - **Agency** — Multiple site licenses for agencies

    Select **Get Started** for Solo or Agency, or **Get Professional** for Business, to continue to checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your purchase" title-type="p">
    On the checkout page:

    1. Review your order.
    2. Enter your email address, first name, and last name.
    3. Choose a payment method.
    4. Complete the payment.

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      If you don't already have a WP Travel Engine account, one is created automatically using the email address you enter at checkout.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your license key" title-type="p">
    After checkout, you're redirected to the Purchase Confirmation page. Under **License Keys**, copy the license key for your Altus AI plan.

    <Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
      Your license key is also emailed to you. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your license key later (optional)" title-type="p">
    To retrieve your license key at any time:

    1. Sign in to your WP Travel Engine account.
    2. Open **My Account**.
    3. Go to the **License Key History** tab.
    4. Copy the license key for your Altus AI license.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate your license on your site" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard:

    1. Go to **Altus AI > License**.
    2. Paste your license key into the **License Key** field.
    3. Click **Activate**.

    Once activation succeeds, you'll see:

    - Status: Connected
    - Plan
    - Expiration Date
    - Sites Used

    <Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
      If activation fails, confirm you copied the entire license key without extra spaces, and that you're activating it on a site covered by your license.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Verify the installation

1. Log in to your WordPress dashboard.
2. Look at the left-hand menu — you should see **Altus AI** listed there.
3. Click it to open the **Modules** page, the main screen for the plugin.

## Turn on a module

Repeat these steps for each module you want to use:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Modules page" title-type="p">
    Go to **Altus AI > Modules**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate the module" title-type="p">
    Find the box for **Content Generation** or **MCP**, whichever you want to enable.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the switch" title-type="p">
    Click the switch on that module's box.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your settings" title-type="p">
    Scroll down and click **Save Settings** in the bottom-right corner.

    <Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
      This step matters — clicking the switch alone doesn't save your change.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm it's active" title-type="p">
    Once saved, the box now reads **Active**, and a **Configure** link appears so you can set up that module.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The two modules don't depend on each other. Turn on just one, both at once, or switch either off later without affecting the other.
</Callout>

## Configure the AI provider

Each module needs a working AI provider connection before it can generate anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Providers" title-type="p">
    Go to **Altus AI > AI Providers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a provider" title-type="p">
    Pick **Anthropic**, **Google**, **OpenAI**, or **OpenRouter**. Each provider shows its own status badge until connected.

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      To use Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI, you'll need a separate companion plugin. Clicking Install redirects you to that plugin's install page.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the companion plugin (if prompted)" title-type="p">
    Click **Install**, then return to the Altus AI page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your API key" title-type="p">
    Click the provider to expand it, then enter your API key.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test the connection" title-type="p">
    Test the connection to confirm the key works and to pull in that provider's model list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the provider settings" title-type="p">
    Save the settings once the connection test succeeds to apply the provider configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Consider OpenRouter if you'd rather manage one key that reaches several models instead of a separate key per provider.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="Altus AI doesn't show up in the menu at all" default-open="true">
  Go to **Plugins** and confirm Altus AI is activated there. Then double-check that your license key was entered and activated under **Altus AI > License**.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="The module still says Inactive after I turned the switch on" default-open="true">
  Make sure you clicked **Save Settings** at the bottom of the Modules page. The switch by itself doesn't save anything until you do.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="I don't see a Configure link" default-open="true">
  The Configure link only appears after a module shows as Active. Try refreshing the page after saving.
</Expandable>

## FAQs

<Expandable title="Do I have to turn on both modules?" default-open="false">
  No. Turn on whichever one(s) you actually plan to use. They work completely independently of each other.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Will this cost me extra money on top of what I already pay?" default-open="false">
  That depends on which module you use and how. Content Generation uses whatever AI provider you connect (like Anthropic or OpenAI), and you're billed by that provider directly, not by Altus AI. See each module's guide for more detail on costs.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What version of WP Travel Engine do I need?" default-open="false">
  Version 6.8.5 or newer. If you're on an older version, update WP Travel Engine before installing Altus AI.
</Expandable>
