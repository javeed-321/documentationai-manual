## Overview

Reviews are crucial for both developers and potential users. To leave a review for the WP Travel Engine plugin on [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/), you need a WordPress.org account.

<Callout kind="info">
  If you don't have a WordPress.org account yet, create one before continuing. The steps below assume you're already logged in.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- A WordPress.org account
- WP Travel Engine installed on your site (recommended, so your review reflects real usage)

## Submitting your review

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Find the WP Travel Engine plugin reviews page">
    Go to the [WP Travel Engine reviews page](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/wp-travel-engine/reviews/) on WordPress.org.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new review">
    Click **Add your own review**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rate the plugin">
    In the review form, select a star rating and write your review.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit your review">
    Click **Submit**. Your review is sent and may take some time to appear on the plugin page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verifying your review

Revisit the [WP Travel Engine reviews page](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/wp-travel-engine/reviews/) after a short while to confirm your review has been published.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If you appreciate the [WP Travel Engine](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-travel-engine/) plugin, share a detailed review and consider giving it a 5-star rating — reviews help other tour operators discover the plugin.
</Callout>

Contact our [support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for any other assistance.
