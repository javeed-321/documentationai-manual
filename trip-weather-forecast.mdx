## Show weather on your trip pages

Trip Weather Forecast lets you show live weather details on each trip page. That helps travelers picture the experience before they book.

For example, you might show mountain weather on a trek page, city conditions on a day tour, or a short forecast for a multi-day package during peak season. The add-on lets you pick from more than 800,000 locations worldwide, so each trip can show weather that matches the destination.

## What this add-on gives you

- **800,000+ Location Support:** Choose from over 800,000 global locations to display accurate weather data for each trip.
- **Real-Time Weather Updates:** Weather data updates automatically every minute for accuracy.
- **Customizable Widget:** Add a weather widget to your trip pages with custom title and header options.
- **Hourly & Daily Forecasts:** Display up to 5 hours of hourly forecast and 3 days of daily forecast.
- **Per-Trip Configuration:** Select a specific city for each trip individually from the trip edit screen.
- **Hide Option:** Hide the weather widget on selected trips if needed.
- **Seamless Integration:** Fully integrates with WP Travel Engine trips without requiring custom coding.

This gives you one more useful detail on the trip page without extra manual updates.

## What you'll need before you start

A plugin is a small tool you add to WordPress to give your site more features. A widget is a content block you place in an area of your site, such as a sidebar.

Before you set up Trip Weather Forecast, make sure you have:

- WP Travel Engine plugin v5.7.6 or later installed and activated.
- Trip Weather Forecast add-on installed and activated with a valid license key.
- A weather API account created. An API key is a unique code that connects your site to the weather service.
- The API key saved in **WP Travel Engine → Settings → Extensions → Trip Weather Forecast**.
- At least one trip created, so you have somewhere to assign a weather location.
- The WP Travel Engine sidebar active wherever you plan to place the widget.

Once those pieces are ready, you can move through the setup without guessing.

## Add Trip Weather Forecast to a single trip page

The add-on gives you two places to work. You will add the Trip Weather Forecast widget in the widgets area. You will also choose a location for each trip inside that trip's edit screen.

In the widget area, you can set things like unit, language, current weather conditions, and hourly and daily forecasts. Then, inside each trip, you type a city name and choose the correct location from the dropdown list.

For example, you could show Pokhara weather on an Annapurna trek page and keep Kathmandu weather on a city day tour page. That keeps each trip page matched to the destination your traveler is viewing.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The widget does not yet support locations that do not appear in the dropdown.
</Callout>

### Add the Trip Weather Forecast widget

This part controls how the weather box appears on your trip pages. You will choose where it sits and what forecast details it shows.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the WP Travel Engine Sidebar widgets" title-type="p">
    A sidebar is the area on your site where widgets appear. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Appearance → Widgets → WP Travel Engine Sidebar** and click the **+** icon.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032141822-oskr2tdtsjs-trip-weather-forecast-appreance-widgets.trip-weather-forecast-appreance-widgets?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="910" height="709" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find and add the widget" title-type="p">
    This adds the weather block to the sidebar area used by your trip pages. Type **trip weather forecast** in the search box, then click the **Trip Weather Forecast** widget.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032178026-i37t2y993r-trip-weather-forecast-adding-widget.trip-weather-forecast-adding-widget?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="737" height="809" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the title and header" title-type="p">
    This helps travelers understand what they are looking at. Enter a title in the **Title** box to show a heading above the widget, such as "Weather Forecast". Leave it blank if you do not want a heading.

    You can also enter text in the **Header** box to show a custom label inside the widget, such as "New Delhi".

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032243088-xx08gdp4sp-trip-weather-forecast-display-header.trip-weather-forecast-display-header?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="724" height="493" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure forecast ranges" title-type="p">
    This controls how much forecast information your traveler sees at a glance. Use the dropdown menus in the **Hourly** and **Daily forecast** sections to show or hide forecasts for up to 5 hours and 3 days.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032363314-z197kyb5nub-trip-weather-forecast-hourly-and-gaily-forecast.gif?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="824" height="706" alt="" />

  <Step title="Save the widget" title-type="p">
    This makes your widget settings live. Click **Update** in the top right corner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Now the weather widget is in place. Next, you will tell each trip which city it should use.

### Configure weather for each trip

This part links a specific destination to a specific trip. That is what makes one trip show Everest-region weather and another show weather for a city or trailhead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trip's weather configuration" title-type="p">
    Go to one of your trip pages and click **Edit**. Then scroll down to the **Trip Weather Configuration** section in the right sidebar.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032400053-a97qmj7ctv-trip-weather-forecast-trip-settings.trip-weather-forecast-trip-settings?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="910" height="512" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the city" title-type="p">
    This tells the widget which destination weather to display. Choose your **City** location from the dropdown menu.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032431377-jterq02i4fd-trip-weather-forecast-trip-settings-location.trip-weather-forecast-trip-settings-location?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="707" height="478" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optionally hide the widget for this trip" title-type="p">
    This is useful when weather is not relevant for a specific package. If you want to hide the weather widget for this trip, toggle **Hide Weather Widget** to **Off**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787032462652-rk1loihyrs-trip-weather-forecast-hide-weather-widget.trip-weather-forecast-hide-weather-widget?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="691" height="417" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes" title-type="p">
    This saves the city and display settings for that trip. Click **Update** in the top right corner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The dropdown menu appears when you type the city name into the input field. If the API key has not been saved yet, the dropdown will not appear and the widget will not display on the trip page.
</Callout>

At this point, the weather widget should be ready on your trip page.
