## Overview

WP Travel Engine allows you to use a PayPal Standard account as your primary payment gateway.

## How to add PayPal Standard

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Log in to your WordPress dashboard">
    Log in to your WordPress Dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to PayPal Standard settings">
    Navigate to **Dashboard > WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > PayPal Standard**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your PayPal or merchant account ID">
    Enter your PayPal or merchant account ID.

    <Callout kind="info">
      Make sure the ID is valid, since this is used to process payments for your trips.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable PayPal Standard as a payment option">
    Navigate to **Dashboard > WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > General**, then check the box to enable PayPal Standard so it appears as a payment option during checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes again">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once completed, PayPal Standard is available as a payment gateway for travelers to use when booking trips on your site.

<Callout kind="alert">
  PayPal Standard currently supports transactions only in USD (United States Dollar). Other currencies will not work for receiving payments through this integration.
</Callout>
