The Enquiry Email feature lets you manage email notifications for customer inquiries. This includes setting up the notification customers receive when they submit an enquiry.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-27-1024x490.png" width="1024" height="490" alt="Enquiry email settings in WP Travel Engine" />

## Enquiry Email settings

When you enable the **Notification** option, additional settings become available for customization.

- **Name**: Enter the title of the email, which reflects the purpose of the message being sent.
- **Subject**: Enter the subject line for the inquiry confirmation email. There are different [personalization tags](/email-notifications/email-personalization-tags) available that you can use to personalize it as needed.
- **Email Content**: Customize the email content and use personalization tags to make it more functional.
