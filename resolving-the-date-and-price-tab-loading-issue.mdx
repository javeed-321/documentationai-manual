  Fix a Date and Price tab that keeps loading while editing or adding a price by
  switching your WordPress Permalink structure to Post name.

---
Sometimes the **Date and Price** tab keeps loading indefinitely while you're editing a trip or adding a new price to it.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/image-11-1024x491.png" width="1024" height="491" alt="Date and Price tab stuck in a loading state while editing a trip" />

## Solution

This issue is caused by the permalink structure selected in your WordPress dashboard.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open your Permalink settings">
    Go to **Settings → Permalinks** in your WordPress admin dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the Post name structure">
    Under **Permalink structure**, select **Post name**.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/image-12-1024x468.png" width="1024" height="468" alt="WordPress Permalink settings page with the Post name option selected" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If **Post name** is already selected, click **Save Changes** anyway and check whether the issue persists. Re-saving the permalink structure regenerates your site's rewrite rules, which often resolves the error on its own.
</Callout>

## Still having trouble?

If the Date and Price tab still won't load after following these steps, contact the [WP Travel Engine support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for further assistance.
