## Start here

WP Travel Engine turns your WordPress website into a travel booking website.

A plugin is an add-on you install inside WordPress to give your site new features. With WP Travel Engine, you can add trips with a detailed itinerary and let travelers enquire about and book those trips on your website.

You can also organize the trips you offer by destinations, activities, and trip types. For example, you might group a 1-day city tour under one destination and a 10-day trek under another, so visitors can find the right trip faster.

WP Travel Engine also helps you manage your customers from your WordPress dashboard. The WordPress dashboard is the admin area you see after logging in to your site. You can view how many bookings a customer has made, manage bookings, manage inquiries, manage customers, and define your own payment method.

If this is one of your first WordPress setups, that is fine. You do not need to learn everything at once. Start with the basics below, then move through the onboarding screen and Home area one step at a time.

## Install the plugin and open onboarding

What you'll need before you start: a WordPress site where you can log in to the dashboard and activate plugins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate WP Travel Engine" icon="play-circle" title-type="p">
    After you activate the plugin, WP Travel Engine sends you straight to the onboarding screen. That saves you from hunting through menus on your first visit.

    Once activated, you'll be redirected to the **WP Travel Engine Onboarding** screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use onboarding for the first setup" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    If you're a first-time user, activating the plugin redirects you to the **WP Travel Engine Onboarding** page. This screen walks you through the basic settings needed to make your site ready for trips, inquiries, and bookings.

    You are not locking everything in forever here. You can fine-tune many of these settings later as your tour business grows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open the Home screen

The Home screen gives you one place to find help, videos, updates, and basic site information.

In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Dashboard > WP Travel Engine > Home**. The dashboard menu sits on the left sidebar after you log in to WordPress.

The Home screen is organized into these tabs: General, Tutorials, Themes, Go Premium, FAQs, System Info, and What's New. You do not need to use every tab right away, but knowing what each one does will save you time later.

## Know what each Home tab does

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the General tab as your starting point" icon="home" title-type="p">

    This tab includes:

    - An **intro message** explaining what WP Travel Engine does.
    - A **Create a Trip** button to add your first tour or activity.
    - A **Visit our Website** button that opens the official site for themes, add-ons, and resources.
    - An **intro video** - a short YouTube video showing how the plugin works.
    - A **Join the Community** link to the Facebook community for questions and tips.
    - A **Feature Request** link to submit ideas for new features.
    - A **View Knowledge Base** link to the detailed product documentation.

    If you are setting up your first package, start with **Create a Trip**. For example, you might use it to add a weekend wildlife safari or a half-day walking tour.
