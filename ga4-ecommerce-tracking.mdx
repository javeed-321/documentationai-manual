Google Analytics 4, or GA4, is Google's tool for seeing what people do on your website. The GA4 eCommerce Tracking add-on connects your WP Travel Engine booking activity to GA4, so you can see when someone views a trip, starts checkout, and completes a booking.

That means you can follow the booking journey instead of only looking at page visits. For example, if lots of travelers view your Everest Base Camp trek but very few start checkout, you know where to take a closer look.

## Features

- Use your current GA4 setup if it is already connected to your site, or add a new connection if you have not set it up yet.
- Track the key booking moments automatically, including trip views, checkout starts, and completed bookings.
- Import a ready-made GTM container file that already includes the booking events you need.

## What you'll need before you start

A few things need to be ready before you begin. Once these are in place, the setup is much smoother.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  - **WP Travel Engine** is installed and active on your WordPress site.
  - You have a Google Analytics 4 property set up in your Google Analytics account. A property is the place inside Google Analytics where your website's data is collected.
  - You have installed and activated the **GA4 eCommerce Tracking** add-on.
</Callout>

## Connecting GA4 to your site

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This is a global setting. It applies to your whole site, not to one trip at a time.
</Callout>

You only need to set this up once. After that, the add-on sends booking activity from all your trips through the same connection.

### GA4 Connection

This setting asks one simple question. Is GA4 already connected to your website, or do you need to connect it now?

- **Use Existing Connection** - Pick this if GA4 is already connected to your site through another plugin. A plugin is a tool you install in WordPress to add features. One common example is Site Kit by Google.
- **Set Up New Connection** - Pick this if GA4 is not connected yet. This opens the **Connection Type** and **Tracked Events** settings, where you add your details.

Say you already installed Site Kit by Google to see your website traffic. That means GA4 is already connected. Pick **Use Existing Connection** and you're done.

### Tracked Events

These events are the three moments in the booking process that your website automatically reports to Google Analytics.

| Event name       | Trigger                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `view_item`      | Fires when a traveler opens a trip detail page.                                                                                                   |
| `begin_checkout` | Fires when a traveler moves from the booking window to the checkout page after choosing dates, traveler count, and any add-ons.                   |
| `purchase`       | Fires when a traveler's payment goes through and the booking is confirmed. For deposits or installments, this fires once after the first payment. |

Click **Save Settings** after you finish the connection setup.

## What gets tracked

The add-on tracks three points in the booking journey, and it does that in the background while travelers use your site.

- **Traveler views a trip:** This is tracked when someone opens a trip detail page. Use this to spot trips that get attention but do not lead to many bookings. For example, if your Annapurna Circuit page gets lots of views but few checkouts, you may want to review the price, dates, or trip details.
- **Traveler starts checkout:** This is tracked when someone moves from the booking window to the checkout page after choosing dates, traveler count, and add-ons. If people reach this point but do not finish, check your checkout flow, payment options, or add-on pricing such as gear rental or airport pickup.
- **Traveler completes a booking:** This is tracked when payment goes through and the booking is confirmed. This is your main conversion event in GA4. The amount recorded is what the traveler actually paid after discount codes are applied.

A few payment situations are worth knowing before you compare numbers.

Say a traveler books your 5-day Himalayan trek and pays a 20% deposit online. The `purchase` event fires once, right after that deposit payment. Their later installments will not trigger another `purchase` event.

| Payment scenario                              | Tracked?                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full payment                                  | Yes. Tracked once when the full amount is paid.                                 |
| Deposit or first installment                  | Yes, but only after the first payment. Follow-up payments aren't tracked again. |
| Pay Later, bank transfer, or offline payments | No. Only online payments are tracked.                                           |

## Viewing your data in GA4

Once the connection is live, your booking data appears in GA4 under **Reports > Drive Sales > Ecommerce purchases**.

If you want to see the full path from trip view to purchase, use **Explore > Funnel exploration** in GA4. Funnel exploration is a report that shows how many people move from one step to the next, such as viewing a trip, starting checkout, and completing a booking.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  GA4 can take 24 to 48 hours to show new data after you first connect.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### I don't see the GA4 settings page

The add-on may not be activated. Go to **Plugins** in the left sidebar of your WordPress dashboard and check whether the GA4 add-on is active. If it is there but the settings page still does not show, deactivate it and activate it again.

### Nothing is showing up in Google Analytics

First, check that you clicked **Save Settings** after entering your Measurement ID or GTM Container ID. Then remember that GA4 can take 24 to 48 hours to show new data. If it has been longer than that, go back to [Confirming data is flowing](#confirming-data-is-flowing) and run through those checks.

### I'm using Site Kit by Google - which option do I pick?

Pick **Use Existing Connection**. Site Kit already connects your site to GA4, so the add-on sends booking events through that existing connection. You do not need to set up anything new.

### My booking numbers in GA4 don't match my actual bookings

GA4 only tracks online payments. If a traveler booked with **Pay Later**, bank transfer, or another offline method, that booking will not appear as a `purchase` event in GA4. For deposits or installments, the `purchase` event fires once after the first payment.

### The settings page loads but looks blank or broken

A caching plugin may be showing an older version of the page. Clear your cache if you use a caching plugin, then refresh the page. If that does not help, try a different browser or check whether another plugin may be conflicting.

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Do I need both GA4 and Google Tag Manager?" default-open="false">
    No. Pick one connection type. If you are not sure which one to use, **Measurement ID** is the more direct option.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I track additional events beyond the three listed?" default-open="false">
    Not through this add-on directly. If you need custom events, you would manage those separately through your own GTM container setup.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="I already use Site Kit by Google. Will this add-on conflict with it?" default-open="false">
    No. Pick **Use Existing Connection** and the add-on will send booking events through your current GA4 setup.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why don't I see any data in GA4 after connecting?" default-open="false">
    GA4 can take 24 to 48 hours to show new data after you first connect. If it has been longer than that, go back to [Confirming data is flowing](#confirming-data-is-flowing) and check whether events are reaching GA4.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Does switching from Measurement ID to GTM Container keep my old data?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Events that were already sent to GA4 stay in your reports. Switching connection types only changes how future events are sent.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will offline bookings, like Pay Later or bank transfers, show up in my revenue reports?" default-open="false">
    No. Only online payments are tracked as a purchase event, since GA4 can only record payments completed through your site.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
