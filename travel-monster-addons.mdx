Travel Monster Pro ships with a set of premium addons that extend the base Travel Monster theme. This page covers the **Custom Fonts** addon, which lets you upload your own font files for use across the theme's typography options, and the **Notification Bar** addon, which displays an announcement bar in your site header.

You can find and manage both addons from **Admin Dashboard > Appearance > Travel Monster > Addons**.

## Custom Fonts

The Custom Fonts addon lets you upload your preferred custom fonts to your website. Once uploaded, the fonts become available across every typography option provided by the theme, including the site customizer.

### Activating the Custom Fonts addon

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Addons panel">
    Go to **Admin Dashboard > Appearance > Travel Monster > Addons**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Custom Fonts">
    Locate **Custom Fonts** in the addons list and toggle it on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_113218_AM-1024x529.jpg" alt="Custom Fonts addon toggle in the Travel Monster Addons panel" width="1024" height="529" />

### Configuring Custom Fonts settings

Once the addon is active, click **Configure** next to Custom Fonts to open its settings.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_113249_AM-1024x269.jpg" alt="Custom Fonts configuration link in the Addons panel" width="1024" height="269" />

<Steps title-type="h4">
  <Step title="Name the font">
    Enter a font name to identify it in your typography settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a font weight">
    Choose the font weight that matches the file you're uploading.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the font file">
    Upload the font file. Only **.woff2** and **.ttf** formats are supported.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply the new font.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Check [woff2 browser support](https://caniuse.com/#search=woff2) before uploading. If your font is in a different format, convert it first using a tool such as this [OTF to WOFF2 converter](https://cloudconvert.com/otf-to-woff2/).
</Callout>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_112735_AM.jpg" alt="Custom Fonts upload form with font name, weight, and file fields" width="800" height="450" />

After a font is saved, it appears in your font list, where you can replace the font file, adjust the font weight, or delete the font entirely.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_112931_AM.jpg" alt="List of uploaded custom fonts with edit and delete options" width="800" height="450" />

Every font you upload becomes available in the theme's Typography settings, including the site customizer.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_113120_AM.jpg" alt="Custom fonts appearing as options in the theme Typography settings" width="1522" height="787" />

## Notification Bar

The Notification Bar addon displays an announcement bar on your website header — useful for promotions, seasonal offers, or important site-wide messages.

Enable it from **Dashboard > Appearance > Travel Monster > Addons**.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122925_021610_PM-1024x347.jpg" alt="Notification Bar addon toggle in the Travel Monster Addons panel" width="1024" height="347" />

<Callout kind="info">
  For step-by-step Notification Bar configuration, see the [General Settings](/general-settings-2) page.
</Callout>
