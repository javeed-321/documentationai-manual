  **💵 Accepted Currencies**

  DriveWealth only accepts United States Dollars (USD) for deposits and withdrawals.

Customers can fund or withdraw from their brokerage accounts through two primary methods: ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers and Wire transfers. Each method offers distinct benefits depending on the customer's needs for speed, cost, and convenience.

## 🔁 ACH Transfers

**Overview:** ACH is a widely used method for electronically moving money between bank accounts within the United States.

**Processing Time:** Typically 4-5 business days.

**Fees:** Generally low or no fees, depending on the bank. DriveWealth charges a $0.25 fee for outbound ACH transfers

**Eligibility:** Requires a linked U.S. bank account.

## ⚡ Wire Transfers

**Overview:** Wire transfers provide a faster way to move funds electronically between financial institutions.

**Processing Time:** Same business day if initiated before the bank's cutoff time; otherwise, next business day. For Outbound wires, DriveWealth processes these same day if requested prior to 5PM ET.

**Fees:** Typically higher fees, which vary by bank. DriveWealth charges outbound WIRE transfer fee for each individual wire.

**Eligibility:** Requires accurate wiring instructions, including customer's Virtual Account Number details.
