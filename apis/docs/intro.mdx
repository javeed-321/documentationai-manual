DriveWealth is a fully regulated, API-first brokerage infrastructure platform purpose-built for financial institutions, global banks, and technology companies seeking to embed world-class investing experiences into their products.

Trusted by clients across the United States, Latin America, EMEA, and APAC, DriveWealth powers the full investment lifecycle — from account opening and funding to trading, custody, clearing, and regulatory reporting — through a single, modern API integration.

---

## What You Can Build

DriveWealth's platform supports a wide range of investment products and client models, including:

- **Embedded brokerage** — offer self-directed trading of US equities, ETFs, mutual funds, options, and fixed income natively within your existing product
- **Robo-advisory & managed accounts** — automate portfolio construction, rebalancing, and AUM fee collection via AutoPilot
- **Round-up & fractional investing** — DriveWealth pioneered fractional share investing and provides the infrastructure to support micro-investment experiences at scale
- **Retirement accounts** — offer traditional and Roth IRAs alongside standard brokerage accounts
- **Global access** — enable non-US customers to invest in US markets with built-in KYC, AML, and cross-border compliance support

---

## Built for Enterprise

DriveWealth's platform is designed to meet the standards of the world's leading financial institutions:

- **Regulatory compliance** — fully FINRA and SEC registered, with built-in support for KYC, AML, tax withholding (W-8/W-9, 1446(f), 871(m)), and required investor disclosures
- **Reliability at scale** — modern cloud-native architecture built to handle high transaction volumes across global markets
- **Real-time infrastructure** — event-driven notifications via SQS keep your platform in sync with every order, position, and account change as it happens
- **Shared technical support** — every client is assigned a Relationship Manager and has access to our Solutions Engineering team to guide integration and ongoing product expansion
