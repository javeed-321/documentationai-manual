<Callout kind="info">
  **Account management**

  This page discusses features principally used when DriveWealth’s [Account Management product](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/docs/choosing-your-integration-model) is enabled. Clients managing customers on their own won’t have these workflows available.
</Callout>

During the customer onboarding process, DriveWealth utilizes the information sent through the [create User](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/reference/post_users) request to generate either a W9 or W-8BEN tax form. This process happens automatically and requires no additional input or validations by you or the user.

| Customer filing status                               | Forms  |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :----- |
| Domiciled or Citizen of the US, or US Resident Alien | W9     |
| All other customers                                  | W-8BEN |

Tax forms on file are stored as [Physical Documents](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/reference/get_users-userid-documents) and can be retrieved from that API.

## Non-Resident Dividend Tax

When a customer receives a dividend, if they are not a US customer, an amount of that dividend will be withheld and paid to the US IRS based on that customer’s country of tax residency.

For example, if the following dividend gets paid to a customer based in the United Kingdom:

## Publicly Traded Partnership (PTP) Withholding

DriveWealth supports IRS-compliant withholding for U.S.-listed Publicly Traded Partnerships (PTPs) under Section 1446(f) of the Internal Revenue Code.

For non-U.S. persons holding PTPs—such as certain master limited partnerships (MLPs)—DriveWealth automatically handles the 10% withholding on gross proceeds from sales.

The [Account Transactions API](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/reference/get_accounts-accountid-transactions) will have a `PTP_WITHHOLDING` transaction created for orders in scope.

## Non-Resident Withholding Tax on Dividend Equivalent Payments

Under IRS Section 871(m), a withholding tax is applied to Dividend Equivalent Payments (DEPs) received by foreign investors from derivatives or securities that reference dividends paid by U.S. Equities. Certain options transactions are eligible for this withholding.

This withholding is applied on options transactions in certain underlying securities, in cases where the option contract substantially replicates the economic conditions of the underlying security. Specifically, this means it applies on option contracts where Delta = 1.

When a customer holds a position that qualifies for this withholding, if they are not a US customer, an amount of that position value will be withheld and paid to the US IRS.

For example, if the Account holds a position in an eligible security during a dividend payment, and the delta of the held option contract is equal to 1, then a DEEM transaction will be applied to the Account.

## Ongoing tax documentation

Throughout the lifecycle of an account, tax documents are generated as a result of activity incurred and taxes withheld by DriveWealth. All tax forms are calculated based upon the US tax season which is between January 1st, and December 31st.

Generally before the end of February, all tax forms are submitted by DriveWealth to the IRS for processing and acceptance. The day in which end of year forms are made available to end customers varies each year:

| Initial form filed | End of year tax document | Availability time |
| :----------------- | :----------------------- | :---------------- |
| W-9                | 1099-Composite           | March             |
| W-8BEN             | 1042-S                   | April             |

**Not all customer accounts receive an end of year tax form**. To understand if a customer qualifies for a tax form generation they must meet the following thresholds:

- Interest and Dividends exceeds $10, or
- There were sell side transactions throughout the year that exceeded $20, and
- The account is not a tax-advantaged account (e.g. a retirement account)

Review the [Official documents](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/docs/official-documents) section to learn how to retrieve these files.

## W-8BEN information updates

The IRS requires that a customer updates their W-8BEN every 3 years, after the date of account creation. To facilitate the change in information, DriveWealth makes available two methods:

- A hosted website: [DriveClient](https://client.drivewealth.com/login)
- The [User](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/reference/patch_users-userid)  API
- The[`user.inforequired.created`](user.inforequired.created) Event

The primary points of information that need to be either confirmed or changed include:

- Full Legal Name
- Address
- Tax Identification Number

### Events & DriveClient

After subscribing to the [`user.inforequired.created`](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/v1.67.0/reference/user-events#user-info-required-created) **event**, clients should notify the relevant customer to update the above information or in some cases upload a document when the [`user.inforequired.created`](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/v1.67.0/reference/user-events#user-info-required-created) event is generated. . This can be done all via the [DriveClient](https://client.drivewealth.com/login).

After this information is submitted DriveWealth will automatically generate a new tax document on behalf of the customer. A [`taxform.created`](https://developer.drivewealth.com/apis/reference/statements-events#tax-form-created)  will automatically be generated upon completion of the generation.
