## Overview

The Navigation Menu widget lets you display a menu on your pages using Elementor. You can configure the menu source, layout direction, and display options to match your site design.

## General settings

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122425_030514_PM.jpg" alt="Navigation Menu widget general settings in Elementor" width="1304" height="264" />

### Select menu

Choose the menu you want to display from the dropdown. Only menus that exist under **Appearance > Menus** appear in this list.

### Menu direction

Determine the layout direction of the menu items:

- **Row (Horizontal)** – menu items are displayed side by side
- **Column (Vertical)** – menu items are stacked vertically

### Stretch menu

Toggle the **Stretch Menu** option to make the menu span the full width of the container:

- **Yes** – the menu stretches to full width
- **No** – the menu retains its natural width
