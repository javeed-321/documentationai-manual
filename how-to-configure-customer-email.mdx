The Customer Notification feature lets you send email alerts to customers when a booking, payment, account registration, or enquiry takes place. You can enable or disable these notifications according to your preferences.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Screenshot_042825_012120_PM-1024x507.jpg" width="1024" height="507" alt="Customer email settings in WP Travel Engine" />

To access Customer Email, navigate to **Dashboard → WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Customer**.

## Booking Confirmation settings

When you enable the **Notification** option, additional settings become available for customization.

- **Name**: Enter the title of the email, which reflects the purpose of the message being sent.
- **Subject**: Enter the subject line for the booking confirmation email. There are different [personalization tags](/email-notifications/email-personalization-tags) available that you can use to personalize it as needed.
- **Email Content**: Customize the email content and use personalization tags to make it more functional.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Screenshot_042825_012024_PM-1-1024x594.jpg" width="1024" height="594" alt="Booking confirmation email content editor for customer notifications" />

Additional customer email notifications — Payment Confirmation, Account Registration, Forgot Password, and Enquiry — are also available. You configure each of these the same way as the settings described above.
