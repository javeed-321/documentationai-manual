## What an invoice contains

An invoice is a formal record of a purchase made through WP Travel Engine. It documents the package you purchased, the price, and the payment details — useful for expense records, accounting, or reimbursement.

## Download your invoice

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    Visit [WP Travel Engine My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) and log in with your credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Purchase History">
    Go to the **Purchase History** section of your account to see all of your past transactions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your transaction">
    Locate the transaction you need an invoice for and click **View Invoice** next to it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    A pop-up window displays your invoice. From there, download it as a PDF for your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Need help?

If you can't find a transaction or run into any issues generating your invoice, [contact WP Travel Engine support](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for assistance.
