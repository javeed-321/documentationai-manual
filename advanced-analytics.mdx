The Advanced Analytics Addon for WP Travel Engine is a powerful tool designed to give you deep insights into your travel business. It provides interactive visualizations and comprehensive reports of your trip performance, helping you understand customer behavior, optimize offerings, and grow your revenue.

The add-on transforms your raw trip data into meaningful reports. It helps travel business owners like you track performance metrics such as:

- Total number of bookings
- Total earnings
- Most sold trips
- Most booked destinations
- Most popular activities
- Booking trends over time
- And much more

## Installation and activation

### Requirements

- WP Travel Engine version later than 6.5.4.
- A valid license for the Advanced Analytics add-on (Premium).

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the add-on" title-type="p">
    Go to your WordPress **Dashboard → Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**. Upload the `wp-travel-engine-advanced-analytics.zip` file you received, then click **Install Now** and **Activate**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate your license" title-type="p">
    Navigate to **WP Travel Engine → Settings → License**, enter your license key provided at the time of purchase, and click **Activate License**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accessing Advanced Analytics

Once activated:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Analytics dashboard" title-type="p">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine → Analytics** from your WordPress dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the tabs" title-type="p">
    You'll find multiple tabs with data-rich visualizations and filtering options.
  </Step>
</Steps>
