## Menu locations

The Travel Monster theme supports three menu locations: two in the header and one in the footer. You can create and assign menus either from the WordPress Customizer or directly from the dashboard's Menus screen.

<Tabs tabs="From the Customizer,From the Dashboard" default-tab="1">
  <Tab>
    <Steps title-type="h3">
      <Step title="Open the Menus panel">
        Go to **Appearance > Customize > Menus**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Start a new menu">
        Click **Create New Menu**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Name the menu and choose its location">
        Enter a **Menu Name**, select a **Menu Location**, then click **Next**.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-50.png" width="342" height="727" alt="Creating a new menu and selecting its location in the WordPress Customizer" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add items to the menu">
        Click **Add Items** to browse pages, posts, categories, and custom links. Click the **+** icon next to each item you want to add.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-51.png" width="342" height="727" alt="Adding items to a menu in the WordPress Customizer" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Arrange and publish">
        Drag and drop the added items to rearrange them, then click **Publish**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    <Steps title-type="h3">
      <Step title="Open the Menus screen">
        Go to **Appearance > Menus**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Create the menu">
        Enter a **Menu Name** and click **Create Menu**.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-52-1024x405.png" width="1024" height="405" alt="Creating a new menu from the WordPress Menus dashboard screen" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add items to the menu">
        Select items from the Pages, Posts, Custom Links, or Categories panels, then click **Add to Menu**.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-53.png" width="342" height="727" alt="Adding pages, posts, and custom links to a menu" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Arrange the items">
        Drag and drop the added items to rearrange them.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Assign a display location">
        Check the menu's display location — Primary, Secondary, or Footer — depending on where you want the menu to appear.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-54-1024x546.png" width="1024" height="546" alt="Assigning a menu display location in WordPress" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Save the menu">
        Click **Save Menu**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
