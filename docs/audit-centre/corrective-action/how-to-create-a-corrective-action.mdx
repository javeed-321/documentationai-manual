## What is a Corrective Action?

In Scrut, a **corrective action** is a systematic approach to resolving compliance issues, non-conformities, or discrepancies within your organization. It’s designed not only to fix the immediate problem but also to prevent it from recurring. Here are a few example scenarios of when to use corrective actions:

- When you discover compliance non-conformities or discrepancies during regular operations
- When auditors identify areas of non-compliance or deficiencies during the audit process

## How To Create a Corrective Action

In Scrut, you can create a corrective action in three ways:

1. [**Via Audit Findings**](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/creating-corrective-action-from-audit-finding): Address specific non-compliance issues identified by auditors as findings.
2. [**Via Audit Requests**](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-requests/creating-corrective-action-from-audit-request): Respond to auditor requests by creating targeted corrective actions to meet their requirements.
3. [**From the Corrective Actions module**](/docs/audit-centre/corrective-action/creating-a-corrective-action): Create standalone corrective actions through the Corrective Action module for issues not tied to a specific audit.
