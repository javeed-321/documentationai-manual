The **Audit Logs** for the Corrective Action module facilitate easy tracking and monitoring of all actions within the module, thereby enhancing transparency and control. There are two distinct types of audit logs in the Corrective Actions module:

- [Audit Logs for Individual Corrective Actions](/docs/audit-centre/corrective-action/audit-logs-for-corrective-actions#audit-logs-for-individual-corrective-actions)
- [Audit Logs for Corrective Actions Settings](/docs/audit-centre/corrective-action/audit-logs-for-corrective-actions#audit-logs-for-corrective-action-settings)

## Audit Logs for Individual Corrective Actions

This log provides a detailed record of all activities performed on a specific corrective action. To access this log, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Audit → Corrective Action** on the left navigation panel, and select your preferred corrective action.

2. Scroll down and navigate to the **Audit Logs** tab.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-17-at-15.30.58.png" alt="" />

## Audit Logs for Corrective Action Settings

This log captures all activities that occur on the Corrective Action settings page. To access this log, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Audit → Corrective Action** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Settings** icon on the top-right.

   <Image src="/images/settings.png" alt="Settings icon" />

2. Navigate to the **Audit Logs** tab.
