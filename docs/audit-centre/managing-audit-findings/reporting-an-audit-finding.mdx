<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

In Scrut, you can create an audit finding in two ways:

- [Create a Single Finding Manually](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/reporting-an-audit-finding#create-a-single-finding-manually)
- [Bulk Import Multiple Findings](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/reporting-an-audit-finding#bulk-import-multiple-findings)

## Create a Single Finding Manually

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center**.

2. Click on the audit for which you want to create a finding.

   <Image src="/images/Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll and click the **Findings** tab.

4. Click the **Add Findings** button and select **Manually**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1762180790267.png" alt="" />

5. Enter the required details, such as:

   - Select the **Entities** associated with this finding. Choose Organization Wide to associate it with all entities.

   - In the **Finding** field, provide a clear and descriptive name that accurately reflects the issue.

   - Upload any relevant documents by dragging and dropping files or selecting them from your device.

   - In the **Assignees** dropdown menu, select one or more users responsible for resolving the finding, or choose No Assignee if you're unsure who to assign.

   - Choose the **Nature of Finding**:

     - **Major**: Indicates a significant issue that requires immediate attention.
     - **Minor**: Refers to a less critical issue that still requires attention.
     - **Observation**: Use this option for issues that may require further investigation but aren't necessarily findings.
     - **Opportunity for Improvement**: Highlights areas where processes or practices can be enhanced.

   - Select the **department** that the finding belongs to.

   - Use the **description** field to add additional details that may aid in understanding the finding.

     <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-01-13-at-20.15.04.png" alt="" />

6. In the next step, you can [create a corrective action for the finding](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/creating-corrective-action-from-audit-finding). Feel free to skip this if it's not applicable.

   **Note:** You can also create a corrective action later, if required.

7. Next, [link or unlink any relevant controls to the finding](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/linking-controls-to-audit-findings).

8. Finally, review all the information you've entered to ensure accuracy, and then click **Create**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-03-at-20.13.57.png" alt="" />

## Bulk Import Multiple Findings

You can add multiple findings at once by bulk-importing them. Follow these steps:

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center**.

2. Click on the audit for which you want to create a finding.

   <Image src="/images/Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll and click the **Findings** tab.

4. Click the **Add Findings** button, then select **Import via** **CSV/XLSX**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1762333998081.png" alt="" />

5. Click **Download Sample Template**. Replace the contents of the file with your list of findings.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-14.43.43.png" alt="" />

6. Upload the filled template by dragging and dropping it or selecting it from your device.

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     See here for [instructions on filling the template](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/reporting-an-audit-finding#tips-for-using-the-template).
   </Callout>

7. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-14.44.28.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-14.46.45.png" alt="" />

Scrut checks the template file for errors. If any errors are detected, download the spreadsheet from the **Upload Status** message to view them. Fix the spreadsheet and reupload it.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-14.44.35.png" alt="" />

#### Tips for Using the Template

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-01-13-at-20.20.16.png" alt="" />

- Do NOT remove the first row as it contains column headers.
- You can enter the findings from the second row.
- Create a row for each finding.
- Mandatory fields: Finding Title, Nature of Finding, and Department.
- All other fields are optional.
- For the Nature of Finding and Department fields, the values must match those created in Scrut.
- For the Assignees field, enter the assignees' email addresses, separated by commas. Email should match the assignees’ emails in Scrut.
