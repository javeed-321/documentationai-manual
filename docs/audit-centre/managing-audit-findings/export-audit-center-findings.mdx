  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

By exporting audit findings, you can quickly share them with external auditors and other stakeholders who do not have access to Scrut. In this article, we explain how to export audit findings in two ways:

- \#1: [Export Findings Specific to an Audit](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/export-audit-center-findings#1-export-findings-specific-to-an-audit)
- \#2: [Export Findings for all Audits](/docs/audit-centre/managing-audit-findings/export-audit-center-findings#2-export-findings-for-all-audits)

## How To Export Audit Findings

### 1: Export Findings Specific to an Audit

1. Navigate to **Audit → Audit Center** via the left navigation menu.

2. Click the **Audit** tab and open the audit for which you want to export the findings.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161944221-0nzl3zfnfg4-Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Findings** tab and click the **Export** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162205655-89xxu3toufd-image-1762422069065.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The file will be downloaded to your system in CSV format.

### 2: Export Findings for all Audits

If you’re looking to export findings across all audits, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to **Audit → Audit Center** via the left navigation menu.

2. Go to the **Findings** tab and click the **Export** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162206985-cjxmfiohs9-image-1762422113381.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Findings tab" />

The file will be downloaded to your system in CSV format.
