## What are Audit Findings?

**Audit findings** refer to compliance gaps and other discrepancies that auditors discover during an audit. In other words, findings serve as a formal method for auditors to document, categorize, and track their observations during the audit.

## Viewing Audit Findings

The **Findings tab** in the Audit Center serves as a centralized repository for audit findings, enabling auditors and stakeholders to manage and address concerns systematically. It also makes it easy for all relevant parties to quickly view all identified issues. This, in turn, facilitates timely corrective actions and strategic improvements, ultimately enhancing the overall integrity and effectiveness of the audit process.

You can also zero down and view findings specific to an audit. For this, navigate to **Audit → Audit Center** and open your preferred audit. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Findings tab** to view all findings specific to an audit.
