<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut streamlines the process of addressing audit findings by enabling you to create risks directly from within the Audit Center. This cohesive connection between audit findings and risks enhances efficiency and transparency in your audit management process.

## How To Create a New Risk from an Audit Finding

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation menu.

2. Select the audit for which you want to create a risk.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161944221-0nzl3zfnfg4-Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Findings** tab, and open the finding to which you want to add a risk.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166044931-atpr6q3cdis-Selecting-a-Finding.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Alternatively, you can directly navigate to **Audit → Audit Center → Findings** tab and open the finding to which you want to add a risk.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162198870-b9532qd28mv-image-1762335900236.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Findings tab" />

5. Scroll to the **Linked Items** section, click the **Add** button and select **Risk**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162200211-23e0jdb1qy2-image-1762336212430.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Enter the necessary details and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164618986-6fkeuhui0s-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-15-20-49.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Scrut will create the risk, and you can view it under the **Linked Items** section. The risk is hyperlinked; clicking it takes you directly to the created risk in the Risk Management module.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164620401-6jahov7thvv-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-15-22-24.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164621765-zo1wxoqipni-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-15-24-26.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If necessary, you can create and link multiple risks to a single finding.
</Callout>

## How To Link an Existing Risk to an Audit Finding

In addition to creating a new risk, you can also link existing risks to audit findings. Follow these steps:

1. Once you’re on the **Findings** page, scroll to the **Linked Items** section.

2. Click the **Link Existing** button and select **Risk**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162213825-cl1ecqi7oq5-image-1762751997092.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Choose the risks you want to link by clicking the checkboxes next to them.

4. Click **Link**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164635246-hhama5pbski-Screenshot-2025-11-10-at-10-53-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. The selected risks will now be linked to the finding.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164636619-y9hnqlpwoam-Screenshot-2025-11-10-at-10-54-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
