title: "Reopen a Closed Audit Finding"
description: "Learn how to reopen a closed finding."
---

Auditors and admins can reopen closed findings and use the Request Revision button to initiate updates or changes, as needed. This ensures that findings meet compliance requirements without adding unnecessary complexity to the workflow.

## How to Reopen a Closed Audit Finding

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation menu.

2. Select the audit corresponding to the finding.

3. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Findings** tab, and open the closed finding that you want to send for revision.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-06-at-19.45.25.png" alt="" />

4. Click the **Request Revision** button on the top-right of the page.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-06-at-19.45.44.png" alt="" />

5. Enter the reason for requesting a revision, and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-06-at-19.46.11.png" alt="" />

6. This updates the finding’s status from **Closed** to **Needs Revision**.

7. Scrut also notifies the assignees via email. The assignee will be able to view the auditor's reason for the revision.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-06-at-19.46.25.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up!**

  Once the audit is marked as completed, you can no longer request revisions for closed findings.
</Callout>
