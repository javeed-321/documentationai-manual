<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Editing and deleting an audit finding in the audit center allows you to modify or remove a previously recorded observation or issue identified during an audit. This feature allows auditors and authorized users to make necessary updates to the information associated with a specific finding.

## How To Edit an Audit Finding

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation menu.

2. Select the audit corresponding to the finding.

3. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Findings** tab, and open the finding that you want to edit.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166044931-atpr6q3cdis-Selecting-a-Finding.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Alternatively, you can directly navigate to **Audit → Audit Center → Findings** tab and open the finding you want to edit.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162203042-mpcmpk26fe-image-1762420903248.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Findings tab" />

5. Click the edit icon next to the field you want to edit. You can modify the following details:

   - Finding Name
   - Description
   - Assignees
   - Department
   - Nature of Finding

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162204360-0mpf5rq6h34i-image-1762420994591.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Make the necessary changes and click **Save**.

## How To Delete an Audit Finding

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up!**

  Deleting a finding involves permanently removing it from the audit record. So, proceed with caution.
</Callout>

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation menu.

2. Select the audit corresponding to the finding.

3. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Findings** tab, and click the delete icon next to the finding you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162201663-hjj7cuyrpxm-image-1762420835442.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Delete** to confirm.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  When you delete an audit finding, Scrut will unlink the controls linked to it and the status of the controls will revert accordingly.
</Callout>
