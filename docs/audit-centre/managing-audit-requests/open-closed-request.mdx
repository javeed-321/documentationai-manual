---
title: "Reopen a Closed Audit Request"
description: "Learn how to reopen a closed audit request in Scrut."
---

Auditors and admins can reopen closed requests and use the **Request Revision** button to initiate updates or changes, as needed. This ensures that requests meet compliance requirements without adding unnecessary complexity to the workflow.

## How to Reopen a Closed Audit Request

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation menu.

2. Select the audit corresponding to the request.

3. Once you’re on the audit page, scroll to the **Requests** tab, and open the closed request that you want to send for revision.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164664958-ccblqst52me-Screenshot-2025-11-12-at-19-05-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click the **Request Revision** button on the top-right of the page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163286403-8j7n2mhntkr-Request-Revision-for-Closed-Request.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Request Revision for Closed Request" />

5. Enter the reason for requesting a revision, and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164666351-46jo1m2rws6-Screenshot-2025-11-12-at-19-07-07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. This updates the request’s status from **Closed** to **Needs Revision**.

7. Scrut also notifies the assignees via email. The assignee will be able to view the auditor's reason for the revision.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164667720-1ew41goi0mt-Screenshot-2025-11-12-at-19-07-17.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up!**

  Once the audit is marked as completed, you can no longer request revisions for closed requests.
</Callout>
