## Editing an Audit

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click on the audit you want to edit.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.53.14.png" alt="Selecting an audit from the Audit Center" />

3. Click the **three-dots** **icon** on the top-right.

4. Click **Edit Audit**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.52.47.png" alt="" />

5. Make the required changes and click **Update**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.54.41.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.54.54.png" alt="" />

## Deleting an Audit

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click on the audit you want to delete.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.53.14.png" alt="Selecting an audit from the Audit Center" />

3. Click the **three-dots** **icon** on the top-right.

4. Click **Delete Audit**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.57.22.png" alt="" />

5. Click **Delete** to confirm.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.57.36.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-13.05.15.png" alt="" />
