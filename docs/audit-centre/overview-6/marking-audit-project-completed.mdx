<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Once the audit is complete, you must manually mark it as complete.

1. Click **Audit → Audit Center**, and open the audit you want to mark as completed.

2. Click the **Mark Audit Complete** button.

   **Heads Up!** Marking an audit as completed is irreversible. So, review your data carefully before proceeding.

   <Image src="/images/Mark-Audit-Complete.png" alt="" />

3. Click **Mark as Complete** to confirm your action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-07-23-at-12.41.24.png" alt="" />

## What Happens When You Mark an Audit Complete

When you click Mark As Complete, Scrut freezes the audit in its current state as of that date. Future changes you make to frameworks, policies, controls, and artifacts elsewhere in Scrut are not reflected in this audit. This includes:

- **Compliance percentages:** Policy completion, Evidence completion, and Test completion percentages
- **Controls:** All Controls associated with the audit, along with their status
- **Frameworks and Requirements:** The Framework and its Requirements as they existed at completion
- **Artifacts:** The status of linked policies, evidence, and tests at the time of completion

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  The audit, its findings, and its requests become read-only after completion. Corrective actions linked to the audit remain editable.
</Callout>
