---
title: "Create an Audit By Control"
description: "Learn how to create an audit for specific controls."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Scale** plan
</Callout>

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **+New Audit** button and select **By Control** in the dropdown.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.31.21.png" alt="" />

3. Enter a name for the audit.

4. Choose the **Audit Type**:

   - **Internal:** Conducted within your organization
   - **External:** Conducted by a third-party audit firm

5. From the Entities dropdown, select the organizational entity or entities that the audit belongs to.

6. In the **Audit Date** field, select the specific date when the audit will take place.

7. In the **Observation Period** field, select the **Start Date** and **End Date** and set an observation period for the audit project to the period for which you are being audited. The evidence you’ve collected will be scoped based on these dates.

8. Click **Next**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.32.39.png" alt="" />

9. Select the controls by which you want to create the audit. Use the filters and the search bar at the top of the modal to quickly find specific controls. Once you’ve selected the required controls, click **Next**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.33.31.png" alt="" />

10. Click **Create**.

    <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.33.49.png" alt="" />

    <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12.34.08.png" alt="" />

### Note:

After creating an audit by controls, you can view the control details, such as name, code, and description, in the Controls tab within the audit. These represent the specific criteria defined by the selected framework, ensuring clarity and alignment with compliance objectives.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.07.28.png" alt="" />

Click the **Link/Unlink Controls button** to add or remove controls from the audit.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11.09.51.png" alt="" />
