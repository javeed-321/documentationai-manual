---
title: "Revert Non-Personnel Status"
description: "Learn how to revert non-personnel status for employee accounts in Scrut."
---

If you’ve accidentally marked an employee as Non-Personnel, you can restore them to active employee status by following these steps:

## Mark as Personnel

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees** using the left navigation panel.

2. Select the account that you want to revert to personnel.

3. Click the three-dot menu ()icon on the employee profile.

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-4.png" alt="Three-dots menu icon" />

4. Select **Mark as Personnel**.

   <Image src="/images/Mark-as-Personnel.png" alt="" />

5. The account will return to its previous status before it was marked as non-personnel.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-23-at-11.26.24.png" alt="" />

#### Note

Alternatively, you can also perform this action from the **All Employees** table. Click on the **Mark Personnel icon** in the **Actions** column. Enter the reason for reverting in the Justification field and click Save.
