---
title: "Mark an Employee as Non-Personnel"
description: "Learn how to mark an employee as non-personnel in Scrut."
---

In Scrut, the “**Non-Personnel**” tag represents accounts of non-human entities rather than actual employees. Generally, it denotes system accounts, service accounts, automated processes, or placeholder entries. Marking these accounts as non-personnel helps you:

- Streamline compliance tracking by excluding non-human accounts from mandatory training, certifications, and policy acknowledgements.
- Improve reporting accuracy by ensuring compliance metrics reflect only actual employees.
- Reduce administrative overhead by eliminating unnecessary compliance notifications and reminders for system accounts.
- Maintain data accuracy by distinguishing between human users and automated or system entities.

## What Happens When an Account is Marked as Non-Personnel?

When an account is marked as Non-Personnel:

- The account is excluded from all compliance requirements and tracking
- Compliance reports will no longer include this account in calculations or metrics
- Outstanding compliance tasks assigned to this account are removed
- The account will not receive future compliance notifications

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Important:**

  When you mark an employee account as non-personnel, it immediately revokes platform access. They will no longer be able to log in to Scrut.
</Callout>

## Mark an Individual Employee Account

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees** using the left navigation panel.

2. Select the employee account that you want to mark as non-personnel.

3. Click the three-dot menu ()icon on the employee profile.

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-4.png" alt="Three-dots menu icon" />

4. Select **Mark as Non-Personnel**.

   <Image src="/images/Three-dot-menu-actions-on-employee-profile.png" alt="" />

5. Enter a reason in the **Justification** field.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-23-at-11.24.23.png" alt="" />

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-23-at-11.24.34.png" alt="" />

## Mark Multiple Employee Accounts (Bulk)

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees** using the left navigation panel.

2. Select the checkboxes for employee accounts that you want to mark as non-personnel.

3. Click the **Edit icon** ( )and select **Mark as Non-Personnel**.

   <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Edit-icon-for-multiple-employees.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-23-at-11.27.46.png" alt="" />

**Note:** When marking multiple employees as Non-Personnel, Scrut automatically adds the justification “Not an Employee” for all accounts.
