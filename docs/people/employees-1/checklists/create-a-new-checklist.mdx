---
title: "Create a New Checklist"
description: "Learn how to create a new employee checklist in Scrut."
---

Follow these steps to create a new checklist:

1. Navigate to **People → Employee** module, click the **Settings** icon on the top-right.

   <Image src="/images/image-1763444000730.png" alt="" />

2. Navigate to the **Checklists** tab.

3. Click the **Add Checklist** button.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Checklist-Button.png" alt="" />

4. Enter the name for your checklist.

5. Configure required ongoing and offboarding tasks.

6. Review the selections you made in the previous steps and save your changes.

7. The new checklist will be visible in the **People → Employee → Settings → Checklists** tab.
