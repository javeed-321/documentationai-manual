---
title: "Create an Empty Checklist"
description: "Use empty checklists to create placeholder campaigns in advance."
---

Scrut provides you the flexibility to create and assign checklists without any tasks initially included. This is useful when:

- Setting up campaigns in advance
- Creating placeholder assignments for future task distribution
- Managing employees with custom or pending task assignments

## How to Create an Empty Checklist

1. Navigate to **People → Employees** and click the **Settings** icon on the top-right.

   <Image src="/images/Employee-Settings.png" alt="" />

2. Click the **Checklists** tab and click the **Add Checklist** button.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Checklist-Button.png" alt="" />

3. Enter a suitable name for your checklist.

4. Leave all tasks unchecked and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-21.56.48.png" alt="" />

Next, you can assign this empty checklist to specific employee groups, as needed.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-21.57.22.png" alt="" />

### Employee Experience

Employees assigned to empty checklists will see a **"No Tasks Assigned"** message on their employee portal. This provides clarity rather than leaving them uncertain about their task status.

## Finding Employees Without Tasks

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees** and click the **Filter** button.

   <Image src="/images/All-Employees-Page.png" alt="" />

2. In the filter menu, scroll to **Task Status** and select **No Tasks Assigned** and click **Apply Filters.**

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-22.04.38.png" alt="" />

This displays all employees with empty checklists. This makes it easy to identify who needs task assignments.
