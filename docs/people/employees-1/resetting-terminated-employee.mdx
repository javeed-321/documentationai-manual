---
title: "Reset Terminated Employee"
description: "Learn how to reset an employee's termination status in Scrut."
---

Use this option to revert an employee’s termination status if it was marked in error.

1. Go to **People → Employees** in the left navigation panel.
2. Select the employee who was marked as “Terminated.” The employee status should be “Offboarding Needed.”
3. Click the three-dots menu in the employee profile.
4. Select **Reset Termination**.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-22-at-21.14.18.png" alt="" />

#### What Happens Next?

The employee’s status will revert to “Active.”
