---
title: "Reset Employee Offboarding"
description: "Learn how to reset employees' offboarding status in Scrut."
---

You may need to reset an employee's offboarding status in several scenarios:

- The offboarding process was marked as complete prematurely or by mistake
- A former employee is returning and needs their status updated
- Changes in employment decisions require reversing the offboarding

In this article, we explain how to reset employee offboarding.

1. Go to **People → Employees** in the left navigation panel.

2. Select the employee who was marked as “**Offboarding Completed**.”

3. Click the **Reset Offboarding** button in the employee profile.

#### What Happens Next?

After clicking Reset Offboarding:

- The employee's status automatically changes from "Offboarding completed" to "Offboarding Needed."

- You can now reset the employee’s termination status.

- Alternatively, you can complete the rest of the offboarding process, like uploading offboarding documents, and then mark it as Offboarding Completed, if needed.
