Organizations assign policies to employees to maintain compliance and handle data responsibly. It’s the responsibility of employees to review and accept each assigned policy.

## How To Accept a Policy Assigned to You

- **Log in to the Scrut Platform:** Access the Scrut platform, which will direct you to the Employee Portal. Refer to the Employee Portal Login Guide for detailed instructions.

  <Image src="/images/image-1783572717430.png" alt="" />

- **Navigate to List of Policies:** In the Employee Portal, locate the "List of Policies" section, where you'll find a comprehensive list of policies assigned to you by your organization.

<Image src="/images/image-J8J9IN8B.png" alt="" />

- **Policy Details:** Click on the name of any specific policy to delve into its details and understand its requirements.
- **Review and Accept and continue:** After reviewing the policy, you'll find an option to accept it. Click on the "Accept" option to confirm your acceptance of the policy and continue to the next policy to accept.

<Image src="/images/image-AI17F9VS.png" alt="" />

- **Status Update:** Once you accept a policy, the status will be updated to "Accepted," indicating that you have acknowledged and agreed to comply with the specified policy.

<Image src="/images/image-K1D24ZS3.png" alt="" />
