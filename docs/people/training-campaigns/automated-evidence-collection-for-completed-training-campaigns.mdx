Automated Evidence Collection eliminates manual work by automatically gathering and organizing evidence when your training campaigns are complete. Once a campaign reaches 100% completion or its status changes to Completed, the system instantly populates relevant evidence in your evidence task section.

- **Save Time**: Skip manual evidence gathering
- **Ensure Compliance**: All necessary documentation is captured automatically
- **Stay Organized**: Evidence is filed correctly in the appropriate evidence tasks
- **Maintain Accuracy**: Scrut automatically verifies and updates evidence as needed

## How to Enable Automated Evidence Collection for Training Campaigns

1. Click **People → Training Campaigns** on the left navigation panel and choose a specific training campaign.

2. Click the **Settings** tab and turn ON the **Automatically Generate Evidence** toggle.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-tab-for-a-specific-training-campaign.png" alt="" />

### What Happens Next?

When the training campaign reaches either:

- 100% completion rate, or
- Completed status

Scrut automatically gathers and attaches the evidence for the following evidence tasks:

- Training Campaign Report
- Refresher Training on ISMS

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What happens if I enable this toggle for already completed campaigns?" default-open="true">
    If you enable automated evidence for a campaign that's already marked as Completed:

    - The system checks for existing attachments
    - If changes are detected, evidence is updated automatically
    - If everything is current, no action is taken
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
