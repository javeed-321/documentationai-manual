Scrut provides in-platform [training campaigns](/docs/people/training-campaigns/understanding-security-awareness#what-is-training-campaigns) you can customize to educate your employees on critical cybersecurity topics, ensuring compliance with industry standards.

This article will guide you through the step-by-step process of creating a training campaign in Scrut.

## Step 1: Enter Campaign Details and Select Training Material

### Create a Campaign

[Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and go to **People → Training Campaigns** using the left navigation panel. Click the **Add Campaign** button at the top right.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161810531-rwjc2u1qkh-Add-Campaign.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Enter Campaign Name and Description

Enter a suitable **Campaign Name**, such as ISO 27001 training, HIPAA training, etc. Optionally, in the **Description** field, enter a short description of the campaign.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163684021-3y11rhhw8mq-Screenshot-2025-06-18-at-18-50-41.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Choose the Training Materials

A campaign can include multiple training materials. Scrut provides a variety of training resources and quizzes that you can easily choose from.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  When creating or editing an employee training campaign, only training modules linked to your purchased frameworks will be displayed. This ensures you see training options relevant to your frameworks.
</Callout>

Here’s a breakdown of how you can use these materials:

1. **Default Materials:** Scrut comes with a set of pre-existing training materials and quizzes. You can select the ones that best fit your campaign needs.
2. **Customization Options:** Even when selecting standard materials, you can still edit the questions within them. You can also customize the passing criteria to align with your unique requirements, ensuring that the training meets your specific goals.
3. **Adding Custom Training Materials:** You can also include your custom training materials in the campaign.

### Schedule Campaign

Select the following dates:

- **Launch Date & Time:** Select the date and time when you want the campaign to begin.
- **Timezone:** Select the appropriate timezone for when the campaign will go live. Set this according to your target audience’s availability.
- **End Date & Time:** Select the date and time to close the campaign. If you’re running an evergreen campaign without a designated end date, ensure that the checkbox labeled '**This campaign does not have an end date**' is selected. Evergreen campaigns are ideal when you want all future employees to be automatically assigned this training.
- **Campaign SLA:** \[Optional\] You can specify the campaign due date in days. The SLA is the number of days within which employees must complete the training to avoid an **Overdue** status. While employees can still complete the training after the SLA, Scrut will mark their status as **Completed (Late)**.

## Step 2: Review Information

Take a moment to carefully review all the information you entered in the previous step. If you notice any errors or details that need updating, use the **Back** button to return to that section and make the necessary changes. Once you are satisfied with your entries and everything looks correct, click on the **Next** button to proceed with creating your campaign.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163687924-rmfxw5zjwc-Screenshot-2025-06-18-at-19-13-50.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Step 3: Add to Checklist

Once you’ve created the training campaign, the final step is to add it to a checklist so it’s assigned to specific employee groups. Select the checklists you want to add the campaign to. You can add a campaign to more than one checklist. Click **Save & Publish Campaign**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163689339-fhbs1tvqkt-Screenshot-2025-06-18-at-19-14-21.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

If you decide not to add the campaign to a checklist right away, you can click **Skip For Now**. Later, you can navigate to **People → Training Campaigns** and select the campaign you want to work with. Once you have your preferred campaign open, click the **Add Checklist To Publish** button located at the top right of the screen.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163690655-jabjxpe91pk-Screenshot-2025-06-18-at-19-20-39.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The final step is to assign the checklist to an employee group, and the campaign will become part of the tasks that employees in that group must complete. Refer to the [groups guide](/docs/people/employees-1/groups#creating-and-managing-groups) to learn how to assign a checklist to a specific employee group.

## FAQs & Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I preview the training materials before assigning them to employees?" default-open="true">
    Yes, when creating the campaign, click the eye icon to view the training material PDF and verify the information before sharing it with your employees. You can replace a default PDF with a customized one according to your organizational requirements.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162099704-4p2r10ux1ou-eye-12109.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" width="31" height="31" />
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="If a new employee joins after the ISMS training is completed, will they be added automatically?" default-open="true">
    Yes. As long as the campaign is not complete or is an evergreen campaign, the new employee will be automatically assigned the campaign, provided you have added it to the appropriate checklists. Scrut’s integration with the identity provider fetches the employee list regularly, and new employees will be automatically assigned to ongoing training campaigns.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I add more training materials to the training deck?" default-open="true">
    Yes. While creating the campaign, choose **Document** and click **+Add Training** to add more training materials, as required.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if an employee does not pass the training?" default-open="true">
    If an employee does not pass a training, their status changes to **Failed** in the **People → Employee → Trainings** section. The training will be marked as **Due** in the **People → Training Campaigns → Employees** section until the employee completes it with a passing score.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can an employee retake a quiz if they’ve failed it?" default-open="true">
    Yes. If an employee has failed the quiz, they can retake it as many times as needed until they get a passing score.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if an employee completes the training past the deadline (SLA)?" default-open="false">
    If an employee completes past the deadline (SLA), they will have a status “Completed (Late).”

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163737435-z8zuncwzuze-Screenshot-2025-06-22-at-11-04-18-PM.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Where can I see employee quiz scores?" default-open="false">
    Go to **People → Employees → All Employees**, and click the Employee for whom you want to view the quiz scores. Click the **Training** tab to view employee results and their scores.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163703592-kqxtnmz9tx8-Screenshot-2025-06-18-at-21-12-58.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens after an employee completes the training and passes the quiz?" default-open="false">
    Upon completing the training with a passing score, employees will receive a **Training Completion Certificate** via email. Ensure that the Email Notification for Training Certificates toggle is turned on in **Settings → Notifications → Notification Workflow**.
