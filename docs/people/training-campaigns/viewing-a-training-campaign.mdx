This guide will help you **navigate the** **Training Campaign Details page** in Scrut, where you can view comprehensive information about your security training campaigns and track employee participation.

## Accessing the Training Campaign Details Page

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **People → Training Campaigns** on the left navigation panel.

2. Select the campaign for which you want to view further details.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166064290-7ohogcr9bpu-Selecting-an-individual-training-campaign.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Once selected, you’ll be taken to the Training Campaign details page, which contains the following tabs: **Overview, Employees, Settings** and **Audit Log**.

## Overview Tab

The Overview tab provides a comprehensive snapshot of your training campaign's key information and performance metrics.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161938796-nzr07lt6w6-Campaign-Detail-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Campaign Information

**Campaign Name, Status, and Description**

The campaign name appears at the top of the page with its current status displayed next to it. Status indicators include: **Draft, Upcoming, Active**, or **Completed**. To learn more about campaign statuses, [see here](/docs/people/training-campaigns/understanding-security-awareness#campaign-statuses). The description you entered when creating the campaign appears in the Campaign Description section.

**Campaign Timeline**

- **Launch Date & Time**: When the campaign started
- **End Date & Time**: When the campaign is scheduled to conclude
- **Timezone**: The timezone to mark launch and end dates
- **SLA**: The number of days within which employees must complete the training to avoid an overdue status

### Training Content

**Training Type**

Indicates the format of training materials:

- Document
- Video
- Link

**Document Name**

View the document name and preview training content such as videos, documents, and other learning materials directly within the page.

### Checklists

Displays the checklists that include this training campaign.

### Completion Metrics

**Completion Rate**

A graph showing the number of employees who have completed the training versus the total number of participants.

**% Completed Within SLA**

See a breakdown of employees who have completed the training within the specific SLA (in days).

## Employees Tab

The Employees tab provides detailed tracking of individual employee participation in the training campaign, allowing you to monitor progress and identify who needs follow-up.

### Training Completion Status Cards

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164858780-z7q5aoo63u-Screenshot-2025-12-17-at-13-29-33.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

At the top of the Employees tab, you'll see status cards that provide a quick overview of campaign progress. These cards show the count of employees in each completion category:

- **Due**: Employees with an active assignment but haven't completed the training yet (deadline not passed).
- **Overdue**: Employees who have missed their training deadline but can still complete it.
- **Expired**: Employees whose training window has closed completely.
- **Ignored**: Employees for whom this training is [marked as ignored in People → Employees → Tasks](/docs/people/employees-1/checklists/disabling-specific-tasks-for-employees#how-to-turn-off-specific-tasks-for-employees). It also includes employees marked as on leave and temporarily exempt from training.
- **Completed**: Employees who finished the training on or before the SLA deadline.
- **Completed (Late)**: Employees who finished the training after the SLA deadline.

These status cards help you quickly assess campaign health and identify which groups may need attention or reminders.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Clicking on any status card automatically filters the employee information table to show only employees with that specific status.
</Callout>

### Employee Information Table

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164860080-c69f8ibzii-Screenshot-2025-12-17-at-13-29-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The table provides comprehensive details about each participant in the training campaign:

- **Name and Email**: Employee names and email addresses
- **Reporting Manager**: The direct manager of the employee (if this information is available in Scrut)
- **Assigned Date**: When the training was assigned to the employee
- **Completion Date**: When the employee completed the training (if applicable)
- **Completion Status**: Current status (Due, Overdue, Expired, Ignored, Completed, or Completed - Late)

**Search Box** Quickly locate specific employees by searching for their name or email address. This is helpful when checking on individual progress or following up with specific team members.

**Filters** Refine the employee list using multiple filter options:

- **Reporting Manager**: Filter by manager to see how specific teams are performing
- **Assigned Date**: Filter employees by when they received the training assignment

**Export** Download a spreadsheet containing all employee data, including names, email addresses, reporting managers, assigned dates, completion dates, and completion statuses. This is helpful to share campaign details and progress with external stakeholders.
