<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note**

  Only campaigns with **Draft** or **Upcoming** status can be deleted. Active or completed campaigns cannot be deleted.
</Callout>

## How To Delete a Campaign

1. Click **People → Training Campaigns** and click the delete icon next to the campaign you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162285471-jcogjtu794-image-1765207569852.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Confirm your action.

3. **Heads up!** This action is permanent and cannot be reversed. So, proceed with caution.
