---
title: "End a Training Campaign"
description: "Learn how to end an in-progress training campaign."
---

You can end a training campaign when it needs to be closed ahead of schedule.

**Note:** Only campaigns with an In Progress status can be ended. Campaigns in a Created or Completed state do not have this option.

## Method 1: End a Campaign from the Training Campaigns List

1. Navigate to **People → Training Campaigns**.

2. Locate the campaign you want to end. Confirm its status shows **In Progress**.

3. In the Actions column for that campaign, click the **Reschedule icon** (calendar icon).

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165618767-7r11sarngnl-Screenshot-2026-04-24-at-14-34-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the **End date and time** field, enter the date and time you want the campaign to end. If the campaign should not have a fixed end date, select the **This campaign does not have an end date** checkbox instead.

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165620104-1xfkq7ni9v-Screenshot-2026-04-24-at-14-35-28.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The campaign end date is updated, and the campaign closes at the specified time.

## Method 2: End a Campaign from the Campaign Detail Page

1. Navigate to **People → Training Campaigns**.

2. Click the name of the campaign you want to end.

3. On the campaign detail page, click **End Campaign** in the top-right corner.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165621461-wpkv9kkep7k-Screenshot-2026-04-24-at-14-35-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. The end date you set must fall after the campaign's start date plus its SLA duration.

5. In the **Date and Time** field, enter the date and time you want to end the campaign.

6. Click Yes, End to confirm.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165622779-oxfa98zkzb9-Screenshot-2026-04-24-at-14-36-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If you end a campaign before all employees complete their training, their tasks will remain incomplete. Completion Rate data reflects the state at the time the campaign ended.
</Callout>
