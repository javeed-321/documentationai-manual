<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Only campaigns with **Draft** or **Upcoming** status can be edited. Active or completed campaigns cannot be modified.
</Callout>

## How To Edit a Campaign

1. Click **People → Training Campaigns** and click the edit icon next to the campaign you want to edit.

   <Image src="/images/image-1765205016547.png" alt="" />

2. You can edit the following:

   - **Campaign Details:** Update the campaign name, description, launch date, end date, SLA, and time zone
   - **Training Materials & Quiz Configuration:** Update the training materials, quiz questions, and passing criteria
   - **Employee Checklist:** Modify the checklists to which the training is a part of

3. Click **Save** to apply all your changes.
