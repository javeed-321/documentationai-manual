---
title: "Reschedule a Training Campaign"
description: "Learn how to reschedule a training campaign in Scrut."
---

There may be scenarios where you might need to adjust a campaign's timeline:

- Employees need more time to complete their assigned training
- Unexpected circumstances have prevented timely completion
- Change an evergreen campaign (no end date) to one with a deadline, or vice versa
- End a campaign before its due date when it reaches 100% completion

In such cases, you can **reschedule a training campaign**.

## How to Reschedule a Campaign

1. Click **People → Training Campaigns** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Reschedule (calendar) icon** in the **Actions** column next to a specific training campaign.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162296178-mon595bzzwj-image-1766155494446.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Alternatively, click the **Reschedule Campaign button** on the campaign details page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163294300-l2qolm2jrgc-Reschedule-Campaign.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the Reschedule Campaign window:

   - Select your new end date in the **Date field**
   - Toggle the **campaign end date checkbox** on or off to enable or disable the deadline

5. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164869757-bltyt8nr78m-Screenshot-2025-12-19-at-20-17-12.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Points to Know

- **Progress is preserved**: All existing completion data and learner progress remain intact when you reschedule
- **Automatic notifications**: Enrolled employees will automatically receive notification of the new deadline
- **Flexible adjustments**: You can reschedule a campaign as many times as needed
- **Evergreen flexibility**: Toggle the end date on to set a deadline, or off to make the campaign ongoing
