---
title: "Assign and Manage Employees to Training Campaigns"
description: "Learn how to add employees to a training campaign and remove them from specific campaigns."
---

Scrut allows you to assign security training to employees based on the [groups they belong to](/docs/people/employees-1/groups). Since different departments and teams within your organization might have varying training requirements, this group-based approach helps you streamline the training assignment process. This article explains how to add employees to a training campaign and remove them from specific campaigns.

## How To Add Employees to a Training Campaign

Follow these steps to assign employees to a training campaign:

### Step 1: Create a Training Campaign

Before assigning employees, you need to set up a training campaign. Refer to our [training campaign creation guide](/docs/people/training-campaigns/create-security-awareness-campaign) for detailed instructions.

### Step 2: Create an Employee Group

Set up an employee group that will be associated with your training campaign. Follow our [employee group setup guide](/docs/people/employees-1/groups) for step-by-step instructions.

### Step 3: Assign Employees to the Training Campaign

Once your training campaign and employee group are ready:

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees**.

2. Select the employees you want to add to the training campaign by checking the boxes next to their names.

   <Image src="/images/Selecting-multiple-employees.png" alt="" />

3. Click the **Edit** button at the top of the table.

4. Click **Group Actions** and then click **Add to Group**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-08-at-11.52.20.png" alt="" />

5. Next, select the **appropriate group** that includes the checklist for your training campaign.

6. Select the “Notify employees immediately about new task assignments” checkbox if you want assigned employees to receive notifications about the newly assigned training campaign. Employees can access the training materials through their dashboard.

7. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-08-at-11.53.26.png" alt="" />

The selected employees will now be enrolled in the corresponding training campaign.

## How To Remove Employees from a Training Campaign

If you need to unenroll an employee from a training campaign:

### Step 1: Find the Employee

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees.**
2. In the **All Employees table**, select the employees you want to remove from the campaign.

<Image src="/images/Selecting-an-individual-employee.png" alt="" />

### Step 2: Access Ongoing Campaigns

Navigate to the **Tasks → Ongoing** tab to view all active campaigns for this employee.

<Image src="/images/Tasks-Ongoing-Tab.png" alt="" />

### Step 3: Remove from Campaign

1. Expand the Training campaign section to view campaign details.

2. Click the **"Ignore"** option in the action column for the campaign you want to remove.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-08-at-12.01.55.png" alt="" />

3. Enter a justification for this action and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-08-at-12.02.10.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  The employee will no longer see this training campaign in their dashboard and will not receive further notifications about it.
</Callout>
