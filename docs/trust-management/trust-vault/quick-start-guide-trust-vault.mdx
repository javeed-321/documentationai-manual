---
title: "Quick Start Guide: Trust Vault & Trust Portal"
description: "Quick 10-step guide to set up your Trust Portal."
---

Follow this recommended workflow to set up and launch your Trust Portal. We’ve organized these steps to help you build a professional, branded portal that’s ready to share with customers, prospects, and other stakeholders.

## Step 1: Add your logo, page title, and organization name

Let's start with the basics to make your Trust Portal look like yours.

- [Upload your company logo](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#3-logo)
- [Upload a favicon](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#4-favicon)
- [Set your page title](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#1-page-title)
- [Add your organization name](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#2-organization-name) (appears at the top of your Trust Portal)

## Step 2: Customize additional branding (optional)

Personalize your Trust Portal's look and feel to match your company's brand identity by adding these extras:

- [Set your brand color](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#5-brand-color)
- [Choose a typeface](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#6-typeface)

## Step 3: Add a custom domain

[Set up a custom domain](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/add-custom-domain) to host your Trust Portal, such as **trust.yourcompany.com**. This makes your portal look more professional and builds credibility with visitors.

## Step 4: Add content

Now for the important stuff — populate your Trust Portal with the security and compliance information your customers are looking for.

- [Write an organization description](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/organization-description) to introduce your Trust Portal
- [Add your compliance frameworks](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/how-to-add-compliance) and certifications
- [Add security controls](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/creating-and-editing-controls-in-trust-vault/what-are-controls-categories-in-trust-vault) and organize them into categories
- [Add sub-processors](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/how-to-add-subprocessors) to showcase the third-party vendors you use
- [Add FAQs](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/adding-faqs-and-updates) to answer common security questions proactively
- [Publish updates](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/updates) to keep stakeholders informed. Turn on [Subscribe to Updates](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/turn-on-subscriptions-to-trust-portal-updates#how-to-enable-subscriptions-to-updates) to auto-notify customers of any new updates or edits to existing ones.
- [Customize the display order](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/customize-the-display-order-of-your-trust-portal) of your Trust Portal sections and items within each section.

## Step 5: Configure clickwrap NDA

Protect your sensitive documents by setting up a [clickwrap NDA](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/clickwrap-nda). Visitors must agree to your terms before they can access private materials.

## Step 6: Configure access request settings

Customize how visitors request access to your private documents.

- [Add links to your Terms of Service (ToS) and privacy policy](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/add-terms-of-service-tos-and-privacy-policy). This is required, as visitors cannot submit access requests or subscribe to updates until both links are configured.
- Choose whether to allow or turn off the [downloading of reports](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/enable-downloading-of-reports) and documents.

## Step 7: Preview your Trust Portal

Want to see how everything looks? Click the "**View Trust Portal**" button at the top-right of the **Trust → Trust Vault** page to preview your changes and make sure everything looks just right.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  You can click this button at any time during setup to see how your changes will appear to visitors. Feel free to preview as often as you'd like—it's a great way to catch any tweaks before going live.
</Callout>

## Step 8: Launch and announce your Trust Portal

It’s time to go live!

- Share the link to your Trust Portal with customers and prospects
- Add it to your website, email signatures, and sales materials

## Step 9: Manage user access requests

Keep an eye on requests for private documents and respond as they come in.

- Review incoming access requests

- [Approve or decline](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/how-to-grant-access-to-trust-vault) based on your policies

- Configure auto-approval

  - [For contacts and prospects on your Salesforce account](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/configuring-auto-approval-in-trust-vault-via-salesforce-integration)
  - [For access requests from specific domains](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/configure-auto-approval-for-specific-domains)

- [Grant full or partial access](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/granular-access-in-trust-vault) as needed

## Step 10: Review analytics

Track how visitors engage with your Trust Portal using the [analytics dashboard](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/dashboard-analytics/trust-vault-dashboard).

- [See who's visiting](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/dashboard-analytics/trust-vault-dashboard#total-portal-views) and what they're looking at
- Find out [which documents get the most attention](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/dashboard-analytics/trust-vault-dashboard#top-engaged-documents)
- [Monitor customer activity](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/dashboard-analytics/customer-activity) and understand what matters most to them
