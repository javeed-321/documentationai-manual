Enabling Scrut Teammates on your Trust Portal lets visitors ask questions and receive instant, contextual answers via a chat interface.

## How does it work?

Teammates offers an AI-powered chat interface where portal visitors can:

- Ask questions about your company's security practices
- Get clarification on compliance documentation
- Learn about the contents of your Trust Portal in real-time

The chat uses data from your Trust Portal to provide accurate and context-specific answers.

## How to Enable Teammates for the Trust Portal

1. Sign in to Scrut and navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault** using the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** icon in the top-right corner.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166162366-3c7zqggecde-Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Go to the **Additional Settings** tab, and turn on the “**Enable Scrut Teammates on Your Trust Portal**” toggle.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161870819-ycswgnnaimc-Additional-Settings-tab---Trust-Vault.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Choose who can access Teammates on your Trust Portal:

   - Both Signed-In and Anonymous Users
   - Only Signed-In Users

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164598724-efz37iial9u-Screenshot-2025-11-04-at-11-50-20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Once you’ve enabled Teammates for the Trust Portal, visitors will see the handy chat icon in the bottom right corner of the portal.

### FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Is chat available on mobile devices?" default-open="true">
    No. The Teammates chat is only available for desktop and tablet views.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Which documents are referenced for user queries?" default-open="true">
    It depends on whether the user is logged in or is browsing anonymously.

    - **Anonymous users:** Only public documents and information are referenced
    - **Logged-in users:** Access is limited to documents they have permission to view
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What’s the difference between AI Readability and Scrut Teammates for the Trust Portal?" default-open="true">
    The [AI readability toggle](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/ai-powered-features/turn-on-ai-readability) is separate and handles questionnaire automation, while the Teammates toggle enables portal visitors to ask questions and receive instant responses about the page's content.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
