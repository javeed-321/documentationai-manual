Find answers to common questions on Scrut’s Trust Vault and Trust Portal.

### FAQ

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is a Trust Portal?" default-open="true">
    Scrut's Trust Portal is a centralized hub (webpage) where you can share your organization's security, compliance, and regulatory information. It provides a transparent, self-service way for customers, prospects, vendors, partners, and other stakeholders to access the security details they need during their due diligence process.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why use a Trust Portal?" default-open="true">
    A Trust Portal helps you:

    - **Save time**: Reduce back-and-forth emails by letting customers find answers themselves
    - **Build trust**: Demonstrate transparency by proactively sharing your security posture
    - **Accelerate sales cycles**: Enable prospects to complete security reviews faster
    - **Maintain control**: Track who accesses your sensitive documents and when
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What’s the difference between Trust Vault and Trust Portal?" default-open="true">
    Trust Vault is the admin-side module for building and managing your trust page in Scrut. Trust Portal is the live, shareable page your customers and stakeholders visit.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why isn’t a section showing up on my Trust Portal?" default-open="true">
    Trust Portal sections such as Compliance, Controls, Subprocessors, FAQs, and Updates are displayed only when they contain published data. If a section is empty, it's automatically hidden from the customer-facing portal.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="My customer says the Trust Portal looks blank. What does that mean?" default-open="true">
    If no data has been published across any Trust Vault section, visitors will see an empty page, as shown below. To populate your Trust Vault, start by [adding content](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/organization-description).

  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Which sections are automatically hidden in the Trust Portal when empty?" default-open="true">
    All sections, such as Compliance, Controls, Subprocessors, FAQs and Updates, are hidden from your Trust Portal when no data is available, and appear automatically once content is added.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do I need to hide or show sections manually?" default-open="true">
    No. Section visibility is managed automatically based on whether data exists. If you add content to a section, it becomes visible. If a section has no content, it stays hidden — no manual toggling required.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="A section I populated isn’t showing up. What should I do?" default-open="true">
    Make sure the content has been saved. Draft content won't trigger a section to appear in the customer-facing portal.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
