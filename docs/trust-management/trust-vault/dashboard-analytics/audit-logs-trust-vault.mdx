Use the Audit Logs tab to track and monitor team actions on your Trust Vault.

The **Audit Logs** track every action performed within the Trust Vault module. This comprehensive log helps you monitor team activity. To access it, select **Trust → Trust Vault** in the left navigation panel, then click **Audit Logs**. Each entry in the audit log can be expanded to reveal detailed information about the action, including:

- Who performed the action
- What was changed or accessed
- When the action occurred
- Additional context or parameters
