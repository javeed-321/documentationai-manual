Scrut offers powerful branding tools to customize your Trust Portal to reflect your organization's unique identity and style. In this section, we explain how to customize the following elements of your Trust Portal:

- [Page Title](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#1-page-title)
- [Organization Name](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#2-organization-name)
- [Logo](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#3-logo)
- [Favicon](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#4-favicon)
- [Brand Color](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#5-brand-color)
- [Typeface](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/branding/branding-your-trust-vault#6-typeface)

## Step 1: Go to Trust Vault Settings

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Trust → Trust Vault** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **settings icon** on the top right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166162366-3c7zqggecde-Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. This opens the **Trust Vault Settings → Branding** page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161912210-uubuq958zo-Branding-Security-Tab.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Enter your organization's name (appears above the **About** section).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163547323-gfdmibotbad-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-21-13-38.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Shows where the organization name will be displayed in the Trust Vault" caption="Organization Name" />

### #3: Logo

Upload your organization's logo (displays next to the **Page Title**).

- **Format:** .png or .jpeg
- **Size:** 160 × 160 pixels

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163548560-7dirbf3l7zn-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-21-14-49.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Shows where the logo will be displayed in the Trust Vault." caption="Logo" />

### #4: Favicon

Upload the favicon icon. This small icon appears in the browser tab next to the page name.

- **Format:** .ico
- **Size:** 30 × 30 pixels

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163549818-tpbv6nhd7d-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-21-15-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Shows the favicon position in the browser tab." caption="Favicon" />

### #5: Brand Color

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

Use the color picker or enter the hex code to select your primary brand color. Scrut applies this color to:

- Button faces
- Icons
- Hyperlinks

The style preview panel on the right shows how the colors will appear on the Trust Vault in real-time.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166159486-1hea5e7j358-Trust-Vault-Branding.gif?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Click the **Reset to Default** button in the color picker to restore the original colors.
</Callout>

### #6: Typeface

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

Select the font style used for **Headers** and **Descriptions** across the Trust Vault. The style preview panel shows font changes in real time.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165145465-gyx02u85fd8-Screenshot-2026-02-12-at-19-20-06.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  If you feel unsure about your changes, don't worry! Just use the **Preview** button to see a quick glimpse of how everything looks in the Trust Vault.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Preview and Save Changes

Once you’ve completed the branding, click the **View Trust Portal** button in the top-right corner. This will show how your Trust Vault will look with the new branding.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166222072-yny604wvxmg-View-Trust-Portal-button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

If you’re happy with the preview, click the **Save Changes** button. This will update your Trust Vault with your new branding, and the changes will be visible in real-time.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163551155-0dh8hcjas8pp-Screenshot-2025-05-16-at-21-21-20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
