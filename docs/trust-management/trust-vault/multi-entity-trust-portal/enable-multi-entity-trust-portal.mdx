<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Scale** plan
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- You must be a Scrut Admin
- You must have one or more entities configured in your Scrut organization ([Refer to this guide](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-entities))

## How to Enable Multi-Entity in Trust Vault

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Trust → Trust Vault** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** icon at the top right.

   <Image src="/images/Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png" alt="" />

3. Navigate to the **Multi-Entity** tab and toggle **Enable Multi-Entity** to **On**.

4. Select the **entities to display** in the Trust Portal.

5. Choose the **Default Entity** that loads when visitors first access the portal.

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Trust-Vault-Multi-Entity-Tab.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  At least one entity must be enabled to use multi-entity view. If you try to disable all entities, you’ll need to turn off Multi-Entity mode entirely or re-enable at least one entity.
</Callout>

#### Preview and Verify Your Configuration

Click **View Trust Portal** to preview how your Trust Portal will look for each enabled entity.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-24-at-13.57.58.png" alt="" />

By default, portal visitors will land on the page of your selected default entity. They can then switch to other entities by using the dropdown at the top right.
