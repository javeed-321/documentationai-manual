<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

If the default category names don't align with your organization's terminology, you can edit them to suit your specific requirements.

## How to Edit Category Names

1. Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview.**

2. Scroll to the **Controls** section.

3. Click the category card you want to rename.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161942869-zs4cbfwv0t-Click-on-a-Category-Card-in-Trust-Vault.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click the **pencil icon** beside the category name.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165159267-k71pl20h5-Screenshot-2026-02-16-at-13-07-45.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Enter your preferred category name in the text field.

6. Click **Save.**
