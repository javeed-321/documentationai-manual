<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

In addition to the **13 predefined control categories included by default in the Trust Vault**, you can create custom categories to organize controls tailored to your organization's needs. Custom categories allow you to group related security controls that may not fit neatly into the standard framework categories, helping you present a complete picture of your security posture to customers and stakeholders.

## How To Create a Custom Category

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Trust → Trust Vault** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Overview** tab.

3. Click **Add Category**.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Category.png" alt="" />

4. Enter the **Category Name**.

5. Click **Add and Continue**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21.32.12.png" alt="" />

6. Enter the following details to add a control to this custom category:

   - **Control Name & Description**: Enter a suitable name for the control and a clear description.
   - **Implementation Level**: Select either Not Applicable, In-progress, or Fully Implemented.
   - Click **Add Documents** to upload compliance certification, reports or other relevant documents.
   - Select the **Make All Documents Private** checkbox if you want visitors to require authorization to view attached documents.

7. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-16.27.52.png" alt="" />

8. You can view it under the Controls grid.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21.32.44.png" alt="" />

9. To add another control to this category, click on the Category card and click the **Add Control** button.

   <Image src="/images/image-1771229323235.png" alt="" />

10. Repeat the steps above for each control you want to list under this category.
