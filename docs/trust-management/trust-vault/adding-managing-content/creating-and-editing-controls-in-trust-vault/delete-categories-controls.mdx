<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

## How To Delete a Control

1. Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview** and scroll to the **Controls** section.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21.32.44-3.png" alt="" />

2. Click the category card of the control you want to delete.

3. Click the trash can icon beside the control name to delete it.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22.00.41.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22.00.59.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22.01.20.png" alt="" />

## How To Delete a Category

1. Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview** and scroll to the **Controls** section.

2. Click the category card you want to delete.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21.32.44-4.png" alt="" />

3. Click the trash can icon to delete it.

   <Callout kind="danger">
     **Heads Up!**

     To delete a category, you have to first delete all controls within it.
   </Callout>

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22.04.21.png" alt="" />
