Learn how to add, edit, and delete sub-processors on your Trust Portal.

## What are Sub-Processors?

**Sub-processors are third-party vendors your organization uses to deliver products and services**, such as cloud storage providers, email services, payroll processors, analytics tools, etc. These vendors handle sensitive customer data on your behalf.

Scrut offers a dedicated section on your Trust Portal to showcase your sub-processors (aka vendors). You can use this section to list third-party vendors you use to deliver your services. This helps to nurture stakeholder trust, support compliance efforts, demonstrate transparency, and enhance customer confidence.

## Add Sub-Processors to Your Trust Portal

1. Sign in to Scrut and navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault** using the left side panel.

2. Click the **Overview** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162579428-cr5cyplr266-Overview-Tab---Trust-Vault.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Sub-Processors** section and click the **Add Sub-Processor** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161852459-7eo8u5ho9ow-Add-Sub-Processor.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the dropdown, search and select vendors from your existing vendor list. These are vendors already added to your Scrut organization that you want to publicly display as sub-processors on your Trust Portal.

   **Note:** You can select and add multiple sub-processors.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165011173-315wie32glb-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-25-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. **To add a new vendor** that's not in the list:

   - Type the vendor name in the search field

   - Click the **+Create \[Vendor Name\]** button that appears

   - Enter the vendor **Name**, select a **Category**, and choose relevant **Entities**

   - Click **Add**

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165013846-sdpad4nvbge-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-29-06.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     New vendors created here are automatically added to your vendor list in **Risk → Vendors** for ongoing vendor management.
   </Callout>

6. Click **Save and Continue**.

7. For each selected sub-processor, provide the following information:

   - **Display Name** (required): How the vendor appears on your Trust Portal

   - **Website** (required): The vendor's official URL

   - **Location** (required): Geographic location for compliance tracking

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165012477-w7tee1re8x-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-26-44.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     You can add logos and descriptions later by [editing the sub-processor](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/how-to-add-subprocessors#edit-subprocessor-details).
   </Callout>

8. Click **Save**. Your sub-processors are now visible in the Trust Vault.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165015177-frnh0x9o8o7-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-32-10.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

9. Click the **View Trust Portal** button at the top of the Overview tab to see how the sub-processors appear on your public Trust Portal.

## Edit Sub-Processor Details

You can update sub-processor information at any time to keep your Trust Portal up to date.

1. Go to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview**.

2. Scroll to the **Sub-Processors** section and click the sub-processor you want to edit.

3. Click the **Edit** icon in the side drawer.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165017892-u5i8rw85uqn-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-36-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Update any of the following details:

   - **Display Name:** How the sub-processor appears publicly
   - **Website:** Official vendor URL
   - **Logo:** Upload the vendor's logo (JPEG or PNG, max 2 MB)
   - **Location:** Geographic location for compliance purposes
   - **Description:** A brief description of the vendor's role, services, and relevant certifications or compliance standards

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     A clear description helps stakeholders understand why you use this vendor and what data it handles.
   </Callout>

5. Click **Save** to save your changes to the Trust Portal.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165019257-mprzottpkz-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-37-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Remove Sub-Processors

When you no longer work with a vendor, remove them from your Trust Portal to keep information accurate.

1. Go to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview**.

2. Scroll to the **Sub-Processors** section and click the sub-processor you want to remove.

3. Click the **Delete** icon in the side drawer.

4. Confirm the deletion.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165022881-1oidg6f7j6r-Screenshot-2026-01-27-at-21-44-31.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Removing a sub-processor only removes it from your public Trust Portal. The vendor remains in your internal vendor list (**Risk → Vendors**) for record-keeping purposes.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What’s the difference between a vendor and a sub-processor?" default-open="true">
    All sub-processors are vendors, but not all vendors are sub-processors. Vendors are any third-party companies you work with, tracked internally in Scrut. Sub-processors are the specific vendors you choose to publicly display on your Trust Portal because they handle customer data.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will my customers be notified when I add or remove sub-processors?" default-open="true">
    No, currently Scrut doesn't automatically notify stakeholders of changes. You should communicate significant changes to sub-processors directly to your customers, especially if required by your contracts or privacy policies.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="When should I update sub-processor information?" default-open="true">
    Update your sub-processors whenever:

    - You start or stop working with a vendor
    - A vendor changes their name, location, or services
    - You need to add compliance certifications or updated descriptions
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I bulk add sub-processors?" default-open="true">
    Yes. When adding sub-processors, you can select multiple vendors at once.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens to deleted sub-processors?" default-open="true">
    When you delete a sub-processor from your Trust Portal, it only removes the public listing. The vendor record remains in Risk → Vendors for your internal tracking and compliance history.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
