## How to Enable Subscriptions to Updates

By default, subscriptions are turned off. You must enable them to allow visitors to subscribe.

1. Log in to Scrut and navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault**.

2. Click the **Settings icon** at the top right of the page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166162366-3c7zqggecde-Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Go to **Additional Settings**.

4. Turn on the **Trust Portal Subscriptions** toggle.

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161870819-ycswgnnaimc-Additional-Settings-tab---Trust-Vault.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

When subscriptions are enabled, a **Subscribe to Updates** option appears on:

- The Trust Portal Overview
- The Updates section

## What Visitors See When Subscribing

1. The visitor clicks **Subscribe to Updates** from the Trust Portal Overview or Updates section.

2. They enter their email address, already populated for logged-in users.

3. They select the checkbox to accept your organization's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

   **Note:** This checkbox is mandatory; visitors cannot subscribe without accepting it.

4. They click **Subscribe** and confirm their email to complete the subscription.

## Managing Subscribers

Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Subscribers**. The Subscribers table includes:

- Email address
- Subscribed On (date and timestamp)
- Status (Verified / Pending)
- Delete option
- Search functionality

### Add a Subscriber Manually

1. Click **Add Subscriber**.

2. Enter the email address. You can either:

   - Leave **Bypass email verification** unchecked. The subscriber is added with a **Pending** status. Scrut sends the verification email, and they start receiving notifications only after confirmation.
   - Select the **Bypass email verification** checkbox. The subscriber is marked **Verified** immediately and begins receiving update notifications without email confirmation.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What happens after a visitor clicks “Subscribe to Updates” in the Trust Portal?" default-open="false">
    After entering their email and clicking **Subscribe**, the subscriber must confirm their email address.

    - Until confirmed: Their status is **Pending**, and no notifications are sent
    - After confirmation: Their Status changes to **Verified,** and the subscriber receives future update notifications
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What’s the difference between Pending and Verified subscribers?" default-open="false">
    - **Pending:** The subscriber has not confirmed their email. They will not receive any notifications.
    - **Verified:** The subscriber has confirmed their email and will receive update notifications (if enabled when publishing updates).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How does subscription work for logged-in vs anonymous users?" default-open="false">
    - Logged-in users: Their email address is automatically populated. They do not need to enter it manually.
    - Anonymous users: Must manually enter their email address to subscribe.

    After successful verification, the Trust Portal displays Unsubscribe from Updates.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165282821-60v6fxvtybw-Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-12-52-31.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Unsubscribe option shown to logged-in subscribers" />
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if someone tries to subscribe with an email that is already subscribed?" default-open="false">
    The system prevents duplicate subscriptions.

    - No duplicate subscriber record is created.
    - A modal informs the user that they are already subscribed with the email.
    - If the existing subscription is in Pending status, a new confirmation email is triggered.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How can a subscriber unsubscribe from Trust Portal updates?" default-open="false">
    Subscribers can unsubscribe in two ways:

    - Click the Unsubscribe link in any update notification email.
    - Click Unsubscribe from updates directly on the Trust Portal.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
