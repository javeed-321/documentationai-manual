title: "Enable Reports Download"
description: "Learn how to turn on or off the downloading of reports on your Trust Portal."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

On the Trust Portal, you can use the **Enable Downloading of Reports** toggle to control whether visitors can download reports (documents) or only view them.

- **When the toggle is On**: Visitors can **view and download** the reports they have access to.
- **When the toggle is Off**: Visitors can **view reports only**. The download option will not be available.

You can use this setting to control how your documents are shared externally.

## When Downloads are Enabled

When the **Enable Downloading of Reports** toggle is turned **ON**, visitors can both **view and download** the reports they have access to.

- A **Download** button appears alongside accessible documents.
- Visitors can save reports directly to their devices.
- Access permissions still apply. They can only see and download documents that have been shared with them.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-11-at-21.55.16.png" alt="" />

## When Downloads are Disabled

When the **Enable Downloading of Reports** toggle is turned **OFF**, visitors can **view reports only**.

- The **Download** option is not displayed.
- Reports can be opened and reviewed within the portal.
- Documents cannot be saved or exported.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-11-at-21.56.04-1.png" alt="" caption="No download option" />

## How to Enable or Disable Report Downloads

1. Click **Trust → Trust Vault** on the left navigation panel and click the **Settings** icon in the top-right corner of the Trust Vault module.

   <Image src="/images/Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png" alt="" />

2. Navigate to the **Additional Settings** tab.

3. Turn the **Enable Downloading of Reports** toggle **On** or **Off**, as required.

   <Image src="/images/Additional-Settings-tab---Trust-Vault.png" alt="" />

Your changes take effect immediately.
