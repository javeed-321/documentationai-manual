Set up a custom domain for your Trust Portal.

You can set up your organization’s Trust Portal on a custom domain that matches your organization’s website address. This gives you a professional, easy-to-share web link for your Trust Portal that you can share with customers, prospects, and other stakeholders.

Typically, custom domains for Trust Portals use the format: `prefix.company.domain.` For example, [https://trust.scrut.io/](https://trust.scrut.io/) is the custom domain for Scrut’s Trust Portal. Other examples include:

- `trust.company.com`
- `compliance.company.com`
- `security.company.com`

## How to Add a Custom Domain for Your Trust Portal

1. Click Trust → Trust Vault on the left navigation menu and click the **settings icon** on the top right of the Trust Vault module.

2. This opens the **Trust Vault Settings** page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166162366-3c7zqggecde-Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Navigate to the **Custom Domain** tab.

4. Enter your custom domain in the Custom Domain field. (e.g., security.example.com)

5. Click **Save.**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165114506-idkp1xico9-Screenshot-2026-02-09-at-16-55-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  It might take some time for the system to generate CNAME and CVALUE records.
</Callout>

6. Go to your DNS provider portal (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Route53, etc.) and in the provider, choose CNAME. Copy and paste the values for CNAME and CVALUE from Scrut.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important:**

  After adding the record with your DNS provider, contact your Scrut CSM to activate your custom domain.
</Callout>
