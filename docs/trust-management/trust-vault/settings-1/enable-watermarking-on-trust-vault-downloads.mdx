<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

You can add watermarks to unencrypted PDFs downloaded from your Trust Portal. This allows you to add an extra layer of document tracking and protection.

## When Watermarking is Enabled

When the **Enable Watermarking** toggle is turned **ON**:

- A watermark **(Downloaded on Date, Time)** will be applied to every page of the PDF when visitors download it.
- The watermark helps identify the source and control the distribution of your documents.
- Access permissions still apply. Only visitors with appropriate access can download the documents.

## When Watermarking is Disabled

When the **Enable Watermarking** toggle is turned **OFF**:

- No watermark is added to downloaded documents.
- PDFs are provided in their original format.
- Documents can still be downloaded (if download permissions are enabled).

## How to Enable or Disable Watermarking

1. Click **Trust → Trust Vault** on the left navigation panel and click the **Settings** icon in the top-right corner of the Trust Vault module.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166162366-3c7zqggecde-Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Navigate to the **Additional Settings** tab.

3. Turn on the [**Enable Downloading of Reports**](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/settings-1/enable-downloading-of-reports) toggle.

4. This shows the **Enable Watermarking** toggle. Turn it on or off, as required.

   Note: The Watermarking toggle is visible only when the Downloading of Reports toggle is on.

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161870819-ycswgnnaimc-Additional-Settings-tab---Trust-Vault.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Your changes take effect immediately.
