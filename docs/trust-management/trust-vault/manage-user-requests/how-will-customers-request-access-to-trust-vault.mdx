This guide walks you through the visitor's POV (point of view) when requesting access to your Trust Portal.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When a visitor lands on your Trust Portal, they can view all public sections without logging in, including:

- Organization description
- Compliances
- Public documents
- Controls and categories
- FAQs
- Updates

## Requesting Access to Private Documents

If visitors want to view private documents, they need to request access by clicking the **Login/Get Access** button.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162517991-3wbvuruxjo6-Login-Get-Access-button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Here's what happens next:

1. The visitor will be prompted to enter their work email address and click **Continue**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165163629-4z94ufsxygj-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-31-58.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. If this is the visitor's first time requesting access, they'll be prompted to fill out an access request form with the following information:

   - **Personal Information:** First Name, Last Name

   - **Organization Details:** Name, Type, Address, State, and Country

   - **Access Level:** Visitors choose one of the following:

     - **Full Access**: Grants access to all documents in the Controls and Framework sections
     - **Partial Access**: Allows them to select specific documents they need to access

   - **Terms of Service:** Visitors select the checkbox to agree to your organization's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

     - Below the checkbox, they see a disclaimer confirming that Scrut acts solely as a data processor powering the access request software on your organization's behalf, and that their email and IP address are recorded for audit purposes.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166130890-kpc3nd5pahr-ToS-and-Privacy-Policy-acceptance---Trust-Portal.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. The visitor clicks **Request Access** to submit their request. They'll see a confirmation message.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165165047-un7dmnjli8-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-47-20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

At this point, the request appears in the **Trust → Trust Vault → Access** **→** **Pending** tab in Scrut, where you can review and [approve or decline it](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/how-to-grant-access-to-trust-vault).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165166476-dyofweoo14-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-48-54.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## After You Approve the Request

Once you [approve an access request](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/manage-user-requests/how-to-grant-access-to-trust-vault#approve-access):

1. The visitor receives an email invitation to access the Trust Portal.

2. They click the **Login Now** button in the email.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165167827-n3z7clgz5sh-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-52-07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. They're redirected to the Trust Vault login page.

4. Their email address is pre-populated based on their access request form.

5. They click **Login**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165169158-lr22hnr1rkk-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-52-56.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. An OTP (one-time password) is sent to their email.

7. They enter the OTP to verify and log in.

   Note: Both the invitation and OTP emails display your organization's name and logo.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165170473-ata1pjh00zv-Screenshot-2026-02-17-at-11-55-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

They review and accept the NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) by selecting the checkbox and clicking **Accept**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162552446-tmzduk77wqf-NDA-Acceptance.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The visitor can now access the Trust Vault documents based on the permissions you granted.

## Access Expiration

Visitor access to the Trust Vault will expire based on the timeframe you set when approving their request. After expiration, they'll need to submit a new access request following the same steps.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  If a visitor already has active access, they can log in to the Trust Vault using OTP verification without submitting a new request.
</Callout>

## Mobile Access

In addition to laptops and desktops, visitors can also access your Trust Portal from mobile devices. They can submit access requests, preview documents, and download documents from any device using the same steps outlined above.

## Logging Out

Visitors can log out of the Trust Vault by clicking the **Logout** button in the top right corner of the page.
