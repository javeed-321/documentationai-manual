<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

You can share links to specific items from your Trust Vault with customers, prospects, partners, vendors, and other stakeholders. All you have to do is copy and share the unique link for a Trust Vault item, such as documents, compliance details, controls, subprocessors, FAQs, and updates. This allows you to provide recipients with direct links to the content they need, enabling easy access without browsing the entire page.

## How to Share Links to Specific Items

- Go to your Trust Vault website (such as [trust.scrut.io](https://trust.scrut.io/)).

- Find the document or section you want to share with the recipient, then click to open the side drawer.

- Hover over the item and click **Copy Link**.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163977179-hph963u9bwb-Screenshot-2025-07-29-at-12-04-36.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- The unique URL for the item will be copied to your clipboard.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163978528-ojs82hc05qr-Screenshot-2025-07-29-at-12-05-00.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- Share the link via email, Slack, chat, or any other messaging tool.

- When the recipient clicks the link, they’ll be taken directly to the specific item or content shared.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163979970-dffc5agk88t-Screenshot-2025-07-29-at-12-05-42.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

  The Copy Link option is available for both desktops and mobile devices.
</Callout>

## FAQs & Troubleshooting

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Which items in Trust Vault have this feature?" default-open="false">
    You can copy links to individual items in all Trust Vault sections, including:

    - Compliance
    - Documents
    - Controls
    - Sub-processors
    - FAQs
    - Updates
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if I share links to private Trust Vault documents?" default-open="true">
    If you share a link to a private document:

    - If the user has access to it, they can view the attachment directly
    - If the user doesn’t have access, they’ll need to click the **Get Access** button to view it.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
