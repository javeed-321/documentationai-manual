---
title: "Configure Auto Approval via Salesforce Integration"
description: "Configure Trust Portal auto approval rules for contacts and prospects in your Salesforce account."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

By default, anyone who visits your Trust Vault must submit an access request to view sensitive information. However, there might be situations where you may want to auto-approve requests from specific users, such as those listed as contacts or prospects in your Salesforce account.

Follow these steps to enable auto-approval for specific users:

1. Click the **settings icon** on the top right of the **Trust Vault → Overview** page.

   <Image src="/images/Trust-Vault-Settings-icon.png" alt="" />

2. This opens the **Trust Vault Settings** page.

3. Go to the **Auto Approve** tab and select the **Auto-Approve If** radio button.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-30-at-12.28.49.png" alt="" />

4. Click the **Integrate** button for Salesforce.
5. Follow the steps listed in the [Integrating Salesforce](/docs/integrations/customer-relationship-management/salesforce-integration) guide to complete the integration.
6. Once the Salesforce integration is complete, configure the auto-approve rules, as required.
7. Click **Save**.

<Image src="/images/image-1770894752585.png" alt="" />

### What Happens Next?

Depending on the rules you’ve configured in the **Auto Approve** tab, whenever a customer/prospect requests access to your organization’s Trust Vault, the request will get auto-approved if the user’s:

- Email matches a contact in your Salesforce account
- Email domain matches a contact associated with an account in Salesforce
- Email matches a prospect in your Salesforce account

If there is a match, you don’t have to approve the access request. Scrut will automatically grant them access to all documents in your Trust Vault. Users who are auto-approved will have the Salesforce tag associated with them. You can check this in the **Trust Vault → Access → Approved** tab.

<Image src="/images/image-1767078942966.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Users auto-approved via Salesforce will have the Salesforce tag associated with them.
</Callout>
