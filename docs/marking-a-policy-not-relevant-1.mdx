When a policy previously marked as not relevant becomes applicable or necessary, you can flag it as Relevant. This ensures its inclusion in all compliance-related metrics on Scrut's main dashboard and policy dashboard.

You have two ways to mark a policy as relevant:

- [Individually](/docs/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1#how-to-mark-a-policy-as-relevant)
- [In Bulk](/docs/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1#how-to-mark-multiple-policies-as-relevant)

## How To Mark a Policy as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policy that you want to mark as relevant.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171362557-eg0rfbgweuw-Screenshot-2025-09-26-at-13-09-40.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Selecting a policy from the All Policies tab" caption=" " />

3. Once you’re on the policy details page, click the **three-dots icon** at the top right and select “**Mark as Relevant**.”

## How to Mark Multiple Policies as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Policies** tab and select the policies you want to mark as relevant.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the policy table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" width="30" height="30" caption=" " />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164476474-4ezbtllqr73-Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16-29-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. In the Edit drawer, scroll to the **Relevance** dropdown and choose “**Relevant**.”

5. Click **Save**.
