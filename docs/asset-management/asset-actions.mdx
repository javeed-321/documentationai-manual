You can perform the following actions on assets added to the Asset Management module:

## #1: Assign an Owner

1. Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type.

2. Select one or more assets for which you want to bulk assign an owner and click **Assign Owner**.

   <Image src="/images/Bulk-selecting-assets.png" alt="" />

3. Choose your desired owner.

   - **Assign Employee**: Choose a team member from the dropdown list
   - **Non-Personnel**: Select if the asset belongs to a system account and is not managed by a specific employee
   - **External Contractor**: Choose if the asset is managed by an external party

4. Click **Save**.

5. Scrut will override the existing owner and assign the new owner.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-09-at-10.44.10.png" alt="" />

## #2: Ignore

If you no longer want to include an asset in your compliance and security requirements, you can mark it as ignored. Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type. Select one or more assets, click **Asset Actions**, and choose **Ignore**. Provide a valid justification for ignoring the assets and click **Save**.

<Image src="/images/Asset-Actions.png" alt="" />

## #3: Monitor

Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type. Select one or more assets, click **Asset Actions**, and choose **Monitor**. Confirm your action. Scrut will mark the selected assets as monitored.

<Image src="/images/Asset-Actions.png" alt="" />

## #4: Mark as Critical

#### What are Critical Assets?

Critical assets are those that store or process sensitive data requiring enhanced security and compliance tracking. A few examples include:

- **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**: Customer names, addresses, social security numbers, etc.
- **Financial records**: Payment data, bank account information, credit card details
- **Operational data**: Systems essential to business operations, proprietary algorithms, trade secrets
- **Protected health information (PHI)**: Medical records and health-related data
- **Authentication credentials**: Passwords, API keys, tokens, certificates

#### Why Mark Assets as Critical?

Marking assets as critical helps your organization:

- Meet compliance requirements that mandate tracking of sensitive data
- Pass compliance tests that verify proper asset classification
- Maintain audit trails for the handling of regulated information

#### How to Mark Assets as Critical

1. Go to **Asset Management** and select your preferred asset type
2. Select one or more assets from the list
3. Click **Asset Actions** and choose **Mark as Critical**
4. Confirm your action

Scrut will mark the selected assets as critical, enabling them to be tracked for compliance purposes.

<Image src="/images/Asset-Actions.png" alt="" />

## #5: Unmark as Critical

Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type. Select one or more assets, click **Asset Actions**, and choose **Unmark as Critical**. Confirm your action. Scrut will remove the critical status from the selected assets.

<Image src="/images/Asset-Actions.png" alt="" />

## #6: Update Environment

1. Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type.

2. Select one or more assets for which you want to update the environment, and click **Asset Actions → Update Environment**.

3. Specify the operational environment:

   - **Development**: For assets in development/testing phases
   - **Test**: For assets in quality assurance or staging
   - **Production**: For live, customer-facing assets

4. Click **Save**. Scrut will override the existing environment details and assign the new environment.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-12-09-at-11.02.53.png" alt="" />

Go to **Asset Management**, and click on your preferred asset type. Click the **Export** button to download asset data as a CSV file.

## #9: Deleting an Asset

Go to Asset Management, and click on your preferred asset type. Select one or more assets that you want to delete, and then click the **Delete** icon that appears at the top of the table.

**Note:** Deleting an asset is a permanent action and cannot be undone. So, proceed with caution.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-06-13-at-12.07.07.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Note:**

  You can delete only assets that you’ve added manually to Scrut. Scrut doesn’t allow you to delete assets that are automatically fetched from your integrations.
</Callout>
