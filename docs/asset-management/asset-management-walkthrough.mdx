The **Asset Management** module in Scrut provides a centralized system for tracking, organizing, and monitoring your organization’s infrastructure assets. It streamlines and simplifies the complex task of tracking digital assets, supporting your compliance efforts.

## Key Benefits

- **Aids Compliance:** Map assets to compliance requirements and generate evidence for audits
- **Audit Readiness:** Maintain up-to-date asset information for audits
- **Reduced Manual Effort:** Eliminate spreadsheet-based tracking of your assets and maintain accurate inventory
- **Enhanced Visibility:** Quickly identify non-compliant or vulnerable assets
- **Resource Optimization:** Identify redundant or underutilized assets and save costs

## Key Features

#### Centralized Asset Repository

Maintain all your infrastructure components from cloud resources to on-premise servers, VPCs, code repositories, serverless functions, users, roles, and groups in a single, searchable inventory with comprehensive metadata.

#### Automated Imports

The module integrates directly with major cloud providers, including Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). It also allows you to manually import infrastructure details into Scrut.
