<Callout kind="plan">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Follow these steps to connect Google Workspace to Scrut and automatically discover SSO applications as vendors.

## Step 1: Integrate Google Workspace

Navigate to **Risks → Vendors → Onboarding** and click **Integrate Now**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162358626-zkx8f7tu8h8-image-1771484965842.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Alternatively, click the **Integrations** tab on the left navigation panel, search for **Google Workspace** and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165191764-vyh32wp3wu8-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-55-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Need help setting up the integration? Refer to Integrating Google Workspace for step-by-step instructions.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Review Discovered Applications

Once connected, Scrut automatically imports the applications authorized in your Google Workspace account. These appear on the **Discovered from Integration** page in **Risk → Vendors → Onboarding**, with the **Source** set to Google Workspace.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165193172-xxw6xhfbiu-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-56-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Review the imported applications and classify each one:

- Click **Mark as Vendor** to add it to your official vendor list.
- Click **Not a Vendor** to exclude it. It stays on this page for reference, but won't appear in your Vendor table.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  📖 For detailed instructions on marking and excluding vendors, see [Discover Vendors through SSO Integration](/docs/discover-vendors-through-sso-integration).
</Callout>

To use the Google Workspace integration, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the Vendor Module and open the **Onboarding** Tab.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163167444-aqrmt3kfk2o-image-WJA4VUOW.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click on **Integrate Now**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162915549-9tnnstcsk9j-image-HY0584NE.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. You will be redirected to the integration page.

4. Search for **Google Workspace** in the Integrations Library.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162649787-srsnqod7gg-image-40WMUH29.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Click **Integrate** to connect with your Google Workspace.

   <Callout kind="tip">
     **Pro Tip!**

     Refer to this guide for [step-by-step instructions on integrating Google Workspace with Scrut](/docs/integrating-google-workspace).
   </Callout>

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163059609-p0qjgjg35e-image-PS4R3AMQ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. Once connected, Scrut will automatically import applications.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162813745-zeeyehynlq-image-F2GV37F9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

7. Review the imported applications and determine whether to classify them as **vendors** or mark them as **non-vendors**_,_ based on organizational needs.

### Add as a Vendor

1. Select the **Add as a vendor** option.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163122314-vamppcqo63-image-T60A4GGL.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. The **Add Vendor** modal will open. Complete the necessary details and click **Create**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162743938-8dp8rc79v4-image-A249MOVT.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. The created Vendor will be present under the [Vendor list](/docs/understanding-vendor-management#vendor-table) on the Vendor dashboard.

### Marking 'Not a Vendor'

1. Select the **Not a vendor** option.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162976858-5sv0t23leix-image-L2NMP2HM.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. **Mark as Not a Vendor** modal will open. Enter the reason and click on **Not a Vendor**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163194334-rxljf8azdtl-image-Y1F41B9K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
