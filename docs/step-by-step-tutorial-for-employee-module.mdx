This article walks you through the recommended workflow for the Employee module.

## Step 1: Import & Manage Employees

Begin by importing your employee data into Scrut:

- **Integrate with your HRIS/HRMS** (recommended)
- **Integrate with your IDP**
- **Import manually** (bulk upload or add individually)

Once imported, manage employees from **People → Employees → All Employees** to view compliance information, upload documents, mark non-personnel accounts, or initiate offboarding.

📖 [Detailed guide: Import & Sync Employees](/docs/adding-an-employee)

## Step 2: Assign Tasks Using Checklists

Create checklists with specific tasks (training, onboarding, security requirements) and assign them to employees or groups.

**Default setup:** Scrut automatically creates a "Scrut Default" checklist assigned to all employees.

📖 [Learn about checklists and task types](/docs/checklist-tasks)

## Step 3: Create Employee Groups

Organize employees into groups for targeted task assignment.

**Best practice:** Create role-specific groups (interns, contractors, permanent staff) or training-specific groups (ISMS, SOC 2, HIPAA) to assign relevant checklists to each group.

**Default setup:** All employees are added to the "Scrut Default" group automatically.

📖 [Detailed guide: Create and Manage Groups](/docs/groups)

## Step 4: Enable Notifications

Maximize task completion rates by turning on automation reminders:

- **Email Notifications:** Scrut sends email notifications to employees when new tasks are added to the employee portal or when they have pending tasks. Refer to this guide on [customizing the email notifications](/docs/email-notifications).

- **Slack Integration:** You can integrate popular communication tools like Slack with Scrut to send messages, notifications, and alerts directly to employees on specific Slack channels. To enable these notifications:

  - Complete [Slack](/docs/integrating-slack) integration
  - [Turn on notifications](/docs/configuring-slack-reminders)

- [**Manual Reminders**](/docs/sending-training-reminders) as needed.

## Step 5: Track Compliance

Monitor employee compliance from the **People → Employees → Overview tab** and the **People → Employees → All Employees tab:**

- View key statistics and progress charts
- Identify employees with pending tasks
- Click into individual employee profiles for detailed information
- Send reminders to non-compliant employees

📖 [Using the Overview and All Employees tabs](/docs/employees-module) 📖 [Viewing Individual Employee Details](/docs/managing-employees)
