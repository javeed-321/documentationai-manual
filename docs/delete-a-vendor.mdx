---
title: "Delete a Vendor"
metaDescription: "Learn how to delete vendors in Scrut."
---

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select the vendor(s) you want to remove from Scrut.

3. Click **More Actions** and select **Delete Vendors**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164720085-u49kseqxt2m-Screenshot-2025-11-20-at-12-42-23.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Click **Delete** to confirm your action.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163784310-62y41ztb6u3-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-21-23-52.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

   <Callout kind="danger">
     **Important:**

     - Deleting a vendor will permanently remove all vendor documents, questionnaires, and mitigation tasks. So proceed with caution.
     - Deleting a vendor will also delete the linked sub-processors.
   </Callout>
