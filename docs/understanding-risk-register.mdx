## What is the Risk Register

The Risk Register is Scrut's central repository for identifying, assessing, tracking, and treating risks across your organization.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  You can also [customize the Risk Register](/docs/customize-risk-register-layout) to meet your specific organizational requirements
</Callout>

## How to Access the Risk Register

1. From the left navigation panel, navigate to **Risk → Risk Management.**
2. Click the **Risk Register** tab.
3. The Risk Register opens, showing all risks along with their current status, assignee, and other details.

## Risk Register Overview

The Risk Register page is built around the following components.

### Add Risk

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161849707-4go94ay1bl3-Add-Risk-button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Click the **Add Risk** button to create a risk using any of Scrut's four methods: Risk Discovery, manual entry, bulk import, or another Scrut module. See [Add a Risk](/docs/adding-a-new-risk) for details on each method.

### Risk Settings

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163311829-988a1h6qo1j-Risk-Settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Click the **Settings** icon in the top right and navigate to the **Table Layout** tab to configure the Risk Register. See here for more details.

### Metrics Overview

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165875830-t731f297lhg-Screenshot-2026-07-10-at-13-49-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

The metric cards across the top show a live count of risks in each status: Open, Assessed, Treatment in Progress, Monitor, Treated, and Closed. Hover over the info icon on any card to view the description of that status. See Risk Statuses for a full explanation of each one.

### Table View Filters

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165877095-89ut4b07wys-Screenshot-2026-07-10-at-13-49-50.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Use the **Assignee, Department, Category**, and **Entities** dropdowns above the metric cards to narrow what the page shows. Click **More Filters** for additional criteria. Click the circular reset icon next to the filters to clear everything you've applied.

### Risk Table

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165878454-bk02m243fas-Screenshot-2026-07-10-at-13-50-17.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

The risk table lists every identified risk with a quick snapshot of its status, assignee, and other details, so you can assess your risk landscape at a glance.

- **Search**: Use the search bar above the table to find risks by title, custom field values, or other keywords.
- **Filters**: Click More Filters to refine the table by Added On, Last Modified, Source Type, Treatment Strategy, Type, Major/Minor Risk, or Recurrence. Apply your selections to view the filtered results.
- **Column Selector**: Click Columns to choose which fields appear in the risk table beyond your organization's default set. See Configure Risk Register Layout for how the default column set and order are configured, and how this selector relates to them.

### Export

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165879758-ogev2to4e5-Screenshot-2026-07-10-at-13-50-33.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Scrut gives you two ways to export risk data.

- Export Risk Report, in the top right of the page, generates a formatted risk report.
- Export, above the risk table, downloads the current table view as a CSV file. See Export the Risk Register for details.
