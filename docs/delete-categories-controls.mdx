  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Trust Advanced Add-On** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans

## How To Delete a Control

1. Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview** and scroll to the **Controls** section.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171104993-0vmunnzrgdxg-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21-32-44-3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click the category card of the control you want to delete.

3. Click the trash can icon beside the control name to delete it.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171109075-7tvhkeehzuc-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22-00-41.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171110455-oca3xcr305e-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22-00-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171111994-45jppbvf518-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-22-01-20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## How To Delete a Category

1. Navigate to **Trust → Trust Vault → Overview** and scroll to the **Controls** section.

2. Click the category card you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171106318-6lpup7ryyh-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-21-32-44-4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click the trash can icon to delete it.

   <Callout kind="danger">
     **Heads Up!**

     To delete a category, you have to first delete all controls within it.
   </Callout>
