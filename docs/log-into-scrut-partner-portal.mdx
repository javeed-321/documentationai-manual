---
title: "Log in to the Partner Portal"
metaDescription: "Learn how to access the Scrut Partner Portal."
---

Once your CSM has created your partner account, you’ll receive an email from Scrut. Click the **Login** button on the email to access the partner portal.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162584666-vzeetsrhm4a-Partner-Login-Email.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Alternatively, you can also navigate to [**console.scrut.io**](https://console.scrut.io/login) and enter the email you’ve registered with Scrut. Click **Continue** to verify your email. Enter the code received in your inbox, and click Continue.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165079728-n50nw1icz5-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-19-12-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Once your email is verified, you’ll land on the **Workspace Console** for Scrut partners.
