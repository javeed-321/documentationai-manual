<Callout kind="custom">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

If you have created a risk for testing purposes, deleting it removes it permanently from your Risk Register.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important:**

  - Deleting a risk is permanent and cannot be reversed.
  - You cannot delete risks that are in Closed status.
</Callout>

## How To Delete a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171295100-s6uy1fzf7pc-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15-02-34.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Delete**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171296475-a1kj07o6ur-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15-02-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Confirm your action to proceed.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171297834-nx688a1zry9-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15-03-06.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Scrut will remove the risk permanently from your Risk Register.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171299185-tmigsvm52g-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15-03-18.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## Deleting Multiple Risks

You can also delete several risks at the same time.

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to delete.

3. Click **Delete**.
