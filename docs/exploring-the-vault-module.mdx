In this article, we’ll explain how to upload documents, create and manage folders in the Vault.

## Managing Folders

You can create folders and sub-folders in the Vault to organize your documents.

### Create a Folder

1. Log in to your Scrut account, expand the **Compliance** tab on the left navigation panel, and click **Vault**.

2. Click the **Add button** and select **Folder**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161829424-y2deo6tp64e-Add-Folder-Document.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Give the folder a name and click **Create**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163865821-54q9nfggx25-Screenshot-2025-07-21-at-17-04-39.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Create a Nested (Sub) Folder

Scrut also allows you to create multiple layers of sub-folders in the Vault for enhanced document organization. To create a nested folder:

- Navigate to the Vault module and open your preferred folder.
- Click the **Create Folder** button.
- Enter the name of the sub-folder and click **Create**.

### Move Multiple Folders and Files

You can also move multiple folders and files at once.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Grid View">
    To move multiple folders and files, follow these steps:

    - **To select a range (contiguous selection)**

      - Click the first item, then hold the _Shift_ key and click the last item.
      - All files and folders in between will be selected.

    - **To select separate items (non-contiguous selection)**

      - Click the first item, hold the _Ctrl key_ (Windows) or _Command key_ (macOS), and click each additional item you want to select.
      - Only the items you click will be selected.

    - Click the consolidated **Move Selected** button at the top, select the destination folder and confirm your action.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164545526-zfrrrhyvq1-Screenshot-2025-10-23-at-13-26-35.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## Managing Documents

You can upload documents to the following locations:

### Upload Directly to the Vault (Root Folder)

1. Navigate to the Vault module, click the **Add** button and select **Document**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161829424-y2deo6tp64e-Add-Folder-Document.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Select and upload your desired file. (Maximum size: 100 MB).

3. Choose the entities for which you want to make this document accessible. By default, organization wide is pre-selected and the document will be accessible by all entities.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161822694-05llzde9kwy4-Add-Document.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. This adds the document directly to the vault's root folder.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162336891-s31jkfhoxtg-image-1770013021088.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Upload to a Folder or Sub-folder

1. Navigate to the Vault module and open your desired folder.

2. Click the **Upload Document** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166195846-azlomfhgpqi-Upload-Document-button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Select and upload your desired file. (Maximum size: 100 MB).

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165068864-tafchsbic9h-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-11-47-52.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. This adds the document to the specific folder.
