# Getting Started with cVRP

cVRP can be integrated into your own app, website, or via a URL link sent directly to end users.

## Pre-requisites

**For Production**

- You have been fully onboarded as a Partner or Direct Customer and have provided us with your secure redirect URL for the production environment.
- We have reviewed and approved your implementation and user interface to ensure it complies with our guidelines and regulatory requirements.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Prerequisites of a cVRP transaction**

  Commercial VRP is scoped to a defined set of eligible sectors. Before building your integration, confirm that your use case falls within one of the categories listed in the [cVRP overview](/docs/cvrp-overview#eligible-use-cases-cvrp1).

  If you are unsure whether your use case is in scope, speak to your Modulr point of contact before proceeding.
</Callout>
