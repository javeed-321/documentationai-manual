---
title: "Access Policy Settings"
metaDescription: "Learn how to access policy settings."
---

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** icon on the top-right of the Policy module.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166231230-pxny24gc5c-settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Settings icon" width="25" height="25" caption=" " />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163268398-clx16menwl5-Policy-Settings-icon.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. This brings you to the **Policy Settings** page, where you can configure policy settings as required.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163269756-fsjnoob1rm5-Policy-Settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
