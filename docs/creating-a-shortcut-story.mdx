## Creating a Shortcut Story

1. Log into app.scrut.io.
2. Form the left navigation panel expand the compliance menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162604422-o6trk59nfy9-image-1OXJTB6P.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click on **Cloud**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163164618-7orxrf09a4l-image-WHQZGX9R.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Navigate to **Test** section.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162689034-3nax6rxfurj-image-6KOH6GR2.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. After landing on the Test page, open the test corresponding to which you want to create a Linear issue.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162752917-wrzbwfc31jf-image-ALPCLKG7.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. After opening the respective test, click on the three vertical dots button on the top right corner. 

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162626037-irv5s66mau-image-2S7MNWKD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

7. Click on _Create Shortcut Story_.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163205973-rrnn1o8trjs-image-Z4PGSANN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

8. Once done, you will be prompted to fill in the story fields that you had selected during integration.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163195816-m56fwqy9hpn-image-YEUOFI5X.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

9. Once done, click on _Create_.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162623333-xhd0b7wetpp-image-2PYB0DBC.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
