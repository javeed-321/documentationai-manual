Modulr's DirectPay product

<Callout kind="alert">
  PIS is only available for GBP payments on UK payment rails
</Callout>

Using the Modulr Open Banking Payment Initiation Service, you can enable end users to make payments directly from an online account held with their bank.

<Callout kind="info">
  In our guides we use the term "bank" to generically refer to the organisation providing the payment account to the end user, also known as the "Account Servicing Payment Service Provider" (ASPSP). This will therefore include non-banks such as e-money institutions and building societies.
</Callout>
