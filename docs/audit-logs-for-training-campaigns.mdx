The Audit Logs in the Training Campaigns module provide a complete history of all activities within the module.

## How To View Audit Logs for Training Campaigns

1. Click **People → Training Campaigns** on the left navigation menu, and click on the campaign for which you want to view the logs.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166064290-7ohogcr9bpu-Selecting-an-individual-training-campaign.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Navigate to the **Audit Log** tab.

3. Here you can find detailed information about actions performed, including:

   - **User Actions**: Specific actions taken by users, such as creating, editing, and updating the selected campaign.
   - **Date and Time Stamp:** Each action logged includes the date and time it was performed, providing a clear timeline of activities.

4. Use the search bar and filters to find specific actions quickly.

   - **Search Bar:** Use it to quickly find specific actions, keywords, or details within the audit logs.
   - **Sort By:** Use this filter to sort the logs by newest or oldest.
   - **Performed By:** Use this filter to find actions performed by a specific user quickly.
   - **Date Filter:** Use this filter to find logs based on specific criteria, such as the current month, the previous month, or a custom range.
