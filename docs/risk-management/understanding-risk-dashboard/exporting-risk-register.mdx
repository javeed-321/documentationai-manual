<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Exporting your risk register provides a snapshot of your organization's risks for reporting, analysis, and record-keeping.

## How To Export the Risk Register

1. Sign in to Scrut and go to **Risk → Risk Management** using the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Risk Register** tab.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-21-at-18.30.08.png" alt="" />

3. Click the **Export** button at the top of the risk table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-21-at-18.30.37.png" alt="" />

4. Select the columns to include in the CSV file and click **Export**.

5. The columns currently visible in your Risk Register table are pre-selected in the export modal.

   - This includes both your organization's default columns from Table Layout and any additional columns you've viewed using the Risk Register's Columns selector.
   - You can select or deselect columns in the export modal, as required.
   - **Note:** Risk ID and Risk Name are mandatory columns and can't be deselected from the Export.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-16.49.44.png" alt="" />

Scrut will download all the risks in your Risk Register as a CSV file. The file includes all risks in your Risk Register with your chosen details, such as risk name, status, department, and assignee.
