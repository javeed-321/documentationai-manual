---
title: "Turn on Risk IDs"
description: "Learn how to enable risk IDs in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Growth** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

The **Risk ID** adds a unique identifier for each risk. When you enable the Risk ID feature, Scrut will add your chosen custom Risk ID prefix to all risks. This makes it easier to find and manage risk-related information. It also helps keep risks organized and easy to identify. Users can quickly locate specific risks using their assigned Risk ID, creating a smoother risk management experience.

## How To Enable Risk ID

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** button in the top right corner.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163311829-988a1h6qo1j-Risk-Settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Go to the **Configurations** tab and turn on the **Enable Risk ID** toggle.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162194941-4b3t5uj269a-image-1762151141761.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the Risk ID prefix field, enter your custom Risk ID.

   **Note:** Risk ID is a mandatory column in Table Layout. It always appears first in the Risk Register table and can't be reordered or hidden.

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     - You can use both letters and numbers in the prefix.
     - Maximum allowed length is 10 characters.
     - Special characters aren’t permitted.
   </Callout>

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164591888-osh7jug14lc-Screenshot-2025-11-03-at-11-48-54.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164593224-9kzr5yhqckw-Screenshot-2025-11-03-at-11-49-01.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Once enabled, the Risk ID becomes the first column in the risk table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164594584-vd06xlasoh-Screenshot-2025-11-03-at-11-49-24.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="success">
  **Note:**

  Risk ID is automatically generated for all existing and new risks in chronological order of risk creation.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  If you want to change the Risk ID prefix in the future, you can do so by disabling and re-enabling the Risk ID toggle in settings.
</Callout>
