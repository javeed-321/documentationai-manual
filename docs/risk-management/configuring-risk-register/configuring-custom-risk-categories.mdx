</Callout>

Custom Categories in Scrut's Risk Register enable users to manage risks more efficiently by creating categories that suit specific needs, ultimately enhancing the overall usability and effectiveness of the risk management module.

## How to Add a Custom Category

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel and click the **Settings** icon on the top-right of the page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163311829-988a1h6qo1j-Risk-Settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Navigate to the **Categories** tab and click the **Add Category** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161844431-uwent4s4z1-Add-Risk-Category.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Enter the category name and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165200243-mczxdgstnwf-Screenshot-2026-02-20-at-16-39-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

You can now select this category while creating or editing risks.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  If a risk category is deleted, all risks associated with that particular category are reassigned to "Others".
</Callout>
