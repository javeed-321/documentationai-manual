<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

If you have created a risk for testing purposes, deleting it removes it permanently from your Risk Register.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important:**

  - Deleting a risk is permanent and cannot be reversed.
  - You cannot delete risks that are in Closed status.
</Callout>

## How To Delete a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to delete.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15.02.34.png" alt="" />

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Delete**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15.02.51.png" alt="" />

4. Confirm your action to proceed.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15.03.06.png" alt="" />

Scrut will remove the risk permanently from your Risk Register.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-15.03.18.png" alt="" />

## Deleting Multiple Risks

You can also delete several risks at the same time.

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to delete.

3. Click **Delete**.
