title: "Link Assets to a Risk"
description: "Learn how to link assets to a risk."
---

In this article, we explain how to link impacted assets to a risk:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Risk → Risk Management** using the left navigation panel.

2. Select one or more risks to which you want to link specific assets.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the risk table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164781402-3xrn8sb84yf-Screenshot-2025-11-26-at-22-11-37.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click the **+** icon next to the **Linked Assets** field.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164784557-co6rlmk27vg-Screenshot-2025-11-26-at-22-14-01.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Select the asset type and choose the specific assets you want to link to this risk.

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164783136-nb380x4pgm-Screenshot-2025-11-26-at-22-12-39.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Link Multiple Asset Types to a Risk

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Risk → Risk Management** using the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Risk Register** and select the risk to which you want to link the asset.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166046257-d0yq3nuniws-Selecting-a-Risk.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click the **edit icon** in the **Linked Assets** field.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164809598-pyrfmh5a17r-Screenshot-2025-11-28-at-19-04-33.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Select the **Asset Type**, and choose the desired assets.

5. Click **Save**.
