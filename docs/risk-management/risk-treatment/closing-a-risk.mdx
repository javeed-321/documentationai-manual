  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

In risk management, closing a risk ensures that a risk is fully addressed through manual confirmation. Admins or Risk Assignees must review and complete all mitigation efforts before closing a risk, providing an extra layer of oversight and validation.

## How To Close a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to close.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171273310-3mgpqkjnyz4-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-35-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Close**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171274668-m5tyj1gx21-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-36-12.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Provide a reason for closing the risk and click Close.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171276039-q606ejn9zf-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-36-32.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The risk will then move to Closed status, and your reason will be displayed.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171277395-9nrelymfnt-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-36-50.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Closing Multiple Risks

You can also close several risks at the same time.

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to close.

3. Click **Risk Actions** and choose **Close**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171269084-sr8n2685jvc-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-30-11.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Provide a reason for closing the risks and click **Close**.
