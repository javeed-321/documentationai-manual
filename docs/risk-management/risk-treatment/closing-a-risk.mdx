In risk management, closing a risk ensures that a risk is fully addressed through manual confirmation. Admins or Risk Assignees must review and complete all mitigation efforts before closing a risk, providing an extra layer of oversight and validation.

## How To Close a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to close.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.35.55.png" alt="" />

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Close**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.36.12.png" alt="" />

4. Provide a reason for closing the risk and click Close.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.36.32.png" alt="" />

The risk will then move to Closed status, and your reason will be displayed.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.36.50.png" alt="" />

## Closing Multiple Risks

You can also close several risks at the same time.

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to close.

3. Click **Risk Actions** and choose **Close**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.30.11.png" alt="" />

4. Provide a reason for closing the risks and click **Close**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.31.04.png" alt="" />
