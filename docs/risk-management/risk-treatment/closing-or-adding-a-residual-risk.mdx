<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

A residual risk in Scrut refers to the level of risk that remains even after controls or mitigation tasks have been applied. Adding a residual risk allows you to assign a residual risk score and officially treat a risk item while acknowledging any remaining risk.

## How To Add Residual Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Risk Register** tab and click on the risk for which you want to add a residual risk.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166046257-d0yq3nuniws-Selecting-a-Risk.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Heads Up!**

     You cannot add residual risk if any mitigation tasks are open for the risk. Close all pending mitigation tasks to proceed.
   </Callout>

3. Scroll to the Residual Risk section, and click **Add Residual Risk**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165072893-1w2f3nyuu4u-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-12-10-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. You will see a warning message that states, “Adding a residual risk will lock this assessment. You cannot add mitigation tasks until the next assessment cycle.”

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165074291-c0dofvs8nr-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-12-11-04.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click **Add Residual Risk** to proceed.

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Tip!**

     If the risk is not yet treated, complete all pending actions with mitigation tasks before proceeding.
   </Callout>

6. Drag the slider for each residual factor to adjust its value, and the residual risk score will be automatically calculated using the [configured risk score formula](/docs/risk-management/configuring-risk-register/configuring-custom-risk-score-formula#how-to-configure-risk-scores).

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165075642-esb6qcy5bf-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-12-15-54.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Set the **Recurrence**. This is the assessment cadence, how frequently this risk should be reviewed after treatment. By default, the recurrence is set to Quarterly. You can choose from: Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly, Monthly, Custom, or Never, depending on the risk and your organizational risk appetite.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165077002-0inb3v8guatp-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-12-22-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

8. The residual risk score is updated.

9. If the [Risk Approval Workflow](/docs/risk-management/risk-treatment/risk-approval-workflow) is turned OFF:

   - It moves to the **Treated** status.
   - You’ll need to click the **Review Risk** button to make any changes to a risk in the Treated status.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165078376-kat81lcp4sf-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-12-28-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

10. If the Risk Approval Workflow is turned ON:

    - The risk remains in the **Treatment in Progress** status.
    - You’ll need to click the **Send for Approval** button to send the risk to the Approver for review.
