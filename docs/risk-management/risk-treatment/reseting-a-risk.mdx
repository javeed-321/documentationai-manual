In risk management, resetting a risk refers to re-evaluating a particular risk based on new information. Resetting a risk ensures that its current status, scores, and linked controls are updated to null, allowing for new evaluations and assessments.

## How To Reset a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to reset.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.57.44.png" alt="" />

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Reset**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.58.04.png" alt="" />

   \4. The risk will then move to **Open** status.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14.58.19.png" alt="" />

## Resetting Multiple Risks

You can also reset multiple risks simultaneously.

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to reset.

3. Click **Risk Actions** and choose **Reset**.
