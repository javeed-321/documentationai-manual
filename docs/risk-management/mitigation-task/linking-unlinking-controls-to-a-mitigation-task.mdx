<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut automatically populates controls when [creating a mitigation task during risk assessment](/docs/risk-management/mitigation-task/automatic-population-of-controls). Additionally, it also gives you the flexibility to manually add or remove controls, if needed. In this article, we walk you through the process of linking/unlinking controls to a mitigation task.

## How to Link/Unlink Controls to a Mitigation Task

1. Sign in to Scrut, go to **Risk → Risk Management** using the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Risk Register** tab, and click on a specific risk.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-21-at-11.25.39-1.png" alt="" />

3. On the risk details page, scroll to the **Mitigation Tasks** tab, and click on the task to which you want to link/unlink controls.

4. Scroll to the **Controls & Requirements** tab, and click the **Link/Unlink Controls** button.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-21-at-13.03.20.png" alt="" />

5. Use the **Frameworks** panel to view controls relevant to your frameworks.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-14.51.47.png" alt="" />

6. Expand a specific requirement to see all controls associated with it.

   - When you select a requirement, all controls within it will be selected by default.
   - If needed, you can then select/unselect specific controls as required.

7. Select the checkboxes next to the controls you want to link.

   **Note:** Any requirement that is not applicable will appear greyed out.

8. Use the search bar at the top to quickly locate controls by name or code.

9. Once you've made the selections, enable the Show Linked Controls toggle to review and verify which controls you have chosen to link.

10. Click **Save** to confirm your selections.

    <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-15.03.02.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Important:**

  - Any new control you link to a mitigation task will be automatically added to the associated risk.
  - However, if you unlink a control from a mitigation task, it will not impact the associated risk.
</Callout>
