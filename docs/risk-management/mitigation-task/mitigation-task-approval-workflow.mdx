## Overview

The Mitigation Task Approval Workflow adds a formal approval step to your mitigation task process:

- The Assignee completes the task and uploads supporting artifacts

- The Approver then reviews the work and either:

  - Approves it (or)
  - Requests a revision before it is marked as Completed

This ensures proper validation of mitigation efforts and strengthens governance within your organization.

## When the Approval Workflow is Enabled

When the Mitigation Task Approval Workflow is enabled, an additional approval step is introduced. The assigned Approver reviews the mitigation task before it can be marked as Completed. The workflow adds two new statuses—**Under Review** and **Needs Revision**—to the standard task lifecycle.

## How to Enable the Mitigation Task Approval Workflow

1. Go to **Risk → Risk Management** and click the **Settings** icon on the top right.
2. Navigate to the **Configurations** tab.
3. Turn on the **Mitigation Task Approval Flow** toggle.

Once enabled, all open mitigation tasks will require approval before they can be marked as **Completed**.

## How to Assign a Mitigation Task Approver

You can assign an Approver when creating the task or update it at any time from the task detail page.

### Default Approver

When creating a mitigation task, the Approver field is pre-filled as follows:

- **If a Risk Assignee exists:** The Approver field defaults to the Risk Assignee.
- **If no Risk Assignee is set:** The Approver field defaults to the task creator.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Scrut recommends assigning a different Approver than the task Assignee to ensure an independent review. You can still proceed with the same person in both roles if needed.
</Callout>

### Updating the Approver After Submission

The Approver can be changed at any time, even after the task has been submitted for Review.

- If you change the Approver while the task is in the Under Review status, the new Approver is notified immediately—without waiting for any further action.
- The previous Approver's action buttons are hidden once they are removed.

## \[For Assignees\] How to Submit a Mitigation Task for Review

Once the Assignee has completed the task and uploaded the required artifacts, they can submit the task for approval.

1. Open the mitigation task from **Risk → Risk Management → Mitigation Task**.
2. Upload the supporting **artifacts** as evidence of task completion.
3. Click **Submit for Review**.

<Image src="/images/Submit-for-Review.png" alt="" />

The task status changes to **Under Review,** and the Approver receives an email notification.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-24-at-11.57.43.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  The Submit for Review button is only active when an attachment is present and an Approver is selected.
</Callout>

## \[For Approvers\] Reviewing a Mitigation Task

When a mitigation task is submitted for your Review, you will receive an email notification.

1. Click the link in your notification email, or go to the Notification Center and click **View Details** to open the task.
2. Review the task details, attached artifacts, and any supporting notes.
3. Choose one of the following actions:

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-24-at-12.02.59.png" alt="" />

### Approve the Task

1. If the task is complete and satisfactory, click **Approve**.
2. The mitigation task status changes to **Completed**.
3. No further edits can be made to the task details or attachments once approved.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-24-at-12.10.27.png" alt="" />

### Request a Revision

1. If the task needs changes, click **Request Revision**.
2. Enter a reason for the revision request in the text field. Provide clear, actionable feedback—explain what is missing and what needs to be done.
3. The task status changes to **Needs Revision**.

Once a revision is requested:

- The task is returned to the Assignee with the status set to **Needs Revision**.
- The Assignee receives a notification to revisit and update the task.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-24-at-12.03.44.png" alt="" />

## \[For Assignees\] Resubmitting After a Revision Request

The Assignee makes the necessary changes based on the Approver's feedback and resubmits the task for Review. The approval workflow repeats as many times as needed until the Approver is satisfied and approves the task.

## What Happens When the Approval Workflow is Toggled Off

If you disable the Mitigation Task Approval Workflow, the following changes apply:

- Tasks that were **Under Review** or **Needs Revision** revert to **Open** or **Overdue**, based on their due date.
- The Approver field is removed from the task detail page, and the Approver column is removed from the mitigation task table.
- Only three statuses remain: **Open, Overdue**, and **Completed**.
