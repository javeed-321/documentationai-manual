<Callout kind="custom">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut makes it easy for auditors to link relevant controls directly with an audit request. This allows auditors to establish a direct relationship between the controls and audit requests.

## How To Link/Unlink Controls to an Audit Request

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Audit → Audit Center** using the left navigation panel.

2. Navigate to the **Audits** tab and click on a specific audit.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164136209-2n2jo3r4cig-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-18-42-12.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. On the Audit page, click the **Requests** tab and open the request to which you want to link/unlink controls.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164143477-3zj0d5h79ht-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-18-56-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. On the Request page, scroll to the **Controls & Requirements** tab, and click the**Link/Unlink Controls** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164144820-5uoakrt4294-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-18-57-06.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Use the **Frameworks** panel to view controls relevant to your frameworks.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164139270-yxw6yq148rj-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-18-46-10.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. Expand a specific requirement to see all controls associated with it.

   - When you select a requirement, all controls within it will be selected by default.
   - If needed, you can then select/unselect specific controls as required.

7. Select the checkboxes next to the controls you want to link.

   **Note:** Any requirement that is not applicable will appear greyed out.

8. Use the search bar at the top to quickly locate controls by name or code.

9. Once you've made the selections, enable the Show Linked Controls toggle to review and verify which controls you have chosen to link.

10. Click **Save** to confirm your selections.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164140677-v8gvlar8x7i-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-18-46-42.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important**

  Once the controls are linked, the status of the linked controls will be automatically changed to **Non-Compliant**.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="success">
  **Note**

  Once the Request is closed by the Assignee or Auditor, the controls status will be automatically changed to **Compliant**.
</Callout>
