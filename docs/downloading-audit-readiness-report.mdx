The **Audit Readiness Report** provides a comprehensive breakdown of your audit progress that you can quickly share with stakeholders. It captures detailed information on all artifacts included in this audit, any findings or requests reported, and the audit control status.

The report includes structured data so auditors can independently verify evidence or run their own tooling over it. You can download the report at any point during the audit lifecycle while the audit is in progress or after completion.

## How To Download the Audit Readiness Report

1. [Via the Audit Center](/docs/downloading-audit-readiness-report#method-1-via-the-audit-center)
2. [Via the Reports Module](/docs/downloading-audit-readiness-report#method-2-via-the-reports-module)

### Method 1: Via the Audit Center

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Open the audit for which you want to download the report.

3. Click the **Export** dropdown and select **Audit Report**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165920786-w9fbd2nj55-Screenshot-2026-07-23-at-14-31-46.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. The report includes all audit requirements and artifacts, including out-of-scope items. Click **Export**.

5. Once it’s ready, you can download it from the **Notification Center**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165919485-bu6f698f8y-Screenshot-2026-07-23-at-14-21-26.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Method 2: Via the Reports Module

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Reports** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Download** button on the **Audit Readiness** report.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171117472-dwas79euku-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11-48-47.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Select the audit and then choose the checkboxes for the data you want to include in the report.

4. Click **Download**.

5. Once the report is ready, you can download it from the Notifications Center.
