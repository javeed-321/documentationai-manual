The **Reports** module provides comprehensive compliance and security reports that you can generate and share with leadership teams, auditors, and other stakeholders.

## How To Download Compliance Reports in Scrut?

1. Sign in to Scrut and scroll to the bottom of the left navigation menu.

2. Click on **Reports**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163282470-us1j91wben-Reports-dashboard.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. The Reports dashboard will display all the available compliance reports for your organization.

4. Click **Export** and select the required attributes.

5. Once the export is ready, you can download it from the Notification Center.

6. A link to the report will also be sent to your email.
