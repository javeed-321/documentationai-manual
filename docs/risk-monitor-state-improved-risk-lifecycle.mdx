  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Dynamic risk monitoring continuously assesses risks to ensure controls are effective and to identify new risks, enhancing the organization’s risk management strategy with greater clarity and control.

## How To Monitor a Risk

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Click on the risk you want to monitor.

3. Click the three-dots icon at the top right, and click **Monitor**.

## Monitoring Multiple Risks

You can also update the status of multiple risks to **Monitor**:

1. Click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left navigation panel, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

2. Select the risks you want to monitor.

3. Click **Risk Actions** and choose **Monitor**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  User actions may impact the status of a monitored risk. For example:

  - Conducting an inherent assessment on a monitored risk changes its status to **Treatment in Progress.**
  - Performing a residual assessment on a monitored risk changes its status to **Treated**.
  - Adding a mitigation task to a monitored risk changes its status to **Treatment-in-progress**.
</Callout>
