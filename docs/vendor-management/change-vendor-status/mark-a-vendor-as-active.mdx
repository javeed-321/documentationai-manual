---
title: "Mark a Vendor as Active"
description: "Learn how to mark a vendor as active in Scrut."
---

Active vendors are those with whom you currently conduct business. This includes any vendor currently providing you with services or products. It is important to mark these vendors as Active to ensure you have an up-to-date record of all your ongoing vendor relationships.

- [Mark a Single Vendor as Active](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/mark-a-vendor-as-active#mark-a-single-vendor-as-active)
- [Mark Multiple Vendors as Active](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/mark-a-vendor-as-active#mark-multiple-vendors-as-active)

## Mark a Single Vendor as Active

1. Click **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select your preferred vendor from the table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166047561-u8rfmomknjn-Selecting-a-Single-Vendor.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Once you’re on the vendor page, click the **More Actions (three-dots) icon** and select **Mark as Active**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162542845-ey974bknb68-More-Actions-on-the-Vendor-Details-page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   Scrut changes the vendor status from prospective to active, and the vendor will be added to your Active Vendors list.

## Mark Multiple Vendors as Active

1. Click **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.
2. Select your desired vendors from the table.
3. Click the **More Actions** button and click **Mark as Active**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162541413-kmwel8iur3c-More-Actions---Multiple-Vendors.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Scrut moves the selected vendors to the Active Vendors list.
