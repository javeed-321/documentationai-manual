---
title: "Mark a Vendor as Prospective"
description: "Learn how to mark a vendor as prospective in Scrut."
---

Prospective vendors are potential vendors you’re evaluating but haven’t yet been approved for active business. It is important to mark these vendors as Prospective, so that you have an accurate record of all your vendor relationships.

- [Mark a Single Vendor as Prospective](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/mark-a-vendor-as-prospective#mark-a-single-vendor-as-prospective)
- [Mark Multiple Vendors as Prospective](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/mark-a-vendor-as-prospective#mark-multiple-vendors-as-prospective)

## Mark a Single Vendor as Prospective

1. Click **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select your preferred vendor from the table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166047561-u8rfmomknjn-Selecting-a-Single-Vendor.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Once you’re on the vendor page, click the **More Actions (three-dots) icon** and select **Mark as Prospective**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164721517-be93goy80em-Screenshot-2025-11-20-at-13-30-31.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   Scrut changes the vendor status from active to prospective, and adds the vendor to your Prospective Vendors list.

## Mark Multiple Vendors as Prospective

1. Click **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.
2. Select your desired vendors from the table.
3. Click the **More Actions** button and click **Mark as Prospective**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162541413-kmwel8iur3c-More-Actions---Multiple-Vendors.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Scrut moves the selected vendors to the Prospective Vendors list.
