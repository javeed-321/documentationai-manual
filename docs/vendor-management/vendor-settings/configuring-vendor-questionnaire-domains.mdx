With customizable vendor domains, you can tailor the assessment process to precisely fit your organization's requirements.

## Configuring Vendor Questionnaire Domains

1. From the left navigation panel, expand the *Risk* menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Settings**.

   <Image src="/images/image-18HR7SRJ.png" alt="" />

3. Click **Domains**.

<Image src="/images/image-4P0O6ZH8.png" alt="" />

4. By default, users will see 9 pre-configured domains. Users can add more domains by clicking New Domain button.

<Image src="/images/image-P647AINM.png" alt="" />

5. Enter domain name and click **Save**.

<Image src="/images/image-TC4992KF.png" alt="" />
