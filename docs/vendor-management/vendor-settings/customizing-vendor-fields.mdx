<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Custom vendor fields give you the power to tailor the vendor profiles to meet unique needs. It gives you the flexibility to define and capture additional information about vendors, ensuring the vendor profiles are personalized and adaptable.

## Configuring Custom Vendor Fields

1. From the left navigation panel, expand the _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Settings**.

   <Image src="/images/image-52F22VNW.png" alt="" />

3. Select the **Custom Field** tab.

<Image src="/images/image-L7IWSRE1.png" alt="" />

4. Click **New Field** to create a new vendor custom field.

<Image src="/images/image-NLGGTETR.png" alt="" />

Add Custom Field window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-BTN9QC60.png" alt="" />

5. In _Custom Field Name_, enter the name of the custom field.
6. Click **Save**.

The custom fields created will be displayed on the [Vendor details](/docs/vendor-management/editing-vendor-details/understanding-vendor-details-page) page.
