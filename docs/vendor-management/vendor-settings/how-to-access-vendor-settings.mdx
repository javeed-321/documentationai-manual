The first step to effectively using Scrut's Vendor Management module is to customize it to your organization's specific needs. The Vendor Settings page includes the following:

- [Custom Vendor Fields](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-settings/customizing-vendor-fields)
- [Custom Vendor Categories](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-settings/configuring-vendor-custom-categories)
- [Vendor Questionnaire Domains](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-settings/configuring-vendor-questionnaire-domains)
- [Settings Audit Logs](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-settings/vendor-settings-audit-logs)

## How To Access Vendor Settings

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Risk → Vendors** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** icon on the top-right.
