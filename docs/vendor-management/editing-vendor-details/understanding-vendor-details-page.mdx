Knowing your vendors is a crucial part of your compliance journey. The **Vendors** page in the **Vendor Management** module provides a comprehensive overview of each vendor profile. This article explains the different components and sections of this page.

## How To Access the Vendor Page

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Risk → Vendors** using the left navigation menu.

2. Click the **Vendors** tab.

3. Click on any vendor in the list to view their details.

4. This opens the Vendor page, where you can view and edit vendor information.

### Vendor Metrics

The vendor overview page contains various sections, each presenting specific metrics to provide immediate insights. These sections include:

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163790890-4jqz3l8z9is-Screenshot-2025-06-25-at-11-17-46.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- **Average Risk Score**: Specifies the average risk score of the questionnaires that the vendor has submitted.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Calculation Method**

  1. Retrieves all questionnaires from the vendor that are in an accepted state.

  2. Obtains all questions in a given questionnaire that are in an accepted state and calculates the average.

     - Average = (Sum of the risk scores \[if no risk score, then 0 is added\]) / Total number of questions in that questionnaire in an accepted state.

  3. Calculate the average risk score for the vendor.

     - Average = (Sum of all the risk scores calculated in step 2) / Number of questionnaires in an accepted state.
</Callout>

- **Open Mitigation Tasks**: Displays all vendor mitigation tasks that are in an open state.
- **Questionnaires Not Submitted**: Shows the number of questionnaires that the Vendor has not submitted.
- **Questionnaires Not Reviewed**: Shows the number of questionnaires submitted by the vendor that have not been reviewed yet.

### Vendor Details

The Details tab provides you with in-depth information about the vendor.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164033421-380kjbohyqa-Screenshot-2025-08-08-at-10-49-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- **Service Description:** Briefly describe the reasons for engaging this vendor’s services.
- **Vendor URL:** Link to the official vendor website.
- **Assigned to:** Identify the individual responsible for monitoring and managing this vendor relationship. If required, you can easily reassign this role through a user-friendly dropdown.
- **Category**: The most appropriate category that represents the vendor's services or products.
- **Vendor Tier:** Categorize vendors based on their risk level or business impact to prioritize risk management efforts effectively. (e.g., Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3).
- **Inherent Risk:** Gain insights into the inherent threat that the vendor poses to your organization before implementing any risk mitigation measures. This information is crucial when establishing a vendor profile within the platform. Options are Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
- **Residual Risk:** Understand the risk that remains after implementing risk mitigation controls. Based on the validated risk score, users can categorize the vendor's risk as Critical, High, Medium, or Low.
- **Entities**: This dropdown displays a comprehensive list of entities.
- **Sub-Processor:** Shows if the vendor is [linked to sub-processors](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/adding-managing-content/how-to-add-subprocessors) that will be displayed in the Trust Vault.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  When the vendor is in assessed status, the "Recurrence" and "Next Assessment Date" fields will be visible in the vendor details.
</Callout>

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164034815-y3d18h60ys-Screenshot-2025-08-08-at-10-52-02.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- **Recurrence:** Determine how frequently you wish to evaluate the vendor's risk. Options include Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly, Monthly, Never or allowing customization to align with your organization's risk assessment strategy.
- **Next Assessment:** Keep track of the vendor's upcoming risk assessment date.

### Questionnaires

The Questionnaires tab provides a list of questionnaires you have sent to the vendor for risk assessment in the past. This tab provides details on questionnaire names, submission deadlines, review dates, reviewers, statuses, and actionable items. For a deeper dive, learn more about [Vendor Questionnaires](/docs/vendor-management/creating-vendor-questionnaire-template-1/what-are-vendor-questionnaire-templates).

### Customizing Columns in the Vendor Table

Follow these steps to customize the visible columns in the Vendor Table :

- Go to the Vendor Management Module.
- Click on the column selector in the Vendor Table.
- From the list of available columns, select or deselect the columns you want to display.
