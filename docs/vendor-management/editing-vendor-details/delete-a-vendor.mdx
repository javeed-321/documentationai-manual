---
title: "Delete a Vendor"
description: "Learn how to delete vendors in Scrut."
---

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select the vendor(s) you want to remove from Scrut.

3. Click **More Actions** and select **Delete Vendors**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-20-at-12.42.23.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Delete** to confirm your action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-21.23.52.png" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="danger">
     **Important:**

     - Deleting a vendor will permanently remove all vendor documents, questionnaires, and mitigation tasks. So proceed with caution.
     - Deleting a vendor will also delete the linked sub-processors.
   </Callout>
