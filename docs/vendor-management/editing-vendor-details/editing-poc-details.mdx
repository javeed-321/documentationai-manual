Managing vendor relationships involves keeping accurate and up-to-date Point of Contact (POC) information. When a vendor's POC changes or additional POCs need to be added, it's crucial to reflect these changes within the Scrut platform for effective communication.

The system now allows you to add multiple POCs, providing greater flexibility and improved coordination. Here's a step-by-step guide on editing and managing POC details:

### 1. Accessing POC Details:

- Each vendor designates a Point of Contact (POC) for communication and risk assessment purposes.
- In cases of POC changes, it's essential to update this information within the scrut platform.

### 2. Editing POC Details:

- To modify POC details, start by opening the specific Vendor Details page within the scrut platform.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162612540-ydsp46fsb6j-image-2592H253.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### 3. Navigating to the POC Details:

- Once on the Vendor page, go to the _Point of Contact_ section.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162734594-4lmaezn52y7-image-93A1M1R9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### 4. Initiating the Edit Process:

- To edit the POC details, click the **Edit** icon.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162692967-qlusvgj7e3-image-6PKOC0E4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### 5. Editing POC Information:

- Upon clicking the **Edit** icon, a pop-up window named _Edit POC_ will appear.

- Within this window, you can make changes to the POC's name and email address as necessary.

- After making the desired updates, click the **Update** button to confirm the changes.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163066943-xcsx01ph06-image-QF06XTYG.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Warning**

  Editing vendor POC email to another domain is restricted.
</Callout>
