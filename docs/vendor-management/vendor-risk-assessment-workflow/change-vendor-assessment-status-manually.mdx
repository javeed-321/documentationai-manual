---
title: "Change Vendor Assessment Status Manually"
description: "Learn how to manually change the vendor assessment status in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Important:**

  Scrut recommends following the [vendor risk assessment workflow](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-risk-assessment-workflow/steps-to-complete-vendor-risk-assessment) to capture assessment statuses. This ensures vendor responses, risk scores, and evaluation history are captured in the platform.
</Callout>

We recommend **manually setting the assessment status only when you have assessed a vendor outside of Scrut** and need to reflect that in the vendor module.

## How To Change Vendor Assessment Status Manually

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For a Single Vendor">
    1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors → Vendors (Tab)** and open a specific vendor.

    2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Change Assessment Status**.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162540042-c3thzc61pw9-More-Actions---Individual-Vendor.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

    3. Select the **Assessment Status**: Not Assessed, In Progress, or Assessed.

    4. Enter the **reason** for the status change and include any other relevant details.

    5. Click **Save**.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164715976-2h59wik3404-Screenshot-2025-11-19-at-22-17-41.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="For Multiple Vendors">
    1. Select the vendor(s) whose status you want to update.

    2. Click the **Edit** button and select **Assessment Status**.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162436460-6jttenm5uot-image-1775047536491.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

    3. Select the **Assessment Status**: Not Assessed, In Progress, or Assessed.

    4. Enter the **reason** for the status change and include any other relevant details.

    5. Click **Save**.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162437781-1spc9onqaz6-image-1775047556730.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  As you’re changing the assessment status manually, you’ll notice a cautionary warning. You can ignore this and proceed if you’ve manually assessed the vendor outside Scrut.
</Callout>
