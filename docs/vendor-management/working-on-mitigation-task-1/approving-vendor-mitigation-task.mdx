---
title: "Approve a Vendor Mitigation Task"
description: "Learn how to approve a vendor mitigation task in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

You can approve a mitigation task in two ways:

- Method #1: [Via the Vendor Overview Page](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/approving-vendor-mitigation-task#method-1-via-the-vendor-overview-page)
- Method #2: [Via the Mitigation Task Tab](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/approving-vendor-mitigation-task#method-2-via-the-mitigation-task-tab)

## Method #1: Via the Vendor Overview Page

1. From the left navigation panel, expand *Risk* menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-NKXVJRKO.png" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to approve the mitigation task for.

   <Image src="/images/image-SR2O0M7C.png" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-5YJ5X9PB.png" alt="" />

5. Click on the mitigation task you want to approve.

   <Image src="/images/image-7X2SD9IW.png" alt="" />

6. Click on Actions in the top right corner, then click **Approve**

   <Image src="/images/image-7YDSHUNF.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info">
  **Note**: Task status changes to **Closed**.
</Callout>

<Image src="/images/image-LTTDCAMX.png" alt="" />

## Method #2: Via the Mitigation Task Tab

1. From the left navigation panel, expand *Risk* menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="/images/image-L5Z0JRTN.png" alt="" />

3. Select the mitigation task for which you wish to approve. Optionally, you can use the search bar to search for the task.

   <Image src="/images/image-KRWK71LA.png" alt="" />

4. Click on Actions in the top right corner, then click **Approve**.

<Image src="/images/image-7YDSHUNF.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info">
  **Note**: Task status changes to **Closed**.
</Callout>

<Image src="/images/image-WHW07C9Q.png" alt="" />
