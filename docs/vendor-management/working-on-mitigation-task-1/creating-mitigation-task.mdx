<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

In the context of vendor risk management, a mitigation task is a specific action or set of actions that an organization takes to address and reduce the risks associated with a particular vendor. Mitigation tasks are a key component of an organization's overall risk management strategy when working with vendors and suppliers.

Mitigation Tasks can be created in two ways.

- Method #1: [From the Vendor Overview Page](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/creating-mitigation-task#method-1-from-the-vendor-overview-page)
- Method #2: [From Submitted Questionnaires](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/creating-mitigation-task#method-2-from-submitted-questionnaires)

## Method #1: From the Vendor Overview Page

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-NKXVJRKO.png" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to create mitigation task.

   <Image src="/images/image-SR2O0M7C.png" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-5YJ5X9PB.png" alt="" />

5. Click **+ Start Creating A New Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="/images/image-2B8HNQ35.png" alt="" />

Create Mitigation Task window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-KT23RXNZ.png" alt="" />

6. In the _Task Name_ field, provide a descriptive name for the mitigation task.
7. Optionally, in _Description_, enter description of the mitigation task.
8. From the _Assignee_ drop-down menu, select a user who would work on the mitigation task.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Note**: Assignee will be notified via email about the assigned task.
</Callout>

9. select the due date for the task in the **Due Date** field.
10. Click **Create Mitigation Task**.

## Method #2: From Submitted Questionnaires

If there is a risk identified based on vendor's submitted answer, click **Add Mitigation Task**.

<Image src="/images/image-0IUPACAW.png" alt="" />

Create Mitigation Task window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-IGZ19JOQ.png" alt="" />

1. In _Task Name_ field, enter the title of the mitigation task.
2. Optionally, in _Description_ field, enter description of the task.
3. From the _Assignee_ drop-down menu, assign the task either directly to the vendor, or to someone within the internal team..
4. Select the due date for the task in the Due Date field.
5. Click **Create Mitigation Task**.

Once created you can view the Mitigation Task within the Question.

6. Click on the mitigation task created.

<Image src="/images/image-MD31HWN0.png" alt="" />

Linked Mitigation Task window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-ALS3E9TQ.png" alt="" />

7. Click on the Mitigation Task name to view the linked task.

<Image src="/images/image-6SLKYD70.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info">
  **Note**: After creating mitigation task, click **Accept** or **Flag as Issue** to finish reviewing the question.
</Callout>

8. Audit logs- Provides clear and accurate logging of vendor-level activities, minimizing confusion and enhancing usability.

<Image src="/images/image-CJJCWG3S.png" alt="" />
