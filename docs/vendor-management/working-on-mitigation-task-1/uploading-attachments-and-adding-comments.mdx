---
title: "Upload a Mitigation Task Document"
description: "Learn how to upload a vendor mitigation task document in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

You can add documents to a mitigation task in two ways:

- Method #1: [Via the Vendor Overview Page](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/uploading-attachments-and-adding-comments#method-1-via-the-vendor-overview-page)
- Method #2: [Via the Mitigation Task Tab](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/uploading-attachments-and-adding-comments#method-2-via-the-mitigation-task-tab)

## Method 1: Via the Vendor Overview Page

1. From the left navigation panel, expand the _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163019022-d8wfiym06t7-image-NKXVJRKO.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to upload a document for the mitigation task.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163116819-6woyltjvtei-image-SR2O0M7C.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162685158-ekwwxvxf5tg-image-5YJ5X9PB.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Open the mitigation task you want to upload a document to.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162712053-0mjwysc69ztc-image-7X2SD9IW.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Click **Upload files**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162965314-znget3fpocn-image-KGCE2H1A.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The attachment window opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162760902-lxtg3wykxkr-image-BF305KSD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Choose one of the following options:

   - File: Choose this option if you wish to upload a file by either dragging and dropping or selecting from your system.
   - Link: Choose this option to link an external file by providing its link.

8. Optionally, in the _Notes_ field, enter notes related to the document.

9. Click **Submit**

## Method 2: Via the Mitigation Task Tab

1. From the left navigation panel, expand the _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162978306-r7qia2gr99-image-L5Z0JRTN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Select the mitigation task for which you wish to upload the document. Optionally, you can use the search bar to search for the task.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162969585-oyu52sq3b9-image-KRWK71LA.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Upload files**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163075526-sjseh23eube-image-QNWDMZTE.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Add attachment window opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162760902-lxtg3wykxkr-image-BF305KSD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Choose one of the following options:

   - File: Choose this option if you wish to upload a file by either drag and drop or selecting from your system.
   - Link: Choose this option if you wish to link an external file by prvoding the file link.

6. Optionally, in _Notes_ field, enter notes related to the document.

7. Click **Submit**

## Adding Comments

- To add comments within the mitigation task, simply enter your comments in the provided text field and press _Enter_ on your keyboard.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163106877-lqqjbbv1fjf-image-S3Y8MFDZ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- The added comments will appear under the _Comments section_ for reference.
