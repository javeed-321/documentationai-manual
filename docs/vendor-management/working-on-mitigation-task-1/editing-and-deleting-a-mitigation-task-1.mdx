---
title: "Edit or Delete Vendor Mitigation Tasks"
description: "Learn how to edit and delete vendor mitigation tasks in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Mitigation Tasks can be edited and deleted in two ways:

- Method #1: [Via the Vendor Overview Page](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/editing-and-deleting-a-mitigation-task-1#method-1-via-the-vendor-overview-page)
- Method #2: [Via the Mitigation Task Tab](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/editing-and-deleting-a-mitigation-task-1#method-2-via-the-mitigation-task-tab)

## Method 1: Via the Vendor Overview Page

### Editing a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163019022-d8wfiym06t7-image-NKXVJRKO.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to edit the mitigation task for.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163116819-6woyltjvtei-image-SR2O0M7C.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162685158-ekwwxvxf5tg-image-5YJ5X9PB.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Open the mitigation task you want to edit.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162712053-0mjwysc69ztc-image-7X2SD9IW.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Click on the pencil icons beside the name and description to edit the task.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163132359-0u4va701lfx-image-TJ60U5Q9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

**Audit logs:** Audit logs within the mitigation task section capture the audit for mitigation task creation, changes, and document updates.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162986533-tqm5ik6sgn-image-LRV4EYE4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Deleting a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163019022-d8wfiym06t7-image-NKXVJRKO.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to delete the mitigation task for.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163116819-6woyltjvtei-image-SR2O0M7C.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162685158-ekwwxvxf5tg-image-5YJ5X9PB.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click on the delete icon situated on the right corner of the mitigation task under Actions.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162111719-2rpg6vcg4a2-image-05NLGU7K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Delete confirmation window opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162656358-f5fg38uk7j5-image-4H36I5ET.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Click **Yes, Delete**.

## Method 2: Via the Mitigation Task Tab

### Editing a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162978306-r7qia2gr99-image-L5Z0JRTN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Under Actions click on the pencil icon.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162605891-4iry1mj4dex-image-1PUP9S4D.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Edit Mitigation Task window opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162828228-458c11r7ir3-image-G933PJMD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Make the necessary changes and click **Submit**.

### Deleting a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162978306-r7qia2gr99-image-L5Z0JRTN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Under Actions click on the delete icon.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162680019-6hdh75erzmn-image-5PLHV1UD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Delete mitigation task window opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163074070-5v86bc6m22w-image-QMRU81YR.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click **Yes, Delete**.
