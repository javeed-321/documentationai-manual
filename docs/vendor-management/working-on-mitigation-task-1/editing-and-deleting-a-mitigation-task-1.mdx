---
title: "Edit or Delete Vendor Mitigation Tasks"
description: "Learn how to edit and delete vendor mitigation tasks in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Mitigation Tasks can be edited and deleted in two ways:

- Method #1: [Via the Vendor Overview Page](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/editing-and-deleting-a-mitigation-task-1#method-1-via-the-vendor-overview-page)
- Method #2: [Via the Mitigation Task Tab](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-mitigation-task-1/editing-and-deleting-a-mitigation-task-1#method-2-via-the-mitigation-task-tab)

## Method #1: Via the Vendor Overview Page

### Editing a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-NKXVJRKO.png" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to edit the mitigation task for.

   <Image src="/images/image-SR2O0M7C.png" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-5YJ5X9PB.png" alt="" />

5. Open the mitigation task you want to edit.

   <Image src="/images/image-7X2SD9IW.png" alt="" />

6. Click on the pencil icons beside the name and description to edit the task.

<Image src="/images/image-TJ60U5Q9.png" alt="" />

**Audit logs:** Audit logs within the mitigation task section capture the audit for mitigation task creation, changes, and document updates.

<Image src="/images/image-LRV4EYE4.png" alt="" />

### Deleting a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Vendors** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-NKXVJRKO.png" alt="" />

3. Click on the vendor you want to delete the mitigation task for.

   <Image src="/images/image-SR2O0M7C.png" alt="" />

4. Click on the **Mitigation Tasks** tab.

   <Image src="/images/image-5YJ5X9PB.png" alt="" />

5. Click on the delete icon situated on the right corner of the mitigation task under Actions.

<Image src="/images/image-05NLGU7K.png" alt="" />

6. Delete confirmation window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-4H36I5ET.png" alt="" />

7. Click **Yes, Delete**.

## Method #2: Via the Mitigation Task Tab

### Editing a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="/images/image-L5Z0JRTN.png" alt="" />

3. Under Actions click on the pencil icon.

   <Image src="/images/image-1PUP9S4D.png" alt="" />

4. Edit Mitigation Task window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-G933PJMD.png" alt="" />

5. Make the necessary changes and click **Submit**.

### Deleting a Mitigation Task

1. From the left navigation panel, expand _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="/images/image-7VUAOC2E.png" alt="" />

2. Click **Mitigation Task**.

   <Image src="/images/image-L5Z0JRTN.png" alt="" />

3. Under Actions click on the delete icon.

   <Image src="/images/image-5PLHV1UD.png" alt="" />

4. Delete mitigation task window opens.

<Image src="/images/image-QMRU81YR.png" alt="" />

5. Click **Yes, Delete**.
