title: "Quick Start Guide: Vendor Management"
description: "End-to-end workflow guide for third-party vendor management in Scrut."
---

Scrut's Vendor Management module helps you track, assess, and manage your third-party vendors through their full lifecycle, from initial evaluation to offboarding. This guide walks you through the end-to-end workflow.

## Step 1: Add Vendors

By default, Scrut [automatically adds vendors when you connect to any integration](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/added-via-integrations). Additionally, you can also add vendors using any of the following methods:

- Add vendors through the [**Onboarding → Discovery tab**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/discover-vendors-through-sso-integration), where Scrut surfaces potential vendors from your connected SSO integrations.
- [**Share the Intake Form**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/vendor-onboarding-intake-form/how-to-onboard-vendors-using-an-intake-form) with employees so they can submit vendor requests directly.
- [**Manually create a vendor**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/adding-a-single-vendor#add-a-single-vendor) and fill in the relevant details.
- [**Import vendors**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/adding-a-single-vendor#bulk-import-multiple-vendors) in bulk using the Scrut-provided CSV template.

When adding a vendor, select one of the following [vendor lifecycle stages](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/how-to-change-vendor-status):

- **Prospective:** The vendor is under evaluation and has not yet been approved for active use.
- **Active:** You are currently using services from this vendor.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Use the Prospective status to run your full due diligence workflow before formally onboarding a vendor as Active.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Collect Documents

Gather the documentation you need to assess the vendor's security and compliance posture.

1. Use Scrut Teammates to [auto-fetch the vendor’s publicly available documents](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-vendor-document/auto-fetch-documents-via-scrut-teammates), such as the Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ), Trust Center and security and privacy pages.
2. [Upload documents manually](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-vendor-document/adding-a-document) to the Documents tab on the vendor profile.
3. If the vendor has a Scrut-hosted Trust Portal, [fetch their documents directly from the vendor’s Trust Portal](/docs/vendor-management/working-on-vendor-document/adding-a-document#fetching-documents-from-a-vendors-trust-portal) to the Documents tab.

## Step 3: Create a Vendor Questionnaire

Send the vendor a questionnaire to collect information relevant to your assessment. You have two ways to do this:

- **Leverage AI:** Use Scrut Teammates to generate a custom questionnaire based on the vendor's risk level and other attributes.
- **Manually:** Create a [questionnaire template](/docs/vendor-management/creating-vendor-questionnaire-template-1/what-are-vendor-questionnaire-templates) and then create a vendor questionnaire from the template.

## Step 4: Assess the Vendor

Review all collected material to evaluate the vendor's risk.

1. Review the vendor's responses to the questionnaire.
2. Review the documents collected from the vendor.
3. Use Scrut Teammates to identify risks across documents and questionnaire responses.
4. Create risks directly from the assessment to log any gaps or concerns uncovered during your review.
5. Create mitigation tasks to assign follow-up actions and track remediation for each identified risk.

## Step 5: Update the Vendor Status

After completing your assessment, update the vendor's status to reflect your decision.

- If the vendor was added as Prospective and has been approved, mark them as Active.
- If the vendor does not meet your requirements and you no longer want to evaluate or engage them, mark them as Archived.

## Step 6: Run Reassessments

Repeat Steps 2 through 5 when the vendor is due for their periodic or annual reassessment.

## Step 7: Offboard a Vendor

When you are no longer working with a vendor, archive them to remove them from your active vendor list.

1. Navigate to the vendor profile.
2. Click Archive and provide a justification for offboarding.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  You can [unarchive a vendor](/docs/vendor-management/change-vendor-status/archive-a-vendor#how-to-unarchive-a-vendor) at any time if you need to reactivate or re-evaluate them.
</Callout>
