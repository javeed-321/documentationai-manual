You can download any document from a vendor's profile to your local system to share with stakeholders as needed.

## How to Download a Vendor Document

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors**.
2. Click the vendor whose document you want to download.
3. Click the **Documents** tab.
4. Locate the document you want to download.
5. Click the download icon in the Actions column.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-20.45.05.png" alt="" />

The file downloads to your system.
