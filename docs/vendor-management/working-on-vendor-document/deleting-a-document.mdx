<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

You can permanently delete documents from a vendor's profile when they are no longer needed for your assessment.

## How to Delete a Vendor Document

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors**.
2. Click the vendor whose document you want to delete.
3. Click the **Documents** tab.
4. Locate the document you want to delete.
5. Click the delete icon in the Actions column.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-20.40.24.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important:**

  Deleting a document is permanent. It cannot be undone.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Documents with the source type "Questionnaire" cannot be deleted. These are retained for record-keeping and compliance purposes.
</Callout>
