---
title: "Export Vendor Documents"
description: "Learn how to export vendor documents in Scrut."
---

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select the vendor(s) for whom you want to export their associated documents.

3. Click the **Export Documents** button.

   <Image src="/images/image-1763628121517.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Yes, Export** to continue.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-21.21.55.png" alt="" />

Once the export is ready, you can download it from the notification center.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-20.47.40.png" alt="" />
