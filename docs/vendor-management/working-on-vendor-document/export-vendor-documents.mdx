---
title: "Export Vendor Documents"
description: "Learn how to export vendor documents in Scrut."
---

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors** and go to the **Vendors** tab.

2. Select the vendor(s) for whom you want to export their associated documents.

3. Click the **Export Documents** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162238715-iu7ggfa0zi-image-1763628121517.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Yes, Export** to continue.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163783015-hvq0na0skx6-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-21-21-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Once the export is ready, you can download it from the notification center.
