---
title: "Discover Vendors via Azure AD"
description: "Connect Azure AD to Scrut and automatically discover SSO applications as vendors."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Follow these steps to connect Azure AD to Scrut and automatically discover SSO applications as vendors.

## Step 1: Integrate Azure AD

Navigate to **Risks → Vendors → Onboarding** and click **Integrate Now**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162358626-zkx8f7tu8h8-image-1771484965842.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Alternatively, click the **Integrations** tab on the left navigation panel, search for **Azure AD** and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165189081-autbnqs9b9p-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-40-32.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Need help setting up the integration? Refer to [Integrating Microsoft Entra (Azure AD)](/docs/integrations/integrating-cloud-providers/integrating-azure) for step-by-step instructions.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Review Discovered Applications

Once connected, Scrut automatically imports the applications authorized in your Azure AD account. These appear on the **Discovered from Integration** page in **Risk → Vendors → Onboarding**, with the **Source** set to Microsoft Entra (Azure AD).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165190407-bfj5mlj7gsa-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-49-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Review the imported applications and classify each one:

- Click **Mark as Vendor** to add it to your official vendor list.
- Click **Not a Vendor** to exclude it. It stays on this page for reference, but won't appear in your Vendor table.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  📖 For detailed instructions on marking and excluding vendors, see [Discover Vendors through SSO Integration](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/discover-vendors-through-sso-integration).
</Callout>
