<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Managing third-party vendors starts with knowing what's already in your environment. By integrating with your organization's SSO provider — Google Workspace or Azure AD — Scrut automatically fetches all the apps in use across your organization.

Instead of relying on team members to self-report the tools they use, Scrut gives you a real-time, integration-driven view of your vendor landscape — directly from the source of truth. This is particularly useful for surfacing shadow IT or tools that weren't formally procured.

#### Why does this matter?

For frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, and others, maintaining an accurate, up-to-date vendor inventory is a core requirement. Gaps in your vendor list — whether due to shadow IT or simple oversight — can create compliance risks. By connecting Scrut to SSO tools, you ensure that newly authorized applications are automatically synced, keeping your vendor list up to date with minimal manual effort.

## Supported Integrations

Scrut currently supports vendor discovery through:

- [**Google Workspace**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/google-workspace-integration-to-discover-vendors): Discovers apps authorized via your Google Workspace account
- [**Azure AD**](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/azure-ad-integration-to-discover-vendors): Discovers apps connected through your Azure Active Directory tenant

## How It Works

### Step 1: Integrate with Google Workspace or Azure AD

Once you integrate Scrut with [Google Workspace](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/google-workspace-integration-to-discover-vendors) or [Azure AD](/docs/vendor-management/managing-your-vendors/discover-vendors-through-integrations/azure-ad-integration-to-discover-vendors), Scrut automatically pulls in the third-party applications that are active in your account. These appear on the **Risks → Vendors → Onboarding → Discovered from Integration** page, where you can review and act on each one.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  The Source column specifies from where the vendor originates: Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra (Azure AD).
</Callout>

### Step 2: Mark as Vendor

1. Review the discovered applications and click **Mark as Vendor** for the applications you want to officially track.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162533409-qae6lexphdo-Mark-as-a-Vendor---Discovered-from-Integration.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Enter the vendor details, including Status, URL, Category, Service Description, Assignee, and Entities. Include the vendor POC details (optional).

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165180610-jig76ym3o3-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-00-09.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Review the details and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165181957-yibqts6lg6p-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-00-31.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. The vendor is now included in the official vendor list in the **Vendor** table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165183281-ptsl295uhji-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-01-10.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  For vendors discovered via Google Workspace or Azure AD SSO integrations, the Source in the vendor table will be: SSO Discovery.
</Callout>

### Step 3: Exclude an Application

1. If an application isn't a vendor you want to track, click **Not a Vendor.**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165184702-h0zrod8c6j-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-14-41.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Provide a brief justification for your action.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165186269-syc3exg2d6-Screenshot-2026-02-19-at-12-16-40.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. The application stays on the Discovered from Integration page for reference, but won't appear in your Vendor table.

4. To reverse this decision, click **Add as Vendor** to move a previously ignored application to the Vendor table.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Any actions you take on the Discovered from Integrations page are automatically captured in the corresponding Audit Logs.
</Callout>
