<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Included in the **Scale** plan
  - Available in the **Vendor Advanced Add-on** for **Foundation** and **Growth** plans
</Callout>

When an employee submits a vendor procurement request, you review the responses in the intake form and decide whether to approve or decline the vendor. Approved vendors are added directly to your Vendor list in the Risk → Vendor Management module.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Admins receive an email notification whenever an employee submits a new vendor procurement request via the intake form.
</Callout>

## How to Review Vendor Procurement Requests

1. Navigate to **Risk → Vendors** using the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Onboarding** tab.

3. Select the **Added by Intake Form** tab to view requests submitted by employees.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161867635-g2n9pnkfsff-Added-by-Intake-Form.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click a vendor request to open it.

5. Optionally, assign a reviewer from your team using the **Reviewer** dropdown. Use this if you want someone else to assess the request rather than reviewing it yourself.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165420707-mmao2zbrkp-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-01-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   **Note:** The assigned reviewer receives an email notification to begin their review.

6. Read through the responses from the employee.

7. Once done, select **Approve** or **Decline**.

### Approving a Request

If the vendor meets your requirements:

1. Select **Approve**.

2. Select the **vendor's status**:

   - **Prospective:** The vendor has not yet been engaged. Choose this if you're approving them for potential future use.
   - **Active:** The vendor is currently engaged and providing services. Choose this if onboarding is already underway.

3. Optionally, enter any remarks in the **Remarks** field.

4. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165422132-erviq1patrr-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-24-15.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The vendor is added to the Vendor list, and the onboarding status changes from **Review Pending** to **Approved**. The employee who submitted the request receives an email notification confirming the decision.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165423593-jn3x1wp2ibk-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-24-28.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The vendor is added to the Vendor table with the Source type as **Intake Form**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165428024-yh3gadnqfrc-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-29-11.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Source Type: **Intake Form**" />

### Declining a Request

If the vendor does not meet your requirements:

1. Select **Decline**.

2. Enter the reason for declining in the Remarks field.

3. Click **Decline**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165425094-ew7kwic7ckm-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-27-32.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

The onboarding status changes to **Declined,** and the employee who submitted the request receives an email notification with your remarks.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165426552-64cgfyz3o79-Screenshot-2026-03-26-at-14-27-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Tracking Activity with Audit Logs

The Audit Logs tab on each vendor request records every action taken during the review process, including submissions, assignments, approvals, and declines.
