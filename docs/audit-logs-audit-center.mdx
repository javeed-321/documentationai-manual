The Audit Logs in the Audit Center provide a complete picture of all activity related to an audit, including actions taken across the audit, its findings, requests, and corrective actions.

## How to View Audit Logs for an Audit

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Open the audit you want to review.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161944221-0nzl3zfnfg4-Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click **Audit Logs**. The log displays all activity associated with the audit, sorted chronologically.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165663653-sr7k6cjd4l-Screenshot-2026-05-12-at-17-20-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Each Audit Finding, Request, and Corrective Action has its own audit logs, which shows activity scoped to that item only.
</Callout>
