## How Policies Work in Scrut

1. Scrut gives you three ways to add a policy to your workspace:

   - [Draft a policy](/docs/uploading-policy#1-create-a-blank-policy) using one of Scrut's built-in templates, created and maintained by security and compliance experts, or start from scratch using Scrut’s policy editor.
   - [Upload an existing policy document](/docs/uploading-policy#2-upload-policy-document) if your organization already has approved policies in place in Google Drive, Confluence, or SharePoint.
   - [Link to a policy](/docs/uploading-policy#3-link-an-existing-policy) hosted externally.

2. Once a policy is added, it goes through a review and [approval workflow](/docs/understanding-policy-approval-workflow). You can add up to five approvers for a policy. When all approvers sign off, the policy is published.

3. Published policies become part of your compliance evidence that is shared with auditors. You can also add policies to employee onboarding or periodic checklists so that relevant employees can confirm they have read and understood them.
