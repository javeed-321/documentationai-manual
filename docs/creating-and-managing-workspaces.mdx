In this article, we explain how to create and manage workspaces in the partner console.

## How To Create a New Workspace

To onboard a new customer:

1. Click **Add Workspace.**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162586115-wwre16aqebk-Partner-Portal.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Partner Portal" caption=" " />

2. Fill in the required details:

   - **Workspace Name:** Enter a name for the workspace. This is how the workspace will appear in your Partner Portal.

   - **Legal Name:** Enter the customer's organization's legal name.

     **Note:** Both the Workspace Name and Legal Name fields accept letters, numbers, spaces, and the following special characters: . (dot), , (comma), - (hyphen), _ (underscore), & (ampersand), ' (apostrophe), ( ) (parentheses), and / (forward slash). For example: Acme LLP (Canada), ABC Pvt. Ltd., O'Reilly, or Johnson & Johnson.

   - **Region:** Select the region where the customer's data will be hosted.

   - **Framework:** Select one or more compliance frameworks for this workspace. This is optional and can be updated later.

   - **Pricing Plan:** Select the pricing plan for this workspace. The available plans are Foundation, Growth, and Scale.

   - **Add-ons:** Select the add-ons to enable for this workspace. Turn on the toggle next to each add-on you want to include.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165691518-ukqjv5snksm-Screenshot-2026-05-18-at-18-27-49.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

     <Callout kind="alert">
       **Note:**

       - You are automatically added as an Admin in any workspace you create.
       - The Pricing Plan and Framework options available for a workspace are configured by your Scrut admin. For more options, contact your CSM. Alternatively, click **Request Plan** to upgrade.
     </Callout>

3. Click **Submit** and wait a couple of minutes for the workspace to be created.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162586115-wwre16aqebk-Partner-Portal.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Partner Portal" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Each partner account has a limit on the number of workspaces that can be created. If you need to increase this limit to onboard more customers, click **Contact CSM** to reach out to your Scrut Customer Success Manager.
</Callout>

## Managing Workspace Details

Once a workspace is created, click on it to view the workspace detail page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165695672-d3ket3da6w5-Screenshot-2026-05-18-at-21-28-32.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

From here, you can:

- [Add Frameworks](/docs/add-frameworks)
- [View Tests](/docs/creating-and-managing-workspaces#view-tests)

### Add Frameworks

1. Click the **Add Framework** button and select the frameworks to add to the workspace.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161832127-idgh2w8vngk-Add-Framework.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click **Request more frameworks** to contact your CSM to add additional frameworks not displayed here.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165694337-dutkqrp7np8-Screenshot-2026-05-18-at-21-27-09.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### View Tests

The workspace detail page also displays tests added to the customer account.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Tests are visible here once AWS, Azure, or GCP is integrated in the workspace (customer account).
</Callout>

## Deactivating & Deleting Workspaces

### Deactivate a Workspace

1. Log in to the Workspace Admin console and click the workspace you want to deactivate.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166123164-fiuygbmvwln-Three-dot-options-in-workspace.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Deactivate Workspace**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165093835-i42eopbebjh-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-20-55-34.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Deactivating the workspace will temporarily remove access for all users. You can restore it anytime by reactivating it.
</Callout>

### Delete a Workspace

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up!**

  Deleting a workspace is permanent and removes all data associated with the account. So, proceed with caution.
</Callout>

1. Log in to the Workspace Admin console and click the workspace you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166123164-fiuygbmvwln-Three-dot-options-in-workspace.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Delete Workspace**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165092502-iumox4gswjm-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-20-55-22.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
