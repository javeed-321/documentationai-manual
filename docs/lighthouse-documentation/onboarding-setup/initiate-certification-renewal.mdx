---
title: "Initiate Certification Renewal"
---

Two months before your certification expires, Scrut will send you an email reminder about the renewal process. To request a renewal, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to Scrut and navigate to the **Task Center**.
2. Click the **Request Renewal** button. This action will send an email to your CSM team, who will handle your request.
3. After your request is processed, the framework cycle for your certification will be reset.
4. The Audit Center will then return to the Audit Prep stage for the current renewal cycle.

<Callout kind="success">
  **Note:**

  Even though you are starting a new framework renewal cycle, all your previous certificates will still be available in Scrut. Additionally, all your policies and evidence attachments will be preserved for your records, ensuring you have everything you need at your fingertips.
</Callout>
