Congrats on signing up for Scrut! We’re excited to have you on board. **Scrut is a web-based platform that runs in your browser; you don’t need to install any apps or software.**

## For First-Time Users

You would have received an email from Scrut with your unique access link. Click the **Login** button in the email and follow the on-screen instructions.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162059246-8ybc7j76q8b-First-time-user-login-email.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="First time user login email" />

<Callout kind="success">
  **Heads Up!**

  If it’s the first time someone from your organization is logging into Scrut, you'll be directed to [set up your organizational profile and customize your experience](/docs/lighthouse-documentation/onboarding-setup/onboarding-tasks).
</Callout>

## For Returning Users

1. Open the Scrut login page: [**https://app.scrut.io/**](https://app.scrut.io/)

2. Enter your registered email (usually your work email) and click **Continue**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162520639-v2hkq31mssh-Login-Screen---Enter-Email.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Login Screen - Enter Email" />

3. Log in using your preferred SSO option (if enabled for your organization) or click **Get Verification Code** to request a one-time code sent to your email.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162521961-5u6rlhvqjm9-Login-Screen---SSO-and-OTP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Login Screen - SSO and OTP" />

That’s it! You can now access Scrut and get started on your compliance journey.
