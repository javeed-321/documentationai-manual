---
title: "Download Framework Certificates"
---

Congrats! You’ve completed your external audit and achieved your framework certification. In this article, we explain how to view and download your certificates in Scrut.

- Method #1: Via the Task Center
- Method #2: Via the Frameworks module

## Method 1: Via the Task Center

Once your external audit is finalized, the Scrut Team will upload the framework certification to the platform. To download it:

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Task Center** on the left navigation panel.
2. Select your preferred framework and entity using the selectors at the top of the tab.
3. Click the **Download Certificate** button to download the certificate for your chosen framework and entity.

## Method 2: Via the Frameworks Module

1. Sign in to Scrut and navigate to **Frameworks** → **Framework Certificates**.
2. On this page, you can view all frameworks that you’ve previously achieved via Scrut.
3. Click **Download Certificate** to download a specific certificate.
