The **Risk Dashboard** in the Risk Module features intuitive graphs and metrics cards that provide a visual representation of your risks. It also includes a **Risk Heat Map** and **Risk Trends graph** that provide a clear overview of your risk landscape. Learn how to use the Risk Dashboard to understand your risks better.

## How to Access the Risk Dashboard

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Risk → Risk Management** on the left side panel.
2. This brings you to the **Risk Dashboard**, where you can view all the relevant risk metrics and graphs.

## Dashboard Filters

The dashboard provides four primary filters to refine the data according to your requirements. They are :

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164095867-3s2pg2kyxtr-Screenshot-2025-08-21-at-18-03-49.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

- **Assignee:** The Assignee dropdown provides a complete list of Risk assignees, with priority given to "No Assignee" and an option to filter Risks assigned to you.

- **Department:** Users can easily navigate through all available departments and filter relevant ones with this dropdown.

- **Category:** The Category dropdown provides a list of categories to which each risk belongs.

- **Entities:** This dropdown contains a comprehensive list of entities that the user has configured in the Entities section.

  - **Note**: The [Risk Trend Graph](/docs/risk-trend-graph) and Mitigation Tasks chart do not support filtering by entities.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Click the circular **undo** icon to reset the applied filters.
</Callout>

## Risk Heat Map

The **Risk Heat Map** offers a visual representation of risks, enabling users to quickly assess and prioritize them based on severity and potential impact. This helps decision-makers identify areas where additional resources may be needed.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162914004-el5fr3791wb-image-HOZJ6EA4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

You can generate Risk Heat Maps using any Inherent and Residual Risk formula of your choice. Customize the X and Y-axes assignments to fit your preferences. Additionally, the Risk Heat Map includes a Treatment Strategy filter, enabling you to drill down based on treatment strategies.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162830834-gssze52i5t8-image-GKR2MYRY.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## Risk Trend Graph

<Callout kind="custom">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Growth** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

- Tracks the number of risks across all statuses over time.
- Filter by the last 7, 30, or 90 days, or a custom range.
- View data in daily, weekly, or monthly aggregation.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important**

  The Risk Trend graph will continue to display organization-wide data and is not affected by the entity filter at the top of the dashboard.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="success">
  **Note:**

  Check [this guide](/docs/risk-trend-graph) for a detailed explanation of the data shown in the Risk Trend graph and how it works.
</Callout>
