title: "Vault: Walkthrough"
metaDescription: "Overview of the Vault module in Scrut and how to use it to organize your compliance files."
---

The **Vault** serves as a central repository for uploading and maintaining all your essential documents within the Scrut platform. Think of it as your own secure **Scrut drive** for organizing and storing all compliance and audit documentation.

Additionally (depending on your configuration), Scrut Teammates can also access and refer to these documents while [auto-filling security questionnaires](/docs/ai-powered-questionnaire-autofill-walkthrough) and [answering compliance queries](/docs/ask-anything-to-scrut-teammates).
