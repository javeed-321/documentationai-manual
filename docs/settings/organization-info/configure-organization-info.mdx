The **Organization Info** tab in **Settings** allows you to configure essential details about your business. These details ensure Scrut accurately tailors policies and organizes your evidence.

## How to Configure Organization Info

Follow these steps to set up your organization's profile:

1. Navigate to the **Settings** page and click the **Organization Info** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166078739-lgetvt8pe9-Settings-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Upload your company **Logo**.

3. Enter your **Organization Name**, the public-facing name your organization uses.

4. Enter your organization's legal name. This is the official name of your business, as registered with government authorities.

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     Both the Organization Name and Legal Name fields accept letters, numbers, spaces, and the following special characters: . (dot), , (comma), - (hyphen), _ (underscore), & (ampersand), ' (apostrophe), ( ) (parentheses), and / (forward slash). For example: ABC Pvt. Ltd., O'Reilly, or Acme (Asia/Pacific).
   </Callout>

5. Enter your website address in the **Company URL** field.

6. Select your company's **Work Arrangement**: **Remote**, **Hybrid**, or **Office**. This is essential because, depending on the work arrangement, some policies might not be relevant and are automatically marked as such.

7. Paste your entire office address in the **Address** field.

8. Choose your **Client Industry or vertical** from the dropdown menu. If your industry is not listed, select **Other** and enter it manually.

9. Select your **Geographical Scope**. This is a multi-select option that lets you choose multiple countries if your business operates across multiple regions.

10. Select your [fiscal year format](/docs/settings/organization-info/set-fiscal-year-format). You can choose between **Calendar Year (Jan - Dec)** or **Financial Year (April - March)**. This setting is crucial because Scrut uses it to organize your evidence buckets into the corresponding date buckets.

11. Ensure all mandatory fields are filled out.

12. Click **Save Changes**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161784714-pquih0dypia-1772695529637-8312990e.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Your changes take effect immediately.
