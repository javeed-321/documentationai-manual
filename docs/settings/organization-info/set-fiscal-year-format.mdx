---
title: "Set Fiscal Year Format"
description: "Set the fiscal year for your organization."
---

Attachments added to Evidence Tasks will be categorized in one of the following formats:

- January to December
- April to March

To set the fiscal year for your organization:

1. Click **Settings** in the left navigation panel, then go to **Organization Info**.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-Page.png" alt="" />

2. Scroll to the bottom of the page and choose your preferred format.

   <Image src="/images/Fiscal-year-format.png" alt="" />
