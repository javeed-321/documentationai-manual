---
title: "Edit Users"
description: "Learn how to modify the details of existing users in your Scrut account."
---

1. [Sign in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Settings** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click **Manage Users**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166078739-lgetvt8pe9-Settings-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click the **edit icon** in the **Actions** column for a specific user.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" width="46" height="46" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162388011-m3o9hlompk-image-1773121452874.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. You can modify all fields except for the user email.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162389410-djre2i1x7ih-image-1773121477680.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Make the required changes and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162390736-yhwzjtpvr2g-image-1773121520215.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
