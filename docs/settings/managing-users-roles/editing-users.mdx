---
title: "Edit Users"
description: "Learn how to modify the details of existing users in your Scrut account."
---

1. [Sign in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Settings** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click **Manage Users**.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-Page.png" alt="" />

3. Click the **edit icon** in the **Actions** column for a specific user.

   <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="/images/image-1773121452874.png" alt="" />

4. You can modify all fields except for the user email.

   <Image src="/images/image-1773121477680.png" alt="" />

5. Make the required changes and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1773121520215.png" alt="" />
