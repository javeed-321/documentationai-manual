---
title: "Add Users"
description: "Learn how to add users to your Scrut organization."
---

To add a user to your Scrut account:

1. [Sign in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Settings** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click **Manage Users**.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-Page.png" alt="" />

3. Click the **Add User** button on the top right.

   <Image src="/images/Add-User-Button.png" alt="" />

4. Enter the user details, including Name, Email, and Department.

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     Once you enter the username, Scrut will check if it exists in the employee list. If it does, the email will auto-populate. If not, you can manually enter the email.
   </Callout>

5. Select the user role. You can choose from: [**Admin, Contributor, or Auditor**](/docs/settings/managing-users-roles/user-roles).

   <Image src="/images/Add-User-modal.png" alt="" />

6. If you’re adding a user as a contributor or auditor, you’ll have to select the modules to which you want to grant them access. **Module selection is not required for admins, as they have access to all modules by default.**

7. For contributors, you can select the departments to which you want to grant them access.

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     You can **limit contributor access by department** for specific modules like policy, evidence, vendor management, and risk management. Contributors will only see artifacts linked to their accessible departments and cannot view those mapped to No Department.
   </Callout>

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-15-at-11.08.04.png" alt="" />

8. Click **Add User**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-08-15-at-11.09.56.png" alt="" />
