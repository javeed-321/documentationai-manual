[**Scrut Teammates**](https://www.scrut.io/products/scrut-teammates) is an **AI-powered risk and compliance agent** embedded directly in the Scrut platform. It multiplies your compliance team’s impact by automating execution, eliminating context switching, and removing bottlenecks across frameworks, tools, and stakeholders, while keeping you in complete control. It leverages a proprietary knowledge graph and a system of specialized AI agents that combine GenAI and ML models. It helps your team move 10x faster, allowing you to **focus on strategy rather than checklists**.

## Enterprise-Grade Security

Scrut Teammates is built with enterprise security at its core:

- **ISO 42001 Certified:** Scrut is among the first GRC platforms to achieve compliance with the gold standard for AI risk management.
- **No Data Sharing or Cross-Training:** Your data is never used to train shared models. All processing is secure and isolated.
- **Privacy by Design:** All data is encrypted and processed with strict guardrails.
- **Your Controls, Your Choice:** Scrut Teammates is opt-in and configurable—use what you want, when you want.
- **Audited AI Workflows:** Scrut is committed to the [responsible use of AI systems](https://www.scrut.io/company/security). All models and agents are regularly reviewed for compliance and security.
