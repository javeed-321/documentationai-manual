[Scrut Teammates](https://www.scrut.io/platform/scrut-teammates) is Scrut's AI agent, built to reduce the manual effort that slows down compliance and risk management work. From reviewing policies to fixing cloud misconfigurations, Teammates works across the platform to help you move faster and stay audit-ready.

## Accelerate your Compliance Workflows

Compliance programs involve a lot of repetitive, busy work such as reviewing policies, identifying gaps, evaluating evidence, and fixing test failures. Teammates automates the most time-consuming parts of these tasks, so your team can focus on decisions that actually require human input.

### 1: AI-Driven Policy Control Mapping

Mapping policies to controls manually is slow and prone to inconsistency, especially when your policy library is large or your framework coverage spans multiple standards. Teammates analyzes your uploaded policy documents and suggests which controls each policy satisfies, so you can validate coverage quickly instead of working through it line by line.

Learn more about [AI-Driven Policy Control Mapping](/docs/policy/ai-powered-features-1/ai-suggested-policy-control-mapping).

### 2: AI-Detected Policy Gaps

Even well-maintained policy libraries can have gaps that only surface during an audit. Teammates evaluates your policies against your compliance controls and flags content gaps before they become audit findings, helping you fix them proactively.

Learn more about [AI-Detected Policy Gaps](/docs/policy/ai-powered-features-1/ai-detected-policy-gaps).

### 3: AI-Powered Evidence Checker

Knowing what evidence to submit and what makes it acceptable is one of the most common pain points in [manual evidence collection](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/upload-attachments-to-an-evidence-task/uploading-evidence#how-to-upload-evidence-manually). Teammates reviews your evidence submissions before they're finalized, identifies problems and gaps, and surfaces recommendations aligned with best practices and industry standards. This helps you improve evidence quality and submit with confidence rather than guesswork.

Learn more about [AI-Powered Evidence Checker](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/ai-powered-features-2/automated-evidence-checker).

### 4: Code Fix with Teammates for Test Remediation

When a cloud test fails, Teammates generates an infrastructure fix directly on the Test Details page so you can resolve the misconfiguration quickly. The fix defaults to Terraform, with the option to switch to AWS CLI or CloudFormation to match your team's workflow. Each fix includes a confidence score so you can gauge reliability before applying it, and all affected resources are listed in the same view. From there, you can copy the fix, raise a Jira ticket, ask Teammates a follow-up question, or regenerate the fix if something doesn't look right.

Learn more about [remediating test findings with Teammates](/docs/continuous-compliance/cloud/working-a-test-finding/remediating-a-test-finding#1-code-fix-with-teammates).

## Build Trust with External Stakeholders

Your Trust Portal is often the first place customers and prospects go to evaluate your security posture. Teammates helps you respond faster and more accurately to external stakeholders.

### 5: Scrut Teammates on Trust Portal

Activating Teammates on your Trust Portal lets visitors ask questions directly through a chat interface and get instant, contextually relevant answers based on the content in your Trust Vault, without waiting for your team to respond.

Learn more about [Scrut Teammates on Trust Portal](/docs/trust-management/trust-vault/ai-powered-features/enable-scrut-teammates-on-trust-portal).

### 6: AI-Powered Questionnaire Autofill

Security questionnaires from customers, prospects, and stakeholders are necessary but notoriously time-consuming to complete. Teammates automates this process by drawing on the information already in your Scrut organization to generate accurate, tailored responses to each questionnaire. What used to take multiple days can be completed in minutes.

Learn more about [AI-Powered Questionnaire Autofill](/docs/trust-management/questionnaires/ai-powered-questionnaire-autofill-walkthrough).

## Strengthen Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

Third-party risk assessment involves time-consuming manual research and coordination before you can form a clear risk picture. Teammates automates this process, so your team can focus on risk decisions rather than administrative work.

### 7: AI-Powered Vendor Assessment

Assessing third-party vendors typically involves extensive manual research, repeated follow-ups, and significant back-and-forth. Teammates applies intelligent automation throughout the vendor assessment lifecycle, handling the administrative groundwork so your team can focus on strategic risk decisions rather than data gathering.

Learn more about [AI-Powered Vendor Assessment](/docs/vendor-management/vendor-risk-assessment-workflow/ai-enhanced-vendor-assessment).

## Get Expert Compliance Help, Wherever You Work

Compliance and risk management questions don't always have straightforward answers, and searching through documentation or relying on generic guidance rarely gives you what you need. Teammates acts as a contextual compliance assistant so you can get answers tailored to your organization, not one-size-fits-all advice.

### 8: Chat with Teammates

You can ask specific questions about your compliance program directly within Scrut and get guidance tailored to your organizational context and compliance goals.

Learn more about [how to chat with Teammates](/docs/settings/scrut-teammates/ask-anything-to-scrut-teammates).

### 9: Teammates Slack App

The Teammates Slack App brings compliance expertise directly to your Slack workspace so your team can get answers without switching context. Once installed, invite the Teammates bot to any channel and ask questions on compliance, infosec, and the Scrut platform to get instant, contextual answers. You can also send direct messages to the bot for questions that don't need a whole channel.

Learn more about the [Teammates Slack App](/docs/settings/scrut-teammates/scrut-teammates-slack-app).
