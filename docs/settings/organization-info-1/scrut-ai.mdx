## How to Enable Scrut Teammates

To turn on Scrut Teammates,

1. [Log in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/login) and click **Settings** in the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Scrut AI** tile.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-Page.png" alt="" />

3. Turn on the **Enable Scrut AI** toggle.

   <Image src="/images/image-1772203062060.png" alt="" />

4. Watch out for the success notification.

5. Scrut Teammates is now enabled for your Scrut organization and ready for use.
