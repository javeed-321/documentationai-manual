description: "Learn how to Manage Evidence Attachments from the Settings section in Scrut."
---

The **Settings → Manage Evidence Attachment** section lists all attachments linked to various evidence tasks. This page allows you to perform the following actions:

- [Archive Attachments](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#archive-evidence-attachments)
- [Unarchive Attachments](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#unarchive-evidence-attachments)
- [Delete Attachments](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#delete-evidence-attachments)
- [Export Attachment Details](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#export-attachment-details)
- [Download Attachments](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#download-attachments)

## Archive Evidence Attachments

Archiving attachments removes them from the corresponding evidence tasks. This is helpful when attachments become outdated or irrelevant. You can archive them for future reference, without cluttering evidence tasks.

1. Click **Settings** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Manage Evidence Attachments** section.

   <Image src="/images/Settings-Page.png" alt="" />

2. Select the attachments that you want to archive. You can select one or more as needed.

3. Click the **Archive** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.39.15.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Archive** again to confirm and complete the action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.39.30.png" alt="" />

## Unarchive Evidence Attachments

Unarchiving restores previously archived attachments and reattaches them to the corresponding evidence tasks.

1. Click **Settings** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Manage Evidence Attachments** section.

2. Select the attachments that you want to unarchive. You can select one or more as needed.

3. Click the **Unarchive** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.39.58.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Unarchive** again to confirm and complete the action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.40.14.png" alt="" />

## Delete Evidence Attachments

Deleting permanently removes attachments from Scrut. Deleted attachments cannot be restored.

1. Click **Settings** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Manage Evidence Attachments** section.

2. Select the attachments you want to delete. You can select one or more as needed.

3. Click the **Delete** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.40.34.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Delete** again to confirm and complete the action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.40.50.png" alt="" />

## Export Attachment Details

To export the attachments table:

1. Click **Settings** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Manage Evidence Attachments** section.

2. Apply the necessary filters to refine the evidence table, as required. For example, you can apply the Uploaded On filter to find attachments added in the last 7 days, among other options.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.41.07.png" alt="" />

3. Use the **Columns** button to display and hide table columns as needed.

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     The Note column in the table provides contextual details about the attachment.
   </Callout>

4. Click the **Export** button.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.54.55.png" alt="" />

Scrut downloads the items visible in the table with the applied filters as a CSV file.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up:**

  Exporting downloads only the table; and not the actual attachments.
</Callout>
