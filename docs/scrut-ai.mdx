## How to Enable Scrut Teammates

To turn on Scrut Teammates,

1. [Log in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/login) and click **Settings** in the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Scrut AI** tile.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166078739-lgetvt8pe9-Settings-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Turn on the **Enable Scrut AI** toggle.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162382618-sokb7ucg54f-image-1772203062060.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Watch out for the success notification.

5. Scrut Teammates is now enabled for your Scrut organization and ready for use.
