---
title: "Assign a Flagged Resource"
description: "Learn how to assign a non-compliant resource in a test finding to a specific team member."
---

Assigning flagged (non-compliant) resources within a test finding establishes clear ownership and accountability at the most granular level. Rather than assigning responsibility for an entire test finding, this feature lets you designate specific individuals or teams for each flagged resource, ensuring the right people are responsible for addressing the exact issues they can control.

There are two ways of assigning flagged resources:

- [Assign a Single Resource](/docs/continuous-compliance/cloud/working-a-test-finding/assigning-a-resource-in-a-failing-test#assign-a-single-resource)
- [Bulk Assign Multiple Resources](/docs/continuous-compliance/cloud/working-a-test-finding/assigning-a-resource-in-a-failing-test#bulk-assign-multiple-resources)

## Assign a Single Resource

1. Click **Compliance → Cloud** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Tests** tab, and select your desired test.

   Pro Tip: Use the search bar and filters at the top to quickly find specific tests.

   <Image src="/images/Select-an-individual-test.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Resources** section and find the resource you want to assign.

   Pro Tip: Use filters to narrow down resources by region, flag, or tags.

4. In the **Actions** column for your selected resource, click the **Assignee icon**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1764229649063.png" alt="" />

5. The **Manage Assignee** window opens. From the dropdown menu, select the appropriate assignee for this resource.

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-27-at-12.59.39.png" alt="" />

## Bulk Assign Multiple Resources

1. Click **Compliance → Cloud** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Tests** tab, and select your desired test.

   Pro Tip: Use the search bar and filters at the top to quickly find specific tests.

   <Image src="/images/Select-an-individual-test.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Resources** section and select the resources you want to assign.

   Pro Tip: Use filters to narrow down resources by region, flag, or tags.

4. Click the **Assign to Users** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-28-at-10.47.25.png" alt="" />

5. The **Manage Assignee** window opens. From the dropdown menu, select the appropriate assignee for the selected resources.

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-28-at-10.47.43.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="success">
  **Heads Up!**

  Scrut will notify the assignee about their new responsibility. The assignee can proceed with remediation following the recommended workflow.
</Callout>
