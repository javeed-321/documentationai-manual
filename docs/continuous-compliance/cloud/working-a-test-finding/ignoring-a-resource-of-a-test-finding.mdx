---
title: "Ignore a Resource Within a Test Finding"
description: "Learn how to mark a resource as ignored in a failing test."
---

Ignoring a resource within a test finding allows you to intentionally exclude certain resources from further consideration while still addressing the overall test finding. This feature is valuable when you want to acknowledge an issue but determine that remediation is not required for particular resources.

1. Click **Compliance → Cloud** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Tests** tab, and select your desired test.

   Pro Tip: Use the search bar and filters at the top to quickly find specific tests.

   <Image src="/images/Select-an-individual-test.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Resources** section and select one or more resources that you want to ignore.

   Pro Tip: Use filters to narrow down resources by region, flag, or tags.

4. In the **Actions** column for your selected resource(s), click **Ignore Selected**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1764230209149.png" alt="" />

5. In the **Justification** field, enter the reason for ignoring this specific resource.

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-27-at-13.27.26.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Ignoring a resource will prevent it from being included in future scans.
</Callout>

## How To View Ignored Resources

1. Click the **Filters** button at the top of the resources table.

   <Image src="/images/image-1764230375057.png" alt="" />

2. In the **Flag** dropdown, select **Ignored** and click **Apply Filters**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-27-at-13.28.39.png" alt="" />

3. You can view all ignored resources in the **Flag** column.

   **Pro Tip!** Click the circular reset icon to remove the applied filters.

   <Image src="/images/reset.png" alt="Reset filters icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-27-at-13.28.48.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If you mark all resources as “Ignored” in a failing test, the test status automatically changes to Ok. However, it doesn’t mean you’ve fixed the issue, as the test might fail for new resources discovered in future scans.
</Callout>

## Reverting an Ignored Resource

There may be scenarios where you have wrongly marked a resource as ignored. You can revert this action by clicking the thumbs-up icon in the actions column of the ignored resource.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-28-at-11.19.35.png" alt="" />

The resources will move to the Monitoring status.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-28-at-11.20.14.png" alt="" />
