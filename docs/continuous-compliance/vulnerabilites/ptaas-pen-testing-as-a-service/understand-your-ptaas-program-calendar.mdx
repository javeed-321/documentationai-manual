The Program Calendar shows your PTaaS schedule for the entire contract year in one place, so you always know what your program includes, what's coming up next, and where each phase currently stands.

## Where is the PTaaS Program Calendar

1. Navigate to **Vulnerabilities → Targets**.
2. Click the target you want to review.
3. The Program Calendar appears on the Target Detail page.

## What’s Included in the PTaaS Calendar

The calendar summarizes what your PTaaS contract includes for this target:

- **Full Pentest:** the number of Full Pentests included per year

- **Release Scans:** how often Release Scans run, for example, weekly

- **Reports:** the number of reports included per year

- **Verification SLA:** how quickly Scrut verifies your submitted fixes, broken out by severity. For example, 1 day for Critical and High findings, 3 days for Medium and Low findings.

- **Timeline:** The Timeline lays out every phase in your program for the year, in order, as a row of cards you can scroll through horizontally.

  **Note:** Both Full Pentests and Release Scans appear as separate cards on the timeline, in the order they occur across your contract year.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Click on any card in the timeline to view further details on it.
</Callout>
