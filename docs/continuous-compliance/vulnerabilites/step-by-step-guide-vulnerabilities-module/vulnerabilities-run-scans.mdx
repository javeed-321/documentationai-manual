title: "Step 2: Run Scans"
description: "Understand how scans run on your defined targets in the vulnerabilities module in Scrut."
---

**Scrut automatically runs DAST scans on your target at the chosen frequency.** However, you can also initiate a manual scan, as required.

1. Click the target you’ve configured from the target list.

2. Click **Start Scan** to begin the scan manually.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-16.49.05.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-16.49.24.png" alt="" />

3. The scans will run automatically in the background. The status tag shows the scan status in real-time.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-16.49.43.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-16-at-16.52.25.png" alt="" />

#### Points to Note

- The scan duration varies depending on several factors, including the target size, number of paths/pages discovered, and the complexity of the application.

- Generally, quick scans take around 20 to 30 minutes to complete, while full scans take several hours.

- The time taken to complete the scan is directly proportional to the volume of the application.

- You can check the status of the scan on the **Vulnerabilities → Targets** page.

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164355015-arqcljpuur-Screenshot-2025-10-08-at-20-22-10.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- While the scan is in progress, you will not be able to edit the target details.
