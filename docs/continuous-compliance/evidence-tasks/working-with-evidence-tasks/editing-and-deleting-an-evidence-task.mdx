## Editing Evidence Task Name

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select your preferred evidence task.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166059935-4puue3rs0zc-Selecting-an-individual-Evidence-Task.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click the edit icon next to the task’s name.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162256253-32azavzsgln-image-1763991000108.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Modify the task name as required.

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164756858-mior6u8foe-Screenshot-2025-11-24-at-19-00-27.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Changing the name of the evidence means you are just updating the task's title. The actual evidence content stays the same.
</Callout>

## Editing the Details of an Individual Evidence Task

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select your preferred evidence task.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166059935-4puue3rs0zc-Selecting-an-individual-Evidence-Task.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. This brings you to the Evidence Details page.

4. You can edit evidence details, such as:

   - Requirement
   - Assigned To
   - Entities
   - Effort Estimate
   - Department
   - Recurrence
   - Next Review Date

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162257646-203k0c5rb34-image-1763991128351.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Editing Evidence Details in Bulk

Scrut also gives you the flexibility to edit several evidence tasks at once:

1. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidence tasks that you want to edit.

2. Click the **Edit** button at the top of the Evidence Tasks table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162259034-65qiyfwu04c-image-1763991673428.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. This opens the Edit details drawer. You can edit the following fields:

   - Department
   - Assignee
   - Entities
   - Recurrence
   - Relevance (Mark selected tasks as relevant or not relevant)

4. Click **Save**. The changes will be applied to all selected evidence tasks.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164758235-26myifhtj1z-Screenshot-2025-11-24-at-19-10-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  If you wish to retain the existing values for a field, simply leave it unchanged. Scrut will only update the fields you've modified in this window, ensuring that the other values remain intact.
</Callout>

## Deleting an Evidence Task

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the tasks you want to delete.

3. You can select one or more tasks, as required.

4. Click the **Delete** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164369838-blb7mpu80tr-Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-17-03-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click Delete to confirm your action.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164371198-bmhpf7lga1-Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-17-03-56.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Once you delete the evidence task, it cannot be recovered. Review the selected tasks carefully before confirming deletion. If you only want to **delete evidence attachments**, [see here](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/removing-an-evidence).
</Callout>
