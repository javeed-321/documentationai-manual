## How to View Evidence Audit Logs

Audit logs provide a detailed record of all actions taken on an evidence task, helping you track changes and maintain compliance.

1. Click **Compliance → Evidence Task** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **All Evidences** tab, and select the evidence task for which you want to view the audit logs.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166059935-4puue3rs0zc-Selecting-an-individual-Evidence-Task.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll down and click the **Audit Logs** tab to view the detailed record of evidence actions.
