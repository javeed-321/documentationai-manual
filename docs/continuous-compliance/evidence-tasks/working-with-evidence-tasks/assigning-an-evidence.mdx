---
title: "Add Assignees to Evidence Tasks"
description: "Learn how to add assignees to an evidence task in Scrut."
---

When creating a custom evidence, you can easily add an assignee while [creating the task](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/how-to-create-evidence-tasks#how-to-create-a-custom-evidence-task). For default Scrut-provided evidence tasks, you have two different ways to add assignees:

- [Individually for each evidence task](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/assigning-an-evidence#adding-an-assignee-individually-to-an-evidence-task)
- [Through bulk assignments](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/assigning-an-evidence#bulk-assignment)

## Adding an Assignee Individually to an Evidence Task

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidence for which you want to add an assignee.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19.02.05.png" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. Click the **+** icon under the **Assigned to** section.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-11.51.15.png" alt="" />

4. Choose the required assignees from the dropdown.

5. To designate a primary assignee, click the **Make Primary** option next to the desired name.

6. Add as many assignees as required.

7. Click **Save** to finalize the assignments.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-11.52.19.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-11.52.59.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="success">
  **Heads Up!**

  Once added, Scrut will notify all assignees via email regarding their new task assignment.
</Callout>

## Bulk Assignment

Scrut also gives you the flexibility to add assignees to several evidence tasks at once:

1. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidences that require assignees.

2. Click the **Edit** button at the top of the Evidence Tasks table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-12.25.28.png" alt="" />

3. Click the **+** icon next to the **Assignees** field.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-12.25.49.png" alt="" />

4. Choose the required assignees from the dropdown.

5. To designate a primary assignee, click the **Make Primary** option next to the desired name.

6. Add as many assignees as required.

7. Click **Save** to complete the bulk assignments.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-12.26.18.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  If you wish to retain the existing values for fields like Department, Recurrence, Entities, and Relevance, simply leave them unchanged. Scrut will only update the fields you've modified in this window, ensuring that the other values remain intact.
</Callout>
