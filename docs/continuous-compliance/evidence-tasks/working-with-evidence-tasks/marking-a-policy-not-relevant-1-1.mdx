---
title: "Mark an Evidence Task as Relevant"
description: "Learn how to revert previously marked \"Not Relevant\" tasks to \"Relevant\" status."
---

When an evidence task that was previously marked as “Not Relevant” becomes applicable or necessary, you can flag it as "Relevant." This ensures that it’s included in all compliance-related metrics on Scrut's main dashboard and Evidence dashboard. You have two ways to mark an evidence task as relevant:

- [Individually](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1-1#how-to-mark-an-evidence-task-as-relevant)
- [In Bulk](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1-1#how-to-mark-multiple-evidence-tasks-as-relevant)

## How To Mark an Evidence Task as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidence that you want to mark as relevant.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19.02.05.png" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. Once you’re on the evidence details page, click the **three-dots icon** at the top right and select “**Mark as Relevant**.”

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166128372-gwpnszpuppv-Three-dots-icon-4.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Three-dots menu icon" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164449118-b1r2ddbo7vv-Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-12-04-00.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Scrut will mark it as “**Relevant**” and revert it back to its previous status.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164450479-jeg8mspe2b-Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-12-04-49.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## How to Mark Multiple Evidence Tasks as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidences you want to mark as relevant.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the evidence table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164397908-ud4bh1huxik-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-12-39-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the Edit drawer, scroll to the **Relevance** dropdown and choose “**Relevant**.”

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162155008-m68c0sg3ji-image-1760339953488.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
