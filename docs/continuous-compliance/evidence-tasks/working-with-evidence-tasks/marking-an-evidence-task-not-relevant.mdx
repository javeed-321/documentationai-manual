---
title: "Mark an Evidence Task as Not Relevant"
description: "Learn how to mark outdated or out-of-scope evidence tasks as \"Not Relevant\" in Scrut."
---

When an Evidence Task is no longer applicable or necessary, you can flag it as "not relevant," ensuring that it is excluded from all compliance-related metrics in Scrut's main dashboard.

You have two ways to mark an evidence task as not relevant:

- [Individually](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/marking-an-evidence-task-not-relevant#how-to-mark-an-evidence-task-as-not-relevant)
- [In Bulk](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/marking-an-evidence-task-not-relevant#how-to-mark-multiple-evidence-tasks-as-not-relevant)

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  You can mark any evidence as “Not Relevant,” regardless of its status; whether it's Not Uploaded, Draft, Needs Attention, or Uploaded.
</Callout>

## How To Mark an Evidence Task as Not Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidence that you want to mark as not relevant.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19.02.05.png" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. Once you’re on the evidence details page, click the **three-dots icon** at the top right and select “**Mark as Not Relevant**.”

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164445029-d0rdyjqbnd-Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-12-02-16.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Enter a justification and click **Save**.

## How to Mark Multiple Evidence Tasks as Not Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidences you want to mark as not relevant.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the evidence table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" width="30" height="30" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164395241-0pwncxspnonq-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-12-15-26.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. In the Edit drawer, scroll to the **Relevance** dropdown and choose, “**Not Relevant**.”

5. Enter a justification and click **Save**.
