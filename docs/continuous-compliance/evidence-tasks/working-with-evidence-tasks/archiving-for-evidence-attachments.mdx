---
title: "Archive Evidence Attachments"
description: "Learn how to archive evidence attachments in Scrut."
---

As you work on getting compliant, you’re likely to accumulate a variety of evidence files and documentation, some of which might become irrelevant over time. By archiving irrelevant or outdated evidence attachments, you can keep your current files organized and clutter-free.

In Scrut, you can archive evidence attachments in two ways:

- [Individually](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/working-with-evidence-tasks/archiving-for-evidence-attachments#archive-evidence-attachment) (OR)
- [Through bulk archiving](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment#archive-evidence-attachments) (via the Settings → Manage Evidence Attachments section)

## Archive Evidence Attachment

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select your preferred evidence task.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19.02.05.png" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. Once you’re on the Evidence Details page, scroll to the **Attachments** section.

4. Expand the attachment you want to archive and click the **Archive icon** next to it.

   <Image src="/images/archive-icon.jpg" alt="Archive icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.22.59.png" alt="" />

5. Click **Archive** to confirm your action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-18.23.21.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="success">
  **Heads Up!**

  Archiving removes the attachment from the corresponding evidence task. However, you can restore it if needed by unarchiving from the [**Manage Evidence Attachments section in the Settings module**](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/manage-evidence-attachment).
</Callout>
