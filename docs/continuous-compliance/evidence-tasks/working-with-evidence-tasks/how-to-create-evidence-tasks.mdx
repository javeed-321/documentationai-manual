---
title: "Create a Custom Evidence Task"
description: "Learn how to create a custom evidence task in Scrut."
---

Scrut offers an extensive library of over 400 evidence tasks, pre-mapped with various standard requirements, including ISO, SOC2, GDPR, and more. For tailored needs, you have the flexibility to create a custom evidence task to match your unique organizational requirements.

## How To Create a Custom Evidence Task

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences tab**.

3. Click the **Add Evidence** button at the top right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161874749-qdv7xffjhj-All-Evidences-Tab.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Enter the evidence details, such as:

   - **Name:** Enter a suitable name for the evidence task.

   - **Assignee:** Use the dropdown to select one or more users who will be assigned the evidence task.

     <Callout kind="alert">
       **Note:**

       The assignee(s) will be notified via email about the assigned evidence task.
     </Callout>

   - **Recurrence:** Select the frequency for the evidence task. For example, if the evidence needs to be reviewed once every quarter, select Quarterly.

   - **Department:** Select the department to which the evidence task belongs.

   - **Entity:** Select the organizational entities to which the evidence belongs. By default, all evidences are linked to “Organization Wide.”

   - **Effort Estimate:** Optionally, you can also select the effort required to complete this evidence task. You can classify it as **Low, Medium, or High**.

   - **Evidence Requirement** \[Optional\]: Enter a description for the task.

5. Click **Next**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164755471-hjbkis3ggp4-Screenshot-2025-11-24-at-18-56-32.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. The next step is to link the controls for the evidence.

   - Use the **Frameworks** panel to view controls relevant to your frameworks.

   - Expand a specific requirement to see all controls associated with it.

     - When you select a requirement, all controls within it will be selected by default.
     - If needed, you can then select/unselect specific controls as required.

   - Select the checkboxes next to the controls you want to link.

   - **Note:** Any requirement that is not applicable will appear greyed out.

   - Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate controls by name or code.

   - Once you've made the selections, enable the "Show Linked Controls" toggle to review and verify the controls you've chosen to link.

   - Click **Next** to confirm your selections.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164342413-cpiii7y26m8-Screenshot-2025-10-07-at-19-31-37.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Review the information and click **Create**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164343763-jsm0urnrz5-Screenshot-2025-10-07-at-19-32-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164345278-8rgl3lb1clo-Screenshot-2025-10-07-at-19-32-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Scrut will create the custom evidence task.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  The **Source** column on the evidence details page for this task will be marked as Custom. This is to easily differentiate custom tasks from those provided by Scrut.
</Callout>
