---
title: "Remove an Evidence Attachment"
description: "Learn how to remove attachments from an evidence task in Scrut."
---

Sometimes, evidence attachments might become outdated or irrelevant because of organizational changes, new regulatory requirements, or evolving industry standards and best practices. Or, there may be instances where an incorrect file was accidentally attached. In such situations, it’s important to remove the wrongly uploaded attachment. This article walks you through how to remove attachments from evidence tasks to ensure that all documentation remains relevant and accurate.

## How To Remove an Evidence Attachment

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** tab and select the evidence from which you want to remove an attachment.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171338815-t7h2s51syd-Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19-02-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. Scroll to the **Attachments** section and expand the timeline folder in which your evidence was uploaded.

4. Click the **Delete** icon next to the evidence that you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164392454-vz9gnn7znd-Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-19-57-09.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click Delete to confirm your action.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164393886-u9qpvc8oqhj-Screenshot-2025-10-10-at-19-57-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Heads Up!**

  - Removing an attachment is permanent and cannot be undone.
  - Removing all attachments from an evidence task reverts its status to “Not Uploaded.”
</Callout>
