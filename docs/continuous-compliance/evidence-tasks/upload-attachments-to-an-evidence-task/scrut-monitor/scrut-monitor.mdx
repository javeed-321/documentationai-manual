Scrut Monitor is a tool designed to simplify evidence collection, ensuring your compliance data is always up-to-date without requiring manual effort.

## Key Benefits

- **Automated Evidence Collection:** Scrut Monitor automatically gathers the required evidence from the tools you’ve integrated with Scrut, reducing manual data collection and minimizing human error.
- **Efficient and Time-Saving:** By automating evidence collection, your team can save significant time and focus on other critical compliance tasks.
