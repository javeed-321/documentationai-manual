The **Google Drive integration** allows you to easily upload evidence files directly from your Google Drive. This feature streamlines the process of gathering and managing compliance documents. It also ensures your files are stored securely and meet compliance requirements.

## How To Upload Evidence Attachments Directly from Google Drive

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Evidence Tasks** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Evidences** dashboard, and click the evidence task for which you want to upload attachments directly from your Google Drive.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-19.02.05.png" alt="Selecting an evidence task from the All Evidences tab" />

3. On the Evidence Details page, scroll to the **Attachments** section and click the **Add Attachment** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164409385-c0zdykilun-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-46-06.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Select **Google Drive** from the options.

5. Click **Connect**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164410715-8jspylmm1l9-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-46-43.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Select the Google account that contains the files you want to upload.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164412111-q8wxkod65jn-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-47-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Review and grant the required permissions. Click **Continue** to proceed.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164414782-8ybk9zu64sp-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-48-05.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

8. Select the files from your Google Drive that you want to upload as evidence. You can select multiple files if required.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164416172-kxex6wxepii-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-49-03.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

9. Select the evidence date and, optionally, add **Notes** (additional comments about the attachments).

10. Click **Save**. Scrut adds the file as an attachment to the evidence task.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164417571-jonl34g9vqj-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-50-07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164418914-prde3i0tbo-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-50-27.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Will other Scrut users in my organization be able to access the Google Drive uploaded evidence file?" default-open="true">
    Yes. Any file that you upload from your Google Drive acts like a [locally uploaded evidence file](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/upload-attachments-to-an-evidence-task/uploading-evidence). Admins, contributors, and auditors having access to the Evidence Tasks module will be able to view and download these files.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What are the permissions that Scrut requires for the Google Drive integration?" default-open="true">
    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164413437-6ec3mes70g2-Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-13-48-05-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

    Scrut requires the following permissions to attach files from your Google Drive to evidence tasks:

    - See and download your Google Drive files
    - See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with Scrut
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
