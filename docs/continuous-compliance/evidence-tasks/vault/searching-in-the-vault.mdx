Use the search bar at the top of the Vault module to quickly locate files and folders across your entire document repository.

## How To Search in the Vault

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Vault** to access the Vault module.

2. Type your desired search term or phrase into the search bar at the top of the page.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-07-21-at-18.21.33.png" alt="" />

3. Scrut performs a comprehensive scan across all folders and nested subfolders, displaying matching files and folders.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-07-21-at-18.25.52.png" alt="" />

4. When you find the item you’re looking for, clicking on the result takes you directly to the exact folder location.

5. Alternatively, click **All Results** to view all the search results for a specific keyword.

6. For documents, clicking on a search result shows the Preview. You can click the **Download** button to download the file quickly.

<Image src="/images/Searching-in-the-Vault.gif" alt="" />

Additionally, you can use the **Created By** and **Document Type** filters in the Vault to quickly locate specific files and folders.
