In this article, we’ll explain how to upload documents, create and manage folders in the Vault.

## Managing Folders

You can create folders and sub-folders in the Vault to organize your documents.

### Create a Folder

1. Log in to your Scrut account, expand the **Compliance** tab on the left navigation panel, and click **Vault**.

2. Click the **Add button** and select **Folder**.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Folder-Document.png" alt="" />

3. Give the folder a name and click **Create**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-07-21-at-17.04.39.png" alt="" />

### Create a Nested (Sub) Folder

Scrut also allows you to create multiple layers of sub-folders in the Vault for enhanced document organization. To create a nested folder:

- Navigate to the Vault module and open your preferred folder.
- Click the **Create Folder** button.
- Enter the name of the sub-folder and click **Create**.

<Image src="/images/Nested-Folder---Vault.gif" alt="" />

### Move Multiple Folders and Files

You can also move multiple folders and files at once.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Grid View">
    To move multiple folders and files, follow these steps:

    - **To select a range (contiguous selection)**

      - Click the first item, then hold the *Shift* key and click the last item.
      - All files and folders in between will be selected.

    - **To select separate items (non-contiguous selection)**

      - Click the first item, hold the *Ctrl key* (Windows) or *Command key* (macOS), and click each additional item you want to select.
      - Only the items you click will be selected.

    - Click the consolidated **Move Selected** button at the top, select the destination folder and confirm your action.

    <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-23-at-13.26.35.png" alt="" />

    <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-23-at-13.26.53.png" alt="" />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="List View">
    To move multiple folders and files, select the checkboxes next to the folders and files you want to move. Click the consolidated **Move Selected** button at the top and select the destination folder.

    <Image src="/images/Moving-Multiple-Folders.gif" alt="" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Managing Documents

You can upload documents to the following locations:

### Upload Directly to the Vault (Root Folder)

1. Navigate to the Vault module, click the **Add** button and select **Document**.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Folder-Document.png" alt="" />

2. Select and upload your desired file. (Maximum size: 100 MB).

3. Choose the entities for which you want to make this document accessible. By default, organization wide is pre-selected and the document will be accessible by all entities.

   <Image src="/images/Add-Document.png" alt="" />

4. This adds the document directly to the vault's root folder.

   <Image src="/images/image-1770013021088.png" alt="" />

### Upload to a Folder or Sub-folder

1. Navigate to the Vault module and open your desired folder.

2. Click the **Upload Document** button.

   <Image src="/images/Upload-Document-button.png" alt="" />

3. Select and upload your desired file. (Maximum size: 100 MB).

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-11.47.52.png" alt="" />

4. This adds the document to the specific folder.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-11.48.01.png" alt="" />

### Document Actions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Grid View">
    Click the three-dots icon on your desired document.

    <Image src="/images/Document-Actions---Grid-View.png" alt="" />

    You can perform the following actions on documents in the vault:

    - **Manage Entities:** Set which entities the document is accessible to.

    - **Download**: Click the **Download** icon next to the file, choose your target location and click **Save**.

      <Image src="/images/downloads.png" alt="Download icon" />

    - **Move**: To organize documents in the Vault, you can move them to your desired folder. Click the three-dot icon on the file, select **Move** , and choose your destination folder.

      <Image src="/images/folder-move.svg" alt="Move to folder icon" />

    - **Rename**: Click the three-dot icon, select **Rename** , enter a new name, and click **Save**.

      <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

    - **Delete**: Click the three-dot icon, select **Delete** , and confirm by clicking **Delete** again.

      <Image src="/images/delete.png" alt="Delete icon" />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="List View">
    <Image src="/images/Document-Actions---List-View.png" alt="" />

    Click the icons next to your desired file.

    - **Manage Entities:** Set which entities the document is accessible to.

    - **Download**: Click the **Download** icon next to the file, choose your target location and click **Save**.

      <Image src="/images/downloads.png" alt="Download icon" />

    - **Move**: To organize documents in the Vault, you can move them to your desired folder. Click the three-dot icon on the file, select **Move** , and choose your destination folder.

      <Image src="/images/folder-move.svg" alt="Move to folder icon" />

    - **Rename**: Click the three-dot icon, select **Rename** , enter a new name, and click **Save**.

      <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

    - **Delete**: Click the three-dot icon, select **Delete** , and confirm by clicking **Delete** again.

      <Image src="/images/delete.png" alt="Delete icon" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
