---
title: "View Audit Logs in Vendor Settings"
metaDescription: "Learn how to access the vendor settings audit logs in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="plan">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

1. Click **Risk → Vendors** on the left navigation panel, and click the **Settings** icon on the top right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171127298-2iatcm3fasy-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-12-20-04.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Navigate to the **Audit Logs** tab. This tab provides a complete chronological record of all actions taken in Vendor Settings. It helps you monitor configuration updates for better oversight and accountability.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171128632-nt67a5agtd-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-12-20-47.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
