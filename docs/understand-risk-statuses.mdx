Every risk in your Risk Register moves through multiple statuses during the risk management lifecycle. Scrut tracks six statuses, shown as metric cards at the top of the Risk Register.

- **Open:** Identified risks. The risk has been recorded in Scrut but hasn't yet been assessed or scored.
- **Assessed:** Initial risk assessment completed and mitigating controls linked. Scrut has calculated an inherent risk score for this risk.
- **Treatment in Progress:** Risk treatment strategy selected, with a mitigation task or residual risk assessment still pending. See [the Risk Assessment Workflow](/docs/risk-assessment-workflow) to understand how a risk reaches this status.
- **Monitor:** Risk is under continuous monitoring, with residual risk still pending. See [Monitor a Risk](/docs/risk-monitor-state-improved-risk-lifecycle) for instructions on moving a risk to this status and how it can transition further.
- **Treated:** Residual risk computed and the mitigation plan finalized. The risk has been fully assessed and treated.
- **Closed:** The risk is no longer relevant and has been closed.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Hover the info icon on any metric card in the Risk Register to see these descriptions without leaving the page.
</Callout>
