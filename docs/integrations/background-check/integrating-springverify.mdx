Integrating your SpringVerify account with Scrut helps you to automatically collect evidence related to employee background checks for your compliance requirements.

## Integrating SpringVerify

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162735854-jq0i4zso6ld-image-9CXHGH6K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the Background Check section and find **_SpringVerify_**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162665353-6ryk2llrkqu-image-4YNP8GC3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Integrate**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163108279-m89fpq7vu1m-image-S7ODRODQ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. In _API Token_ field, enter your SpringVerify account API Token.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163088361-vq3m7u39tw-image-R7QZQOE9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info">
  **How to obtain an API Token?**

  Refer to this [**SpringVerify**](https://support.springworks.in/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-generate-an-api-key-on-springverify-portal) Guide for obtaining an API Token.
</Callout>

6. Click **Submit**. Upon successful integration, you will receive a notification at the top indicating successful integration.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Info**:

  - Background check data syncs every 24 hours.
  - Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.
</Callout>
