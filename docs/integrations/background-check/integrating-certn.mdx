title: "Integrate Certn"
---

Certn integration enables users to collect evidence related employee background checks, within the Scrut platform.

## Integrating Certn

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162735854-jq0i4zso6ld-image-9CXHGH6K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to _Background Check_ section and find _Certn_.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162665353-6ryk2llrkqu-image-4YNP8GC3.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162820186-2jygehxhvdt-image-FN4AS7VP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. In _API Token_ field, enter your Certn account API Token.
:::(Info) (How to obtain API Token?)
Refer to this [Certn Guide](https://docs.certn.co/api/) for obtaining API Token.
:::
6. Click **Submit**.
Upon successful integration, you will receive a notification at the top indicating that the integration was successful.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162635448-gbsutbkk236-image-395.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

:::(Info)
**Info**: 
- Background check data syncs every 24 hours.
- Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.

:::
