Integrating Scrut with EC Council saves hundreds of hours by automatically collecting evidence of an employee's security awareness training.

## How to Integrate EC Council with Scrut

1. From the left navigation menu, click **Integrations**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162822803-lt4okpzgacg-image-FUDOPG55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

\2. Click on **Available**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162825539-29g34j5jnkp-image-FX0BN1JF.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the Miscellaneous section, find EC Council, and click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162666749-3aguabvv5an-image-503JZILS.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

\4. Enter the Organization ID and Click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162118488-2ougpvmf279-image-0ME06TCE.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Once the integration is complete, click on the three dots in the top right corner and select **Configure Campaigns.**
6. A list of available campaigns will be displayed.
7. Toggle on the campaigns you want to enable.
8. Once configured, the campaign details will be shown under the employee tab of the people section.
