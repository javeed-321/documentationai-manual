The **Scrut - Microsoft Teams integration** sends notifications from Scrut directly to your [Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/group-chat-software) environment, keeping you and your colleagues up to date on compliance tasks and activities. With this integration, you can:

- Send **reminders** to employees of their incomplete security tasks.
- Enable **real-time alerts from Scrut** for events like Trust Vault requests, test and integration failures, comment mentions, and more.
- Receive a **personalized, summarized notification** of your pending security tasks.
- Receive the **Scrut digest** (a summary of your organization’s compliance status delivered to your selected Teams channel).

In this article, we walk you through the steps to complete the Microsoft Teams integration in Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Make sure you have permission to install the Scrut app in Microsoft Teams. If you don’t, please contact your IT team for support.

### Permissions

The Scrut app will have permission to:

- **Read and access information related to you:** Access profile information such as your name, email address, company name, and preferred language.
- **Read and access information in your chats, channels, and meetings:** Access information from your team or chat, such as team or chat name, channel list, and roster (including team or chat members’ names and email addresses), and use this info to contact them.
- **Send and receive messages and notifications** in a channel or chat.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  We recommend logging into your Microsoft Teams account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate Microsoft Teams with Scrut

### Step 1: Add the Scrut App to Microsoft Teams

1. Go to **Apps** in Microsoft Teams, and type **Scrut** in the search bar.

2. Click **Add** to install the Scrut app in Teams.

   **Note:** See here to learn more about [adding an app to Microsoft Teams](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/add-an-app-to-microsoft-teams-b2217706-f7ed-4e64-8e96-c413afd02f77).

3. Select your preferred channel to receive Scrut notifications and click **Go**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163579397-aa9xowwz1dl-Screenshot-2025-05-26-at-19-51-33.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads up!**

  You cannot select a private channel, as Microsoft doesn’t allow it. To learn more, [see here](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/private-channels#private-channel-limitations).
</Callout>

### Step 2: Connect Microsoft Teams to Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Integrations Library** tab, and in **Categories**, scroll to **Communication Tools**.

3. Click the **Integrate** button on the MS Teams card.

4. Click **Connect**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163555113-xjm0925ta1-Screenshot-2025-05-20-at-17-42-29.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. If you haven’t logged in to your Microsoft Teams account, Scrut will prompt you to sign in using your credentials.

## Synchronization Details

Once the integration is completed, Scrut will begin to send notifications in Teams, according to the schedule set up in the [**Settings → Notifications → Notification Settings**](/docs/settings/notifications/configuring-slack-reminders) page.

## Next Steps

Configure the notifications, reminders, and alerts you want to receive in Teams from Scrut. To learn how to customize these notifications, go to [**Settings → Notifications**](/docs/settings/notifications/configuring-slack-reminders).
