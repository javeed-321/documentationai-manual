---
title: "Integrate Heroku"
description: "Learn how to connect your Heroku account with Scrut."
---

1. From the left navigation panel, click **Integrations**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162713353-0u7ywm3fgfgo-image-7XCYMDYP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Available**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163046498-sa5hnqrt917-image-P7RH97RY.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Go to the Cloud Provider section and find Heroku.

4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162785737-ujjwszuq4mq-image-CCT9JG4K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click **Connect**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162617917-nc00ydkzn4-image-2IS44QAN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

It will redirect to Heroku's authentication page.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162741162-fa8lpoujz8-image-9UHBNO2T.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Enter the admin account credentials to integrate, and this will redirect to the integration page on successful integration, and a connected flag will be shown on the app.
