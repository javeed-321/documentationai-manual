---
title: "Integrate Auth0"
description: "Learn how to integrate your Auth0 account with Scrut."
---

Scrut uses an inbuilt Auth0 Management API to connect with Auth0. To use this API, you must create an authorized machine-to-machine Application in Auth0 before integrating with Scrut.

## Creating Auth0 Application

1. In your Auth0 Dashboard, click **Create Application**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162636692-iucn7nsrx1r-image-39BNTZY8.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Name your new application, choose the _Machine To Machine Applications_ click **Create**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163055208-8rrb2f8czu3-image-POYRXU55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. A dropdown with Authorized APIs should appear. Choose the **Auth0 Management API** option.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162962250-5tinnds01j-image-KDRNJIXJ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Enable the following permissions for your application by clicking on the checkboxes under the _Permissions_ section:

- read:client_grants
- read:organizations
- read:organization_members
- read:users
- read:roles
- read:role_members

5. To create the new application linked to the Auth0 Management API, click **Authorize**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163192807-3t4c8jpesnk-image-Y1F21KUU.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Once done, Auth0 will redirect you to the _Quick Start_ tab from your newly created application.
7. Go to Advanced Settings by navigating to the bottom of the Settings tab.
8. Check if the _Client Credentials_ grant type is enabled under the _Grant types_ tab where the Client Credentials grant should be enabled.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162696891-kxpi3ibvkl-image-6UP9XAA9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

10. Navigate to the top of the _Settings_ tab in your recently created application.
11. Copy the **Domain, Client ID**, and **Client Secret** values from your application.

## Integrating Auth0 with Scrut

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162735854-jq0i4zso6ld-image-9CXHGH6K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the _Identity Providers_ section and find _Auth0_. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162939911-l7bd0dhwob-image-JCHR3LJK.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Paste the _Domain, Client ID_, and _Client Secret_ values from your application. Click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163120902-waqeqcs8nim-image-T5J069RA.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
