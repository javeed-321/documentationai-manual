The **Scrut - Okta (IDP)** integration enables you to sync users and user group data from your Okta account to Scrut. In this guide, we walk you through the process of **connecting your organization’s Okta account with Scrut as an Identity Provider**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If you’re looking to connect Okta as an SSO provider, [see here](/docs/integrations/integrating-identity-providers/integrating-okta).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Access

- You must be a Scrut Admin (or Contributor with access to the Integrations module)
- You need Admin access to your organization’s Okta account

### Permissions

Scrut requires the following read-only permissions:

- okta.users.read: Allows Scrut to read existing users’ profiles and credentials
- okta.groups.read: Allows Scrut to read information about groups and their members in your Okta organization
- okta.roles.read: Allows Scrut to read administrative role assignments for users in your Okta organization

To learn more about Okta permissions, [see here](https://developer.okta.com/docs/api/oauth2).

## How To Integrate Okta IDP With Scrut

### Step 1: Fetch Client ID, Client Secret and Domain from Your Okta Account

1. Log in to your Okta Admin Console.

2. Navigate to **Applications → API Service Integration → Add Integration**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1763465562439.png" alt="" />

3. Search for and choose **Scrut Automation.** Click **Next**.

   <Image src="/images/image-1763465614006.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Install & Authorize**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-18-at-17.05.35.png" alt="" />

5. Copy the **Client Secret** to a safe location. It will not be displayed again.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-18-at-17.08.54.png" alt="" />

6. Copy the **Client ID** and **Okta Domain**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-18-at-17.09.39.png" alt="" />

### Step 2: Go to Integrations in Scrut

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab, choose **Identity Providers** in the **Categories** section.

3. Scroll to the **Okta (IDP)** tile and click **Integrate**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-18-at-17.14.07.png" alt="" />

4. Enter the details you fetched in the previous step:

   - Client ID
   - Client Secret
   - Okta Domain

5. Click **Submit**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-11-18-at-17.15.21.png" alt="" />

Watch for the success toast message and the Connected status flag indicator.
