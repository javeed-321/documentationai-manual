The **Scrut-Endpoint Central Integration** enables Scrut to monitor device compliance, security settings, and patch management across your organization. It tracks device configurations and ensures that all endpoints meet your security and compliance requirements.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following permissions for your Endpoint Central account:

- **Account Information:** Your Endpoint Central login email address

- **Admin Access:** You must be an Endpoint Central admin to grant the following permissions:

  - **Read access:** To allow Scrut to read your inventory data

## How To Integrate Scrut With Endpoint Central

### Step 1: Copy Client ID and Client Secret from Endpoint Central

1. Sign in to your **Zoho API console** and click **Add Client**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163794981-iadz57sby6g-Screenshot-2025-06-25-at-13-49-58.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Choose **Server-based Applications**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163796334-yfm5azw7z8c-Screenshot-2025-06-25-at-13-50-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Enter a name for the integration, such as Scrut Automation.

4. For **homepage URL**, enter: [**https://app.scrut.io/**](https://app.scrut.io)

5. Enter the **Redirect URL**, based on your Scrut region:

   - **India:** [**https://app.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central**](https://app.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central)
   - **US:** [**https://app.us.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central**](https://app.us.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central)
   - **EU:** [**https://app.eu.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central**](https://app.eu.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central)
   - **Australia:** [**https://app.au.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central**](https://app.au.scrut.io/settings/integration/endpoint_central)

6. Click **Create**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163797601-hp51xniqp1v-Screenshot-2025-06-25-at-13-51-03.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Copy the **Client ID** and **Secret** shown on the screen. Make sure to save these credentials carefully, as you’ll need to enter them in Scrut in the next step.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162153714-sk822xfp79-image-1759989188513.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Step 2: Connect Your Endpoint Central Account in Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and choose **Mobile Device Management Tools** in the **Categories** section.

3. Scroll to the **Endpoint Central** tile and click **Integrate**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164356369-6x152bg2m6q-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11-23-34.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. On the Endpoint Central integration page, enter the following details from [Step 1](/docs/integrations/integrating-mobile-device-management-tools/integrating-endpoint-central#step-1-copy-client-id-and-client-secret-from-endpoint-central).

   - **Client ID**
   - **Client Secret**

5. Click **Submit**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164357780-tblnjd6gou9-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11-25-03.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Click **Accept** to grant the required permissions.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164360382-3m5gdpfrbti-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11-32-56.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Once authorization is done, watch out for the success toast message and the **Connected** status indicator.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164361729-cpsiebmq2g-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11-33-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Step 3: Configure Devices

The final step of the integration is to configure the devices you sync with Scrut.

1. Go to the Endpoint Central integrations page and click **Configure Devices** on the top right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164363100-d3ox3yo9nto-Screenshot-2025-10-09-at-11-35-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Select the devices you want to sync with Scrut, and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163914536-scmpm7vdon-Screenshot2014-24-02.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Watch out for the “**Scope Updated Successfully!**” notification message.

## What Happens Next?

Upon completing the integration, Scrut will display the fetched data in the following modules:

- [Evidence Tasks](/docs/continuous-compliance/evidence-tasks/evidence-tasks-walkthrough)
- [Tests → Automated Evidence](/docs/continuous-compliance/automated-tests/getting-started-with-tests-in-scrut)
