title: "Integrate KiteCyber"
description: "Learn how to connect your KiteCyber account with Scrut."
---

The KiteCyber integration fetches employees' endpoint/device data to Scrut. This data is displayed in the People Dashboard and the People section's Employee module. Scrut fetches the following data from Kitecyber:

- Device name
- OS version
- Antivirus Installed(Yes/No)
- HD Encrypted(Yes/No)
- Screenlock Enabled(Yes/No)

#### Integrating KiteCyber

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.
2. Click on _Available_.

<Image src="/images/image-SK39XRRJ.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the Mobile Device Management Tools section and find KiteCyber. Optionally, use the search bar to locate the integration.
4. Click on _Integrate_.

<Image src="/images/image-O5IFNYCK.png" alt="" />

5. Enter the Base URL without the prefix, the Client ID, and the Client Secret, and click on _Submit_.

<Image src="/images/image-RTW0K8E3.png" alt="" />

6. Once done, you will see a connected flag.

<Image src="/images/image-T9NHE3X3.png" alt="" />

## How to retrieve Credentials from KiteCyber

- **Login**: Access KiteCyber using an admin account.
- **API Details**: Navigate to **Settings > API** to retrieve the Client ID. The Client Secret will be sent directly to your email from KiteCyber.
- **Base URL**: Locate the Base URL by going to **Settings > Account > Contact Info**.
