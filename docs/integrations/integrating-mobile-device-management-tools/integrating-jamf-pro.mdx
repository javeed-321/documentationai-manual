title: "Integrate Jamf Pro"
description: "Learn how to connect your Jamf Pro account with Scrut."
---

Jamf Pro integration fetches employees' endpoint/device data to Scrut. This data is displayed in the People Dashboard and the People → Employee module. ​ The following data is fetched from Jamf Pro:

- Device name
- OS version
- Serial number
- Antivirus Installed(Yes/No)
- HD Encrypted(Yes/No)
- Screenlock Enabled(Yes/No)
- List of Applications installed on the device

## Integrating Jamf Pro

### Step 1: Create a read-only user in JamfPro

1. Log in to your Jamf Pro administrator account.

2. Create a new user with the _Auditor_ privilege set. If you have the option to select an access level, select _Full Access_.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162495002-v6uhv87uqr-Jamf-Pro-3-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Copy the username/password–you’ll need to add them to Scrut.

### Step 2: Enter the user's credentials in Scrut

1. From the left navigation menu, click Integrations.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162621897-votxu2vjiw-image-2PA6HLVP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Available**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162759579-srdp92d6mo-image-B8SI1UVU.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to _Mobile Device Management Tools_ section and find _Jamf Pro_.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162973994-kdr0bi2fv19-image-L0XN7NGA.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Click **Integrate**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162946929-e4jt48f7hpc-image-JMUK8585.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Jamf Pro integration page opens.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162119905-xzbnaudeuuh-image-0SEXJS4R.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. In _Jamf Cloud URL_ field, enter the cloud url of your jamf pro instance.
6. In _username_ and _password_ field, enter the credentials obtained in step 1.
7. Click **Connect**.

You will receive either a confirmation that the credentials are configured correctly or an error message explaining how they’re misconfigured.

8. Once the integration is successful the user will have to click on the **Configure** icon in the integration page and select Configure Profiles in Jamf Pro.
9. A list of Profiles will be displayed.
10. Toggle On the Profiles you want to enable.
11. Once configured, AV Installed and Screenlock details will be visible in the people section.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Info**:

  - Employees device data will start syncing immediately. Incremental data will be synced every 24 hours.
  - Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the **Sync Now** option.
</Callout>
