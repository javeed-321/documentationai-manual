The **Scrut - ServiceDesk Plus** integration enables you to create tickets for compliance tasks and issues directly from Scrut in your ServiceDesk Plus account. This article offers a step-by-step guide to help you connect your ServiceDesk Plus account with Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following permissions for your ServiceDesk Plus account:

- **Account Information:** Login credentials for your ServiceDesk Plus account

- **Permissions:** Scrut requires the following permissions:

  - Create new requests
  - Read all requests

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip:**

  We recommend logging into your ServiceDesk Plus account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate Scrut With ServiceDesk Plus

### Step 1: Copy the Service Domain URL and Portal Name from ServiceDesk Plus

1. Log in to your ServiceDesk Plus account, and click the hamburger icon on the top-left and select **ESM Directory**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163894425-rexrmf76ur-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-16-33-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Click **Service Instances** on the left menu.

3. Copy the **Service Domain URL** and **Portal Name**. In this example:

   - **Service Domain URL:** [https://sdpondemand.manageengine.in](https://sdpondemand.manageengine.in)

     **Note:** Do not include the trailing slash when copying the domain URL.

   - **Portal Name:** itdesk

4. You’ll need to enter these details in the next step.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163897006-g1rg0iy71nj-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-16-50-56-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Step 2: Integrate Your ServiceDesk Plus Account in Scrut

1. Sign in to Scrut and click **Integrations** on the left side panel.

2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab, and in **the Categories section**, scroll to **Ticket Management**. Alternatively, you can search for ServiceDesk Plus using the search bar at the top of the page.

3. Click the **Integrate** button in the ServiceDesk Plus tile.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163895683-us62p0hc2d-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-16-41-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. On the ServiceDesk Plus integration page, enter the **Service Domain URL** and **Portal Name** you copied in the previous step and click **Submit**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163898292-se5o6xo2cnn-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-04-57.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. If you haven’t logged in to ServiceDesk Plus, Scrut will prompt you to complete the login process.

6. Next, click **Accept** to grant Scrut the requisite permissions for the integration.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163899572-6buf5yb1isy-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-08-30.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Watch out for the success notification and the Connected flag.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163901187-goeqr0316ut-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-08-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Creating a ServiceDesk Plus Ticket in Scrut

Once you have completed the integration, you can create ServiceDesk Plus tickets directly from Scrut. You can create and manage tickets across all modules, including:

- Policies
- Evidence Tasks
- Tests
- Cloud
- Risk Management
- Audit → Audit Center → Requests
- Audit → Audit Center → Findings
- Audit → Audit Center → Corrective Actions
- Vulnerabilities → Findings

To create a ticket:

1. Navigate to your desired artifact page, click the three-dot icon on the top-right, and select **Create ServiceDesk Plus Ticket**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163902430-qg9vqwt5m2g-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-23-03.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

2. Scrut automatically fills in the ticket **Title** and **Description**. You can edit these details if required.

3. Select the **Assignee** who will handle the ticket and set a **Due Date**.

4. Click **Submit**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163903744-miv2jm763yj-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-42-11.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Scrut will create the ticket in your ServiceDesk Plus account. Watch out for the success notification.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163905144-8u4g8jysdf-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-17-42-50.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Scrut also adds the ticket link and creator’s details to the **Audit Logs**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163906464-riyldznfkw-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-00-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Sample audit log details for a ServiceDesk Plus ticket creation" />

7. The **Tickets (ServiceDesk Plus)** tab provides details on all ServiceDesk Plus tickets you’ve created for this artifact. It includes information such as:

   - ServiceDesk Plus ID
   - Title
   - Status
   - Technician
   - Email IDs to Notify
   - Created Date
   - Due Date

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163907788-muq1o8jgwu-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-01-50.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     Clicking the ServiceDesk Plus ID of the ticket will take you directly to the corresponding ticket details page in ServiceDesk Plus.
   </Callout>

## Linking Existing ServiceDesk Plus Tickets in Scrut

Scrut allows you to link existing tickets in your ServiceDesk Plus dashboard to specific artifacts. To do this:

1. [Log in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and navigate to your preferred artifact.

2. Scroll to the **Tickets (ServiceDesk Plus)** tab.

3. Click the **Create** button and select **Link Existing Tickets**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163909060-erorha6mksr-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-03-16.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Copy and paste the ticket URL and click **Link**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163910337-i5wvdgbcni-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-04-00.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. The ticket will be linked to the artifact.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163911633-r5wqvbxk69c-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-07-21.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Updating Tasks in ServiceDesk Plus

The integration between Scrut and ServiceDesk Plus is bidirectional and syncs in real time. **When you change any synced fields in your ServiceDesk Plus tickets, those changes will automatically update in Scrut.** For example, if you mark a task as completed in ServiceDesk Plus, that completion status will immediately appear in Scrut.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166193173-4ikwtb1caif-Updating-Ticket-Status-in-ServiceDesk-Plus.gif?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Limitations

When creating a ServiceDesk Plus ticket from Scrut, you cannot assign a Technician. The Assignee you select in Scrut will be added to the Email to Notify field.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163912921-mqowa494po-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-52-41.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

However, if you edit the request in ServiceDesk Plus to add a Technician, the details will be reflected automatically in Scrut.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163919421-v99xqgn2fwj-Screenshot-2025-07-23-at-18-53-44.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
