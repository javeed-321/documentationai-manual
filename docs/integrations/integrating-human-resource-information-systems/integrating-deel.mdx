The **Scrut-Deel integration** fetches employee data from your Deel account and syncs it with Scrut. In this guide, we walk you through the process of integrating your Deel account with Scrut.

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have the following access and permissions:

### Account Requirements

- You must be a Scrut Admin (or Contributor with access to the Integration module)
- You need permissions to generate an [API access token in your Deel account](https://developer.deel.com/docs/api-tokens-1)

### Permissions

Scrut requires **read-only access** to the following data in Deel:

- **Organizations:read**

  - View basic information about your organizations, teams, legal entities, and agreements

- **People:read**

  - Read worker’s information such as profile, time off, etc.

- **Time-off:read**

  - Read time off related data like time off requests, balances, policies, and more

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  We recommend logging into your organization’s Deel account before starting the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

## How To Integrate Deel With Scrut

### Step 1: Fetch Access Token from Deel

<Callout kind="alert">
  For step-by-step instructions on how to generate the access token, refer to the [Deel help documentation](https://developer.deel.com/docs/api-tokens-1).
</Callout>

1. Log in to your organization’s Deel account, and go to **More → Developer**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-18.58.06.png" alt="" />

2. Go to the **Access Tokens** tab and click **Generate new token**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-18.58.59.png" alt="" />

3. On the first step of the Generate access token wizard, enter a **label for the token** (e.g., Scrut), select the **token type as Organization**, and click **Next**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-19.00.00.png" alt="" />

4. In the next step, select the following **token scopes** and click **Next**.

   - **Organizations:read**
   - **People:read**
   - **Time-off:read**

   <Image src="/images/markuphero-EpDbmslZZ5AZldn6eSX4.jpg" alt="" />

5. In the Permissions level, set organization-level.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-24-at-13.49.49.png" alt="" />

6. In the next step, ensure the “Hide all personally identifiable information (PII) toggle is turned OFF, as Scrut requires this access to read employee name and email. Click **Next**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-19.11.55.png" alt="" />

7. In the final step, click **Generate**.

8. Copy the access token and save it in a safe location. You’ll need to enter it in Scrut in the [next step](/docs/integrations/integrating-human-resource-information-systems/integrating-deel#step-2-navigate-to-integrations-in-scrut).

   <Image src="/images/image-1760354255845.png" alt="" />

### Step 2: Navigate to Integrations in Scrut

1. Sign in to your Scrut account and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

2. Navigate to the **Integrations Library** tab and select **Human Resource Information Systems** from the Categories section.

3. Scroll to the **Deel** tile and click **Integrate**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-16.48.28.png" alt="" />

4. Click the **Connect** button at the top right of the Deel integration page.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-16.49.11.png" alt="" />

5. Enter the following details:

   - **Deel Access Token:** Paste the Deel access token you generated in the [previous step](/docs/integrations/integrating-human-resource-information-systems/integrating-deel#step-1-fetch-access-token-from-deel).
   - **Deel Environment (Optional):** Select your application environment. By default, it is set to production.

6. Click **Submit**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-13-at-16.50.11-1.png" alt="" />

7. Once your Deel account is successfully integrated, you will see a connected flag on the Deel integrations page in Scrut.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-14-at-19.17.18.png" alt="" />

## Synchronization Details

- **Initial Sync**: Upon completing the integration, Scrut will immediately sync employee data from your Deel account.
- **Recurring Sync**: After this, Scrut performs the sync **every 24 hours** to fetch incremental data.
- **Manual Sync:** You can also initiate a manual sync by clicking the **Sync Now** button on the Deel integrations page.

## Data Fetched from Deel

Scrut fetches the following data for all your employees from Deel:

- Employee name (First and last name)
- Email address
- Joining date
- Exit date (if applicable)
- Department and Role (if available)
- Email of reporting manager (if available)
- Leave data (start date, end date, and status)

## Viewing Deel Data in Scrut

To view Deel data in Scrut:

1. Go to **People → Employees → All Employees**.
2. Click on any employee to view their details, including name, email, joining date, reporting manager, and more.
