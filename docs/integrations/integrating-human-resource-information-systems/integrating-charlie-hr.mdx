Integrating Charlie HR with Scrut helps you sync employee details like names, emails, departments, managers, joining dates, and exit dates, streamlining HR management.

## How To Integrate Charlie HR with Scrut

1. From the left navigation panel, select **Integrations**.
2. Click on **Available**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162735854-jq0i4zso6ld-image-9CXHGH6K.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Human Resource Information Systems** section and find Charlie HR.
4. Click **Integrate.**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163109648-96eo7xz9ojj-image-S7ZJUMOD.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click **Connect**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162948361-lrijil7syt-image-JMYFW1VV.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Fill in the following details:

- **Enter your Charlie HR Client ID**: To get the Client ID, log in to your CharlieHR account and go to the Integrations -> API Keys page.
- **Enter your Charlie HR Client Secret**: To get the Client Secret, log in to your CharlieHR account and go to the Integrations -> API Keys page.

7. Once the credentials are entered, click **Submit**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163156228-1tb97vqoery-image-VO6I9FFP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

8. Once your Charlie HR account is successfully integrated with Scrut, you will see a connected flag on the screen.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Note:**

  Employee data will start syncing immediately. Incremental data will be synced every 24 hours. Data can also be manually synced by clicking the three dots and choosing the Sync Now option.
</Callout>
