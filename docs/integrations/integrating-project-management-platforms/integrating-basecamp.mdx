title: "Integrate Basecamp"
---

The Basecamp integration enables Scrut to monitor project-level permissions and user roles within your workspaces. It tracks user activity, project access changes, and collaboration settings to ensure compliance with data access policies.

## Integrating Basecamp

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.

2. Click on **Integrations Library**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163185610-kha1tyqorr-image-XM6B33XK.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to Project Management Platforms section and find Basecamp.
4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162963880-f8m9zwvh22f-image-KFEMTTIP.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Click **Connect**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162737170-iwv89yvz6bb-image-9F465YQ9.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Enter your Basecamp account email to authenticate the login.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162742557-kkk2fbqqzbn-image-9VZ8WWKV.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Allow access for Scrut automation to the Basecamp account.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162717472-coj5zqynn3k-image-813ESVPI.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. You will be redirected to the integration page and there will be a connected flag on successful integration.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163178702-hdx88pmf3yl-image-X6OCMSUQ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Using the Integration

- After successful integration, you will see different accounts displayed in the Scope Configuration section. Each account represents a unique workspace.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163096913-mmpxn0apcj9-image-RNP4D5BN.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- Under each account, you will find a list of projects available for scope selection.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162927586-ej2amx2cxbk-image-ILAZDDHF.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- To utilize this integration, navigate to any compliance task (e.g., Policies, Evidence, Risk Management or Tests).

- Open any policy and click on the three dots located at the top-right corner.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163200062-id32dcwta9-image-YUNSS4UO.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- Similar to other integrations, you can create a reference by selecting a project from the list.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163214327-gflsa9s8yii-image-ZW6BCYFT.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

- While creating a task, you will need to provide a Title and Description (both required fields).

- Once these steps are completed, a task is automatically created in the Basecamp account.
