title: "Integrate Basecamp"
---

The Basecamp integration enables Scrut to monitor project-level permissions and user roles within your workspaces. It tracks user activity, project access changes, and collaboration settings to ensure compliance with data access policies.

## Integrating Basecamp

1. From the left navigation panel, select Integrations.

2. Click on **Integrations Library**.

<Image src="/images/image-XM6B33XK.png" alt="" />

3. Scroll to Project Management Platforms section and find Basecamp.
4. Click **Integrate**.

<Image src="/images/image-KFEMTTIP.png" alt="" />

5. Click **Connect**.

<Image src="/images/image-9F465YQ9.png" alt="" />

6. Enter your Basecamp account email to authenticate the login.

<Image src="/images/image-9VZ8WWKV.png" alt="" />

Allow access for Scrut automation to the Basecamp account.

<Image src="/images/image-813ESVPI.png" alt="" />

7. You will be redirected to the integration page and there will be a connected flag on successful integration.

<Image src="/images/image-X6OCMSUQ.png" alt="" />

### Using the Integration

* After successful integration, you will see different accounts displayed in the Scope Configuration section. Each account represents a unique workspace.

<Image src="/images/image-RNP4D5BN.png" alt="" />

* Under each account, you will find a list of projects available for scope selection.

<Image src="/images/image-ILAZDDHF.png" alt="" />

* To utilize this integration, navigate to any compliance task (e.g., Policies, Evidence, Risk Management or Tests).

* Open any policy and click on the three dots located at the top-right corner.

<Image src="/images/image-YUNSS4UO.png" alt="" />

* Similar to other integrations, you can create a reference by selecting a project from the list.

<Image src="/images/image-ZW6BCYFT.png" alt="" />

* While creating a task, you will need to provide a Title and Description (both required fields).

* Once these steps are completed, a task is automatically created in the Basecamp account.
