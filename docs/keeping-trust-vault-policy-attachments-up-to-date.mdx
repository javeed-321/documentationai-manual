When you attach policies to your Trust Vault, they are added as PDF files. If a policy is updated and republished, Trust Vault will not automatically sync the changes. Instead, you'll need to manually sync the attachment to ensure your Trust Vault displays the latest version.

## How to Sync Policy Attachments

### Identify Policies That Need Syncing

When a new version of an attached policy becomes available, Trust Vault will display a **sync indicator** next to the compliance or control. This indicates that an updated version is ready to be synced.

### Preview & Sync Updated Policies

1. Click on the Compliance or Control that shows a sync indicator.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165005621-pacfdd8scq-Screenshot-2026-01-23-at-13-01-35.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click the **Preview and Sync** button to review the updated policy.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165006956-lsgodfygdvj-Screenshot-2026-01-23-at-13-01-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Review the changes in the preview window to ensure the new version is what you want to display.

4. Click **Sync Latest Version to Trust Portal** to update the attachment to the latest published version.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165008307-23dalm94rcc-Screenshot-2026-01-23-at-13-02-17.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Once synced, the policy attachment in Trust Vault will be replaced with the latest published version as a PDF.

#### Important Notes

- Only published policies can be attached to the Trust Portal
- Syncing is manual: No auto updates; You control when attachments are updated
- Sync is only available for policies edited through Scrut’s Policy Editor. It does not work for policies uploaded as an external file or linked via Confluence or SharePoint.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tips!**

  - Check the Trust Vault periodically for sync indicators to keep policies current
  - Coordinate with your policy management team to stay informed about policy changes
</Callout>
