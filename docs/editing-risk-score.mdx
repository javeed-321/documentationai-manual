<Callout kind="plan">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Growth** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Risks are not static. They’re dynamic and constantly changing. As a result, framework mandates require periodic reassessment of risk assessments. In this guide, we explain how to edit risk scores, ensuring your assessments remain accurate and up-to-date.

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Important!**

  You cannot edit the score for risks in Closed status.
</Callout>

## Edit Inherent Risk Score

To edit the inherent risk score:

1. Go to **Risk → Risk Management → Risk Register** and select the risk for which you want to edit the score.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171259575-boyyif9pug-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-01-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Scroll to the **Inherent Risk** section and click the pencil icon.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" width="30" height="30" caption=" " />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171260938-iqd1syrkqwf-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-02-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. In the Edit Risk Score window, drag the slider for each factor to modify its value and click Save.

4. Scrut will automatically update the risk score based on the risk score formula saved during its creation.

## Edit Residual Risk Score

To edit the residual risk score:

1. Go to **Risk → Risk Management → Risk Register** and select the risk for which you want to edit the score.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171259575-boyyif9pug-Screenshot-2025-09-18-at-14-01-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Scroll to the **Residual Risk** section and click the pencil icon.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163251064-wl55crsqzn-Pencil-icon-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Edit icon" width="30" height="30" caption=" " />
