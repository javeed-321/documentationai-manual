<Callout kind="plan">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut makes it easy to edit risk details such as assignee, department, category, linked assets, custom fields, and more. In this article, we walk you through the process of editing risk details and updating risk scores.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Editing a Single Risk">
    To edit risks individually:

    1. Click **Risk → Risk Management**, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

    2. Click on the risk you want to edit.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166046257-d0yq3nuniws-Selecting-a-Risk.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

    3. This opens the Risk details page, from where you can edit its details.

    4. To edit any field, click the **pencil icon** next to it. For fields with dropdowns, click the dropdown to make your selection.

    5. If you’ve added custom fields, these will be present below the default fields. Click the pencil icon adjacent to it to edit the values for custom fields.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163306162-466wncxb3zk-Risk-Details-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Editing Multiple Risks">
    To edit details of multiple risks simultaneously:

    1. Click **Risk → Risk Management**, and go to the **Risk Register** tab.

    2. Select the risks you want to edit.

    3. Click the **Edit** button at the top. This opens the bulk actions modal.

       <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166067073-k02dsnunt0o-Selecting-multiple-risks.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

    4. Use the sidebar to bulk edit risk details, including Assignees, Departments, Category, Linked Assets, and Entities, and other custom fields you’ve created.

       <Callout kind="alert">
         **Heads Up!**

         If you don’t choose a value for a specific field, then no updates will be made to it. Existing details will remain unchanged for this field.
       </Callout>

    5. Make the necessary edits and click **Save**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165053178-kt9drmudhu9-Screenshot-2026-01-30-at-11-30-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

    <Callout kind="danger">
      **Note:**

      For fields like Risk Name and Description, you cannot bulk edit as they are unique to each risk. Go to the specific risk details page to update these fields.
    </Callout>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Note:

Any edits you make to risk details are captured in the Audit Logs of the corresponding risks.
