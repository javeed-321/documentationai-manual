---
title: "Create an Empty Checklist"
metaDescription: "Use empty checklists to create placeholder campaigns in advance."
---

Scrut provides you the flexibility to create and assign checklists without any tasks initially included. This is useful when:

- Setting up campaigns in advance
- Creating placeholder assignments for future task distribution
- Managing employees with custom or pending task assignments

## How to Create an Empty Checklist

1. Navigate to **People → Employees** and click the **Settings** icon on the top-right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162022497-kglt3v1besa-Employee-Settings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click the **Checklists** tab and click the **Add Checklist** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161813370-dg28yd0mgsc-Add-Checklist-Button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Enter a suitable name for your checklist.

4. Leave all tasks unchecked and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164849244-e0jfen0tylv-Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-21-56-48.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Next, you can assign this empty checklist to specific employee groups, as needed.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164850560-76a9gf9w4ht-Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-21-57-22.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Employee Experience

Employees assigned to empty checklists will see a **"No Tasks Assigned"** message on their employee portal. This provides clarity rather than leaving them uncertain about their task status.

## Finding Employees Without Tasks

1. Navigate to **People → Employees → All Employees** and click the **Filter** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161873431-3ltz7mp5ysa-All-Employees-Page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. In the filter menu, scroll to **Task Status** and select **No Tasks Assigned** and click **Apply Filters.**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164851960-uglbfcgo0va-Screenshot-2025-12-16-at-22-04-38.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" width="517" height="794" caption=" " />

This displays all employees with empty checklists. This makes it easy to identify who needs task assignments.
