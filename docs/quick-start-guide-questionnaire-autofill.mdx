The Questionnaire Autofill feature helps you respond to questionnaires, RFPs and RFQs faster and more accurately with AI-generated, contextual responses. Here’s the recommended workflow to get the most out of this feature:

- **Step #1:** [**Set up your autofill sources**](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#configure-your-autofill-sources)**.** Navigate to Trust → Questionnaire → Autofill Sources and configure which sources feed into autofill.
- **Step #2:** [**Build your answer library selectively**](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#adding-answers-to-the-answer-library)**.** Import answers from past questionnaires or add a small set of exact Q&A pairs for boilerplate content.
- **Step #3:** **Add a questionnaire.** [Upload a spreadsheet](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#adding-a-spreadsheet-or-document-questionnaire) (or document), [use the Chrome Extension](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#autofilling-a-website-or-portal-questionnaire) for portal-based questionnaires, or capture questions directly from a vendor portal.
- **Step #4:** [**Run autofill**](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#autofilling-a-security-questionnaire)**.** Click Autofill with Scrut AI and let Teammates generate responses in batches. You can start reviewing early answers while it works through the rest.
- **Step #5:** [**Review and approve**](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#how-to-autofill-a-questionnaire)**.** Accept, edit, or assign responses to SMEs for review.
- **Step #6:** [**Complete and Export**](/docs/quick-start-guide-questionnaire-autofill#how-to-complete-and-export-a-questionnaire)**.** Download the completed questionnaire in your preferred format and send it to the recipient.

## Configure Your Autofill Sources

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Scrut Teammates always checks the Answer Library first, before referencing other autofill sources. If a matching question is found there, it uses the stored answer exactly as written. Other sources are used only when no match is found.
</Callout>

### What are autofill sources?

**Autofill Sources** is where you configure and manage the sources Scrut Teammates draws on when generating questionnaire responses. In addition to your Answer Library, Scrut Teammates can draw from three categories of autofill sources.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161902917-iuc1wdlhkl-Autofill-Sources-page.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

The Autofill Sources section lets you control which of these Teammates should refer to.

| Source | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| Policies | Published policy documents stored in the Policies module |
| Trust Vault | Documents uploaded to your Trust Vault with AI-readability enabled |
| Vault | Additional documents uploaded to the Vault with AI readability enabled |

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Before running autofill on a questionnaire, confirm that Teammates has access to the right sources.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Note:**

  Currently, Teammates can read files in .pdf, .docx, and .xlsx formats only. Documents in other formats will not be read, even if AI readability is turned on.

### Best Practices

The more relevant, current, and specific your sources are, the more accurate Teammates' responses will be. A few things that help:

- Policy documents should be **published** and up to date. Policies in other statuses will not be read.
- Vault documents should be specific to your security posture, such as penetration test reports, SOC 2 attestations, and ISMS documentation.
- Past questionnaires in the Questionnaire module also serve as a source. Scrut Teammates can use previously approved responses to inform new ones. You can also upload previously answered security questionnaires to the Vault and enable AI readability for Teammates to refer to them.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  After configuring your sources, run autofill on a test questionnaire to confirm that the responses are contextual and drawing from the relevant content.
</Callout>

## Adding Answers to the Answer Library

The **Answer Library** is a curated set of exact, verbatim Q&A pairs that Scrut Teammates checks first when generating questionnaire responses. Use it for a small number of high-frequency answers where you need the wording to be consistent every time: company name and address boilerplate, standard certification statements, and similar content.

### When to Use the Answer Library (and When Not To)

The Answer Library returns exact matches. It does not rephrase or adapt answers to the phrasing of a new question. This makes it reliable for boilerplate content, but the wrong tool for most security questions, which vary too much in phrasing to benefit from verbatim matching.

For the majority of your questionnaire content, autofill sources are more effective. Scrut Teammates reads those sources to match each question's context, producing more accurate and natural responses.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If your Answer Library contains more than 15 entries, you will see a persistent prompt in the module. This is a signal to shift new content to Vault or other autofill sources instead of adding more manual entries. The Answer Library works best when it stays small and focused.
</Callout>

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161877483-k41und75dwh-Answer-Library-Empty.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165349759-kvcpwqxsnki-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-11-21-01.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## Adding a Spreadsheet (or Document) Questionnaire

Organizations often receive security questionnaires as Excel, CSV, DOC, DOCX, or other document files. Upload these directly to Scrut so Scrut Teammates can extract the questions and generate responses.

### Before You Start

Questionnaires must be in XLSX format before you upload them. If you have received the questionnaire in a different format (such as CSV, DOCX, PDF, scanned images, or handwritten notes), first convert it to XLSX.

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  The XLSX file can follow any layout or structure. Scrut Teammates will parse the file, extract the questions, and let you choose which ones to import.
</Callout>

### How to Add a Spreadsheet Questionnaire

1. Click **Trust → Questionnaire** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Questionnaire** tab and click **Add Questionnaire → Create in Scrut** to upload a document or spreadsheet.

   <Callout kind="tip">
     **Pro Tip!**

     If your questionnaire is on a vendor portal or website, use the Chrome Extension instead. See Autofilling a Website or Portal Questionnaire.
   </Callout>

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161840339-lce6cvzs06t-Add-Questionnaire-button.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Enter the questionnaire details:

   - **Questionnaire Name:** a name that helps you identify this questionnaire later.
   - **Customer:** the organization that sent the questionnaire.
   - **Reviewer:** the team member responsible for reviewing responses.
   - **Due Date:** the deadline for completing the questionnaire.
   - **Additional Note (optional):** any context for the reviewer.

4. Drag and drop the XLSX file into the upload area, or click to select it.

   <Callout kind="tip">
     **Pro Tip!**

     If your file is not yet formatted as an XLSX, you can download the sample template, paste your questions into it, and upload that.
   </Callout>

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165352733-95f0i4drw3s-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-11-35-17.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. Complete field mapping to match the fields in the security questionnaire to Scrut’s format.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165354184-5pcqmtu6418-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-11-38-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

7. Scrut parses the file and extracts the questions. Select the questions you want to include in this questionnaire.

8. Click **Add Questionnaire**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165355647-ydgkydages-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-11-39-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Watch for the "Security Questionnaire Created" confirmation. Your questionnaire is now in the Questionnaire module, ready for autofill.

## Autofilling a Security Questionnaire

After a questionnaire has been added to Scrut, use the Autofill with Scrut AI feature to generate first-draft responses. Scrut Teammates draws from your Answer Library and autofill sources to produce answers that reflect your organization's security posture.

### Before You Start

Autofill works best when your knowledge sources are in good shape. If you are running autofill for the first time or getting a high number of unanswered questions, review Configuring Your Autofill Sources before proceeding.

### How to Autofill a Questionnaire

1. Navigate to **Trust → Questionnaire**.

2. Click the questionnaire you want to autofill.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166053845-09vlg8sxhq3-Selecting-a-single-questionnaire-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click **Autofill with Scrut AI** and select whether to generate responses for All Questions or just the Unanswered ones.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161904211-x16zza1wlvc-Autofill-with-Scrut-AI-XLSX.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  You can run Autofill with Scrut AI up to three times per questionnaire. Use reruns selectively, for example after adding new sources or uploading additional Vault documents.
</Callout>

5. Scrut Teammates begins generating responses in batches. You do not need to wait for all responses before starting your review.

6. Teammates also checks each subjective question in the background to see if it can be interpreted in more than one way. This does not block your review, so you can start reviewing questions that are ready while background checks continue on the rest.

7. For each generated answer, you can:

   - Click **Accept** to approve the response.
   - Click **Edit** to modify it, then save your changes.
   - Click **Add Attachment** to attach a supporting document to the response.
   - Click **View Autofill Sources** to see which documents or Answer Library entries informed the answer.
   - Click **View Interpretations** to open a side panel for each reading of the question, each with its own answer and sources. Select the one that best fits your needs.

8. To accept all responses in one action, click **Accept All** at the top of the page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162425879-u1dhcfwcgpl-image-1773642591159.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Heads Up!**

     For questions where Scrut Teammates includes a document attachment automatically (for example, "Share your privacy policy"), the system will identify and attach the relevant file without additional input from you.
   </Callout>

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165360071-2w0hch769iv-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-12-12-37.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

9. Click the three-dot icon on any question for additional options:

   - **Add to Answer Library**: Saves this Q&A pair for use in future questionnaires.

   - **Add to Trust Vault FAQ**: Adds the pair to your Trust Vault FAQ section.

   - **Regenerate Answer**: Generates a new response. You can regenerate up to three times per question.

   - **Delete Question**: Removes the question from the questionnaire. This action cannot be undone.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165358556-m0yxe102hud-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-12-00-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

10. Use the **Select Reviewer** dropdown to assign a reviewer to individual questions.

11. Use the thumbs-up or thumbs-down icon to submit feedback on response quality.

### Handling Questions with Multiple Interpretations

Some questionnaire questions can reasonably be read more than one way. For example, "What is your background verification process?" could refer to your employees or your third-party vendors. Scrut Teammates checks every subjective question for this kind of ambiguity.

When a question has more than one plausible reading, Teammates writes a single consolidated answer that addresses all of them, along with a **Match score**. The Match score reflects how well the readings Teammates found cover the different ways the question could be interpreted. It does not measure how accurate the answer's wording is.

Click **View Interpretations** next to the answer to see each individual reading of the question side by side with its own answer and sources. Accept the consolidated answer if it fits, or accept a specific interpretation instead if it is a closer match to your context.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166016660-e6mpgph2eyd-Screenshot-2026-08-28-at-10-55-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Objective questions, such as yes/no, multiple choice, or numeric questions, are not checked for ambiguity. They always use the single-answer flow.

### What to Do with Unanswered Questions

If Scrut Teammates cannot generate a response, the question is tagged as Unanswered. This usually means the relevant information is not yet in your knowledge sources. To resolve this:

- Upload relevant documents to the Vault, turn on AI readability, and regenerate the answer.
- Publish related policy documents in the Policies module and regenerate the answer.
- Click Add Answer to enter a response manually.

## Reviewing and Exporting a Security Questionnaire

Before sending a completed questionnaire to a stakeholder, review the AI-generated responses to confirm they are accurate and aligned with your organization's current policies. After reviewing it, export the questionnaire in your preferred format.

### \[For Reviewers\] How to Review a Questionnaire

1. Navigate to **Trust → Questionnaire**.

2. Click the questionnaire you want to review.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166053845-09vlg8sxhq3-Selecting-a-single-questionnaire-1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. For each response, you can accept, edit, or add an attachment.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165357061-07hh44onyfd-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-11-57-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Click **Edit** to make changes, then click Save.

5. To attach the source document that informed an answer, click **Add Attachment**. Scrut Teammates displays the relevant sources it used so that you can attach them directly.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165361429-6thtenu9bvk-Screenshot-2026-03-16-at-12-19-19.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

6. After reviewing an answer, click **Accept** to confirm it.

7. To accept all responses at once, click **Accept All** at the top of the page.

8. For unanswered questions, you can:

   - Click Add Answer to enter a response manually.
   - Upload relevant documents to the Vault, turn on AI readability, and regenerate the answer.
   - Add related policy documents to the Policies module and regenerate the answer.

### How to Complete and Export a Questionnaire

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Important:**

  Once you click Complete Questionnaire, you cannot edit any answers. Review all responses carefully before proceeding.
</Callout>

1. After accepting all answers, click **Complete Questionnaire**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161961990-elksrzk9rgl-Complete-Questionnaire.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click **Proceed** to confirm.

3. Click **Export** to download the responses.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162048545-lpjx7nk3oej-Export-Questionnaire.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Select a format:

   - Scrut Format: exports questions, answers, and source citations in Scrut's native format.
   - Original Format: exports in the same format as the original questionnaire.
   - Package Format: exports the questionnaire and source files as a Zip file.

5. Scrut emails the export to you.

**Heads Up!** It may take a few moments for the export to arrive in your inbox.

<Callout kind="success">
  **Final Step:**

  Share the completed questionnaire with the recipient via email.
</Callout>
