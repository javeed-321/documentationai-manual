<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut makes it easy to link multiple requirements across one or more frameworks to a single control.

## Linking Requirements to a Control

To link one or more requirements to a control, follow these steps:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Controls** tab and select the control you want to link requirements to.

3. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific control by name or code.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166051471-74l4gfupc9i-Selecting-a-single-control.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Scroll to the **Linked Requirements** tab and click the **Link Requirement** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165338606-pf0k1fithd-Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-18-32-22.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Use the Frameworks panel to view the requirements for a particular framework.

6. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific requirement by its name or code.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164149295-91ybwoja24s-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-19-19-37.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Select the checkbox or checkboxes that correspond to the requirements you want to link.

8. Use the **Show Linked Requirements** toggle to review and verify your selections.

9. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164150702-v6mqjbszsrh-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-19-20-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Unlinking Requirements to a Control

To unlink a requirement from a control, follow these steps:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Controls** tab and select the specific control from which you want to unlink requirements.

3. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific control by name or code.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166051471-74l4gfupc9i-Selecting-a-single-control.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Scroll to the **Linked Requirements** tab and click the **Link Requirement** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165338606-pf0k1fithd-Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-18-32-22.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Use the Frameworks panel to view requirements for a particular framework.

6. Optionally, use the search bar at the top to quickly locate a specific requirement by its name or code.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164149295-91ybwoja24s-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-19-19-37.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

7. Deselect the checkbox or checkboxes corresponding to the requirements you want to unlink.

8. Use the **Show Linked Requirements** toggle to review and verify your selections.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164150702-v6mqjbszsrh-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-19-20-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

9. Click **Save**.
