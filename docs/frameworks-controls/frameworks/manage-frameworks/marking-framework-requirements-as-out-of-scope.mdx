<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Empower your organization to efficiently identify and categorize compliance framework requirements that aren't applicable, allowing you to sharpen your focus on the essentials.

## How To Mark a Requirement as Out of Scope

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Compliance → Framework** using the left side panel.

2. Click the **My Frameworks** tab, and select the framework for which you want to mark a requirement as “**Out of Scope**.”

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163738696-4zv77nux3rt-Screenshot-2025-06-23-at-12-29-26.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Click on the requirement category and click the **three-dot icon** next to the requirement name.

   <Callout kind="danger">
     **Heads Up!**

     You cannot mark a mandatory requirement as Out of Scope.
   </Callout>

4. Click **Mark Out Of Scope**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163747175-5qb0dwykqte-Screenshot-2025-06-23-at-12-50-38.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Provide a valid justification for marking the requirement as out of scope and click **Save**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164461477-bfre6jjj3qa-Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-12-28-28.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Watch out for the confirmation message.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163748454-figxmbfvpzs-Screenshot-2025-06-23-at-12-51-54.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
