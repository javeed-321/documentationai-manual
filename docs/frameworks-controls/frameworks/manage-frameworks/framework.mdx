Click **Compliance → Frameworks** on the left navigation panel and go to the **Frameworks Library** tab. This tab shows a list of [all frameworks that Scrut supports](/docs/frameworks-controls/frameworks/framework-overview) out of the box. Use it to explore and add new frameworks to your Scrut account.

## How To Add a New Framework

To add a new framework to your Scrut account, follow these steps:

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), and click **Compliance → Frameworks** on the left navigation panel. Go to the **Frameworks Library** tab.

2. Explore the available frameworks. The Frameworks Library tab displays a comprehensive list of all the frameworks that Scrut supports out of the box. Search to find the framework that you’re looking for.

3. Click on the **Add Framework** button to initiate a request.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-22-at-11.20.51.png" alt="" />

4. After clicking the Add Framework button, you'll see an option to **Contact Your CSM**. Click this, and your CSM will reach out to you to complete the request for the new framework.
