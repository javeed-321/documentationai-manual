<Callout kind="info">

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

The **Framework Compliance Report** helps to identify and address non-compliant elements within specific frameworks. This report can be shared with your organization's leadership team, auditors, and other stakeholders.

## How To Download Framework Compliance Report

Users can download a framework-level report in two ways:

### Method 1: Via the Framework Detail Page

Open the framework detail page, click the three-dots icon on the top right, and select **Export Framework Compliance Report**.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18.28.20.png" alt="" />

Select the options to include in the report and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18.28.39.png" alt="" />

### Method 2: Via the Reports Module

Click **Reports** on the left navigation panel. Click the **Download** button on the Framework Compliance report.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18.31.51.png" alt="" />

Choose the Framework, select the options, and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18.32.16.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Once you request the report, Scrut will share the link to download the report to your email. This link will **expire in 7 days**.
</Callout>
