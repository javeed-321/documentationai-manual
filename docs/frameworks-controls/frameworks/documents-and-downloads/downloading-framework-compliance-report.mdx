<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

The **Framework Compliance Report** helps to identify and address non-compliant elements within specific frameworks. This report can be shared with your organization's leadership team, auditors, and other stakeholders.

## How To Download Framework Compliance Report

Users can download a framework-level report in two ways:

### Method 1: Via the Framework Detail Page

Open the framework detail page, click the three-dots icon on the top right, and select **Export Framework Compliance Report**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171093482-r4wzjvyjxod-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-28-20.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Select the options to include in the report and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171094821-ly8dbwywr2e-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-28-39.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### Method 2: Via the Reports Module

Click **Reports** on the left navigation panel. Click the **Download** button on the Framework Compliance report.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171096178-1oxebml5zidj-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-31-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Choose the Framework, select the options, and click **Download**. Scrut will email the report to you.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171097641-18rc58fy8s7-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-32-16.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Once you request the report, Scrut will share the link to download the report to your email. This link will **expire in 7 days**.
</Callout>
