- [Delete Multiple Controls](/docs/frameworks-controls/controls-1/editing-and-deleting-control#delete-multiple-controls)
- [Mark a Control as “Out of Scope”](/docs/frameworks-controls/controls-1/editing-and-deleting-control#mark-a-control-out-of-scope)
- [Mark a Control as “In Scope”](/docs/frameworks-controls/controls-1/editing-and-deleting-control#mark-a-control-in-scope)

## Edit a Control

1. [Log in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/), expand the **Compliance** menu on the left navigation panel, and click **Controls**.

2. Click **All Controls** and click the control you wish to modify. Use the search bar at the top to quickly find a specific control.

   <Callout kind="info">
     **Pro Tip!**

     You can search a control by name or code.
   </Callout>

<Image src="/images/image-1758871110568.png" alt="" />

3. Click the pencil icon next to the field you want to edit. You can modify all fields, including:

   <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon.png" alt="" />

   - Control Name
   - Description
   - Question
   - Control Code
   - Assignee
   - Control Domain
   - Function Grouping
   - Entities

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.02.10.png" alt="" />

4. Watch for the success notification message indicating that the control has been updated.

## Edit Multiple Controls

Rather than modifying controls individually, you can select multiple controls at once and perform bulk updates or deletions.

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Controls → All Controls** and select the ones you want to modify. You can select multiple controls at once.

2. After selecting the controls, click the consolidated **Edit** icon at the top of the table.

3. This allows you to update key fields for the selected controls, such as:

   - **Assignee**: Reassign the controls to different team members
   - **Control Domain**: Change the control domain
   - **Function Grouping**: Update the function grouping of the controls
   - **Entities**: Modify which entities are associated with the controls
   - **Control Scope**: Set controls in or out of scope

4. Click **Save**.

## Delete a Control

<Callout kind="danger">
  **Warning!**

  Once you delete a control, it cannot be undone. So, proceed with caution.
</Callout>

1. Navigate to the **Controls → All Controls** dashboard and click on a specific control.

2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Delete Control.**

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-3.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.03.15.png" alt="" />

3. A confirmation window opens.

   <Image src="/images/image-1758871173143.png" alt="" />

4. Click **Delete Control.**

## Delete Multiple Controls

1. Navigate to **Compliance → Controls → All Controls** and select the ones you want to delete. You can select multiple controls at once.

2. After selecting the controls, click the consolidated **Delete** icon at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-15-at-15.30.49.png" alt="" />

3. Click **Delete** again to confirm your action.

## Mark a Control Out of Scope

1. Navigate to the **Controls → All Controls** dashboard and click on a specific control.

2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Mark Out of Scope**.

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-3.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.03.15.png" alt="" />

3. Enter your justification for marking the control out of scope.

   <Image src="/images/image-1758871200941.png" alt="" />

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Note:**

     It’s mandatory to provide a justification to mark a control out of scope.
   </Callout>

Watch out for the success notification, “Control marked out of scope successfully!”

### Key Points:

- Once you mark a control out of scope, Scrut updates its status to **Not Applicable**.

- The justification you provide will be visible on the control details page.

  <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.04.24.png" alt="" />

- To reverse the action, click the three-dot icons and select **Mark In Scope**. Scrut will remove the previously added justification and show the success message, “Control marked in scope successfully!”

- The audit log captures all scope changes.

  <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.05.27.png" alt="" />

## Mark a Control In Scope

1. Navigate to the **Controls → All Controls** dashboard and click on the control you want to mark in scope.

2. Click the three-dots icon on the top-right and select **Mark In Scope**.

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-3.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17.06.02.png" alt="" />
