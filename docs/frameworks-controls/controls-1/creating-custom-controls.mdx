<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Growth** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut offers an extensive library of over 1,100 controls, carefully designed to align with a diverse range of regulatory and industry standards, including GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001, and others. These controls have been thoughtfully mapped to specific requirements outlined by these standards, making it remarkably efficient for organizations to tailor their compliance efforts.

However, we recognize that each organization might have its own distinct operational processes, security needs, or other specific requirements. In such cases, our platform allows you to create a custom control that is unique and not covered by our existing library.

You can create a control in either of the two ways:

1. [Create a single control](/docs/frameworks-controls/controls-1/creating-custom-controls#create-a-single-control)
2. [Import multiple controls](/docs/frameworks-controls/controls-1/creating-custom-controls#import-multiple-controls)

## Create a Single Control

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click **Add Custom Control**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161817350-9k5dge5ggc9-Add-Custom-Control.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Enter the required details:

   - From the **Control Domain** dropdown, select the domain to which the control pertains.

   - In the **Control Name** field, enter a descriptive name for the control.

   - In the **Control Code** field, enter a unique code for the control.

   - Optionally, in the **Description** field, enter a detailed description of the control.

   - Optionally, in the **Control Question** field, enter a question that the control should ask your organization.

   - From the **Functioning Group** dropdown, select the functioning group to which the control pertains

   - Once the page is filled, click **Next**.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165328571-rfqrztoeu4l-Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-16-59-11.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   - Click **Add Assignee** to assign one or more users to the control.

   - Click on **Entities** to assign an Entity to the control.

   - Click **Next**.

     <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171361299-usl9ihhzp1r-Screenshot-2025-09-26-at-12-40-52.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Review the data and click **Create Control.**

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165329995-6d9l1agxn78-Screenshot-2026-03-13-at-17-00-18.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

## Import Multiple Controls

1. Download the template provided below.

[Import Controls Template.xlsx](https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171802834-4l7sd42ouam-Import-Controls-Template.xlsx)

2. Replace the file's contents with your own controls.

<Callout kind="success">
  **Helpful Tips!**

  - Do NOT remove the first row. It contains column headers.
  - Remove the second row. It contains a description of each column.
  - Create a row for each control.
  - Control Code must be unique.
  - The Control Name, Domain, and Functioning Group are mandatory fields.
  - Template headers are case-sensitive; ensure that the downloaded format matches the imported format.
</Callout>

3. After filling the template, please share it with your Customer Success Manager, who will import it from the backend.
