<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

## How to Export Controls

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Controls** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Controls** tab.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171099025-4gzdqfnzqrt-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-42-51.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Select the controls that you want to export, and click **Export**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171100435-xl3zzjea12e-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-43-25.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Scrut will automatically download the selected controls.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171101770-4q3de65xwe6-Screenshot-2025-09-09-at-18-43-42.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />
