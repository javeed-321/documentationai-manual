The Audit Center makes it easy for auditors to preview relevant policy and evidence files without needing full access to the policy and evidence modules.

## How Do Auditors Access Scrut?

### Prerequisites

- You need to grant access to your Scrut organization for the auditor and the audit team. See here for instructions on [adding auditors to the platform](/docs/adding-users).

### Viewing Audits

1. Once you grant the auditor access, they will be able to view the Audit module and all audits within it.
2. When an auditor clicks on an audit, they will see all relevant controls under the Controls tab for that audit.
