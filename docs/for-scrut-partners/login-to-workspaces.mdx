## Logging Into Customer Accounts

You can easily access customer accounts directly from the Partner Portal:

1. From the **Workspaces** tab, find the customer workspace that you want to log into.
2. Click the **Login** action.
3. You'll be redirected to [**app.scrut.io**](http://app.scrut.io), where you'll be logged into that customer's account.

### Switching Between Customer Accounts

Once logged into a customer account at [app.scrut.io](http://app.scrut.io), you can switch between different customer workspaces (that you have access to) using the workspace switcher in the interface, without needing to return to the Partner Portal.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165082479-86ww0dbo59o-Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-19-59-35.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

### How Users Access Their Accounts

- **Users with Workspace Console Access:** Can log in through [**console.scrut.io**](http://console.scrut.io) into their preferred workspace.
- **Users without Workspace Console Access:** Must log in through [**app.scrut.io**](http://app.scrut.io) and use the workspace switcher to navigate between their assigned customer accounts.
