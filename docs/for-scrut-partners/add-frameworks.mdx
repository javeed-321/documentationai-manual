---
title: "Add Frameworks"
description: "Learn how to add frameworks to the Scrut Partner Portal."
---

By default, new workspaces have no frameworks assigned. In this article, we explain how to add compliance frameworks to individual workspaces.

1. Log in to the **Partner Console (**[**console.scrut.io**](https://console.scrut.io/login)**)** and click the workspace for which you want to add a framework.

   <Image src="/images/Selecting-a-Workspace.png" alt="Selecting a Workspace" />

2. Click **Add Framework.**

   <Image src="/images/Add-Framework.png" alt="" />

3. Select from the available frameworks enabled for your partner account (the frameworks available vary depending on your partner agreement).

4. Click **Request more frameworks** to send a request to your CSM.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-05-18-at-21.27.09-1.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  Click the icons in the Actions column of the Frameworks table to:

  - Download Customer Mapping
  - Download Master Mapping
  - Remove a framework from your workspace
</Callout>

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-02-02-at-20.36.48.png" alt="" />

### Request Additional Frameworks

If you need frameworks beyond those currently enabled (such as HIPAA, GDPR, or any of Scrut's 70+ supported frameworks):

1. Click **Request Framework.**
2. Select the frameworks you wish to request for this customer account.
3. This will trigger an email to your Scrut CSM, who will follow up shortly.
