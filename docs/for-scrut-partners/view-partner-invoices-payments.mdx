The **Settings → Billing** page shows every invoice Scrut has raised across all your customer workspaces. You can quickly see what's due, review payment status, and identify exactly which customer an invoice belongs to.

## Access the Billing Screen

1. Log in to the Partner Portal at [console.scrut.io](http://console.scrut.io).

2. Click **Settings** in the left navigation panel.

3. Click the **Billing** card.

   **Note:** Invoices and payments are updated within 24 to 48 hours on working days. Wait for some time for your invoices to be visible.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-07-07-at-17.32.39.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  If you have at least one invoice overdue a sticky banner appears at the top reminding you to complete the payment at the earliest.
</Callout>

## Review Outstanding Payments

The **Outstanding Payments** tab opens by default when you access Billing. It shows a card for every invoice that is currently Unpaid or Partially Paid. Each card includes invoice details such as:

- Customer/workspace name
- Invoice number
- Invoice status
- Invoice amount
- Invoice date and due date
- Description

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-07-07-at-17.33.44.png" alt="" />

Use the search bar to find an invoice by workspace name, invoice number, or description. Use the **Payment Status** filter to narrow the list to Unpaid or Partially Paid invoices. You can also switch between card and list layouts using the view icons next to the search bar.

## Review Invoice History

The **Invoice History** tab lists every invoice raised for your account, both paid and unpaid, in a sortable table. Click **Columns** to customize the table fields. Use the **search bar** to find an invoice by workspace name, invoice number, or description. Use the **Workspaces** filter to narrow the table to one or more specific customer workspaces. Use the **Payment Status** filter to narrow the table to Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid invoices.

<Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-07-07-at-17.34.32.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  The customer name shown on each invoice comes from Scrut's HubSpot record for that customer, not the workspace name you set in the Partner Portal. If you named a workspace differently from how the customer is recorded internally, the Billing screen shows the HubSpot version.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why does the customer name on an invoice not match the workspace name?" default-open="true">
    The customer name in the invoice comes from Scrut’s HubSpot records, not the workspace name you set in the Partner Portal. If your workspace name and the HubSpot record differ, the invoice always displays the HubSpot name.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I download an invoice as a PDF from the Partner Portal?" default-open="true">
    Not currently. Invoice PDFs are sent to you by email when the invoice is raised. Contact your account manager or email [receivables@scrut.io](mailto:receivables@scrut.io) if you need a copy resent.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why don’t I see any invoices for a newly onboarded customer?" default-open="true">
    New customer invoices can take up to 24 to 48 hours to appear after your workspace is set up on Scrut's side. If invoices still don't appear after that window, contact your CSM.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
