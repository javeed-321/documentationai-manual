The **Request Plan** button in the Partner Portal allows you to submit a request to your CSM to upgrade your plan or activate add-ons.

## How to Request a Plan or Add-On

1. Log in to the partner portal (console.scrut.io) and navigate to the Workspaces tab.

2. Click **Request Plan** in the top-right corner of the page.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163285038-2nxbss75gtu-Request-Plan.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Under Select Plans, select the plans you want to request: **Foundation, Growth, or Scale**.

4. Under Select Add-Ons, select any add-ons you want to include.

5. Click **Submit Request**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165744445-5ls4g26mvdm-Screenshot-2026-05-26-at-18-17-02.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

Your CSM will follow up to confirm and activate your selections.

## Available Plans

You can request any combination of the three available plans:

- Foundation
- Growth
- Scale

## Available Add-Ons

Add-ons extend the capabilities of a workspace beyond what the base plan includes. Available add-ons include:

| Add-On Name | Available For Plans | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vendor Advanced | Foundation, Growth • Auto included in Scale plan | Unlocks the intake form and AI-powered vendor assessment. |
| Trust Advanced | Foundation, Growth • Auto included in Scale plan | Enables watermarking, custom controls in Trust Vault, and Salesforce integration for access requests. |
| Additional Questionnaires | Foundation, Growth, Scale | Adds additional questionnaires to the customer's annual limit for uploading and auto-filling using Scrut AI. |
| AppSec Standard | Foundation, Growth, Scale | Enables vulnerability scanning and reporting for web applications. |
| AppSec Advanced | Foundation, Growth, Scale | Enables vulnerability scanning and reporting for web applications. |
| Additional Entities | Growth, Scale | Enables support for more entities. |
