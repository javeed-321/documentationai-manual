The **Scrut - Trello Integration** allows you to create [Trello](https://trello.com/) tickets directly from Scrut. This integration enhances your workflow by allowing you to create and assign tasks to team members without switching to your Trello environment. This article provides a step-by-step guide for integrating your Trello account with your Scrut account.

## How to Connect Trello With Scrut

### Prerequisites

Before you begin the integration, make sure to have the following permissions for your Trello account:

- **Account information:** This is for Scrut to identify your Trello account details.

- Admin access to Trello

  - **Read access:** To enable Scrut to fetch Trello workspaces, boards, and task details for tracking.
  - **Write access:** To create and update tasks in Trello.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip!**

  We recommend logging into your Trello account before beginning the integration to avoid doing so later.
</Callout>

### Step 1: Navigate to Integrations in Scrut

1. [Log in to your Scrut account](https://app.scrut.io/) and click **Integrations** on the left navigation panel.

2. In the **Categories** section, click **Project Management Platforms**, and click the **Integrations Library** tab on the top.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166134836-rjnvi7jokfo-Trello-Integration-12.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Find the **Trello** card, or use the search bar on the top of the page to find Trello and click the **Integrate** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166136084-4s2m6znck56-Trello-Integration-13.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Step 2: Connect Your Trello Account

1. From the Trello integration page, click the **Connect** button on the top-right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166137397-djzy0u0plz-Trello-Integration-14.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Scrut will redirect you to the Trello authorization page.

   <Callout kind="alert">
     **Heads Up!**

     If you haven’t logged into your Trello account, Scrut will prompt you to login before you can continue.
   </Callout>

3. Click **Allow** to complete the authorization process.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166138638-zpz7i0cmcd-Trello-Integration-15.jpeg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Watch out for the **"Connected Successfully"** message and the **Connected** status indicator.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166139886-h6gx0hcvz9w-Trello-Integration-16.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Step 3: Configure the Scope of Integration

1. From the Trello integration page in Scrut, click the **Configure Scope** button on the top right.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166141155-sr0odfanvdb-Trello-Integration-17.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Select your Trello workspace and the boards you want to connect with Scrut. You can connect multiple Trello workspaces/boards as needed.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166142479-l5kzgahkag-Trello-Integration-18.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click **Save**.

### Step 4: Create a Trello Ticket

Once you have configured the scope of the integration, you can create a Trello ticket from any compliance task module, such as:

- Policies
- Evidence Tasks
- Risk Management
- Tests

Navigate to your required policy/evidence task/risk/automated test, click the three-dots icon on the top-right of the page, and select **Create Trello Ticket**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166143736-ofe2f9eve9-Trello-Integration-19.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Enter the ticket details: Name, Workspace, Board, List, Assignees, and Description.

5. Click **Create**.

## Further Insights

#### **1: How long does it take for a ticket created in Scrut to reflect on my Trello board?**

The action is real-time, and the ticket you create will reflect immediately in Trello.

#### **2: Can I link multiple Trello workspaces to my Scrut account?**

Yes. Click the **Configure Scope** button on the Trello integration page in Scrut, and select multiple workspaces.

#### **3: How do I revoke Scrut's access to my Trello account?**

Click the **Disconnect** button from the Trello integration page to stop sharing data from your Trello account to Scrut.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166146356-ad5l7bytxq5-Trello-Integration-21.jpg?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

#### **4: Is the integration bidirectional?**

No. Currently, the integration is unidirectional, meaning data passes from Scrut to Trello and not the other way around.
