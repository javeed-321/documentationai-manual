With customizable vendor domains, you can tailor the assessment process to precisely fit your organization's requirements.

## Configuring Vendor Questionnaire Domains

1. From the left navigation panel, expand the _Risk_ menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162710673-nf4t6q5a7nc-image-7VUAOC2E.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. Click **Vendors** and go to **Settings**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162599223-yqsel85hcrh-image-18HR7SRJ.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Click **Domains**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162661414-7b7s7yw9x7f-image-4P0O6ZH8.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. By default, users will see 9 pre-configured domains. Users can add more domains by clicking New Domain button.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163045069-zr0voec3v59-image-P647AINM.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Enter domain name and click **Save**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163129583-5yyrp0bqzd7-image-TC4992KF.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
