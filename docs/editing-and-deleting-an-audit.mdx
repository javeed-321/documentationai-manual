## Editing an Audit

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click on the audit you want to edit.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171330704-779s0v0e6cb-Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12-53-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Selecting an audit from the Audit Center" caption=" " />

3. Click the **three-dots** **icon** on the top-right.

4. Click **Edit Audit**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171120395-w0zpdnjrvpp-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11-52-47.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Make the required changes and click **Update**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171123068-5k408o0oj47-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11-54-54.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## Deleting an Audit

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click on the audit you want to delete.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171330704-779s0v0e6cb-Screenshot-2025-09-19-at-12-53-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="Selecting an audit from the Audit Center" caption=" " />

3. Click the **three-dots** **icon** on the top-right.

4. Click **Delete Audit**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788171124441-sioc7zofihr-Screenshot-2025-09-10-at-11-57-22.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Click **Delete** to confirm.
