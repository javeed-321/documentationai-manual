title: "Upload Evidence"
metaDescription: "Understand the three different ways to upload compliance evidence in Scrut."
---

In Scrut, you can upload evidence files to an evidence task in three ways:

1. [**Setting up a Scrut Monitor**](/docs/scrut-monitor) **-** Automatically fetch the required evidence files from your integrations using Scrut Monitor (Scrut’s automated evidence collector)
2. [**As an Attachment**](/docs/uploading-evidence) **-** Manually upload an evidence file
3. [**From Google Drive**](/docs/google-drive-integration-upload-evidences-from-google-drive) **-** Upload the evidence file directly from your Google Drive
