## For Employees

Follow these steps to log in to Scrut’s **Employee Portal**:

### Step 1: Visit the Login Page

Go to [https://app.scrut.io/login](https://app.scrut.io/login).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163770987-qhalqccmmyr-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-10-54-23.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Step 2: Enter Your Email Address

Enter the email address you have registered on Scrut (Ideally, your work email).

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163772344-7yo4f2k8jc7-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-10-54-58.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

### Step 3: Select Your Login Method

On this page, you can view the login methods enabled for your organization. There are two different ways to log in:

#### Via Single Sign-On (SSO)

- Choose the SSO option for the identity provider enabled by your organization.
- You will be redirected to the respective authentication page.
- Confirm the authentication to log in.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163773640-u4m6h8lyufn-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-11-01-18.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

#### Via Verification Code

Alternatively, you can also log in using the one-time password (OTP) sent to your email.

Click **Get Verification Code** to receive an OTP in your email inbox.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163774942-0v9qovfbjlk-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-11-03-59.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Scrut will send a 6-digit OTP to your email. Copy-paste the code and click **Continue**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163776309-bpiy2552ms6-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-11-05-56.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

Once verification is complete, you will be signed in to the Scrut Employee Portal, where you can view the tasks, [policies](/docs/accepting-policies-in-the-employee-portal), and [training](/docs/completing-assigned-security-campaigns) assigned to you.

## For Scrut Users

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788163777565-0vzlf9dqgvi-Screenshot-2025-06-24-at-11-07-34.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

To access the employee portal as a platform user, **click your profile icon** in the top-right corner, then select **Training**. This will take you to the employee portal, where you can view and complete your required training campaigns and accept organizational policies.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162293562-xgrs6l1czl-image-1765949488196.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

To return to Scrut, click **Dashboard** in the top-right corner of the employee portal.
