<Callout kind="plan">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When an auditor reports an audit finding, an assignee has three ways to resolve it:

1. [**Upload an Artifact**](/docs/uploading-the-attachments-for-audit-findings#1-upload-an-artifact): The assignee can upload relevant documentation or evidence directly to the audit finding.
2. [**Link Existing Artifacts**](/docs/uploading-the-attachments-for-audit-findings#2-link-existing-artificats): If relevant documentation is already available in Scrut, the assignee can link it to the finding.
3. [**Create a New Artifact and Link It**](/docs/uploading-the-attachments-for-audit-findings#3-create-a-new-artifact-and-link-it): If no suitable artifacts are available, the assignee can create a new artifact addressing the issue and link it to the finding.

Let’s take a closer look at each of these three options.

## 1: Upload an Artifact

Upload an artifact to associate it with the audit finding.

1. [Log in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/login) and click **Audit → Audit Center** on the left menu.

2. Click on the audit you want to add the artifact for.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788161944221-0nzl3zfnfg4-Click-on-an-audit-in-Audit-Center.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Scroll to the **Findings** tab and click on the finding you want to add artifacts to.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166044931-atpr6q3cdis-Selecting-a-Finding.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Click the **Upload** button in the **Uploaded Artifacts** section.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788166199723-3h6kqq80749-Upload-button-in-Upload-Artifacts---Findings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Upload the relevant artifact file from your system.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164623107-yv6lrk7pkxl-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-16-41-03.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

## 2: Link Existing Artifacts

You can also link an existing artifact to the audit finding.

1. In the **Findings** page, scroll to the **Linked Items** section and click the **Link Existing** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162514112-mnsk1yorm68-Link-Existing-Items-in-Findings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

2. You can link an existing artifact by selecting a relevant **Policy, Evidence,** **Automated Test or Risk**.

3. Select the checkboxes corresponding to the existing policies, evidence, automated tests, or risks you want to link.

4. Click **Link**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164624429-mv0lyj2rhfq-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-16-47-02.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Pro Tip:**

  To unlink artifacts, just click the Unlink button next to the artifact you want to remove.
</Callout>

## 3: Create a New Artifact and Link It

1. In the Findings page, scroll to the **Linked Items** section and click the **Add** button.

2. Choose whether you want to create a new **Policy, Evidence**, or **Risk** and link it to the finding.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162515441-j9o78w6g9jo-Linked-Items-in-Findings.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

3. Enter Policy, Evidence, or Risk details and click **Next**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164625779-ozp0hkpw1di-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-16-59-00.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

4. Select the controls you want to link to the artifact and click **Next**. The selected controls will also be linked to the finding.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164627115-bhlntppss6m-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-16-59-15.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />

5. Verify the details and click **Create**.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164628487-hvppuyh9b9n-Screenshot-2025-11-05-at-17-01-14.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption=" " />
