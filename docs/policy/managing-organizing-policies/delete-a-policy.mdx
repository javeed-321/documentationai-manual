---
title: "Delete a Policy"
description: "Learn how to delete policies in Scrut."
---

<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

To delete one or more policies:

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policies you want to delete.

3. You can select one or more policies, as required.

4. Click the **Delete** button at the top of the table.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-21.21.40.png" alt="" />

5. Click Delete to confirm your action.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-21.23.25.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  Once you delete the policy, it cannot be recovered. Review the selected policies carefully before confirming deletion. If you only want to **delete policy content**, [see here](/docs/policy/export-policies/removing-policy-content).
</Callout>
