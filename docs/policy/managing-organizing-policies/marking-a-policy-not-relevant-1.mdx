<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When a policy previously marked as not relevant becomes applicable or necessary, you can flag it as Relevant. This ensures its inclusion in all compliance-related metrics on Scrut's main dashboard and policy dashboard.

You have two ways to mark a policy as relevant:

- [Individually](/docs/policy/managing-organizing-policies/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1#how-to-mark-a-policy-as-relevant)
- [In Bulk](/docs/policy/managing-organizing-policies/marking-a-policy-not-relevant-1#how-to-mark-multiple-policies-as-relevant)

## How To Mark a Policy as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policy that you want to mark as relevant.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-26-at-13.09.40.png" alt="Selecting a policy from the All Policies tab" />

3. Once you’re on the policy details page, click the **three-dots icon** at the top right and select “**Mark as Relevant**.”

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-4.png" alt="Three-dots menu icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.58.06.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.58.23.png" alt="" />

## How to Mark Multiple Policies as Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Policies** tab and select the policies you want to mark as relevant.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the policy table.

   <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.29.53.png" alt="" />

4. In the Edit drawer, scroll to the **Relevance** dropdown and choose “**Relevant**.”

5. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.58.44.png" alt="" />
