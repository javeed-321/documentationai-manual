<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When a policy is no longer applicable or necessary, you can flag it as "not relevant." Policies marked as “Not Relevant” are excluded from all compliance-related metrics in Scrut's main dashboard and policy dashboard.

You have two ways to mark a policy as not relevant:

- [Individually](/docs/policy/managing-organizing-policies/marking-a-policy-not-relevant#how-to-mark-a-policy-as-not-relevant)
- [In Bulk](/docs/policy/managing-organizing-policies/marking-a-policy-not-relevant#how-to-mark-multiple-policies-as-not-relevant)

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Heads Up!**

  You can mark a policy as “Not Relevant,” regardless of its status; whether it's Not Uploaded, Draft, Needs Review, or Published.
</Callout>

## How To Mark a Policy as Not Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policy that you want to mark as not relevant.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-09-26-at-13.09.40.png" alt="Selecting a policy from the All Policies tab" />

3. Once you’re on the policy details page, click the **three-dots icon** at the top right and select “**Mark as Not Relevant**.”

   <Image src="/images/Three-dots-icon-4.png" alt="Three-dots menu icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.27.56.png" alt="" />

4. Enter a justification and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.28.40.png" alt="" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.28.47.png" alt="" />

## How to Mark Multiple Policies as Not Relevant

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **Policies** tab and select the policies you want to mark as not relevant.

3. Click the **Edit icon** at the top of the policy table.

   <Image src="/images/Pencil-icon-1.png" alt="Edit icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.29.53.png" alt="" />

4. In the Edit drawer, scroll to the **Relevance** dropdown and choose “**Not Relevant**.”

5. Enter a justification and click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2025-10-16-at-16.30.24.png" alt="" />
