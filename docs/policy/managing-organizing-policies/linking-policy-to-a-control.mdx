  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut allows you to seamlessly connect your organizational policies with corresponding controls, streamlining your compliance efforts and ensuring a robust adherence to industry standards.

## How To Link/Unlink Controls to a Policy

1. [Sign in to Scrut](https://app.scrut.io/) and go to **Compliance → Policies** using the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab and click on the policy to which you want to link or unlink controls.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164115137-n1xwgn9rzd-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-13-10-28.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scroll to the **Controls & Requirements** tab, and click the**Link/Unlink Controls** button.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164122260-b0gi74c8fw7-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-14-06-18.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Use the **Frameworks** panel to view controls relevant to your frameworks.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164116612-9jc8repv0q7-Screenshot-2025-08-22-at-13-12-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. Expand a specific requirement to see all controls associated with it.

   - When you select a requirement, all controls within it will be selected by default.
   - If needed, you can then select/unselect specific controls as required.

6. Select the checkboxes next to the controls you want to link.

   **Note:** Any requirement that is not applicable will appear greyed out.

7. Use the search bar at the top to quickly locate controls by name or code.

8. Once you've made the selections, enable the Show Linked Controls toggle to review and verify which controls you have chosen to link.

9. Click **Save** to confirm your selections.
