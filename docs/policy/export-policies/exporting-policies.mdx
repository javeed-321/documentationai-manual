In this article, we cover how to export:

- [Policies for a Framework](/docs/policy/export-policies/exporting-policies#how-to-export-policies-for-a-framework)
- [Policy Attachments](/docs/policy/export-policies/exporting-policies#how-to-export-policy-attachments)

## How To Export Policies for a Framework

1. Sign in to Scrut, and click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab.

3. Click the **Framework filter** at the top of the page and select the frameworks for which you want to export the associated policies.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788162173466-byu75mk7c3v-image-1761111496690.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Use the Columns field to select the columns to include in the export.

5. Click the **Export** button at the top of the policy table.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164507109-0trn91k46so-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-08-28.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. Scrut will download the file in the specified location. Check the downloads folder or recent download history on your browser.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164508420-c5lw5e6vekv-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-08-53.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164509780-lrqo68vv3l9-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-09-58.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" caption="Sample Policy Export" />

## How to Export Policy Attachments

Follow these steps to download only policies with attachments:

1. Go to the **All Policies** tab and select the policies for which you want to export the attached documents.

2. Click the **Export Attachments** button at the top of the Policies table.

   **Note:** The Export Attachments button becomes available only when you select at least one policy with an attachment.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164511390-c6ypkejdzdi-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-10-29.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. Scrut will email you the attachments.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164512738-p2gxv5kas7b-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-10-47.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788164514082-6wzdifob4nc-Screenshot-2025-10-22-at-11-10-55.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  - The download link sent to your email is valid for 7 days.
  - Scrut will automatically organize the attachments into folders based on framework requirements.
  - Only attachments are downloaded. If a policy has no attachments, it will not be included in the export.
</Callout>
