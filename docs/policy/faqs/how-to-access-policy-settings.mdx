---
title: "Access Policy Settings"
description: "Learn how to access policy settings."
---

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel.

2. Click the **Settings** icon on the top-right of the Policy module.

   <Image src="/images/settings.png" alt="Settings icon" />

   <Image src="/images/Policy-Settings-icon.png" alt="" />

3. This brings you to the **Policy Settings** page, where you can configure policy settings as required.

<Image src="/images/Policy-Settings.png" alt="" />

#### Audit Log

The Audit Log in the Policy Settings page captures all user actions related to policy settings.
