<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

When you upload a policy to Scrut, **Scrut Teammates AI** automatically reviews the document and recommends control mappings based on its content. You can review each suggestion and adjust mappings manually before saving. This works with both standard Scrut controls and any custom controls you've configured.

## Before You Begin

- Ensure that [Scrut Teammates is enabled for your account](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/scrut-ai#how-to-enable-scrut-teammates).

## How It Works

1. Click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel, and either:

   - **Upload a new policy:** Suggestions run automatically after the upload completes.
   - **Open an existing policy:** Navigate to the policy's mapping view and trigger suggestions from there.

2. Scrut Teammates analyzes the document and surfaces recommended control mappings. **AI suggestions load automatically.**

3. Click **Update Controls** to view AI-recommended controls for your policy.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165270405-u1a7y5sqm8s-Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-09-51-35.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

4. Heads Up! It might take a few minutes for the AI suggestions to load.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165288108-zr9jzfq2j3d-Screenshot-2026-03-06-at-18-44-07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

5. You can accept, reject, or adjust mappings as needed.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165271788-5n0mv1k3dpw-Screenshot-2026-03-03-at-09-52-07.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

6. **Save** your final mappings.

7. \[Optional\] Click **Ask Teammates** to initiate a contextual conversation on what to do next.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Click **Refresh** to trigger the AI analysis again.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Does this work with custom controls?" default-open="true">
    Yes. Scrut Teammates can identify any gaps for both standard Scrut controls and any custom controls you've created.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I don’t agree with a suggestion?" default-open="true">
    You’re in full control. Click Ignore to discard an AI suggestion. Teammates will not resurface it again.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
