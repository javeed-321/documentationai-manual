<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Scrut Teammates proactively evaluates your uploaded policies and highlights specific gaps in the policy content. It surfaces gaps based on:

- Missing coverage for specific control requirements
- Outdated or incomplete policy language
- Sections that need to be added or revised to meet framework expectations

The guidance reflects your current audit period and framework context, so recommendations stay relevant as your compliance scope changes.

## Before You Begin

- Ensure that [Scrut Teammates is enabled for your account](/docs/settings/organization-info-1/scrut-ai#how-to-enable-scrut-teammates).

## How Does It Work?

1. Click **Compliance → Policies** on the left navigation panel, and open any policy.

2. Scrut Teammates analyzes the document and surfaces any gaps in policy content. AI suggestions load automatically.

   <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-be800fa3-4146-42bb-a54c-66f35fe883a5/doc-4eeb4bca-def5-4b53-9be4-c510946b0c05/1788165237891-shj8ocp2rb-Screenshot-2026-02-27-at-19-54-08.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" alt="" />

3. **Review each suggestion.** For each suggestion, you can:

   - **Accept:** Make the recommended change to the policy content, then click **Mark as Done**.
   - **Reject:** Dismiss the suggestion if it doesn't apply by clicking **Ignore**.

4. Click **Ask Teammates** to initiate a contextual conversation on how to fix the gap.

<Callout kind="info">
  **Pro Tip!**

  Click **Refresh** to trigger the AI analysis again.
</Callout>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Are the gap suggestions tailored to my organization’s policies?" default-open="true">
    Yes, suggestions are customized to reflect your specific framework and audit period.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will auditors see the AI-detected gaps?" default-open="true">
    No. The AI-powered guidance is not visible to auditors when they view your account in Scrut.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do gap suggestions update automatically when I upload a new policy version?" default-open="true">
    Yes. When you open a policy, Scrut Teammates automatically re-evaluates the current version and refreshes the gaps shown in the panel.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Are the gaps shown exhaustive?" default-open="true">
    The gaps panel highlights the most significant areas for improvement based on control alignment. It is intended to guide you, not replace a full compliance review.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I trigger the gap analysis for all policies?" default-open="true">
    Click the **Run AI Gap Analysis** button at the top of the All Policies page to run the gap analysis for all policies at once.
