<Callout kind="info">
  **Who can use this feature**

  - Supported on **Foundation, Growth,** and **Scale** plans
</Callout>

Once a policy has been created, the next step is to add the policy content. You can do it in one of the following ways:

1. [Create a Blank Policy](/docs/policy/working-with-policies/uploading-policy#1-create-a-blank-policy)
2. [Upload Policy Document](/docs/policy/working-with-policies/uploading-policy#2-upload-policy-document)
3. [Link an Existing Policy](/docs/policy/working-with-policies/uploading-policy#3-link-an-existing-policy)

## #1: Create a Blank Policy

**When to use:** Use this method to draft policies from scratch or customize Scrut's policy templates to suit your organizational requirements.

1. Sign in to Scrut, click **Compliance → Policies** via the left navigation panel.

2. Go to the **All Policies** tab.

3. Click on a policy that you want to work on.

   <Image src="/images/Selecting-an-Individual-Policy.png" alt="" />

4. Select **Create Policy** to launch the [policy editor](/docs/policy/editing-policy-content/how-to-use-policy-editor).

   <Image src="/images/Create-Upload-Link-Policy.png" alt="" />

5. Scrut provides starter templates to help you get started. If a template is available, it will open in the editor. If not, you can start from scratch.

6. Use the editor to draft and format the policy content, adding headings, paragraphs, lists, and other elements as needed.

   <Image src="/images/Policy-Editor.png" alt="" />

<Callout kind="alert">
  **Note:**

  The policy remains in **Draft** state. It must be approved and published so that associated control status changes to compliant.
</Callout>

## #2: Upload Policy Document

**When to use:** Choose this method when you have an existing policy document you want to bring into Scrut, either by uploading a file from your computer or by syncing directly from an integrated tool such as Google Drive, SharePoint, or Confluence.

1. Click **Upload Policy Document**.

   <Image src="/images/Create-Upload-Link-Policy.png" alt="" />

2. Choose one of the following options:

   - **Upload from Computer:** Upload a DOC, DOCX, or PDF file directly from your device. Compatible files can be opened in the Scrut policy editor; others are saved as attachments.

   - **Sync via Integration:** Sync a policy document from Confluence, SharePoint, or Google Drive. Select the integration from the **Integration type** dropdown, then click **Connect** to authenticate and browse your files.

     <Callout kind="alert">
       **Heads Up!**

       - When you sync **policies from Confluence or SharePoint**, you can **add files as attachments only**. These files are not editable in the policy editor.
       - Google Drive is the only integration that allows you to import a compatible file straight into the editor (or choose attachment instead). For more information, see [Import a Policy from Google Drive](/docs/policy/working-with-policies/import-a-policy-from-google-drive).
     </Callout>

3. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Upload-Policy-Document.png" alt="" />

## #3: Link an Existing Policy

**When to use:** Use this method when your policy document is hosted externally, and you want to reference it in Scrut via a URL. Linked policies are added as attachments and **cannot be edited in Scrut**.

1. Click **Link an Existing Policy**.

   <Image src="/images/Create-Upload-Link-Policy.png" alt="" />

2. Paste the URL of the external policy document in the link field.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-19.44.08.png" alt="" />

3. Click **Save**.

   <Image src="/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-19.44.25.png" alt="" />
