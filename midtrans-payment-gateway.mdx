## Overview

[Midtrans Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/midtrans-payment-gateway/) is an extension for WP Travel Engine that lets tour operators accept trip and tour payments through [Midtrans](https://midtrans.com/), one of Indonesia's popular merchant services.

<Callout kind="info">
  Midtrans Payment Gateway requires [WP Travel Engine](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-travel-engine/) v3.0.6 or later.
</Callout>

## Requirements

You must have an active merchant account with Midtrans to use this payment gateway. You'll need:

- A Merchant ID
- A Client Key
- A Server Key

Only payments in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) can be accepted.

## Installation

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Download the extension">
    Download the extension from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin folder">
    Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin">
    Activate **WP Travel Engine – Midtrans Payment Gateway** from the Plugins menu within WordPress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the redirection URLs

The redirection URL is used to redirect your customer after the payment process through Midtrans is complete.

<Callout kind="info">
  Go to the [Midtrans MAP portal](https://docs.midtrans.com/en/welcome/preparation.html#step4) to configure and save your redirection URLs on the Settings dashboard.
</Callout>

## Configure the gateway

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open Payment settings">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Midtrans">
    You'll see Midtrans as an option to enable the Midtrans Payment Gateway for trip bookings. Click **Midtrans** to configure its settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your merchant credentials">
    Enter your Merchant ID, Client Key, and Server Key. You can find these credentials on the Midtrans MAP Dashboard under **Settings > Access Keys**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the currency to IDR">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Miscellaneous > Currency** and choose Indonesian Rupiah as the Currency Code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField name="Merchant ID" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your Midtrans Merchant ID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField name="Client Key" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your Midtrans Client Key.
</ParamField>

<ParamField name="Server Key" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your Midtrans Server Key.
</ParamField>

Once configured, your customers can pay through Midtrans using any of its available payment methods.
