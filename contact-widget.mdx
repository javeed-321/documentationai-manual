## Overview

The Contact widget lets you display contact information on your pages using Elementor. It supports multiple contact entries, each with its own title, icon, text, and link.

## Contact settings

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122425_025402_PM.jpg" alt="Contact widget settings in Elementor" width="291" height="658" />

### Contact list

Each contact entry in the list supports the following fields:

- **Title** – the contact name or designation, for example `Email`
- **Text** – the display text for the contact, for example `hello@yoursite.com`
- **Link** – a URL or email link for the contact, for example `mailto:hello@yoursite.com`
- **Icon** – an optional icon shown next to the contact; you can disable it, upload a custom icon, or choose one from the Elementor icon library
- **Duplicate button** – creates a copy of the current contact item, including all its settings

### Add item

Click **Add Item** to create a new contact in the list. Each new item has its own title, text, link, and icon.

### Contact direction

Choose how the contact items are arranged on the page:

- **Row (Horizontal)** – items are placed side by side
- **Column (Vertical)** – items are stacked vertically

### Content direction

Configure the alignment of the content within each contact item:

- **Row (Horizontal)** – the icon and text appear side by side
- **Column (Vertical)** – the icon appears above the text

## Troubleshooting missing icons

<Callout kind="alert">
  If the Elementor library icons don't display, enable Font Awesome 4 support.
</Callout>

<Steps title-type="h4">
  <Step title="Open Elementor advanced settings">
    Go to **Elementor > Settings > Advanced**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Font Awesome 4 support">
    Enable **Load Font Awesome 4 Support**.

  </Step>
</Steps>
