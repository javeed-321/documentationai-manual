Trip Info is the set of quick facts that appears on each of your trip pages. It gives travelers an at-a-glance summary of important details like destinations, schedules, accommodations, and activities.

Think of it as the part of the trip page that answers the first questions people usually have. If you run an Annapurna trek, a traveler may want to know the route, how many days the trip takes, where they stay, what activities are included, and whether permits are needed before they book.

## What you'll set up here

**Predefined fields** are the Trip Info fields that come with the plugin. A plugin is an add-on that gives your WordPress website extra features. You can rename these fields, choose a different icon, and turn them on or off. You cannot delete them, and that is by design so they stay available whenever you need them.

**Custom fields** are the ones you create yourself. You can use them for details that matter to your trips, such as **Max Altitude** for multi-day treks or **Permits Needed** for restricted areas. You can rename, reorder, turn off, or delete custom fields at any time.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  These settings apply to all trip pages at once. To add the actual Trip Info values on a specific trip, see [How to Add Trip Info](https://docs.wptravelengine.com/article/trip-info/).
</Callout>

## Finding the Trip Info settings

This is where you control the Trip Info fields that will be available across your site. Once you find this area, you can adjust the built-in fields or add your own.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Before you start: make sure WP Travel Engine is installed and active on your site. A plugin is an add-on that extends what your website can do.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the WP Travel Engine settings" title-type="p">
    A WordPress dashboard is the main admin area where you manage your website. You reach it by signing in to your site, usually at `/wp-admin`.

    On the left sidebar of your WordPress dashboard, find **WP Travel Engine** and click **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the General tab" title-type="p">
    At the top of the settings page, click **General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the Trip Info section" title-type="p">
    Scroll down to the **Trip Settings** area. Then look for **Trip Info**.

## Adding a custom Trip Info field

Custom fields help you show the details that matter most for your trips. For example, if you run multi-day treks, you might want to add a **Max Altitude** field so travelers can check the highest point before they book.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the new field" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Info**. A new blank row will appear.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name it" title-type="p">
    Type the name of the field, such as **Max Altitude** or **Permits Needed**. This name is the label travelers will see on the trip page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick an icon" title-type="p">
    Choose an **Icon** from the dropdown list. An icon is the small picture shown next to the field name.

    Pick something that matches the field. For example, a mountain-style icon works well for altitude, while a document-style icon fits permit information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an input type" title-type="p">
    Select the **input type** you want to use. Input type means the format you will use later when entering the value for each trip.

    Most of the time, a text-based option works well. If you want to add something like **Airport pickup included: Yes**, choose the type that best fits that kind of answer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it on or off" title-type="p">
    Use the toggle switch to enable or disable the field. A toggle switch is the small slider that flips between on and off.

    Turn it on if you want the field available on your trip pages. Turn it off if you want to hide it for now.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reorder if needed" title-type="p">
    Drag the field up or down to change its position. The order here controls the order travelers see on the trip page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes" title-type="p">
    Scroll to the bottom and click **Save Changes**.

  To remove a custom field, click its **Delete** icon. This only works for custom fields. Predefined fields cannot be deleted, but you can turn them off.
</Callout>
