description: >-
  Resolve a 404 error on the trip search result page by switching your WordPress
  Permalink structure to Post name.
type: guide
---
If your search result page returns a **404 error**, this is typically caused by the permalink structure set in WordPress.

## Solution

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open your Permalink settings">
    Go to **Settings → Permalinks** in your WordPress admin dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to the Post name structure">
    If your permalink structure is set to **Plain** or another option, switch it to **Post name**.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/permalinks-set-1024x518.png" width="1024" height="518" alt="WordPress Permalink settings page with the Post name option selected" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If **Post name** is already selected, click **Save Changes** anyway and check whether the issue persists. Re-saving the permalink structure regenerates your site's rewrite rules, which often resolves the error on its own.
</Callout>
