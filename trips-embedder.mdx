## Show trips inside your content

Trips Embedder lets you place selected trips inside your WordPress posts and pages. You can also show those trips on another WordPress website, such as a partner site or affiliate site.

A plugin, also called an add-on, is a small tool you add to WordPress to give it more features. Trips Embedder is the add-on that adds trip embeds to your site.

The add-on uses a shortcode when you insert trips into content. A shortcode is a small line of text you paste into a WordPress page or post so WordPress can display the feature there.

Here is a common example. You might write a blog post about trekking in Nepal and want to show your Annapurna trek inside that post so readers can book it without leaving the page.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The Trips Embedder add-on requires [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/) plugin v2.2.4 or later installed and activated on your website.
</Callout>

## Install the add-on

What you will need before you start: the `wp-travel-engine-trips-embedder.zip` file and access to your WordPress dashboard.

### Upload from your computer

This is the best option for most site owners. You will upload the plugin file from your computer into WordPress.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Plugins area" title-type="p">
    You need to start where WordPress manages installed plugins.

    In your WordPress dashboard, look at the left sidebar and go to **Plugins**. Then click **Add New**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Upload Plugin" title-type="p">
    This opens the area where you can upload the zip file.

    Click **Upload Plugin** near the top of the screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the plugin file" title-type="p">
    WordPress needs the original zip file for installation.

    Select `wp-travel-engine-trips-embedder.zip` from your computer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the plugin" title-type="p">
    This copies the add-on into your WordPress site.

    Click **Install Now**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin" title-type="p">
    Activated means the add-on is turned on and ready to use.

    Click **Activate Plugin** after installation finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Upload via FTP

FTP is a method for transferring files to your website server directly. Use this method if the plugin file is too large for the normal upload in WordPress.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the plugin zip file" title-type="p">
    You need the add-on file on your computer before you can upload it.

    Download `wp-travel-engine-trips-embedder.zip`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Extract the plugin folder" title-type="p">
    FTP uploads the folder, not the zip file.

    Extract the `wp-travel-engine-trips-embedder` directory to your computer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the folder to your site" title-type="p">
    This places the add-on inside WordPress's plugin folder.

    Upload the `wp-travel-engine-trips-embedder` directory to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin in WordPress" title-type="p">
    The upload only adds the files. Activation turns the add-on on.

    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins** on the left sidebar and activate the plugin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once activation is complete, the add-on is ready to use. The next step is connecting your license key so you can receive updates.

## Understand where Trips Embedder works

Trips Embedder works from the two main places where you write content in WordPress. You can embed trips in either a grid layout or a list layout from both editors.

The Classic Editor is the older text editor that comes with WordPress. The Gutenberg editor, also called the block editor, is the newer default editor in WordPress.

A block is a building block of content in the Gutenberg editor, like a paragraph, image, or embed. Once you know which editor you use, the setup feels much more straightforward.

## Embed trips in the Classic Editor

### Insert trips into a post or page

What you will need before you start: a post or page open in the Classic Editor. The Classic Editor is the older text editor in WordPress.

While editing your post or page, click one of the Trips Embedder buttons in the editor toolbar. This opens a popup window where you choose which trips to show.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787033316671-0y98ajrt42t-trips-embedder-classic-editor.trips-embedder-classic-editor?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1440" height="747" alt="" />

The popup has four tabs:

- **Trips** - shows individual trips
- **Destinations** - shows trips from a specific destination, such as all trips in Nepal
- **Activities** - shows trips that match an activity, such as trekking or rafting
- **Trip Type** - shows trips of a certain type, such as day tours or multi-day packages

The add-on inserts a shortcode based on the tab you are using.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Since Trips Embedder inserts different shortcodes for each tab, trips are shown based on the settings and selection in the active tab only, one at a time.
</Callout>

You can choose multiple trips, destinations, activities, or trip types and add them to your selection.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787033386672-1862xjhe4mk-trips-embedder-adding-in-trips.trips-embedder-adding-in-trips?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="550" alt="" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add items to the selection area" title-type="p">
    This builds the set of trips you want readers to see.

    After choosing the trips or terms you want, click **Add to selected trips** to move them to the selection area.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reorder or remove items" title-type="p">
    This helps you control the order readers see first.

    Drag items to reorder them. Click the cross icon beside a title to remove it.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787033449139-vc2z6x6ybso-trips-embedder-sorting-dragging-trips.trips-embedder-sorting-dragging-trips?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="531" alt="" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once your selection looks right, you can fine-tune how it appears.

### Search for trips by name

If you have many trips, the search bar saves time. It helps you find a trip by name instead of scrolling through a long list.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787033480245-ic4yxmhi6xm-trips-embedder-search-trip-name.trips-embedder-search-trip-name?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="497" alt="" />

This is especially useful when your site has many trek variations, seasonal departures, or similar day tours. Next, choose how those results should look on the page.

### Choose a grid or list layout

Below the selection area, choose either a grid or list layout. Grid shows trips in cards side by side. List shows them stacked one below the other.

Grid is selected by default. Grid works well when you want to show three or four trips in a blog post sidebar. List works better when you want more detail for each trip in the main content area.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787033514929-s8y7q4rvrtd-trips-embedder-layout-options.trips-embedder-layout-options?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="918" height="524" alt="" />

After choosing your trips and layout, click **Insert** to place them in your post. WordPress will place the shortcode into the editor for you.

### Control how many trips appear

The **Destinations**, **Activities**, and **Trip Types** tabs include an extra field called **No. of posts**. This field controls how many trips will appear from your selection.

For example, if you select the destination Nepal and set the field to 6, only 6 Nepal trips will appear. If you leave the field blank, WordPress uses its default number of posts.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787034145680-4yh4raw7zw9-trips-embedder-postsnumber.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="703" height="652" alt="" />

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Use **No. of posts** when you want to keep a section short and focused. For example, you might show 4 day tours on a city guide page or 6 multi-day packages on a seasonal promotion page.
</Callout>

That is all you need for the Classic Editor. If you use the newer WordPress editor, use the Gutenberg blocks instead.

## Embed trips in Gutenberg

The add-on also works in the Gutenberg editor. Gutenberg is the newer WordPress editor and is also called the block editor.

A block is a building block of content in Gutenberg. Trips Embedder adds two blocks that let you place trip listings inside a post or page while you are creating content.

## Embed trips on external websites

This feature lets you show your trips on another WordPress website. That could be your second website or a partner or affiliate site.

The other website does not need WP Travel Engine installed. Trips Embedder turns your selection into an iframe. An iframe is a small window inside a web page that shows content from another website.

What you will need before you start: the add-on must be active on your main site where your trips are stored.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new external embed" title-type="p">
    This sets up the trip display that another website will use.

    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine** on the left sidebar. Then open **External Embeds** and click **Add New**.

    Enter a title. Then set the width and height for how the trips will display on the other website.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787034563019-qkh7rlktbk-trips-embedder-external.trips-embedder-external?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="552" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the embed code and choose what to show" title-type="p">
    This is where you decide which trips people will see.

    Click **Create Embed Code**. Choose trips, destinations, activities, or trip types and add them to your selection.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787034602571-ajwng37cawn-trips-embedder-add.trips-embedder-add?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="552" alt="" />

    After choosing items, click **Add to selected trips** to move them to the selection area. Drag items to reorder them. Click the cross icon beside a title to remove an item.

    Use the search bar if you want to find trips by name instead of scrolling.
