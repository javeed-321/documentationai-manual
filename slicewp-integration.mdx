## Run an affiliate program for your trips

Affiliate marketing lets other people help sell your trips. An **affiliate** is a partner who promotes your trips in exchange for a cut of the sales they bring in.

With the SliceWP Integration add-on, you can connect WP Travel Engine to the SliceWP plugin and track those partner sales inside your WordPress site. For example, a travel blogger could share your 7-day trek on their website, and when someone books through that link, SliceWP records the sale and the commission owed to that partner.

This setup helps you grow beyond your own website traffic. You can see which partners are sending bookings, how much they have earned, and when payouts need to be made. Once the connection is in place, the rest of the setup is mostly about adding people and giving them the right links.

## What you'll need before you start

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Before you begin, make sure you have all of these ready:

  - The **SliceWP** plugin installed and activated on your WordPress site.
  - The **SliceWP Integration** add-on installed and activated with a valid license key.
  - At least one trip already created in **WP Travel Engine**.
</Callout>

If those pieces are ready, you can turn on the connection and start setting up your partners.

## Installing the SliceWP Integration add-on

Install and activate the add-on before you move to the setup steps below.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  See [how to activate a Pro add-on license key](/article/activate-pro-addon-license-key/) for the full installation and activation walkthrough.
</Callout>

Once the add-on is active, you can connect WP Travel Engine with SliceWP.

## Step 1: Turn on the WP Travel Engine connection in SliceWP

This step tells SliceWP to start watching for bookings that come through affiliate links. Without it, commissions will not be tracked.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SliceWP settings" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **SliceWP** on the left sidebar. Then open **Settings** and click **Integration**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find WP Travel Engine in the list" title-type="p">
    Look through the available integrations until you see **WP Travel Engine**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the connection" title-type="p">
    Turn on the toggle for **WP Travel Engine**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1786957939053-wkwbfd28wu-slicewp-integration-settings.slicewp-integration-settings?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="882" height="796" alt="" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check that tracking is ready" title-type="p">
    Once the toggle is on, SliceWP can track commissions for trips sold through affiliates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Now that the connection is active, you can add the people who will promote your trips.

## Step 2: Create a user account for your affiliate

In WordPress, every person who logs in needs a user account. You will create one for each affiliate before adding them inside SliceWP.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add New User screen" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Users** on the left sidebar and click **Add New**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the user's details" title-type="p">
    Fill in the required fields such as **Username**, **Email**, and **Password**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the Affiliate role" title-type="p">
    In the **Role** dropdown, select **Affiliate**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register the user as an affiliate" title-type="p">
    Check **Register User as Affiliate** so the new user is also added to SliceWP.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the new user" title-type="p">
    Click **Add New User** to finish.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1786957976334-ccs2b94eioa-slicewp-integration-add-new-user.slicewp-integration-add-new-user?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="575" alt="" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  You can also check SliceWP's own instructions for [creating an affiliate registration page](https://slicewp.com/docs/adding-affiliate-registration-page/), or manually add an existing user as an affiliate directly from the SliceWP dashboard.
</Callout>

If you run trips with local guides, bloggers, or hotel partners, this is the point where each person gets their own login. Next, you will turn that user into an active affiliate record inside SliceWP.

## Step 3: Add the affiliate in SliceWP

This step creates the affiliate profile that SliceWP uses for tracking and payouts. Think of it as the working record behind the person's WordPress user account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Affiliates area" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **SliceWP** on the left sidebar and click **Affiliates**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new affiliate profile" title-type="p">
    Click **Add New**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the affiliate details" title-type="p">
    In the Affiliate Details section:

    - Select the **User** from the dropdown.
    - Enter the **Payment Email** for commission payouts.
    - You can also add the **Website** if they have one.
    - Add a short note about how they plan to promote your trips.
    - Set **Status** to **Active**.
    - Turn on **Send Welcome Email** if you want SliceWP to notify them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the affiliate profile" title-type="p">
    Click **Add Affiliate** to save the profile.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1786958015662-cg7iyr250zp-slicewp-integration-affiliate-user.slicewp-integration-affiliate-user?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="969" height="801" alt="" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the affiliate is active, they are ready to promote your trips. The next part is giving them the correct links.

## Step 4: Share affiliate links for your trips

Affiliate links are how SliceWP knows which partner sent the booking. Each affiliate gets a unique Affiliate ID, and that ID is added to the end of a trip URL.

For example, if a blogger is promoting your 3-day city tour on Instagram or in a trip roundup on their website, they should share the trip link with their own Affiliate ID attached. When someone clicks that link and books, the sale is credited to that affiliate.

**Example of an affiliate link:**

```typescript
https://example.site/trip/5-days-tour-to-philippines/?aff=2
```

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The number 2 at the end of the URL represents the Affiliate ID. The affiliate must replace it with their own Affiliate ID.
</Callout>

Once your affiliates are using these links, SliceWP can begin recording the bookings they bring in.

## Step 5: Track and manage commissions

A **commission** is the amount you owe an affiliate for a booking they helped bring in. This is where you can keep an eye on sales and see what needs to be paid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Commissions screen" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **SliceWP** on the left sidebar and click **Commissions**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the commission list" title-type="p">
    You will see all recorded commissions in one place. The list shows the affiliate name, commission amount, reference number or booking ID, type such as sale or refund, date, and status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A quick check here can tell you which partners are performing well. For example, you might notice that a travel blogger is driving more bookings for your monsoon-season rafting package than for your winter treks.

**Understanding commission status**

| Status   | Meaning                                                               |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending  | The trip has been referred but not yet purchased.                     |
| Unpaid   | The trip has been purchased, but the commission hasn't been paid yet. |
| Paid     | The commission has been processed and given to the affiliate.         |
| Rejected | The trip was canceled, and the commission was not approved.           |

### Checking booking details from the commission page

If you want to confirm where a commission came from, you can open the related booking from the commission list. This is useful when you need to check details for a group booking, a discounted seasonal package, or a trip that included add-ons like gear rental.

In the **Commissions** section, booking reference numbers appear under the **Reference** column. Click a **Reference** link to open the booking details.

## Step 6: Pay your affiliates

This is the final part of the workflow. After you review unpaid commissions, you can process payouts and mark those records as completed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payouts screen" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **SliceWP** on the left sidebar and click **Payouts**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the unpaid commissions" title-type="p">
    Select the unpaid commissions you want to process.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Process the payment" title-type="p">
    Click **Process Payment**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mark the commissions as paid" title-type="p">
    After the payment is complete, mark those commissions as **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
