## Overview

WP Travel Engine's **Bookings** section is where you view, add, edit, and export every trip booking made on your site. This guide covers all three core workflows: reviewing existing bookings, adding a booking manually for an offline customer, and exporting booking data for reporting.

## View all bookings

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Bookings list">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Bookings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the booking list">
    Each row shows the traveler's **name**, **booking status** (Pending, Booked, Canceled, etc.), **booking date**, **trip date**, **trip name**, **total paid**, **remaining payment**, and **total cost**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Narrow results with filters">
    Use the **Filters** above the list to search for bookings by trip name, booking status, or date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit a booking's details

If a booking needs a correction — a payment update, a traveler detail, or a status change — open it directly from the list.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Find the booking">
    Locate the booking you need to update in the Bookings list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Edit">
    Click the **Edit** button on that booking's row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update and save">
    Adjust the necessary information, then save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a manual booking

Use a manual booking when a customer books offline or through a direct inquiry — for example, over the phone or by email — and you need to record it in WP Travel Engine yourself.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Start a new booking">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Bookings** and click **Add New Booking**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in each tab of booking details">
    Work through the tabs described below, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Screenshot_031725_094244_AM-1024x383.jpg" width="1024" height="383" alt="Add New Booking form with tabs for trip, traveler, and payment details" />

### What each tab covers

<Tabs tabs="Trip Details,Traveller(s) Details,Emergency Contact,Payment Details,Billing,Your Notes,Booking Summary">
  <Tab>
    Choose the booked trip from the dropdown, then set the **Booked Date**, trip **Start Date** and **End Date**, **Trip Code** (if applicable), number of travelers, and package name.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Add each traveler's **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Country**. Click **+ Add Traveler** to add more than one traveler to the booking.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Enter emergency contact details — **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Relationship**, **Phone**, and **Country**. Click **Add Emergency Contact** to add more than one contact.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Record the **Payment ID**, **Status**, **Payment Gateway**, **Amount**, **Currency**, **Transaction ID**, and **Gateway Response** for the booking's payment.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Enter billing information — **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Address**, **City**, and **Country**.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Add any internal notes about the booking for future reference. These notes aren't shown to the traveler.
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    Set the **Pricing Category**, **Subtotal**, **Discount**, **Fees**, **Amount Paid**, and **Due Amount** for the booking.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Once every tab is filled in, click **Save** in the top-right corner to confirm the booking. It will then appear in your main Bookings list like any other reservation.
</Callout>

## Export bookings

Export booking data to CSV when you need to report on bookings outside WP Travel Engine — for accounting, analysis, or sharing with a team member.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Bookings list">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Bookings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the export dialog">
    Click **Export Bookings** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your filters">
    In the popup, choose a **Date** range, a specific **Trip Name**, and/or a **Booking Status** (Pending, Booked, Canceled, etc.) to narrow what gets exported.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the file">
    Click **Export** to download the filtered booking data as a CSV file.
  </Step>
</Steps>
