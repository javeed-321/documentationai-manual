## Overview

[PayU Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/payu-biz-payment-gateway/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) is an extension for WP Travel Engine that lets you accept trip and tour payments through PayU, one of the leading financial services in global growth markets.

PayU Payment Gateway supports payment methods like credit card, debit card, internet banking, and PayPal. Travelers can make card payments as well as direct payments through any associated bank.

<Callout kind="info">
  PayU Payment Gateway requires the [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) plugin to be installed.
</Callout>

## Requirements

You must have an active merchant account with [PayU](https://www.payumoney.com/) to use this payment gateway. You'll need:

- A PayU Merchant Key
- A PayU Merchant Salt

## Installation

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Download the extension">
    Download the extension from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin folder">
    Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin">
    Activate **WP Travel Engine – PayU Payment Gateway** from the Plugins menu within WordPress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the gateway

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open Payment settings">
    Go to **Trips > Settings > Payments**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select PayU">
    You'll see PayU as an option to enable the PayU Payment Gateway for trip bookings. Click **PayU** to configure its settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your merchant credentials">
    Enter your Merchant Key and Merchant Salt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField name="Merchant Key" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your PayU Merchant Key.
</ParamField>

<ParamField name="Merchant Salt" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your PayU Merchant Salt.
</ParamField>

Once configured, when a traveler books a trip through the PayU Payment Gateway, they're taken to PayU to complete a secure payment.
