## Overview

[Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/himalayan-bank-payment-gateway/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) is an extension for [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) that lets you accept trip and tour payments through Himalayan Bank, one of the leading banks in Nepal.

Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway works in Nepal with Nepali and US currency only, and supports Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Union Pay cards.

<Callout kind="info">
  This addon works on WordPress and only with the [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) plugin.
</Callout>

## How to install the Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway addon

See [how to activate the Pro addon license key](https://docs.wptravelengine.com/article/activate-pro-addon-license-key/) for instructions on installing and activating this addon.

## How does it work?

Tour operators and travel agencies in Nepal can enable the Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway for trip booking by setting up a Himalayan Bank account with two key details: a Merchant ID and a Secret Key.

When a traveler books a trip through the Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway, they're taken to Himalayan Bank to make a secure payment. After payment, the trip booking is confirmed and the traveler is redirected back to your website.

For redirection, ask Himalayan Bank to set the Backend URL and Frontend URL of your site on their server:

- The **Backend URL** is where you receive the payment response from the server.
- The **Frontend URL** is where the payer is redirected after a successful payment.

<Callout kind="tip">
  WP Travel Engine recommends setting the Frontend URL to the Travelers Information page and adding the `[WTE_HBL_PAYMENT_RESPONSE]` shortcode to that page. For other custom pages, add both the `[WTE_HBL_PAYMENT_RESPONSE]` and `[WP_TRAVEL_ENGINE_BOOK_CONFIRMATION]` shortcodes and assign the page under Confirmation Page Settings.
</Callout>

## Configuration

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open HBL Payment settings">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > HBL Payment**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your bank-issued credentials">
    Enter the Office ID, API Key, and Encryption Key ID. Contact Himalayan Bank to obtain these.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate and enter your merchant keys">
    Enter a Merchant Signing Private Key and a Merchant Decryption Private Key. See the key generation steps below before entering these.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the bank's public keys">
    Enter the PACO Signing Public Key and PACO Encryption Public Key. Contact Himalayan Bank to obtain these.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave the notification URLs at their defaults">
    Keep the Notification URLs in their default values.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save Settings**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to generate your merchant keys

<Steps title-type="h4">
  <Step title="Generate two RSA key pairs">
    Generate two RSA key pairs using any available tool — for example, [devglan's RSA encryption/decryption tool](https://www.devglan.com/online-tools/rsa-encryption-decryption) produces ready-to-use RSA key pairs. The RSA key size must be 4096 bit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide the public keys to the bank">
    Among the two generated key pairs, give the two public keys to Himalayan Bank — one for signing and one for encryption.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the private keys in WP Travel Engine">
    Save the corresponding private keys under HBL Payment Settings, in the Merchant Signing Private Key and Merchant Decryption Private Key fields.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enable HBL Payment at checkout

Enable HBL Payment under **Payment General** settings so customers can pay through the HBL Payment Gateway on the checkout page. Once enabled, travelers can choose the Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway as a payment option at checkout.

When a traveler books a trip through the Himalayan Bank Payment Gateway, they're taken to Himalayan Bank to complete a secure payment.
