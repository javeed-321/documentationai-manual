You need to activate the [Travel Monster Pro](https://wptravelengine.com/wordpress-travel-themes/travel-monster-pro/?utm_source=travel_monster_pro\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_theme) license key to receive plugin updates and get quick support from the WP Travel Engine support team. The plugin is updated regularly to fix compatibility issues, resolve bugs, improve security, and add new features — all of which require an active license.

## Before you begin

You'll need your Travel Monster Pro license key. It's sent in your purchase receipt email, and you can also find it from [your account profile](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/).

## Activate your license key

<Steps title-type="h2">
  <Step title="Open the License tab">
    Navigate to **Admin Dashboard > Appearance > Travel Monster > License**.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Travel-monster-2-1024x369.png" alt="Travel Monster License tab in the WordPress admin dashboard" width="1024" height="369" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your license key">
    On the right side of the screen, enter your license key and click **Save Changes**.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Travel-monster-3-1024x351.png" alt="License key input field with Save Changes button" width="1024" height="351" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the license">
    Click **Activate License** to complete activation.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Travel-Monster-4-1024x394.png" alt="Activate License button in the Travel Monster Pro license settings" width="1024" height="394" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Issues you might face during license activation

WP Travel Engine runs a firewall to protect its licensing servers from bots. If you're having trouble activating your license, your website's IP may have been blocked by that firewall. The firewall blocks IPs that are reported in the [AbuseIPDB](https://www.abuseipdb.com/check/) database.

<Callout kind="info">
  You can find your website's IP address using a tool like [NSLookup.io](https://www.nslookup.io/website-to-ip-lookup/) — just enter your website's domain name.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
  If you're still unable to activate your license key after checking your IP status, contact the [WP Travel Engine support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support/) with your purchase email and the URL of the website where you're trying to activate the license.
</Callout>

## Getting help

If activation problems persist, reach out to the [WP Travel Engine support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for assistance.
