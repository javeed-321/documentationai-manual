Travel Monster Pro gives you additional control over how single posts and archive pages look and behave, including layout choices and social sharing options.

## Single Post setting

You can access the Single Post settings from **Dashboard > Appearance > Customize > Posts & Pages > Single Post**.

### Single Post layouts

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-63.png" alt="Single Post layout options in Travel Monster Pro theme settings" width="326" height="262" />

| Setting                                                 | What it does                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Which layout should the single post page use? (Layouts) | Selects the visual layout for your single post pages. |

### Social share posts

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-64.png" alt="Social sharing configuration options for single posts" width="600" height="400" />

| Setting                                                                        | What it does                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Should social sharing buttons appear on posts? (Enable Social Sharing Buttons) | Shows or hides the social sharing buttons on single post pages.                                                                                      |
| Should this theme manage Open Graph meta tags? (Enable Open Graph Meta Tags)   | Controls whether the theme outputs Open Graph tags. Disable this if you already manage Open Graph tags with Jetpack, Yoast, or another SEO plugin.   |
| What label should introduce the sharing buttons? (Social Share Text)           | Sets the heading text shown above the social sharing icons.                                                                                          |
| Which platforms should appear, and in what order? (Social Sharing Buttons)     | Click the eye icon next to each platform to show or hide its icon, and drag and drop entries to reorder how the platforms appear on your post pages. |

<Callout kind="alert">
  Enabling Open Graph Meta Tags here while another SEO plugin also manages them can cause duplicate or conflicting tags. Keep only one source active.
</Callout>

## Archive setting

You can access the Archive page settings from **Dashboard > Appearance > Customize > Posts & Pages > Archive**.

### Layouts

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-65.png" alt="Archive page layout options in Travel Monster Pro theme settings" width="326" height="262" />

| Setting                                                      | What it does                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which layout should archive pages use? (Archive Page Layout) | Selects your preferred page layout for the Archive pages on your website. |

## Getting help

If you don't see these options in your Customizer, confirm that Travel Monster Pro is installed, activated, and licensed. For further assistance, contact the [WP Travel Engine support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/).
