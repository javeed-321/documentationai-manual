## Overview

You can manage your projects from the Wholechain dashboard at any time.

## Steps

1. Visit `https://dashboard.wholechain.com`
2. Enter your registered email and password
3. Click "Log In"
4. If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password?"
5. Follow the prompt to reset your credentials

<Callout kind="tip">
Bookmark the dashboard URL for quick access.
</Callout>
