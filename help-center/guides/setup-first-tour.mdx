title: Set Up Your First Tour Package
description: Create and publish your first tour on WP Travel Engine.
---

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Tours">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to <b>Tours > Add New</b>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details">
    Enter the tour title, description, price, and itinerary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set images">
    Upload a featured image and gallery photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click <b>Publish</b> to make your tour live. View it on your site to check booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>
