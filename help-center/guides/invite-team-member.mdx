title: Inviting a Team Member
description: Quickly add new users to your Wholechain workspace.
---

## Invite Users

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Go to the dashboard and click on "Team" in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Invite Member'">
    Press the "Invite Member" button at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Email">
    Type in your teammate's email and select their role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click "Send". The user will receive an invite link.
  </Step>
</Steps>
