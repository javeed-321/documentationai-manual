---
title: How to Resend the Booking Confirmation Email
description: >-
  Manually resend a traveler's booking confirmation email from the Bookings
  screen in WP Travel Engine.
type: guide
---
WP Travel Engine automatically sends a booking confirmation email to both the traveler and the admin once a booking completes. In some cases — an email delivery issue, or updated booking details that need a fresh confirmation — you'll want to resend it manually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the WordPress dashboard">
    Sign in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Bookings">
    Navigate to **WP Travel Engine → Bookings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate the booking">
    Use the filter to find the specific booking you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Resend the purchase receipt">
    Click the **Resend Purchase Receipt** button on that booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The system resends the confirmation email to the traveler and the admin, keeping the traveler's information current and communication clear.
