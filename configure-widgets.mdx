## Where widgets can go

The Travel Monster theme lets you add widgets to the sidebar and footer sections of your website. You can add widgets either through the site dashboard or through the Customizer.

<Tabs tabs="From the Customizer,From the Dashboard" default-tab="1">
  <Tab>
    <Steps title-type="h3">
      <Step title="Open the Widgets panel">
        Go to **Appearance > Customize > Core > Widgets**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose a widget area">
        Select the location where you want to add a widget.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add a widget">
        Click **Add a Widget** and select the widget you want to use.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-55.png" width="343" height="447" alt="Adding a widget to a widget area in the WordPress Customizer" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Arrange and publish">
        Drag and drop the added widgets to set their order, then click **Publish**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab>
    <Steps title-type="h3">
      <Step title="Open the Widgets screen">
        Go to **Appearance > Widgets**. Available widgets are listed on the left, and the widget areas provided by the theme are listed on the right.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-56-1024x516.png" width="1024" height="516" alt="Widgets screen showing available widgets and theme widget areas" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add a widget to an area">
        Drag and drop a widget into the widget area you want, or click a widget and select the location to add it to.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-57.png" width="343" height="447" alt="Assigning a widget to a widget area" />
      </Step>

      <Step title="Save the widget">
        Configure the widget's settings, then click **Save**.

        <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-58.png" width="343" height="447" alt="Saving a configured widget" />
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
