## Where the default currency setting lives

WP Travel Engine uses **US Dollar** as the default payment currency out of the box. If your business prices and collects payment in a different currency, you can change it site-wide from the plugin's currency settings — no code or additional configuration required.

## Change the payment currency

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open Currency Settings">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Currency > General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate Payment Currency">
    Find the **Payment Currency** field on this tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your currency">
    Open the dropdown and choose the currency your business charges customers in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Save the changes. Trip prices, the pricing widget, and checkout now display and process payments in the selected currency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Not every payment gateway supports every currency. Before switching, confirm your active payment gateway supports the currency you're selecting, so checkout doesn't fail for customers.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info">
  Want the currency shown as a code (e.g., USD) instead of a symbol (e.g., $)? See [Change Currency Symbol to Currency Code](/article/change-currency-symbol-to-currency-code).
</Callout>
