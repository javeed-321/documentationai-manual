# Control Center Documentation

> Akamai Control Center is the integrated interface for Akamai’s products, services, and platform. Control Center gives businesses the access, insight, and control they need to deliver the best possible online experiences. Control Center delivers intelligent tools for site and stream management, diagnostics, and collaboration to help businesses thrive in the hyperconnected world. Accessible via web, mobile, and API, Control Center makes it easy for businesses to configure their Akamai services, monitor performance, analyze traffic, and resolve end-user issues in real time, from any device, anywhere.
