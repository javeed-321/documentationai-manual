## What Content Generation does

Content Generation writes trip marketing copy for you: the overview, highlights, itinerary, includes/excludes, FAQs, and any custom tab you've built into your trip layout. Point it at real trip details and it drafts copy that matches your brand voice, tone, and reading level, ready to review and publish instead of writing from a blank page.

It's built for agencies and tour operators who list many trips and don't have a dedicated copywriter for each one.

## Benefits at a glance

| Feature                            | User benefit                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trip Wizard generation             | Fill in trip basics once and generate every section, Overview through custom tab, in a single pass instead of writing each one separately |
| Per-tab generation                 | Regenerate just one section, like the Itinerary, without touching content you've already approved elsewhere on the trip                   |
| Custom instructions per generation | Give a one-off instruction for a specific section, useful when a trip needs something your global settings don't cover                    |
| Brand voice & tone controls        | Keep every generated trip sounding like your business, not like generic AI copy, without editing each one by hand                         |
| Custom tab support                 | Generate content for tabs you've added yourself, not just the built-in sections                                                           |
| Multi-provider support             | Connect Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, or OpenRouter and switch providers without changing how you write trips                                |

## Before you start

1. Altus AI is installed and activated with your active license key.
2. An AI provider is configured under **Altus AI > AI Provider**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  To learn how to configure your AI provider, see [Configuring the AI Provider](/altus-ai/getting-started-with-altus-ai#configure-the-ai-provider) in the Getting Started guide.
</Callout>

## Configure the model and parameters

Go to **Altus AI > Content Generation**. These settings apply site-wide, to every trip you generate, until you change them.

### AI Provider

Choose which connected AI provider (like Anthropic, OpenAI, or OpenRouter) Altus should use to generate content. This list only shows providers you've already connected under AI Providers.

### AI Model

Choose which specific model from that provider should do the writing. Different models can affect the quality of the output and how much each generation costs.

### Temperature

Adjust Temperature if needed, from 0.0 (Precise) to 2.0 (Creative). Lower values keep writing literal and structured, which suits itineraries. Most trip content works well between 0.6 and 0.8.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Temperature isn't supported by all models (for example, reasoning models like o1/o3/GPT-5). If unsupported, set it to 1 so it's safely ignored.
</Callout>

### Token Limit

Adjust Token Limit if longer sections, like a multi-day itinerary, are getting cut off. A higher limit can increase your provider's API cost.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If the AI Model dropdown looks empty, it just means your provider hasn't loaded its list of models yet. Go back to AI Providers and test the connection again to fix it.
</Callout>

### Additional Instructions

Fill in **Additional Instructions** with anything Altus should always know, for example "We specialize in small-group treks for solo travelers over 40."

## Customize content templates

Below the global settings, **Content Templates** lets you override the instruction Altus uses for each section.

Altus already knows your **Trip Name**, **Trip Duration**, **Destination**, and **Activities** — you don't need to repeat these in any instruction.

Each section — Overview, Trip Highlights, Itinerary, Includes/Excludes, and FAQs — shows a **Default Instruction** (click **Show more** to read it in full) and a **Custom instruction (optional override)** field. Leave it blank to keep the default. Fill it in to replace the default entirely, for example telling FAQs to always include a cancellation-policy question.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Altus's defaults are already written to avoid generic AI-sounding copy. Override a section only when you need something specific and recurring beyond that.
</Callout>

## Generate content on a trip page

From your dashboard, go to **Trips** and open any trip. There are two ways to generate content once you're editing a trip.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Tab 1">
    ### Method 1: The Trip Wizard

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Trip Wizard" title-type="p">
        Open a trip and click the purple **Altus AI** icon in the top-right corner.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Confirm trip details" title-type="p">
        On **Trip Details**, confirm or adjust Trip Title, Duration, Output Language, Writing Tone, Reading Level, Destinations, and Activities for this trip only.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Choose sections to generate" title-type="p">
        Under **Content to Generate**, check the sections you want written: Overview, Trip Highlights, Itinerary, Includes/Excludes, FAQs, and any custom tab you've added.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Add trip-specific instructions" title-type="p">
        Add anything specific to this trip under **Additional Instructions**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Adjust advanced settings" title-type="p">
        Open **Advanced settings** to toggle on suggesting a better title, including brand voice, or web search, if you want any of those for this generation.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Generate" title-type="p">
        Click **Generate**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Review and apply" title-type="p">
        Review each section, add, delete, or edit anything you want, and click **Apply**.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Finish" title-type="p">
        Click **Done** to apply these settings to your trip.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      Altus uses the AI provider and brand voice settings configured under **Content Generation** for every wizard generation, so set those up first.
    </Callout>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Tab 1">
    ### Method 2: Generating from a specific tab

    Go directly to a section's tab, for example Itinerary, and look at the top-right corner of its content block:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Generate with global settings" title-type="p">
        Click the **Altus AI icon** (write/rewrite) to generate using your global Content Generation settings — tone, reading level, and brand voice — without writing a custom instruction.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Or generate with a custom instruction" title-type="p">
        Click the **pencil icon** (write/rewrite with instruction) to write your own instruction for that generation, useful for a one-off request specific to this trip.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      If the content is already there, this shows a Rewrite button; if the field is blank, it shows a Write button. The icon is the same for both.
    </Callout>

    This works the same way on Overview, Itinerary, Includes/Excludes, FAQs, and any custom tab you've built. On sections with multiple items, like Itinerary days or FAQs, smaller regenerate buttons also let you rewrite a single day or a single FAQ without touching the rest.

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      If WP Travel Engine's Advanced Itinerary add-on is active, itinerary generation adapts to hour-based scheduling and fills in extra fields like meals and overnight stays automatically.
    </Callout>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Whichever method you use, review the result before publishing. Generated content lands as normal, fully editable content, not a locked block.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If you already like most of a section but want one change, use Rewrite with Instruction and describe the change, for example "make this shorter" or "mention the group discount," rather than regenerating from scratch.
</Callout>

## Use cases

### Launching a full trip listing from scratch

**Situation:** You're adding a new 12-day trek and want every section drafted at once instead of writing each one manually.

**Implementation:**

- Fill in Trip Title, Duration, Destinations, and Activities on the Trip Details step.
- Check all sections under Content to Generate: Overview, Trip Highlights, Itinerary, Includes/Excludes, FAQs, Custom tabs.
- Add a line under Additional Instructions noting anything unique to this trip, like a specific base camp route.

**Benefit:** A complete first draft of the listing is ready to review in one pass, instead of switching between tabs to write each section separately.

### Rewriting one section after a trip change

**Situation:** You've already published a trip, but the itinerary changed after a route update, and the rest of the listing is fine as is.

**Implementation:**

- Go to the Itinerary tab.
- Click the pencil icon (rewrite with instructions) and describe the change, for example "day 4 now ends at a different lodge, mention the shorter walking distance."

**Benefit:** Only the Itinerary section regenerates. The Overview, Highlights, and FAQs you already approved stay untouched.

## Tips for better results

- Fill in real trip details before generating. Altus won't invent facts it wasn't given, so accurate destination, activity, and duration details lead to more specific output.
- Set Brand Voice once and mean it. This single field affects every generation more than any other setting.
- Save custom instructions for cases with a specific, recurring need. The built-in defaults are already tuned against generic phrasing.
- Always review before publishing. Treat generated content as a strong draft, not a guaranteed-accurate final copy.

## Costs and usage tracking

Content generation is billed by your connected AI provider, based on their token pricing. Altus does not add any extra charge.

For a full guide on tracking usage and costs, see [Usage & Logs](/altus-ai/usage-logs).

## FAQs

<Expandable title="Can I use a different AI provider for different trips?" default-open="true">
  No. The AI Provider and Model set under Content Generation apply to every trip. Switching providers changes it site-wide.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Does the wizard overwrite sections I've already written by hand?" default-open="false">
  Only the sections you check under Content to Generate are affected. Leave a section unchecked to keep your existing content untouched.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Can I generate content for a custom tab I built myself?" default-open="false">
  Yes. Custom tabs appear alongside the built-in sections in both the wizard's Content to Generate list and the per-tab generation icons.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Does Altus charge extra for content generation on top of my AI provider's fees?" default-open="false">
  No. You're billed directly by your connected provider based on their token pricing. Check usage at **Altus AI > Account > Usage & Logs**.
</Expandable>
