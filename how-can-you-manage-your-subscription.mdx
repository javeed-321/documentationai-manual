## About the subscription process

All WP Travel Engine annual plans are set to auto-renew, so your plugin and its premium features keep working without interruption.

- **Renewal notification** — You'll receive an email reminder approximately one month before your renewal date. It includes your plan details, the renewal date, and the renewal amount.
- **Automatic renewal** — If you don't take any action after the reminder, your subscription renews automatically using the same payment method from your original purchase. You'll get another email once the license has been renewed.

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Go to your account page">
    Visit the [WP Travel Engine Account Page](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in with the email and password associated with your plugin purchase. If you've forgotten your password, use the **Lost Password** link to reset it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Subscription tab">
    Once logged in, go to your dashboard's **Subscription** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cancel">
    Find the subscription you want to cancel and click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Cancelling stops future automatic renewals, but you keep access to premium features until the end of your current subscription period. You can manually renew at any time afterward by making a payment from your account.
</Callout>

## Can you get a refund after a renewal?

Yes. WP Travel Engine provides refunds for automatic renewals if requested within 14 days of the renewal, in accordance with the [Refund Policy](https://wptravelengine.com/refund-policy/).

<Callout kind="alert">
  A 10% fee applies to refunds on renewals, covering administrative costs and non-refundable transaction fees. Refund requests made outside the 14-day window are generally not eligible.
</Callout>

To avoid being charged for a renewal you don't want, cancel your subscription before the renewal date — you'll get an email reminder about a month in advance. If you're eligible for a refund, submit the [Refund Request Form](https://wptravelengine.com/submit-refund-request/) to start the process.

## What happens when a subscription expires

When your subscription expires:

- Access to premium add-ons, features, updates, and support is suspended.
- Your website continues running on the free version of WP Travel Engine, but premium functionality is no longer available.
- You can renew at any time to regain full access.

## If a renewal fails or your subscription has expired

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You'll receive an email notification if a payment fails.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update your payment information">
    Log in to your account and update your payment details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Renew manually">
    Once your payment information is updated, renew the subscription manually from your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Failing to renew on time suspends premium features until the subscription is successfully renewed.
</Callout>

## Can you renew a subscription early?

By default, the renewal option in your account dashboard only appears after your subscription has already expired — you can't manually renew before the expiration date from the dashboard.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If your subscription was previously cancelled, the reminder email sent about a month before expiration includes a renewal payment link. Clicking that link and completing payment lets you renew before your subscription actually expires.
</Callout>
