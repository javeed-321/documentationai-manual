## Overview

Travel Monster comes with a powerful Header and Footer Builder that lets you create fully customized header and footer layouts using Elementor. This gives you more design flexibility than the theme's default pre-built header and footer options.

## Prerequisites

Before using the Header and Footer Builder, confirm that:

- The latest version of the [Travel Monster theme](https://wptravelengine.com/wordpress-travel-themes/travel-monster/) is installed and activated
- The Elementor plugin is installed and activated
- The [WTE Elementor](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wte-elementor-widgets/) plugin is installed and activated

## Building a custom header

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Access the Header Builder">
    From your WordPress admin dashboard:

    1. Navigate to **WPTE Builder > Header Builder**
    2. Click **Add New**
    3. Give your header a name
    4. Click **Publish**

    <Image src="https://docs.rarathemes.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-129-1024x388.png" alt="Creating a new header in the Header Builder" width="1024" height="388" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit with Elementor">
    Click **Edit with Elementor** to launch the Elementor editor, where you can start designing your header using drag-and-drop widgets.

    Travel Monster provides four custom Elementor widgets for building headers:

    - **Navigation Menu** – add your primary or custom menus
    - **Contact** – add contact info such as phone, email, or address
    - **Search** – insert a site-wide search bar
    - **Site Logo** – display your logo with optional links

    <Callout kind="info">
      You can also use any other Elementor widget to add buttons, social icons, announcements, or promotional messages.
    </Callout>

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122625_113731_AM.jpg" alt="Header builder widgets available in Elementor" width="1200" height="675" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the custom header">
    The custom header applies automatically once you select **Builder** as the layout option in the Customizer:

    - Navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Main Header > Header Builder**
    - Under **Type of Header**, select **Builder**
    - Choose the header you created

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122525_112816_AM.jpg" alt="Applying a custom header under Header Builder settings" width="1200" height="675" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Building a custom footer

Building a footer works the same way as building a header — you can use any Elementor widget to custom-build your footer to fit your needs.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Access the Footer Builder">
    From your WordPress admin dashboard:

    1. Navigate to **WPTE Builder > Footer Builder**
    2. Click **Add New**
    3. Give your footer a name
    4. Click **Publish**

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122525_125330_PM-1024x362.jpg" alt="Creating a new footer in the Footer Builder" width="1024" height="362" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit with Elementor">
    Click **Edit with Elementor** to launch the Elementor editor, where you can design your footer using any of the available widgets.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply the custom footer">
    The custom footer applies automatically once you select **Builder** as the layout option in the Customizer:

    - Navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Footer Settings > Footer Builder**
    - Under **Type of Footer**, select **Builder**
    - Choose the footer you created

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Screenshot_122525_010557_PM.jpg" alt="Applying a custom footer under Footer Builder settings" width="1200" height="675" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
