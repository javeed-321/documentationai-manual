## Overview

WP Travel Engine allows you to export your bookings into external files, such as Excel sheets. This feature is useful for record-keeping, reporting, or analyzing your bookings outside of WordPress.

## Prerequisites

- Administrator access to your WordPress dashboard
- At least one booking recorded in WP Travel Engine

## Exporting your bookings

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Bookings page">
    Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard and navigate to **WP Travel Engine > Bookings**. You'll see a list of all your trip bookings displayed in a table format.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Export Bookings popup">
    At the top-right corner of the Bookings page, click **Export Bookings**. A popup opens with options to filter the bookings you want to export.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your export options">
    Filter the bookings using any combination of **Date**, **Trip**, and **Booking Status** — or leave all filters empty to export everything. See the filter details below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export the bookings">
    Click **Export** in the popup. The system generates an Excel file containing all bookings matching your criteria, and the file downloads automatically to your computer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter options

### Filter by date

Click the Date field to select the start date and end date of the period for which you want bookings exported. Leave this field empty to export bookings from all time.

For example, setting the start date to March 12 and the end date to September 30 exports only bookings received between those two dates.

### Filter by trip

Use the **Trip** dropdown to select a specific trip. Only bookings for that trip are exported. Leave this field empty to export bookings from all trips.

### Filter by booking status

Use the **Booking Status** dropdown to choose a status, such as **Pending**, **Booked**, **Refunded**, or **Cancelled**. Only bookings with the selected status are exported. Leave this field empty to export bookings of all statuses.

## Exporting all bookings

If you want to export all available bookings, you don't need to select any filters. Simply click **Export Bookings > Export**, and all bookings from your site are included in the Excel file.

## Tips for smooth exporting

<Callout kind="tip">
  Make sure your browser allows automatic downloads, otherwise the Excel file may not save automatically. If you have a large number of bookings, the export may take a few moments. Double-check your filters before exporting to avoid missing important data.
</Callout>

## Summary

The Export Bookings feature is a fast and reliable way to get your booking data into Excel for accounting, reporting, or analytics. Using the filters, you can export exactly the data you need without manually sorting or copying records.
