## Why an itinerary matters

An itinerary outlines day-by-day activities, key locations, and timings for a trip. It ensures smooth organization, efficient time management, and stress-free travel, helping travellers make the most of their experience while staying on track.

## Configure the Itinerary tab

Go to **Dashboard > Trips > Add New > Trip Settings > Itinerary**.

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/itinerary-section-2-1.png" width="743" height="485" alt="WP Travel Engine Itinerary tab with title and Add Itinerary button" />

<Steps title-type="h3">
  1. Add a title for the **Itinerary** section.
  2. Click **Add Itinerary** to include a new itinerary entry.
  3. Enter the **Itinerary Title** for that specific day or activity.
  4. Add a **Description** with the details for that day or activity.
  5. To remove an itinerary entry, click the **delete icon** next to it.
</Steps>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Itinerary-description.png" width="1024" height="600" alt="Adding an itinerary title and description for a trip day" />
