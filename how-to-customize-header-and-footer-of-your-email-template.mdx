WP Travel Engine lets you personalize your booking-related emails by adding a logo to the email header and customizing the footer text. This ensures that all communication sent from your website looks professional and matches your brand.

## Where can I access the Email Header and Footer settings?

You can access this feature from your WordPress dashboard:

- Go to **WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Settings**.
- Under the **Branding** section, you'll find options to customize the header and footer.

## How do I add a logo to my email header?

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot_091925_020518_PM.jpg" width="1024" height="600" alt="Header Image/Logo Upload field in the Branding section of email settings" />

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Branding settings">
    Navigate to **WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Settings → Branding**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    In the **Header Image/Logo Upload** field, click **Upload**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose an image">
    Choose your logo from the Media Library or upload a new one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the logo to your email header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I customize the email footer text?

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot_091925_020542_PM.jpg" width="1024" height="600" alt="Footer Text field in the Branding section of email settings" />

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the Branding settings">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine → Settings → Emails → Settings → Branding**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the Footer Text field">
    Locate the **Footer Text** field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your footer content">
    Enter the text you want to appear at the bottom of your emails — for example, your business address, customer support info, or a copyright note.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the new footer text.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Can I change the branding later?

Yes. You can update the header logo or footer text anytime by returning to the **Branding** section under email settings. All future emails will reflect the updated branding.

## Best practices for customizing email header and footer

- Use a high-quality logo with a transparent background — PNG format works best.
- Keep the footer text short and professional. Include your business name, contact info, or a support link.
- If your business details change, update the footer text so customers always receive the latest information.
- Test your email template by sending a purchase receipt to yourself before going live.

## Troubleshooting

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Logo not displaying in emails**: Make sure the image file size is optimized and publicly accessible. If you're using an SMTP plugin or transactional email service, check that images aren't blocked.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Footer text not updating**: Confirm that you clicked **Save Changes** after editing, then test again by sending yourself a purchase receipt.
</Callout>
