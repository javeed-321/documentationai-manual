## Overview

Use the Destination/Activities Slider 3 widget to show your Destination, Activity, or Trip Types in a scrolling carousel on any page.

If you are new to this, here are the only terms you need to know. **Elementor** is a page builder plugin that lets you build pages by dragging items into place. A **widget** is one of those ready-made blocks you can drag onto a page. A **taxonomy** is a way to group things on your site, such as destinations like Nepal or activities like trekking.

Say you run treks in Nepal and day tours in Kathmandu. You can use this widget to show those destinations or activities as a moving gallery on your homepage. That helps visitors see what you offer at a glance.

## Add the widget to your page

You will get the best result if you add the widget first, then adjust the settings while watching the preview change.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  **What you'll need before you start:** you need a WordPress page opened in Elementor, plus some destinations, activities, or trip types already created on your tour site.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page in Elementor" icon="edit" title-type="p">
    Open the page where you want the slider to appear.

    If you are editing your homepage, this is a good place to add a moving row of destinations or tour styles. For example, you might place it below your hero banner to show trekking, cultural tours, and family trips.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the Destination/Activities Slider 3 widget" icon="search" title-type="p">
    In the Elementor panel, look for **Destination/Activities Slider 3**.

    Then drag the widget onto your page where you want the slider to appear. You should see the block appear in the page preview.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose what you want to show" icon="settings" title-type="p">
    Start with the layout and query settings first.

    These control what appears in the slider. Once the right items are showing, you can move on to colors, spacing, arrows, and other design details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Layout settings

This section changes how the slider looks on the page.

- **Layouts** - Choose from different layout options to display trips. Several layouts are available, so you can pick the one that fits your page best.

If one layout feels too busy, try another and compare the preview. Once the layout looks right, move on to choosing what content to show.

## Query settings

This section decides which destinations, activities, or trip types appear in the slider.

- **Select Taxonomy** - Choose whether to show **Destination**, **Activities**, or **Trip Types**.
- **Show Term by** - Choose how the taxonomy is shown. You can use the default setup or choose terms from a list.
- **Number of Items** - Enter the total number of items you want to display.

For example, if you want to highlight only Hiking, Cultural, and Adventure trips, choose the right taxonomy and then pick those terms from the list. If the wrong items appear, this is the first place to check.

## Additional settings

These settings help you control how the text appears and how much detail visitors can see.

- **Title Tag** - Choose the heading tag used for the taxonomy title.
- **Show Trip Counts** - Show the number of trips linked to each taxonomy.

Trip counts can be helpful if you want visitors to see how many options they have in a destination or activity. If you prefer a cleaner look, you can leave that turned off.

## Slider settings

This section controls how the carousel moves and how visitors navigate through it.

- **Autoplay** - Turn on automatic slide changes.
- **Autoplay Speed** - Set the time between each slide change, in milliseconds.
- **Loop** - Keep the slider cycling after it reaches the last slide.
- **Transition Speed** - Set how fast each slide moves, in milliseconds.
- **Slider Arrow** - Show arrows for moving forward or backward.
- **Slider Pagination** - Show dots for moving between slides.

If you want a calmer look, use a slower autoplay speed and transition speed. If your visitors need more control, keep arrows or pagination turned on so they can move through the slides themselves.

</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### The slider isn't showing up on my page

First, make sure you added the **Destination/Activities Slider 3** widget to the page in Elementor and clicked **Update** or **Publish**.

If the widget is on the page but still not visible, check whether your layout section or page section is hidden. Then preview the page again.

### The wrong destinations or activities are showing

Check **Select Taxonomy** first.

Then check **Show Term by**. If you chose terms from a list, make sure you selected the right ones. This often happens when a site has similar names like Trekking and Treks.

### The slider is not sliding

Open **Slider settings** and check whether **Autoplay** is turned on.

If **Autoplay** is off, the slides will stay still unless visitors use the arrows or pagination dots. Also check **Loop** if you want the slider to keep moving after the last slide.

### The arrows or dots are missing

Go to **Slider settings** and check **Slider Arrow** and **Slider Pagination**.

If they are turned off, visitors will not see them. If they are on but hard to see, adjust their colors in **Slider arrows and pagination** style settings.

### The images look stretched or cropped

Open the **Image** style section.

Check **Object Fit**, **Width**, **Height**, and **Image Size**. These settings control how the photo fills the space. Try a different image fit if your trekking or destination photos look cut off.

### The trip count is not showing

Go to **Additional settings** and turn on **Show Trip Counts**.

If it is already on, check whether that destination, activity, or trip type actually has trips linked to it. A count will only appear when trips are connected to that term.

### The section looks too cramped or too wide

Use **Padding** in **General style settings** or in the **Content** style section.

Use **Border Radius**, **Border Type**, and **Box Shadow** after that if you want to soften the box or make it stand out more clearly.

## FAQs

### Can I show only a few destinations instead of all of them?

Yes. Use **Show Term by** to choose terms from a list, then pick only the destinations, activities, or trip types you want to display.

### Can I use this widget for activities instead of destinations?

Yes. In **Select Taxonomy**, choose **Activities** instead of **Destination**.

You can also choose **Trip Types** if you want to highlight options like day tours, luxury trips, or multi-day packages.

### Do I need to know code to use this widget?

No. You can set it up from the Elementor panel by choosing options and adjusting styles.

### What does title tag mean?

A title tag is the heading level used for the taxonomy title.

It helps control how the title is structured on the page. If you are unsure which one to use, keep the default unless you already have a page heading plan.

### What happens if I turn off autoplay?

The slider will stop moving on its own.

Visitors can still browse the items if you keep **Slider Arrow** or **Slider Pagination** turned on.

### Can I match the slider to my brand colors?

Yes. Use **General style settings** for the overall section, then use the **Content**, **Title**, **Image**, and **Slider arrows and pagination** sections for more detailed styling.
