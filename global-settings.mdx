---
title: 'Configuring the Global Settings for Trip Fixed Starting Dates '
---
The Global Settings section allows you to configure the features that apply across all trips and bookings on your site. Use these settings to configure how bookings are handled, what trip information is visible, and how travelers experience your site. 

\
You can configure the global settings for Trip Fixed Starting Dates vi&#x61;**&#x20;WP Travel Engine > Settings > Extensions > Fixed Starting Dates.**
