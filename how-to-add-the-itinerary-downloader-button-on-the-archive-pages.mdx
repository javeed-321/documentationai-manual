## Overview

You can enable the Itinerary Downloader button on the Trip Search / Archive Page to let users download the trip itinerary PDF directly from the archive listing, without needing to open the individual trip page.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  The Itinerary Downloader button cannot be added unless the [Itinerary Downloader](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/itinerary-downloader) add-on is installed and active.
</Callout>

## Enabling the button

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Trip Archive display settings" title-type="p">
    In the WordPress admin dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Display > Trip Archive**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the Itinerary Downloader button" title-type="p">
    Under the **Trip Archive** tab, toggle **Show Itinerary Downloader Button** to enable it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes" title-type="p">
    Click **Save** to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once saved, visit your trip archive or search page on the frontend to confirm the download button now appears on each trip listing.

## Use cases and benefits

- Makes it faster for travelers to access trip details
- Allows users to easily compare multiple trips
- Helps travelers save itineraries for offline viewing
- Enables sharing itineraries with friends or family before booking
