## Before you start

You can cancel your WP Travel Engine subscription directly from your account on the WP Travel Engine website — no need to contact support unless you run into an issue.

<Callout kind="info">
  You'll need the email address and password associated with your plugin purchase to log in. If you don't remember your password, you can reset it from the login page.
</Callout>

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Go to your account page">
    Visit the [My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) page on the WP Travel Engine website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in">
    Enter the email and password associated with your plugin purchase. If you've forgotten your password, use the **Lost Password** link on the login page to reset it. Make sure you log in with the same email you used when purchasing the plugin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Subscription tab">
    Once you're logged in, you'll land on your dashboard. Navigate to the **Subscription** tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cancel the subscription">
    Locate the subscription you want to end and click **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Canceling stops future renewal charges but doesn't necessarily revoke access immediately — check your confirmation details after canceling to see when access ends.
</Callout>

## Need help?

If you run into any issues while canceling, [contact WP Travel Engine support](https://wptravelengine.com/support/) for assistance.
