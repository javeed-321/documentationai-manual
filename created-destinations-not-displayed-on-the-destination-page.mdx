---
title: 'Fix: Created Destinations Not Displayed on the Destination Page'
description: >-
  Learn why new Destinations, Activities, or Trip Types don't show on template
  pages until at least one trip is assigned to them.
type: guide
---
After creating a new **Destination**, **Activity**, or **Trip Type**, it won't appear on the template pages until at least one trip is assigned to it.

## Solution

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Assign the destination to a trip">
    Edit an existing trip (or create a new one) and assign the Destination, Activity, or Trip Type you created.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/image-9-1024x491.png" width="1024" height="491" alt="Trip edit page with a Destination assigned to the trip" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify the trip count">
    Go to **Dashboard → Trips → Destinations** and check the **Count** column to confirm the destination now has at least one trip assigned to it.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/destination-count-1024x459.png" width="1024" height="459" alt="Destinations list showing a Count column with the number of assigned trips" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  The same rule applies to **Activities** and **Trip Types** — each one only appears on its template page once at least one trip is assigned to it.
</Callout>
