## How upgrade pricing works

When you upgrade your WP Travel Engine plan, the additional cost is calculated on a pro-rata basis — you only pay the difference for the remaining time on your current billing cycle, not the full price of the new plan.

<Callout kind="info">
  You don't need to contact support to upgrade. The option is available directly from your account dashboard.
</Callout>

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    Go to [My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) and log in using the username and password sent to you when you purchased your plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the upgrade option">
    Once you're logged in, your dashboard shows the available upgrade options for your current plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select and confirm your new plan">
    Choose the plan you want to upgrade to and confirm the change. The pro-rata cost is calculated and applied automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Need help?

If you don't see the upgrade option or run into any issues, [contact WP Travel Engine support](https://wptravelengine.com/support/) for assistance.
