## What is the Trip Expiry Date feature?

The Expiry Date feature lets you control the availability of your trips by setting a specific end date. Once the date passes, the trip is automatically marked as expired and hidden from your website. This ensures your site only displays active and bookable trips.

## What happens when a trip expires?

When a trip reaches its expiry date:

- The trip becomes unavailable for booking
- The trip is removed from the trip archive and search results
- In the WordPress dashboard, the trip appears under the **Expired** tab (next to Published)

## Setting an expiry date for a trip

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open the trip">
    Go to **Trips > All Trips** in your WordPress dashboard, then click **Edit** on the trip you want to update.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Date & Price tab">
    Open the **Date & Price** tab and, under **Date Settings**, locate the **Expiry Date** field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the expiry date">
    Select the desired date from the date picker.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Update** (or **Publish** for new trips) to save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing or removing an expiry date

- **To change it:** Edit the trip, adjust the expiry date, and click **Update**.
- **To remove it:** Clear the expiry date field and click **Update**. The trip returns to normal availability.

## Finding expired trips

Expired trips are grouped under the **Expired** tab in the Trips dashboard, making it easy to manage, review, or republish them if needed.

To view them, go to **Trips > All Trips**, then click the **Expired** tab to see the full list.

## Best practices

- Use expiry dates for seasonal trips, fixed-date tours, or limited-time offers
- Set the expiry a few days after your last departure to allow pending inquiries to finish
- Double-check your WordPress timezone settings to ensure trips expire at the expected time

## Troubleshooting

<Expandable title="Trip still visible after expiry" default-open="false">
  Clear caching (browser, plugin, or CDN), then confirm the correct expiry date and timezone settings on the trip.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="Trip disappeared earlier than expected" default-open="false">
  Check the exact date you selected. If you need the trip visible through a certain day, set the expiry to that same day — the trip is treated as expired starting the following day.
</Expandable>

Contact our [support team](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/) for any other assistance.
