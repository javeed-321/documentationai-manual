## Why you might be locked out

WP Travel Engine's website uses a login protection plugin that temporarily blocks an account from logging in after several failed attempts with incorrect credentials. If you've tried logging in more than once without success, this protection may be the reason you can't get back in.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This lockout is temporary and clears automatically after a short period. Resetting your password is usually faster than waiting it out.
</Callout>

## Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the My Account page" title-type="p">
    Open [My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) on the WP Travel Engine website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the password reset" title-type="p">
    Follow the password reset flow on the page. You'll receive an email with reset instructions immediately or within a minute.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check every inbox folder" title-type="p">
    If the email doesn't arrive right away, check your Spam/Junk, Updates, and Promotions folders — password reset emails sometimes land there instead of your primary inbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the link in the email" title-type="p">
    Open the email and follow the reset instructions to set a new password and log back in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make sure future emails reach your inbox

To avoid missing reset emails or other account notifications going forward, whitelist WP Travel Engine's support email address in your email client.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If you use Gmail, you can drag and drop the email from another folder directly into your inbox. Gmail treats this as a signal to deliver future emails from that sender to your inbox automatically.
</Callout>

## Still can't log in?

If you've reset your password, checked all your folders, and whitelisted the support email but still can't access your account, [contact WP Travel Engine support](https://wptravelengine.com/support/) for further help.
