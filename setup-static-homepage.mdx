## Why use a static homepage

A static homepage lets you show a custom welcome page instead of a list of your latest blog posts. That means when someone visits your site, they see the content you want to highlight first — like an introduction, featured trips, contact details, or anything else that fits your site best.

If you're new to WordPress, don't worry — this only takes a minute. Once it's set up, your homepage and your blog page each have their own job, which makes your site feel more organized and easier for visitors to explore.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Before you start, make sure you've already created the pages you want to use. You'll need one page for your homepage content and, if you want to keep a separate blog section, another page for your posts.
</Callout>

## Set a static homepage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Homepage Settings" title-type="p">
    Head over to **Appearance > Customize > Core > Homepage Settings**. If you're following along for the first time, this is the area where WordPress lets you choose what appears on the front page of your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to a static page" title-type="p">
    Under **Your homepage displays**, choose **A static page**. This tells WordPress that you'd like your homepage to show a specific page instead of automatically listing your latest posts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your homepage and blog pages" title-type="p">
    Now you'll want to pick the pages for each role. Under **Homepage**, choose the page you want visitors to see first when they land on your site. Under **Posts Page**, choose the page where your blog posts should appear.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/1-59.png" width="342" height="656" alt="Homepage Settings panel showing static page and posts page selection in the WordPress Customizer" />

    Once both dropdowns are set, you're almost there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish your changes" title-type="p">
    Click **Publish** to save everything. After that, your new homepage setup will be live on your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the change

Open your site on the front end and take a quick look around. Your homepage should now show the static page you selected, and your blog posts should appear on the page you assigned as the **Posts Page**.

If something doesn't look right, double-check that the correct pages were selected in Homepage Settings. In most cases, a quick review there is all it takes.

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  You did it — your homepage is now set up the way you want. With the main Travel Monster setup complete, you're in a great place to keep customizing your site and explore Travel Monster Pro when you're ready for more advanced features.
</Callout>
