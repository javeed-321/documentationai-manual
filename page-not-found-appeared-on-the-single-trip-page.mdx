  Resolve a Page Not Found error on single trip pages by updating your WordPress
  Permalink structure to Post name.

---
If visiting a single trip page shows a **Page Not Found** error, this is usually related to the permalink structure set in your WordPress dashboard.

## Solution

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open your Permalink settings">
    Go to **Settings → Permalinks** in your WordPress admin dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the Post name structure">
    Under **Permalink structure**, select **Post name**.

    <Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-5-1024x513.png" width="1024" height="513" alt="WordPress Permalink settings page with the Post name option selected" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  If **Post name** is already selected, click **Save Changes** anyway and check whether the issue persists. Re-saving the permalink structure regenerates your site's rewrite rules, which often resolves the error on its own.
</Callout>
