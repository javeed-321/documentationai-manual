You can automatically send emails to customers at key moments in their booking journey — right after they book, when a payment is due, or after their trip ends — using trigger-based notifications.

<Callout kind="info">
  These workflows require the [Advanced Email Automator](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/advanced-email-automator/) add-on to be installed and activated.
</Callout>

## How do I send an email after a tour is booked?

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot_091925_031929_PM-1024x445.jpg" width="1024" height="445" alt="New Trip Booked trigger in the notification settings" />

You can send a booking confirmation email using the **New Trip Booked** trigger.

**Requirements:**

- The [Advanced Email Automator](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/advanced-email-automator/) add-on must be installed and activated.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Go to Create Notification">
    Open **Create Notification**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable and configure the notification">
    Enable the notification and fill in the details:

    - **Name**: e.g., "Booking Confirmation"
    - **Subject**: the email subject line
    - **Trigger**: select **New Trip Booked**
    - **Schedule**: send immediately or after a set number of minutes or days
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the email content">
    Add your booking details, instructions, or any other content the customer needs.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the notification">
    Click **Save Notification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I send a Due Payment Reminder?

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot_091925_032044_PM-1024x436.jpg" width="1024" height="436" alt="Due Payment Pending trigger in the notification settings" />

You can send a reminder for pending payments using the **Due Payment Pending** trigger.

**Requirements:**

- The [Advanced Email Automator](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/advanced-email-automator/) add-on must be installed and activated.
- The [Partial Payment](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/partial-payment/) add-on must be installed and activated.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Create a new notification">
    Open **Create Notification**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the trigger">
    Choose **Due Payment Pending** as the trigger.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the notification details">
    Add the Name, Subject, Schedule, and Email Content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the notification">
    Click **Save Notification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  This trigger only appears after the Partial Payment add-on is active.
</Callout>

## How can I ask customers for a review after a trip ends?

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Screenshot_091925_032113_PM-1024x418.jpg" width="1024" height="418" alt="After Trip End trigger in the notification settings" />

You can automatically send a review request using the **After Trip End** trigger.

**Requirements:**

- The [Advanced Email Automator](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/advanced-email-automator/) add-on must be installed and activated.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Create a new notification">
    Open **Create Notification** and select **After Trip End** as the trigger.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    Choose when the email sends — for example, 1 day after trip completion.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write a thank-you message with a review link">
    Include a thank you message and a link to your review form in the Email Content.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the notification">
    Click **Save Notification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What are the best practices for follow-up emails?

- Keep emails short, friendly, and clear.
- Use [personalization tags](/email-notifications/email-personalization-tags) — customer name, trip name, payment details — to make each email feel specific to the recipient.
- Schedule strategically:
  - **Booking confirmations**: immediately
  - **Payment reminders**: a few days before the due date
  - **Review requests**: 1–3 days after the trip ends

## Troubleshooting common issues

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Emails are not being sent**: Ensure the [Advanced Email Automator](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/advanced-email-automator/) add-on is installed and activated. Check that the notification is enabled and assigned the correct trigger. Confirm your WordPress site can send emails — use an SMTP plugin if needed.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Due Payment Reminder trigger not appearing**: Make sure the [Partial Payment](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/partial-payment/) add-on is installed and activated.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Review emails not sending after the trip ends**: Confirm the trip end date is set correctly, and check that the notification schedule points to a future date.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="true">
  **Emails are being sent multiple times**: Make sure there are no duplicate notifications using the same trigger, and check for caching or email plugin conflicts.
</Callout>
