## Purchasing from Nepal

If you're located in Nepal and want to purchase a WP Travel Engine package plan or a premium theme, you have alternative payment options beyond standard card checkout.

<Callout kind="info">
  WP Travel Engine accepts payments from Nepal through **eSewa** and **bank transfer**.
</Callout>

For further details on completing your purchase, contact the [official support forum](https://wptravelengine.com/support-ticket/), and the team will assist you with the process.
