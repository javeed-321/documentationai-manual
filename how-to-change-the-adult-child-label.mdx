## Why the Adult, Child, and Infant labels matter

WP Travel Engine ships with default pricing categories — usually **Adult**, **Child**, and **Infant** — that appear on the trip pricing widget and at checkout. These labels aren't hardcoded text; they're the category names you set in your pricing category settings, so you can rename them to match how your business actually prices trips (for example, "Senior," "Student," or a local-language label).

## Change an existing pricing category label

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open Pricing Categories">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Trips > Pricing Categories**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the category to rename">
    Find the category you want to relabel — for example, **Adult** or **Child** — in the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Edit the label">
    Click the category to open it, then update the label field with your preferred text.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Save the category. The new label now appears everywhere this pricing category is displayed, including the trip pricing widget and checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/edit-pricing-1-1024x538.png" width="1024" height="538" alt="Editing a pricing category label in the WP Travel Engine Pricing Categories screen" />

<Callout kind="tip">
  Relabeling a pricing category only changes its display name — it doesn't change its price, availability rules, or which trips use it.
</Callout>

## Add a new pricing category instead

If none of your existing categories fit — for example, you need a "Senior" or "Student" rate alongside Adult and Child — create a new one rather than repurposing an existing label.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Go to Pricing Categories">
    Navigate to **Trips > Pricing Categories**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a new category">
    Click to add a new pricing category and give it the label you need.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign it to your trips">
    Enable the new category on the trips where it should appear, and set its price.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Seeing the word "Label" instead of your actual category name on the pricing widget? That's a separate display issue — see [“Label” text is appeared instead of Pricing Category](/article/label-text-is-appeared-instead-of-pricing-category).
</Callout>
