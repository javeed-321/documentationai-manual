# Extended Hours

## Extended Hours

DriveWealth now offers your customers an additional 9 ½ hours of trading each and every weekday. With our extended trading hours, your global customers have more time to trade and more time engaged in their investing experience, even when U.S. markets are closed.\
‍\
Key benefits include:

* The ability to trade stocks before and after typical U.S. stock market trading hours.
* Increased access to trading globally, from any time zone.
* Access to stocks that may have greater volume during pre-and post-market trading.
* Extra time for customers to act quickly on news and events that occur when the exchange is closed.

Hours:

* Pre-market hours: 4:00 AM ET – 9:30 AM ET
* Regular market hours: 9:30 AM ET – 4:00 PM ET
* After hours: 4:00 PM ET – 8:00 PM ET
