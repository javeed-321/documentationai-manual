# Autopilot Updates

## Autopilot Updates

Beginning May 26, 2022, the following enhancements to Autopilot will be enabled in production. These updates will reduce the need for manual rebalances as well as automatically liquidate orphaned positions:

* Accounts will be automatically rebalanced by autopilot if changes are made to the composition of its underlying portfolio. This includes assigning a new portfolio to the account.

* During full rebalances, orphaned positions will be automatically sold in the next money movement rebalance and those proceeds will be reinvested pursuant to the target weightings of the account's current portfolio

**Next Steps:**\
The above updates are available for testing in your UAT environment. If applicable, ***please review and test before May 26, 2022***.

If you have any questions, please contact your PSG Account Manager.
