## API Reference: Administration
- [Get your account](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-account.md): Returns the contact and billing information related to your account.   **Permissions and scopes**  To call this operation, you need the following:  - **Identity and access permissions**. Your user needs a role with these permissions assigned. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/identity-access-cm-available-roles).   - Permissions: `view_account` - **OAuth scopes**. Your user needs these scopes assigned. [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/get-started#oauth).    - Scopes: `account:read_only`   **CLI**  ```shell linode-cli account view ```  [Learn more](https://techdocs.akamai.com/cloud-computing/docs/getting-started-with-the-linode-cli)
- [Update your account](https://techdocs.akamai.com/linode-api/reference/put-account.md): Updates contact and billing information related to your account.
