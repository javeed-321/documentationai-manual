# Object Delivery Documentation

> Akamai's Object Delivery service brings scalability, speed, and availability to the delivery of embedded static content. It removes the need to offload your entire website, which will improve its overall performance. It optimizes availability and delivery from your subdomain of executables over HTTP that are less than 100 MB, such as images, JavaScript, CSS, PDF documents, and XML. It does this while offering configuration options for cacheability and other services that help offload your origin infrastructure.
