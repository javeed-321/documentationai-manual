## Quarterly Round Up

Below are some of our best bits from the past quarter.

### European Payments | Spain and France Branch Enablement

European customer can now create EUR accounts with Spanish (ES) and French (FR) IBANs in addition to our existing Irish (IE) and Dutch (NL) selection of European IBANs, enabling your expansion ambitions within Europe.

For more information on how to access this functionality, please speak to your Customer Success Manager

### Open Banking | Payment Initiation Service UK Expansion

We have expanded our Payment Initiation Service connectivity. You can now initiate payments from;  HSBC - Kinetic, Mettle, Tide, Ulster Bank (UK), Virgin Money and Wise.

You can find our full supported bank list and read about how to use the provider list API in our Developer Guide.

### Modulr Portal | File Upload

We've improved the way you make file upload payments! You now have the option to process payments using file upload as Batch or Bulk.

Batch allows a single approval for the entire batch, making it easier and saving you time. Batch uploads are processed with strict processing ON, this means that if any payment within the batch fails, the entire batch will fail.\
Bulk uploads require multiple approvals, for each payment within the file. Bulk uploads are processed with strict processing OFF, this means that any failed payments will show in pending payments, and successful payments will process as normal.

### Modulr Portal | Invite Your Clients to Complete Applications 📬

Partners can now invite their clients to complete applications themselves, removing admin burden and shares the effort with your clients, making your process more efficient and collaborative.

### Modulr Portal | Payment Proof

You can now navigate to the transaction modal to download a proof of payment certificate following payment completion, making it easier to prove that your payment has been sent.
