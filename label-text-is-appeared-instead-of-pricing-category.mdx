## What causes this issue

If travelers see the literal word **"Label"** instead of your actual pricing category name (like **Adult** or **Child**) after selecting a date on the booking form, this is almost always caused by your WordPress site using the **Plain** permalink structure. WP Travel Engine's pricing widget relies on the **Post name** permalink structure to correctly resolve and display pricing category labels.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  This issue affects the display of pricing category names only — it doesn't affect pricing, availability, or the ability to complete a booking.
</Callout>

## Fix the permalink structure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Permalink Settings" title-type="p">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Settings > Permalinks**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Post name" title-type="p">
    Choose the **Post name** option from the list of available permalink structures.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save changes" title-type="p">
    Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify on the trip page" title-type="p">
    Open a trip page, select a date on the booking form, and confirm the pricing categories now display their correct labels instead of "Label."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Image src="https://docs.wptravelengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/permalinks-set-1024x518.png" width="1024" height="518" alt="WordPress Permalinks settings page with the Post name structure selected" />

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Changing your permalink structure can affect existing URLs on your site. If your site depends on a specific permalink structure for SEO or existing links, review the impact before saving, or plan to set up redirects for any URLs that change.
</Callout>

## Still seeing "Label" after switching?

If the issue persists after switching to Post name permalinks:

- Re-save your permalink settings a second time — this forces WordPress to flush its rewrite rules.
- Clear any caching plugin and your browser cache, then reload the trip page.
- Confirm the pricing category itself has a label set under **Trips > Pricing Categories**. See [How to Change the Adult, Child Label](/article/how-to-change-the-adult-child-label) if a category's label field is empty.
