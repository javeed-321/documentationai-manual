## Before you start

After purchasing a WP Travel Engine package plan, you'll receive an email with the username and password associated with your purchase. You'll need these credentials to download your add-ons and premium themes.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  You must be logged in with the email address used for the purchase to see the download button on any add-on or theme page.
</Callout>

## Download your add-ons and themes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account" title-type="p">
    Go to [WP Travel Engine My Account](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/) and log in using the username and password emailed to you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visit the add-on or theme page" title-type="p">
    Navigate to the page for the specific add-on or theme you want to download. For example:

    - Trip Fixed Starting Dates add-on: [wptravelengine.com/plugins/trip-fixed-starting-dates](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/trip-fixed-starting-dates/)
    - Travel Monster Pro theme: [wptravelengine.com/wordpress-travel-themes/travel-monster-pro](https://wptravelengine.com/wordpress-travel-themes/travel-monster-pro/)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Download" title-type="p">
    Once logged in with the email address used for the purchase, a **Download** button appears on the page. Click it to download the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>
