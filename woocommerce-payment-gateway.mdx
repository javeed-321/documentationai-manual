## Overview

With the [WooCommerce Payment Gateway addon](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/woocommerce-payments/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro), you can accept trip payments through various WooCommerce-supported gateways.

This guide walks you through addon installation, setup, and initial configuration.

### Requirements

To use this payment gateway, you need:

- WP Travel Engine plugin version 5.4.0 or later.
- WooCommerce version 6.4 or later.
- WP Travel Engine – WooCommerce Payment Gateway version 1.1.1 or later.

## How to install the WooCommerce Payment Gateway addon

<Callout kind="info">
  See [how to activate the Pro addon license key](https://docs.wptravelengine.com/article/activate-pro-addon-license-key/) for instructions on installing and activating the plugin.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert">
  To use the WooCommerce Payment Gateway in WP Travel Engine, you must first install the [WooCommerce plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce/).
</Callout>

## How to configure the WooCommerce gateway

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Navigate to WooCommerce payment settings">
    Navigate to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > WooCommerce**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the gateway">
    Toggle on the WooCommerce Payment Gateway to switch it on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save and Continue** to secure your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to set the WooCommerce checkout page

When you enable the WooCommerce payment gateway, a checkout page is automatically assigned for each trip. If no page is assigned, or you'd like to use a different page, follow these steps:

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open Page Settings">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > General > Page Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your checkout page">
    Choose the desired checkout page from the **Checkout Page** dropdown menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure related pages">
    Configure additional settings, such as the Thank You Page and Terms and Conditions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save and Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to set the currency in WooCommerce

The final step is to configure the currency in the WooCommerce plugin so that both WP Travel Engine and WooCommerce use the same currency value.

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open WooCommerce general settings">
    Go to **Dashboard > WooCommerce > Settings > General**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the currency options">
    Scroll down to the **Currency options** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Match the WP Travel Engine currency">
    Select the same currency you've configured for WP Travel Engine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trip checkout demo

Once all required configurations are complete, when a customer books a trip and proceeds to checkout, they're directed to the WooCommerce Checkout page.

Customers fill in the required booking details and use the available payment gateway to complete payment. Once payment is complete, they're directed to the WooCommerce Order Received page.

<Callout kind="info">
  As the admin, you can view booking details on the backend through **WP Travel Engine > Bookings** and **WooCommerce > Orders**.
</Callout>
