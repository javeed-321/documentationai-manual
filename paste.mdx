Resource Management is an add-on that lets you set a maximum bookable quantity for rooms and extra services on your trips, and enforces that limit automatically once it's set.

Use it for a room type with only a handful of physical units, or an extra service like a private transfer or gear rental that can only be fulfilled a limited number of times per day.

Works across trips or per trip, your choice. Everything else on your site stays untouched.

## Key Features & Benefits

| Feature                          | Benefit                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Room and service capacity limits | No more selling more rooms or extra services than you physically have.                                                              |
| One rule, many trips             | Apply the same capacity to every departure that shares a room type.                                                                 |
| Flexible capacity resets         | Decide whether a limit applies per departure or is shared across overlapping stays, depending on how you actually manage inventory. |
| Occupancy Calendar               | See rooms occupied and services booked for any date.                                                                                |
| Built-in Waitlist integration    | Release freed-up capacity to a specific waitlisted traveler and notify them directly.                                               |

## Prerequisites

Before using this add-on, make sure:

- You have WordPress 6.5 or higher installed on your site.
- You have WP Travel Engine installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- You have updated WP Travel Engine to its latest version (Version 6.8.5 or above).
- You have installed and activated the Resource Management add-on, with a valid license key.
- You have installed and activated at least one of the following:
  - Accommodation (Version 1.0.9 or more)
  - Extra Services (Version 2.2.2 or more)

## Creating a Capacity Rule

1. From your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Resource Management**.
2. Click **Add Rule** to open the Configure New Capacity Rule panel.
3. **Rule Name**: Enter a descriptive label, such as "Deluxe Room, Summer Season." This is for your own reference on the rules list. Travelers never see it.
4. **Applies To**: Choose **All Trips** to apply the rule everywhere, or **Specific Trips** to restrict it to certain trips. Selecting Specific Trips reveals a drop-down field to search and select individual trips.
5. **Limit to Date Ranges**: Toggle on to restrict the rule to a specific departure date. Set the **Start Date** and **End Date**, and trips falling between those dates will only be eligible for this rule. This is useful for seasonal limits. Trips outside the range use their normal, unrestricted inventory.
   <Note><p>Leave this off if the rule should stay active indefinitely.</p></Note>
6. **Track Capacity For**: Select **Accommodation**, **Extra Services**, or both. Each choice reveals its own restriction section below, so one rule can control rooms and services together.

### Accommodation Restriction

**How should bookings reduce capacity?**: This tells the system how to count your room limit across different departure dates. Choose between:

- **On the departure date only**: Each departure has its own room count. A multi-night booking won't block rooms for other departures.
  <Note><p>Example: Room A is capped at 10 units. A 3-day trip departs on June 1st and June 2nd. Booking 5 units of Room A on June 1st leaves June 1st with 5 units left, but June 2nd still has all 10, since each departure has its own capacity.</p></Note>
- **Across the entire trip duration**: The room stays booked for every night of that stay, so no other trip can use it during that time.
  <Note><p>Example: Room A is capped at 10 units. A trip departs on June 1st for a 5-night stay, and another trip departs on June 3rd for a 4-night stay. If the June 1st trip books 6 units of Room A, those rooms stay held for its whole stay, through June 6th. Since the June 3rd trip's dates fall inside that same window, only 4 units of Room A are left for it to book.</p></Note>

**Room Types & Upgrades**: Select which rooms and upgrades this rule applies to. Each one appears in a table below with an **Exclusive Capacity** box. Enter how many of that room can be booked. In this screenshot, Room A is set to 5 and Room B is set to 10.

<Tip><p>Leave a room's number empty if you just want to see it on the calendar, without setting a limit.</p></Tip>

### Extra Services Restrictions

**How should bookings reduce capacity?**: This works the same way as it does for rooms in Accommodation Restrictions: **On the departure date only** gives each departure its own separate slots, and **across the entire trip duration** shares slots between departures whose dates overlap.

**Services**: Click this field and select which extra services this rule should apply to, for example, Water or Camera. You can select more than one.

Once you've selected a service, it appears in the table below with a **Total Slots** box next to it. Enter the maximum number of bookings that service can receive. Here, Water is set to 5 and Camera to 4, meaning only 5 travelers can book Water and only 4 can book Camera before they show as full.

<Note><p>A service that runs out on one date won't block a different date unless the entire trip duration is selected.</p></Note>

Once everything is set, click **Add Rule** and click **Save Changes**.

## Capacity Management Settings in Accommodation & Extra Services Settings

Resource Management stores the rules and capacity numbers. What travelers actually see on the trip page is controlled separately, in Accommodation's and Extra Services' own settings.

### Capacity Management in Accommodation

From your WP dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Extensions > Accommodation**. Here, select the tab **Capacity Management**.

- **Show Availability Count**: Toggle ON to show an "X Left" badge next to a room once it's close to selling out.
- **Show Sold-out Room**: Toggle ON to keep a fully booked room visible on the trip page, tagged "Sold out," instead of hiding it.
- **Allow Waitlist When a Room is Fully Booked**: Toggle on to allow travelers to join the waitlist for the room, when the room is sold out.
  <Note><p>You need to make sure that the Waitlist add-on is installed, activated, and updated to its latest version (1.0.4) to use this feature.</p></Note>
  You can release rooms to your waitlisters later from the Upcoming Dashboard. It lists all waitlisters for a specific departure, alongside your Trip waitlisters.

Click **Save Settings** to apply.

### Capacity Management in Extra Services

It works in the same way as Accommodation.

1. From your WordPress Dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Extensions > Extra Services**.
2. Under your existing settings, you'll find the same **Show Availability Count** and **Show Sold-out Service** toggles, doing the same job for extra services instead of rooms.

## What Travelers See on the Trip Page

Based on what you have set in Capacity Management for Accommodations and Extra Services, travelers will see availability counts and sold-out resources when booking.

### Accommodation Step in Booking Modal

With **Show Availability Count** on, travelers see a live count next to each room or service as it fills up. With **Show Sold-out Room** or **Show Sold-out Service** on, a fully booked item stays on the page, tagged as sold out, instead of disappearing entirely.

### Extra Service Step in Booking Modal

Extra Services behave the same way on the front end: a live count badge while slots remain, and a "Sold out" tag once a service is full.

## View Available/Booked Resources in Occupancy Calendar

1. From your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Resource Management > Occupancy Calendar**.
2. Each day shows how many rooms are occupied and how many services are booked, based on every active rule. Click a day to open its breakdown.
3. The detail panel shows all the rooms and extra services booked that day. You can also see how many rooms/services are booked per trip.

<Note><p>The calendar only shows rooms and services that have actually been booked. If you release extra room or seat capacity for someone on the waitlist, that extra capacity does not show up on the calendar right away. It only appears once that traveler actually completes their booking.</p></Note>

## Releasing Room Capacity from Upcoming Tours Dashboard

1. From your WordPress dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Upcoming Tours**. Click **Waitlist Details** on the departure row.
2. Under **Room Capacity Management**, set how many extra seats to release for each room.
3. These released seats are not shown on the trip page for new visitors to book. They're only reserved for travelers already on the waitlist for this trip. Once you release seats, you can choose which waitlisted traveler gets access to them.

<Note><p>Want to know how to notify a waitlisted traveler once you've released seats for them, or how the waitlist feature works overall? Check out our Waitlist documentation.</p></Note>

Additionally, released room capacity can't exceed the total seats released for the trip. A validation message appears if the numbers don't line up.

If a trip uses Activity Tour Booking, travelers choose a specific time, like 9:00 AM or 2:00 PM, instead of just a date. When you release extra capacity for a waitlisted traveler on this kind of trip, that release only applies to the time they were waitlisted for. For example, releasing a seat for the 9:00 AM tour won't open up any extra seats on the 2:00 PM tour of the same day.

## Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Capping a Popular Room Type Across the Season

**Situation:** An operator sells a Deluxe Room with only 10 physical units, but several trip departures list it as bookable. Without a shared limit, overlapping departures could sell more rooms than exist.

**Implementation:**

1. Go to **WP Travel Engine Dashboard > Resource Management** and click **Add Rule**.
2. Name it "Deluxe Room, Season Cap" and set **Applies To** to **All Trips**.
3. Under **Track Capacity For**, tick **Accommodation**.
4. Select the Deluxe Room and set its capacity to 10.
5. Choose the entire trip duration so overlapping stays share the same pool.
6. Click **Save Changes**.

**Benefit:** Every departure checks against the same 10-room limit. No trip can oversell the room, no matter how many departures list it.

### Use Case 2: Reserving a Freed-Up Seat for a Specific Waitlisted Traveler

**Situation:** A trip's Ocean View room sold out, but a returning customer on the waitlist wants first access if a cancellation opens up.

**Implementation:**

1. Go to **WP Travel Engine Dashboard > Upcoming Tours** and open the trip's **View Details**.
2. Under **Room Capacity Management**, release 1 seat for Ocean View.
3. Find the customer's waitlist entry and click **Send Email**.
4. Send the private booking link.

**Benefit:** Only that traveler can book the newly opened room. Their status updates are notified once the email goes out, so it's clear they've been contacted.
