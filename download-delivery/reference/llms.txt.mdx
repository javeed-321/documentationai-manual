# Download Delivery Documentation

> A reliable, high performance file-based content delivery solution, Download Delivery delivers large (>100 MB) file-based content over the Internet. Built on the globally distributed Akamai Platform for superior capacity, scalability, availability, and performance, it provides clear, comprehensive metrics and optional tools that can monitor and manage the entire download process across your customer base. Download Delivery ensures a predictable, high-quality download experience while helping you address your online distribution goals.
