## Add PayPal Standard to your checkout

PayPal Standard lets you accept trip payments through WP Travel Engine. A payment gateway is the service that collects money from your traveler at checkout. Once you turn it on, travelers will see PayPal Standard as a payment option when they book a trip on your site.

If you run guided hikes, day tours, or multi-day packages, this gives your customers another way to pay. For example, someone booking a two-day trekking package could choose PayPal Standard instead of another payment method at checkout. You only need to set it up once. Then you can move on to taking bookings.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Before you start, make sure WP Travel Engine is active on your WordPress site and you have your PayPal or merchant account ID ready. Your WordPress dashboard is the main admin area of your website. The menu you need is in the left sidebar.
</Callout>

## Set up PayPal Standard

What you'll need before you start: WP Travel Engine must already be active on your site, and you need your PayPal or merchant account ID ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your WordPress dashboard" title-type="p">
    This gets you into the part of your website where you change settings. In WordPress, the dashboard is the main admin area you see after you log in.

    Log in to your WordPress dashboard.

    Once you are in, you are ready to open the payment settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to PayPal Standard settings" title-type="p">
    This is where you connect your site to your PayPal account. The sidebar is the menu on the left side of your screen.

    From the left sidebar in your dashboard, go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > PayPal Standard**.

    The settings area is where plugin options are stored. When you reach the PayPal Standard page, you are in the right place to add your account details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your PayPal or merchant account ID" title-type="p">
    This step makes sure booking payments go to the right account.

    Enter your PayPal or merchant account ID.

    <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
      Use a valid ID. WP Travel Engine uses this ID to process payments for your trips.
    </Callout>

    If you offer a trekking package or a city day tour, the payment from that booking will be sent through this PayPal account. That is why the account ID needs to be correct before you continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes" title-type="p">
    This keeps the PayPal Standard details you just entered.

    Click **Save Changes**.

    After you save, you can move on to turning PayPal Standard on for checkout.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable PayPal Standard as a payment option" title-type="p">
    This is the step that makes PayPal Standard visible to travelers during booking.

    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > General**. Then check the box to enable PayPal Standard so it appears as a payment option during checkout.

    For example, when someone books a weekend rafting trip on your site, they will be able to choose PayPal Standard while paying if this box is enabled.

    Once that box is checked, your setup is nearly done.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save your changes again" title-type="p">
    This final save makes the payment option show on your booking form.

    Click **Save Changes**.

    <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-fd8ba769-7008-47cf-98f1-e0d78d98350b/doc-08c376b3-8054-4dc6-b874-0c65a04a623e/1787566612204-ap6ojz6h9g-Paypal-General-setings-1024x399.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1024" height="399" alt="" />

    After that, PayPal Standard should be ready for travelers to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm the setup

After you finish these steps, PayPal Standard is available as a payment gateway for travelers booking trips on your site.

That means your customers can choose it during checkout when they book with you. You are now ready to test a booking or continue setting up the rest of your payment options.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  PayPal Standard currently supports transactions only in USD (United States Dollar). Other currencies will not work for receiving payments through this integration.
</Callout>
