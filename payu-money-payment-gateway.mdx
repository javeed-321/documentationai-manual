## Overview

[PayU Money Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/payu-money-payment-gateway/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) is an extension for WP Travel Engine that lets you accept trip and tour payments through PayU Money, one of India's leading financial services.

PayU Money Payment Gateway supports credit card and debit card payments, and only accepts payment in Indian Rupee (INR) currency.

<Callout kind="info">
  PayU Money Payment Gateway requires [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) v3.0.5 or later.
</Callout>

## Requirements

You must have an active merchant account with PayU Money to use this payment gateway. You'll need:

- A PayU Money Merchant Key
- A PayU Money Merchant Salt

## Installation

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Download the extension">
    Download the extension from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin folder">
    Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin">
    Activate **WP Travel Engine – PayU Money Payment Gateway** from the Plugins menu within WordPress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the gateway

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Open PayU Money Bolt settings">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments > PayU Money Bolt**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your merchant credentials">
    Click **PayU Money Bolt** to configure the settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your currency to INR">
    Go to **Trips > Settings > Misc** and choose Indian Rupee as the currency code, since PayU Money only accepts INR.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField name="Merchant Key" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your PayU Money Merchant Key.
</ParamField>

<ParamField name="Merchant Salt" param-type="string" required="true">
  Your PayU Money Merchant Salt.
</ParamField>

Once configured, when a traveler books a trip through the PayU Money Payment Gateway, they see a payment popup on the same screen to complete a secure payment.
