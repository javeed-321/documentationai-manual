## Add a fee to each booking automatically

Add a small percentage-based fee to each booking at checkout. The fee calculates itself from the booking total, so you do not need to change your prices by hand or ask for extra payment later.

Say you take credit card payments and the processing fees cut into what you earn. Or you want to add a small service charge to cover the time you spend coordinating each booking.

This gives you a clear way to recover part of those costs without changing the listed price of every trek, day tour, or multi-day package. Before you turn it on, it helps to know what the add-on can do.

## Key features

- **Percentage-based fee:** Set one percentage that works across all trips on your site, whether you sell day tours, treks, or longer packages.
- **Custom label:** Call the fee whatever makes sense for your business, like **Service Fee** or **Booking Charge**.
- **Helpful description:** Add a short explanation that appears when customers hover over the fee name, so they understand what they are paying for.
- **Automatic calculation:** You never need to recalculate the fee by hand. You set the percentage once and the add-on does the math for every booking from then on.
- **One-time charge:** The fee is only added to the first payment. If a customer pays in installments, they will not see the fee again on later payments.
- **Visible at checkout:** Customers see the fee listed clearly before they pay, so there are no surprises.

That gives you a steady way to cover extra costs while keeping your prices clear. Next, make sure the basic pieces are in place.

## What you'll need before you start

You need a few things in place before the booking fee can work.

- **WP Travel Engine installed and activated.** A plugin is a small tool you add to WordPress to give your website more features. WP Travel Engine is the plugin that runs your trip booking system.
- **The Booking Fee add-on installed and activated with a valid license key.** An add-on is an extra tool that connects to WP Travel Engine to add one specific feature.
- **At least one trip set up in WP Travel Engine** so you can test the fee on a real booking flow.
- **At least one payment method configured,** like PayPal or Stripe, so customers can complete checkout.

Once these are ready, you can move on with confidence.

## How the fee works with partial payments

This matters if you let customers pay a deposit first and the rest later.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  The booking fee is calculated on the full booking subtotal before any taxes are added. If a customer chooses to pay a partial amount, like a 20% deposit, the booking fee is still calculated on the full subtotal. The deposit amount is then worked out after the fee is added. The fee is only charged once, on the first payment. When the customer pays the remaining balance later, no additional booking fee is added.
</Callout>

Say a customer books a $1,000 trek and you have set a 3 booking fee. The fee is $30, calculated on the full $1,000. If the customer pays a 20 deposit, that deposit is applied after the fee is added. They pay the $30 fee as part of their first payment. When they pay the rest later, no fee is charged again.

It is a good idea to mention the fee in your booking summary or tooltip, so customers know about it before checkout. A short explanation helps people feel comfortable with the charge. If anything does not look right, check the troubleshooting tips below.

## Troubleshooting

### I turned on the booking fee, but it's not showing at checkout

Make sure you clicked **Save** after enabling the fee. Also check that the toggle at the top of the Booking Fee settings is switched on.

If both are in place, test another booking and see if the fee appears.

### The fee amount looks higher than I expected

The percentage is calculated on the booking subtotal, which is the trip price before taxes. If your trip price already includes taxes, the fee might come out higher than you planned. Try lowering the percentage to match what you want to charge.

A small change in the percentage can make a big difference, so test it once after you update it.

### Customers paying in installments are seeing the fee twice

That should not happen. The booking fee is only added to the first payment, not later ones. Try saving your settings again. If it keeps happening, reach out to our support team and we will look into it.

You are not expected to troubleshoot that alone.

### I changed the percentage but the old amount is still showing

Your website might be showing a cached version of the page. A cache is a saved copy of a page that helps it load faster. Wait a few minutes and check again. If it still does not update, ask your hosting provider about clearing the cache.

Once the cache refreshes, the new amount should appear.

### The fee label or description isn't appearing at checkout

Make sure you typed something in the **Booking Fee Label** field. If the description is not showing, confirm you entered text in the **Booking Fee Description** field and clicked **Save**.

If the fee still does not behave the way you expect, the questions below may help.

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I charge a fixed dollar amount instead of a percentage?" default-open="false">
    No. The booking fee is percentage-based. If you need a fixed amount, you can estimate the closest percentage by comparing your desired fee to your usual trip price.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I set a different booking fee for different trips?" default-open="false">
    No. The percentage you set applies to all trips on your site. If you need different fees for different trips, reach out to our support team to talk through your options.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will customers see the fee before they pay?" default-open="false">
    Yes. The fee appears as a separate line item on the checkout page, along with the label and description you set up.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Does the fee apply if a customer adds extra services?" default-open="false">
    Yes. The booking fee is calculated on the full booking subtotal, which includes any extra services the customer adds to their booking, such as gear rental or other paid add-ons.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens to the fee if a customer cancels?" default-open="false">
    The booking fee follows the same refund rules as the rest of the booking amount. How refunds are handled depends on your payment gateway and your cancellation policy.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
