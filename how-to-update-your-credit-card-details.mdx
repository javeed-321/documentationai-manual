## Why this matters

Keeping your payment information current ensures your WP Travel Engine subscription renewals process smoothly and without interruption. Updating your card follows a process similar to managing your subscription.

## Update your credit card

<Steps title-type="h3">
  <Step title="Go to your account page">
    Visit the [WP Travel Engine Account Page](https://wptravelengine.com/my-account/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in with your registered email and password. If you've forgotten your password, click **Lost Password** to reset it. Make sure you use the email associated with your plugin purchase.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Subscription tab">
    Once logged in, go to your dashboard and click the **Subscription** tab to see all your active subscriptions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Update Payment">
    In the **Actions** column next to your subscription, click **Update Payment**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your new card details">
    Fill in the required fields: name on the card, credit card number, and expiration date and CVV if required. Double-check everything for accuracy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and save">
    Click **Update Payment Method** to apply the changes. You'll get a confirmation once your payment details are successfully updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important notes

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keeping your card details updated prevents failed renewals and ensures uninterrupted access to premium features.
</Callout>

- If a renewal fails because your card is expired or invalid, you'll receive an email notification. Updating your card promptly resolves the issue.
- You can update your card details at any time, even before your next renewal date.
