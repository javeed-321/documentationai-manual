## Overview

[PayHere Payment Gateway](https://wptravelengine.com/plugins/payhere-payment-gateway/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) is an extension for WP Travel Engine that lets tour operators accept trip and tour payments through PayHere, one of Sri Lanka's popular merchant services.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  PayHere Payment Gateway requires [WP Travel Engine](https://wptravelengine.com/?utm_source=free_plugin\&utm_medium=docs\&utm_campaign=upgrade_to_pro) v3.0.5 or later.
</Callout>

## Requirements

You must have an active merchant account with [PayHere](https://www.payhere.lk/) to use this payment gateway. You'll need:

- A PayHere Merchant ID
- A PayHere Merchant Secret

Payments from any country can be accepted, as long as you use LKR, USD, EUR, GBP, or AUD as the transaction currency.

## Installation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the extension" title-type="p">
    Download the extension from your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the plugin folder" title-type="p">
    Upload the folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the plugin" title-type="p">
    Activate **WP Travel Engine – PayHere Payment Gateway** from the Plugins menu within WordPress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure the gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment settings" title-type="p">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Payments**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select PayHere" title-type="p">
    You'll see PayHere as an option to enable the PayHere Payment Gateway for trip bookings. Click **PayHere** to configure its settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your merchant credentials" title-type="p">
    Enter your Merchant ID and Merchant Secret.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a supported currency" title-type="p">
    Go to **WP Travel Engine > Settings > Miscellaneous** and choose a supported currency — Sri Lanka Rupee, United States Dollar, Euro, Pound Sterling, or Australian Dollar — as the Currency Code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<ParamField param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="true">
  Your PayHere Merchant ID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField param-type="string" required="true" deprecated="false" show-location="true">
  Your PayHere Merchant Secret code.
</ParamField>

Once configured, when a traveler books a trip through the PayHere Payment Gateway, they're taken to PayHere to complete a secure payment.
